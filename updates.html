分裂八维阿贝尔簇上 Weil 类的代数性

证明中用于得到负双点的稳定化迹论证存在符号错误。按照论文的带符号双点约定，记 I(f1)=−mI(f_1)=-mI(f1​)=−m，其中 m>0m>0m>0；原证明把每个反向稳定化迹的符号取为 +1+1+1，据此声称插入 mmm 个这样的迹后，带符号计数为零。

计入标准尖点两条分支相反的源定向后，每个反向迹在这一约定下的符号应为 −1-1−1，因此新的计数为 Inew=I(f1)−m=−2m≠0I_{\mathrm{new}}=I(f_1)-m=-2m\ne0Inew​=I(f1​)−m=−2m=0。

此次撤回针对证明，并不表示数学命题为假。

英文原文

The proof contains a sign error in the stabilization-trace argument used to obtain negative double points. In the manuscript's signed double-point convention, write I(f1)=−mI(f_1)=-mI(f1​)=−m with m>0m>0m>0. The proof assigns each reverse stabilization trace sign +1+1+1 and therefore claims that inserting mmm such traces makes the signed count zero.

Accounting for the opposite source orientations of the two branches of the standard cusp gives sign −1-1−1 for each reverse trace in this convention. The resulting count is therefore

Inew=I(f1)−m=−2m≠0.I_{\mathrm{new}}=I(f_1)-m=-2m\ne0.Inew​=I(f1​)−m=−2m=0.

The Eliashberg-Murphy cancellation theorem invoked at this step requires zero signed double-point count, so its hypothesis is not met. The subsequent oriented-surgery and embedded-brane construction is therefore unsupported, and the paper does not establish its claimed algebraicity theorem.

This withdrawal concerns the proof; it does not assert that the mathematical statement is false.
