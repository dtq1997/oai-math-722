广义 Mukai 猜想

The generalized Mukai conjecture

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

证明了广义 Mukai 猜想：每个维数为正的光滑复射影 Fano 流形，若维数为 nnn、Picard 数为 ρ\rhoρ、伪指数为 ι\iotaι，则满足 ρ(ι−1)≤n\rho(\iota-1)\le nρ(ι−1)≤n，且等号恰在 (Pι−1)ρ(\mathbb P^{\iota-1})^\rho(Pι−1)ρ 时成立。

这里伪指数是有理曲线的最小反典范次数。

英文原文

Proves the generalized Mukai conjecture: every positive-dimensional smooth complex projective Fano manifold of dimension nnn, Picard number ρ\rhoρ and pseudoindex ι\iotaι satisfies ρ(ι−1)≤n\rho(\iota-1)\le nρ(ι−1)≤n, with equality exactly for (Pι−1)ρ(\mathbb P^{\iota-1})^\rho(Pι−1)ρ.

Here the pseudoindex is the least anticanonical degree of a rational curve.

    The generalized Mukai conjecture

    摘要

    我们证明了广义 Mukai 猜想：每个维数为 n、Picard 数为 ρ、伪指数为 ι 的正维光滑复 Fano 流形都满足 ρ(ι−1)≤n\rho(\iota-1)\le nρ(ι−1)≤n。

    等号成立当且仅当它是 ρ 个 Pι−1\mathbb P^{\iota-1}Pι−1 的乘积。

    英文原文

    We prove the generalized Mukai conjecture: every positive-dimensional smooth complex Fano manifold of dimension n, Picard number ρ, and pseudoindex ι satisfies ρ(ι−1)≤n\rho(\iota-1)\le nρ(ι−1)≤n.

    Equality holds precisely for the product of ρ copies of Pι−1\mathbb P^{\iota-1}Pι−1.
