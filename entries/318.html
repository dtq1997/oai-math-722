chromatic 分裂的反例与滤过

Chromatic splitting: counterexamples and filtrations

OpenAI · 2026-10-065 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

否定了素数 p≥5p\ge5p≥5 时高度三处的强 chromatic 分裂，以及导出 ppp-完备化球谱在高度 ppp（p≥5p\ge5p≥5）和高度 p+1p+1p+1（p≥7p\ge7p≥7）处的弱分裂。

不过，当 n≥1n\ge1n≥1 且 p>n+1p>n+1p>n+1 时，重叠部分 Ln−1LK(n)Sp∧L_{n-1}L_{K(n)}S_p^\wedgeLn−1​LK(n)​Sp∧​ 具有一个由所预测的局部化球谱块组成的 2n2^n2n 阶滤过。

在高度三、素数三处，在导出完备化球谱上的 E(2)E(2)E(2)-局部模范畴中，甚至无法由这样的块进行有限拼装。

英文原文

Disproves strong chromatic splitting at height three for primes p≥5p\ge5p≥5, and weak splitting for the derived ppp-completed sphere at heights ppp (p≥5p\ge5p≥5) and p+1p+1p+1 (p≥7p\ge7p≥7).

Nevertheless, for n≥1n\ge1n≥1 and p>n+1p>n+1p>n+1, the overlap Ln−1LK(n)Sp∧L_{n-1}L_{K(n)}S_p^\wedgeLn−1​LK(n)​Sp∧​ admits a 2n2^n2n-stage filtration by the predicted localized-sphere pieces.

At height three and prime three, even finite assembly from such pieces fails in the category of E(2)E(2)E(2)-local modules over the derived completed sphere.

  2. 高度三的chromatic 交叠：显式滤过及其粘接映射

    The height-three chromatic overlap: an explicit filtration and its attachments

    摘要

    对每个素数 p ≥ 5，我们利用高度三的 chromatic 分裂模式中的局部球谱层，构造 L2LK(3)Sp∧L_2L_{K(3)}\mathbb S_p^\wedgeL2​LK(3)​Sp∧​ 的一个显式八阶段滤过。

    从其第一阶段到交叠对象的映射是典范单位映射，且两个带符号的断裂公式确定所选局部映射与相容同伦的所有粘接映射。

    一个配套的典范映射定理进一步推出，第一个高度一的粘接映射非零。

    英文原文

    For every prime p ≥ 5, we construct an explicit eight-stage filtration of L2LK(3)Sp∧L_2L_{K(3)}\mathbb S_p^\wedgeL2​LK(3)​Sp∧​ by the local-sphere layers in the height-three chromatic-splitting pattern.

    The map from its first stage to the overlap is the canonical unit, and two signed fracture formulas identify all attachments for the chosen local maps and compatibility homotopy.

    A companion canonical-map theorem further implies that the first height-one attachment is nonzero.

  5. 弱chromatic 分裂的反例：球谱核与下降指数

    Counterexamples to weak chromatic splitting: sphere kernels and descent exponents

    摘要

    对于球谱的导出 p 完备化，对每个素数 p ≥ 5，在高度 p 处，以及对每个素数 p ≥ 7，在高度 p+1p+1p+1 处，典范弱 chromatic 分裂映射均不存在同伦意义下的缩回映射。

    经典的球谱乘积 β1(p−1)2\beta_1^{(p-1)^2}β1(p−1)2​ 在这两个范围内都给出一个非零核类。

    英文原文

    For the derived p-completion of the sphere, the canonical weak chromatic splitting map has no homotopy retraction at height p for every prime p ≥ 5, and at height p+1p+1p+1 for every prime p ≥ 7.

    The classical sphere product β1(p−1)2\beta_1^{(p-1)^2}β1(p−1)2​ gives a nonzero kernel class in both ranges.
