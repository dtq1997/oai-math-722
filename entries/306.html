三球面中纽结的 purely cosmetic Dehn 手术猜想

The purely cosmetic surgery conjecture for knots in S3S^3S3

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

对 S3S^3S3 中的非平凡光滑纽结，沿不同斜率进行 Dehn 手术永远不会得到保定向同胚的流形，从而证明了 purely cosmetic Dehn 手术猜想。

该陈述包括子午斜率。

英文原文

Distinct Dehn surgery slopes on a nontrivial smooth knot in S3S^3S3 never produce orientation-preservingly homeomorphic manifolds, proving the purely cosmetic surgery conjecture.

The statement includes the meridional slope.

论文

  1. 三维球面中纽结的 purely cosmetic Dehn 手术

    Purely cosmetic surgery on knots in the three-sphere

    摘要

    我们证明了三维球面中纽结的 purely cosmetic Dehn 手术猜想。

    非平凡光滑纽结上不同的 Dehn 手术斜率，绝不会给出保定向同胚的流形。

    英文原文

    We prove the purely cosmetic surgery conjecture for knots in the three-sphere.

    Distinct Dehn surgery slopes on a nontrivial smooth knot never give orientation-preservingly homeomorphic manifolds.
