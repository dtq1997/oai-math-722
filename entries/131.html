指定度序列的图切换链多项式混合

Polynomial mixing of graph switches with prescribed degrees

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

解决了简单无向图情形的 Kannan–Tetali–Vempala 猜想：对于每个可由图实现的带标签度数序列，惰性换边链都在 O(n8)O(n^8)O(n8) 时间内混合。

对于这些具有相同度数约束的图，还可以用一个几乎必然终止、期望比特运行时间为多项式的算法进行精确均匀采样。

英文原文

Resolves the simple-undirected Kannan–Tetali–Vempala conjecture: the lazy edge-switch chain mixes in O(n8)O(n^8)O(n8) time for every graphical labeled degree sequence.

The same degree-constrained graphs can also be sampled exactly uniformly by an almost-surely terminating algorithm with expected polynomial bit running time.

    We prove the simple-undirected form of the Kannan–Tetali–Vempala conjecture: the switch chain on simple undirected graphs mixes in polynomial time for every graphical degree sequence.

    We also give an exactly uniform sampler for every graphical labeled degree vector.

    It uses unbiased random bits, terminates almost surely, and has expected polynomial bit running time.

# Rapid mixing of graph switches for every degree sequence

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Polynomial mixing of the switch chain for every graphical degree sequence](../../preprints/Polynomial-Mixing-of-the-Switch-Chain-for-Every-Graphical-Degree-Sequence-September-25-2026/main.pdf)

## Scope

The Kannan–Tetali–Vempala conjecture asks for polynomial mixing of the switch chain for every graphical degree sequence. The formalization proves the simple undirected case: for $n\ge4$, the lazy chain that proposes switches on four vertices has total-variation mixing time at distance $1/4$ at most $2n^8$. It also proves switch connectivity, the stated exponential total-variation bound, and a spectral gap of at least $1/(24n^2\binom n4)$ when more than one graph realizes the degree sequence.

The paper's exactly uniform sampling algorithm is outside these selected chain estimates.
