Boone–Higman 猜想与高阶有限性

The Boone–Higman conjecture and higher finiteness

OpenAI · 2026-10-063 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

一个有限生成群的字问题可判定，当且仅当它可嵌入某个有限展示单群，从而证明 Boone–Higman 猜想。

目标群可以具有F∞F_\inftyF∞​型：即有一个在每一维都只有有限个胞腔的分类空间。

还可以用单个F∞F_\inftyF∞​型群包含每个有限展示群。

英文原文

A finitely generated group has decidable word problem exactly when it embeds in a finitely presented simple group, proving the Boone–Higman conjecture.

The target can have type F∞F_\inftyF∞​: a classifying space with finitely many cells in each dimension.

A single group of type F∞F_\inftyF∞​ can also contain every finitely presented group.

  3. 一个 F∞ 型通用群

    A universal group of type F∞

    主结果已列入形式化清单
    摘要

    我们构造了一个包含每个有限展示群的单一的 F∞ 型群。

    它的有限生成子群在同构意义下恰好是所有有限生成递归展示群。

    这回答了高维 Higman 嵌入问题的 F∞ 形式。

    英文原文

    We construct a single group of type F∞ containing every finitely presented group.

    Its finitely generated subgroups, up to isomorphism, are exactly the finitely generated recursively presented groups.

    This answers the F∞ form of the higher-dimensional Higman embedding question.

# Boone–Higman embeddings with higher finiteness

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Finite algebraic envelopes and the Boone–Higman conjecture](../../preprints/Finite-algebraic-envelopes-and-the-Boone-Higman-conjecture-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)
- [Simple $F_\infty$ overgroups of groups with decidable word problem](../../preprints/Simple-F-infinity-overgroups-of-groups-with-decidable-word-problem-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)
- [A universal group of type $F_\infty$](../../preprints/A-universal-group-of-type-F-infinity-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The Boone–Higman conjecture characterizes finitely generated groups with decidable word problem by embeddings into finitely presented simple groups. The formalization proves the equivalence in full: a finitely generated group has a computable word problem for a finite generating set exactly when it embeds by an injective homomorphism into a finitely presented simple group.

The formalization proves the higher-finiteness strengthening of the Boone–Higman conjecture: every finitely generated group with decidable word problem embeds by an injective homomorphism into a nontrivial simple group of type $F_\infty$. Here type $F_\infty$ means that the group has a classifying CW complex with finitely many cells in each dimension. No additional finiteness property of the original group is assumed.

The formalized result constructs one group $H$ of type $F_\infty$ containing every finitely presented group. Here type $F_\infty$ means that $H$ has a classifying space with finitely many cells in each dimension.

The group $H$ is fixed before the groups embedded into it are chosen. No word-problem assumption is imposed, and the classifying space need not be finite-dimensional or have finitely many cells in total. The paper's converse about recursively presented subgroups is not included.
