熵光子数不等式

The entropy photon-number inequality

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

对任意有限个模式中两个相互独立的有限能量玻色输入经分束器混合的情形，证明了熵光子数不等式：输出的熵光子数不小于各输入的熵光子数按透射率加权的平均值。

允许每个输入内部存在任意纠缠，而乘积热态输入即使熵不同也能达到等号。

英文原文

Proves the entropy photon-number inequality for beam-splitter mixing of two independent finite-energy bosonic inputs in any finite number of modes: the output’s entropy photon number is at least the transmissivity-weighted average of the inputs\textquotesingle.

Arbitrary entanglement within each input is allowed, and product thermal inputs attain equality even when their entropies differ.

论文

  1. 熵光子数不等式

    The entropy photon-number inequality

    摘要

    对每个有限的模式数，我们证明了两个独立、平均能量有限的玻色输入的熵光子数不等式。

    这解决了有限能量情形下的熵光子数猜想，同时允许任一输入内部的各模式之间存在任意纠缠。

    作为推论，对同一个热衰减信道的每个有限张量幂，我们在允许模式间纠缠的有限能量输入中，确定了固定输入熵时的精确最小输出熵。

    我们还得到，平均光子数约束下退化的双接收者纯损耗玻色广播信道的精确经典容量区域。

    英文原文

    We prove the entropy photon-number inequality for two independent bosonic inputs with finite mean energy, for every finite number of modes.

    This resolves the entropy photon-number conjecture in this finite-energy setting while allowing arbitrary entanglement among the modes within either input.

    As a consequence, we determine the exact minimum output entropy at fixed input entropy for every finite tensor power of an identical thermal attenuator, over finite-energy inputs that may be entangled across modes.

    We also obtain the exact classical capacity region of the degraded two-receiver pure-loss bosonic broadcast channel under a mean photon constraint.

# The entropy photon-number inequality

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [The entropy photon-number inequality](../../preprints/The-entropy-photon-number-inequality-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The formalization proves the entropy photon-number inequality for two independent bosonic inputs with finite mean energy and any finite positive number $n$ of modes. Write $N(\rho)=g^{-1}(S(\rho)/n)$, where $S$ is von Neumann entropy and $g(x)=(x+1)\log(x+1)-x\log x$ is the thermal entropy per mode. For a beam splitter of transmissivity $0\le\eta\le1$, its output satisfies $N(\rho_C)\ge\eta N(\rho_A)+(1-\eta)N(\rho_B)$.

Entanglement among modes within either input is allowed. The thermal-attenuator minimum-output-entropy and broadcast-capacity consequences in the paper are outside this selected inequality.
