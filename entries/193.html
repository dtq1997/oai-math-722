Serre 交重数猜想

Serre's intersection-multiplicity conjecture

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

证明了任意正则局部环上的非零有限生成模的 Serre 交重数 χR(M,N)\chi^R(M,N)χR(M,N) 严格为正，条件是 M⊗RNM\otimes_R NM⊗R​N 具有有限长度且 dim⁡M+dim⁡N=dim⁡R\dim M+\dim N=\dim RdimM+dimN=dimR。

这解决了正性猜想，包括分歧混合特征的情形。

Proves strict positivity of Serre's intersection multiplicity χR(M,N)\chi^R(M,N)χR(M,N) for nonzero finitely generated modules over any regular local ring, provided M⊗RNM\otimes_R NM⊗R​N has finite length and dim⁡M+dim⁡N=dim⁡R\dim M+\dim N=\dim RdimM+dimN=dimR.

  1. Serre 交重数的正性

    Positivity of Serre's Intersection Multiplicity

    摘要

    我们证明了 Serre 的正性猜想，其中包括分歧混合特征情形。

    若正则局部环上的两个非零有限生成模的张量积具有有限长度，且二者维数互补，则它们的交重数严格为正。

    英文原文

    We prove Serre's positivity conjecture, including ramified mixed characteristic.

    If two nonzero finitely generated modules over a regular local ring have tensor product of finite length and complementary dimensions, then their intersection multiplicity is strictly positive.
