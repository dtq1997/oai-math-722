小 Cohen–Macaulay 模猜想反例

A counterexample to the small Cohen–Macaulay module conjecture

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

构造了一个 C\mathbb CC 上的三维完备 Noether 正规局部整环，它没有非零有限生成的极大 Cohen–Macaulay 模。

一个在 C\mathbb CC 上本质有限型的三维局部整环也具有同样性质，从而否定了小 Cohen–Macaulay 模猜想的整环形式。

英文原文

Constructs a three-dimensional complete Noetherian normal local domain over C\mathbb CC with no nonzero finitely generated maximal Cohen–Macaulay module.

A three-dimensional local domain essentially of finite type over C\mathbb CC has the same property, disproving the domain form of the small Cohen–Macaulay module conjecture.
