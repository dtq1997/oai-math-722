    摘要

    对于每个固定的 ε > 0，我们为 ℝd 上极小点已知且 Hessian 介于 Id 和 2Id2I_d2Id​ 之间的 C2 势函数给出一个采样算法，该算法在每次执行中至多使用 CεdεC_\varepsilon d^\varepsilonCε​dε 次精确一阶查询。

    输出与目标 Gibbs 分布的全变差距离至多为 1/10。

    查询之间的计算不受限制。

    我们还对任意随机自适应算法证明了 Ω(log⁡d)\Omega(\log d)Ω(logd) 的查询下界，从而确定最优维数指数为零。

    英文原文

    For every fixed ε > 0, we give a sampling algorithm using at most CεdεC_\varepsilon d^\varepsilonCε​dε exact first-order queries on every execution for C2 potentials on ℝd with a known minimizer and Hessian between Id and 2Id2I_d2Id​.

    The output has total-variation distance at most 1/10 from the target Gibbs law.

    Computation between queries is unrestricted.

    We also prove an Ω(log⁡d)\Omega(\log d)Ω(logd) query lower bound for arbitrary randomized adaptive algorithms, determining the optimal dimension exponent to be zero.

# Subpolynomial query complexity for log-concave sampling

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Subpolynomial query complexity for well-conditioned log-concave sampling](../../preprints/Subpolynomial-query-complexity-for-well-conditioned-log-concave-sampling-September-26-2026/article.pdf)

## Scope

The formalized result determines the dimension exponent of exact value-and-gradient query complexity for well-conditioned log-concave sampling. For potentials with $V(0)=0$, $\nabla V(0)=0$, and $I\le\nabla^2V\le2I$, the least worst-case query budget achieving total-variation error at most $1/10$ is at most $C_\varepsilon d^\varepsilon$ for every $\varepsilon>0$, and at least $c\log d$ eventually. Hence the infimal polynomial exponent is zero. Algorithms may be measurable, randomized, and adaptive; arithmetic and bit costs are not bounded.
