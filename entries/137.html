以时间的五分之二次幂空间模拟单带图灵机

One-tape time simulation in two-fifths-power space

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

使用 O(T2/5log⁡C(T+2))O(T^{2/5}\log^C(T+2))O(T2/5logC(T+2)) 空间，确定一台固定的确定性单可写带机器截至时间 TTT 的停机情况和有限控制状态，从而改进了平方根指数。

每个磁头每步至多移动一个格子；允许有有限多个只读输入头。

初始内容与 TTT 无关，且带上内容和输入符号均可在多对数空间内访问。

模拟时间不受限制。

英文原文

Determines the halting and finite-control outcome of a fixed deterministic one-writable-tape machine up to time TTT using O(T2/5log⁡C(T+2))O(T^{2/5}\log^C(T+2))O(T2/5logC(T+2)) space, improving the square-root exponent.

Heads move at most one cell per step; finitely many read-only input heads are allowed.

Initial contents are independent of TTT, and contents and input symbols have polylogarithmic-space access.

Simulation time is unrestricted.

论文

  1. 以时间的五分之二次幂空间模拟单带计算

    Simulating One-Tape Time in Two-Fifths-Power Space

    摘要

    我们表明，当给定二进制编码的时间上限 T ≥ 2 时，一台固定的、具有一条可写带和一个读写头的确定性图灵机，可以用 O(T2/5polylog(T+2))O(T^{2/5}\mathop{\mathrm{polylog}}\nolimits (T+2))O(T2/5polylog(T+2)) 位工作空间模拟。

    模拟器计算原机器截至时刻 T 的有限控制状态和停机结果；模拟器自身的运行时间不受限制。

    该结果允许固定数目的只读输入头，并要求一个固定的访问器，它在多对数空间内提供距离相应读写头初始位置至多 T 的每个初始可写符号和只读符号。

    这改进了单带机器的平方根空间指数，回答了 Williams 对这一模型提出的问题。

    英文原文

    We show that a fixed deterministic Turing machine with one writable tape and head can be simulated in O(T2/5polylog(T+2))O(T^{2/5}\mathop{\mathrm{polylog}}\nolimits (T+2))O(T2/5polylog(T+2)) work-space bits when a binary time cap T ≥ 2 is supplied.

    The simulator computes the finite-control and halting outcome by time T; its running time is unrestricted.

    The result allows a fixed number of read-only input heads and requires a fixed accessor that supplies every initial writable and read-only symbol within distance T of the relevant head origin in polylogarithmic space.

    This improves the square-root space exponent for one-tape machines, answering Williams's question for this model.
