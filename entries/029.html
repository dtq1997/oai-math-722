Artin 原根猜想的无穷性部分

Artin's primitive root conjecture: infinitude for every base

对每个这样的底数，在每个充分大的区间 (x,2x)(x,2x)(x,2x) 中，都至少有 cax/(log⁡x)2c_a x/(\log x)^2ca​x/(logx)2 个以 aaa 为原根的素数，其中 ca>0c_a>0ca​>0。

英文原文

Proves the infinitude assertion in Artin's primitive root conjecture for every integer aaa that is neither −1-1−1 nor a square.

For each such base, at least cax/(log⁡x)2c_a x/(\log x)^2ca​x/(logx)2 primes in every sufficiently large interval (x,2x)(x,2x)(x,2x) have primitive root aaa, with ca>0c_a>0ca​>0.

  1. 每个容许整数作为原根

    Primitive roots for every admissible integer base

    摘要

    我们证明了 Artin 原根猜想中的无穷性断言：对于每个既不是 −1 也不是平方数的整数 a，存在某个 ca>0c_a\gt 0ca​>0，使得对每个充分大的 x，(x,2x)(x,2x)(x,2x) 中至少有 cax/(log⁡x)2c_a x/(\log x)^2ca​x/(logx)2 个素数以 a 为原根。

    英文原文

    We prove the infinitude assertion in Artin's primitive root conjecture: for every integer a that is neither −1 nor a square, there are at least cax/(log⁡x)2c_a x/(\log x)^2ca​x/(logx)2 primes in (x,2x)(x,2x)(x,2x) with primitive root a, for some ca>0c_a\gt 0ca​>0 and every sufficiently large x.

  2. 同时原根：素数底数的条件性下界

    Simultaneous primitive roots: a conditional lower bound for prime bases

    摘要

    对于每个固定的、由互不相同的正素数组成的有限集合，我们证明，存在某个 c > 0，使得对所有充分大的 x，(x,2x)(x,2x)(x,2x) 中至少有 cx/(log⁡x)2cx/(\log x)^2cx/(logx)2 个素数以该集合的每个元素为原根。

    该结果以配套的关于容许整数底数的原根论文中明确陈述的四个解析及筛法输入为假设。

    英文原文

    For every fixed finite set of distinct positive primes, we prove that at least cx/(log⁡x)2cx/(\log x)^2cx/(logx)2 primes in (x,2x)(x,2x)(x,2x) have every member of the set as a primitive root, for some c > 0 and all sufficiently large x.

    The result assumes four explicitly stated analytic and sieve inputs from the companion paper on primitive roots for admissible integer bases.
