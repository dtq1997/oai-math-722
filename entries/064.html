曲面奇点的 μ 常数问题

The μ\muμ-constant problem for surface singularities

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

对于 C3\mathbb C^3C3 中每个 Milnor 数恒定的孤立超曲面奇点的全纯单参数族，证明其拓扑右平凡性，从而解决 μ\muμ 常数问题的曲面情形。

缩小参数圆盘和代表元后，环境同胚关于参数与空间变量联合连续，固定原点截面，并保持定义函数。

英文原文

Proves topological right-triviality for every holomorphic one-parameter family of isolated hypersurface singularities in C3\mathbb C^3C3 with constant Milnor number, resolving the surface case of the μ\muμ-constant problem.

After shrinking the parameter disk and representatives, ambient homeomorphisms vary jointly continuously, fix the origin section and preserve the defining functions.

OpenAI · overview.tex

论文

  1. μ 不变的曲面奇点族的拓扑平凡性

    Topological triviality of mu-constant families of surface singularities

    摘要

    我们证明，ℂ3 中 Milnor 数不变的每个孤立超曲面奇点的全纯单参数族都拓扑右平凡。

    这对 μ 不变问题的曲面情形给出了肯定回答。

    该平凡化固定参数和原点截面，并保持定义函数。

    英文原文

    We prove that every holomorphic one-parameter family of isolated hypersurface singularities in ℂ3 with constant Milnor number is topologically right-trivial.

    This gives a positive answer to the surface case of the μ-constant problem.

    The trivialization fixes the parameter and the origin section, and preserves the defining functions.
