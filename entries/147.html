近边界 Birkhoff 猜想

The near-boundary Birkhoff conjecture

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

解决了具有正曲率的光滑严格凸平面台球的近边界 Birkhoff 猜想。

只要一个完整的擦边环带具有由各自不变的本质曲线构成的连续叶状结构，这样的台球区域就是椭圆。

由光滑闭凸焦散线组成的连续物理领状邻域（physical collar）也足以得到这一结论。

英文原文

Resolves the near-boundary Birkhoff conjecture for smooth strictly convex planar billiards of positive curvature.

Such a billiard is an ellipse whenever a full grazing annulus is continuously foliated by individually invariant essential curves.

A continuous physical collar of smooth closed convex caustics also suffices.

  1. 相空间上的连续叶状分解诱导解析焦散领形邻域

    Continuous Phase Foliations Create Analytic Caustic Collars

    摘要

    对于具有正曲率的光滑严格凸平面台球，若整个掠射环域可由逐条不变的本质曲线构成连续叶状分解，则其边界必为解析的，并存在一个由光滑严格凸焦散线构成的联合解析领形邻域。

    姊妹文章中证明的物理领形邻域刚性定理进而推出，该台球区域是椭圆。

    英文原文

    For a smooth strictly convex planar billiard of positive curvature, a continuous foliation of a full grazing annulus by individually invariant essential curves forces an analytic boundary and a jointly analytic collar of smooth strictly convex caustics.

    The physical-collar rigidity theorem proved in the companion article then implies that the table is an ellipse.

  2. 具有连续焦散领形邻域的光滑台球的刚性

    Rigidity of Smooth Billiards with a Continuous Caustic Collar

    摘要

    我们证明，具有正曲率的光滑严格凸平面台球，只要其边界的一个完整邻域可由光滑闭凸焦散线构成连续叶状分解，就必为椭圆。

    这里不假设横跨叶片方向的可微性。

    这解决了所述光滑类中的近边界 Birkhoff 猜想。

    英文原文

    We prove that a smooth strictly convex planar billiard with positive curvature is an ellipse whenever a full neighborhood of its boundary is continuously foliated by smooth closed convex caustics.

    No differentiability across the leaves is assumed.

    This resolves the near-boundary Birkhoff conjecture in the stated smooth class.
