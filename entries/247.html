无限、有限展示且剩余有限的 2-群

An infinite finitely presented residually finite 222-group

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

构造了一个无限的有限展示剩余有限群，其中每个元素的阶都是222的有限次幂，从而即使在此类群中，也对有限展示 Burnside 问题给出否定回答。

该构造还给出一个无限维的有限展示 nil 结合F2\mathbb F_2F2​-代数，以及一个有限展示的无限维代数性添幺代数，从而对相应的幂零性问题和 Kurosh 有限性问题给出否定回答。

英文原文

Constructs an infinite finitely presented residually finite group whose elements all have finite 222-power order, answering the finitely presented Burnside problem negatively even in this class.

The construction also yields an infinite-dimensional finitely presented nil associative F2\mathbb F_2F2​-algebra and a finitely presented infinite-dimensional algebraic unitization, giving negative answers to the corresponding nilpotence and Kurosh finiteness questions.

  1. 一个无限的有限展示剩余有限 2-群

    An infinite finitely presented residually finite 2-group

    摘要

    我们证明，配套论文中的无限、按通常意义有限展示的周期 Steinberg 群 Γ=St12(R)\Gamma=\mathop{\mathrm{St}}\nolimits _{12}(R)Γ=St12​(R) 是剩余有限的。

    它的有限指数子群 G=ker⁡(Γ→St12(F2))G=\ker(\Gamma\to\mathop{\mathrm{St}}\nolimits _{12}(\mathbb F_2))G=ker(Γ→St12​(F2​)) 是无限、按通常意义有限展示且剩余有限的，并且 G 的每个元素的阶均为有限的 2 的幂。

    其元素的阶无界。

    配套论文的分次代数中长字的相位，使有限次数截断能够检测 Γ 的所有元素，包括中心核中的元素。

    英文原文

    We prove that the infinite, ordinarily finitely presented periodic Steinberg group Γ=St12(R)\Gamma=\mathop{\mathrm{St}}\nolimits _{12}(R)Γ=St12​(R) of a companion paper is residually finite.

    Its finite-index subgroup G=ker⁡(Γ→St12(F2))G=\ker(\Gamma\to\mathop{\mathrm{St}}\nolimits _{12}(\mathbb F_2))G=ker(Γ→St12​(F2​)) is infinite, ordinarily finitely presented, and residually finite, and every element of G has finite 2-power order.

    The orders of its elements are unbounded.

    Phases of long words in the companion's graded algebra allow finite degree truncations to detect all elements of Γ, including the central kernel.

  2. 一个无限的有限展示周期群

    An infinite finitely presented periodic group

    摘要

    我们构造了一个具有通常意义下的有限展示的无限群，其中每个元素的阶都有限，从而否定地回答了有限展示的 Burnside 问题。

    我们还构造了一个 𝔽2 上无限维、有限展示、无单位元且每个元素均幂零的结合代数，它是 Jacobson 根代数，但不是幂零代数。

    其添幺代数是有限展示、代数性的，且是无限维的。

    这些代数否定地回答了有限展示的每个元素均幂零的代数及根代数的幂零性问题，以及 Kurosh 代数性有限性问题的有限展示版本。

    英文原文

    We construct an infinite group with an ordinary finite presentation in which every element has finite order, answering the finitely presented Burnside question negatively.

    We also construct an infinite-dimensional finitely presented nonunital nil associative algebra over 𝔽2 that is Jacobson radical but not nilpotent.

    Its unitization is finitely presented, algebraic, and infinite-dimensional.

    These algebras answer the finitely presented nil- and radical-algebra nilpotence questions and the finite-presentation version of Kurosh's algebraic finiteness question negatively.

# An infinite finitely presented residually finite 2-group and a finitely presented nil algebra

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [An infinite finitely presented periodic group](../../preprints/An-infinite-finitely-presented-periodic-group-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The finitely presented Burnside question asks whether a finitely presented group in which every element has finite order must be finite. The formalization gives a negative answer by constructing an infinite finitely presented periodic group, including a witness realized as a Steinberg group over an algebra of characteristic two. Periodicity means that each element has some finite order; no common exponent is asserted. The paper's separate nil-algebra and radical-algebra conclusions are outside these selected statements.
