欧氏 Steinitz–Bergström 猜想

The Euclidean Steinitz–Bergström conjecture

首发 · 2026-10-061 篇论文部分形式化

证明了 Rd\mathbb R^dRd 的欧氏单位球中的任意有限序列都可赋予正负号，使每个部分和的范数不超过 CdC\sqrt dCd​，且该界与序列长度无关。

因此，每个零和族都可重新排序，使不附加正负号的部分和满足同一个界。

一个匹配的下界给出了最优阶 S2(d)=Θ(d)S_2(d)=\Theta(\sqrt d)S2​(d)=Θ(d​)。

英文原文

Proves that any finite sequence in the Euclidean unit ball of Rd\mathbb R^dRd admits signs keeping every partial sum within CdC\sqrt dCd​, independently of length.

Consequently every zero-sum family can be reordered with the same bound on unsigned partial sums.

A matching lower bound gives the optimal order S2(d)=Θ(d)S_2(d)=\Theta(\sqrt d)S2​(d)=Θ(d​).

  1. 欧氏 Steinitz–Bergström 定理

    The Euclidean Steinitz–Bergström theorem

    已列入形式化清单
    摘要

    对于 ℝd 的欧氏单位球中按指定顺序排列的每个有限向量序列，都存在一种符号赋值，使每个带符号前缀和的范数至多为 CdC\sqrt dCd​，其中 C 是与序列长度无关的绝对常数。

    因此，单位球中每个带指标的零和向量族，都存在一种排序，使其不带符号的部分和满足同样的界。

    这在相差绝对常数因子的意义下确定了欧氏 Steinitz 常数，即 S2(d)=Θ(d)S_2(d)=\Theta(\sqrt d)S2​(d)=Θ(d​)，并解决了欧氏 Steinitz–Bergström 猜想。

    英文原文

    Every prescribed-order finite sequence of vectors in the Euclidean unit ball of ℝd has one signing for which every signed prefix has norm at most CdC\sqrt dCd​, with C absolute and independent of the sequence length.

    Consequently, every indexed zero-sum family of unit-ball vectors admits an ordering with the same bound for its unsigned partial sums.

    This determines the Euclidean Steinitz constant up to absolute factors, S2(d)=Θ(d)S_2(d)=\Theta(\sqrt d)S2​(d)=Θ(d​), and resolves the Euclidean Steinitz–Bergström conjecture.

# The Euclidean Steinitz–Bergström bound

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [The Euclidean Steinitz–Bergström theorem](../../preprints/The-Euclidean-Steinitz-Bergstrom-theorem-September-24-2026/The-Euclidean-Steinitz-Bergstrom-theorem-September-24-2026.pdf)

## Scope

The formalized result gives the Euclidean Steinitz–Bergström bound with one absolute constant $C$. For every finite family of vectors in the unit ball of $\mathbb R^d$, signs can be chosen so that every prefix in the prescribed order has norm at most $C\sqrt d$. When the vector sum is zero, a permutation makes every unsigned prefix satisfy the same bound. Repeated and zero vectors are included. The result is existential and does not supply an online algorithm.
