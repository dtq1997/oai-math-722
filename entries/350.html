Yau 结点集猜想的曲面结果与高维反例

Yau's nodal conjecture: surfaces and counterexamples

首发 · 2026-10-063 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

证明了每个固定的光滑闭曲面上结点集长度的最优上界 CλC\sqrt\lambdaCλ​，从而完成了该情形下的 Yau 猜想。

在三维和四维中，存在使这一上界失效的固定光滑度量，其中包括 S3S^3S3 上任意接近圆度量的度量。

在五维中，对某个固定的 ε0>0\varepsilon_0>0ε0​>0，结点集测度的增长可以快于 λ1/2+ε0\lambda^{1/2+\varepsilon_0}λ1/2+ε0​，从而甚至排除了任意小的幂次损失。

英文原文

Proves the sharp CλC\sqrt\lambdaCλ​ upper bound for nodal length on every fixed smooth closed surface, completing Yau's conjecture there.

The upper bound fails for fixed smooth metrics in dimensions three and four, including metrics on S3S^3S3 arbitrarily close to round.

In dimension five, nodal measure can grow faster than λ1/2+ε0\lambda^{1/2+\varepsilon_0}λ1/2+ε0​ for some fixed ε0>0\varepsilon_0>0ε0​>0, ruling out even arbitrarily small power losses.

  1. 光滑曲面上结点集长度的最优界

    Sharp nodal length on smooth surfaces

    摘要

    我们证明，在一个固定的、带 Riemann 度量的光滑闭连通曲面上，特征值为 λ > 0 的非零实 Laplace 特征函数的结点集长度至多为 CλC\sqrt\lambdaCλ​。

    结合已知的下界，这证明了该情形下的 Yau 猜想。

    英文原文

    We prove that a nonzero real Laplace eigenfunction with eigenvalue λ > 0 on a fixed smooth closed connected Riemannian surface has nodal length at most CλC\sqrt\lambdaCλ​.

    Together with the known lower bound, this proves Yau's conjecture in this setting.

  2. 三维与四维中 Yau 结点集上界的光滑反例

    Smooth counterexamples to Yau's nodal upper bound in dimensions three and four

    已列入形式化清单
    摘要

    我们在三维球面上构造了一个在光滑拓扑下任意接近圆度量的光滑度量，并在 S2×T2S^2\times\mathbb T^2S2×T2 上构造了一个光滑度量，使得相应的精确的实 Laplace 特征函数序列的结点集测度除以特征值平方根所得的比值无界。

    每个序列都对应于一个固定的度量。

    因此，Yau 结点集猜想的上界部分在三维和四维的光滑度量情形下不成立。

    英文原文

    We construct a smooth metric on the three-sphere, arbitrarily close to the round metric in the smooth topology, and a smooth metric on S2×T2S^2\times\mathbb T^2S2×T2 for which sequences of exact real Laplace eigenfunctions have unbounded nodal measure divided by the square root of the eigenvalue.

    Each sequence belongs to one fixed metric.

    Thus the upper-bound part of Yau's nodal conjecture fails for smooth metrics in dimensions three and four.

# Yau’s nodal bounds: surfaces and higher dimensions

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Sharp nodal length on smooth surfaces](../../preprints/Sharp-nodal-length-on-smooth-surfaces-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)
- [Smooth counterexamples to Yau's nodal upper bound in dimensions three and four](../../preprints/Smooth-counterexamples-to-Yaus-nodal-upper-bound-in-dimensions-three-and-four-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)
- [Power-law violations of Yau's nodal upper bound](../../preprints/Power-law-violations-of-Yaus-nodal-upper-bound-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

Yau's nodal-set conjecture predicts nodal size of order $\sqrt\lambda$ for Laplace eigenfunctions of eigenvalue $\lambda$. The formalization proves the upper bound on every fixed smooth closed connected Riemannian surface: there is a surface-dependent constant $C$ such that the one-dimensional Hausdorff measure of the zero set of every nonzero real eigenfunction with $\lambda>0$ is at most $C\sqrt\lambda$. The known lower bound is not part of this selected statement.

The formalized results contradict Yau's proposed $O(\sqrt\lambda)$ upper bound for nodal measure in dimensions three and four. On $S^3$, every prescribed smooth neighborhood of the round metric contains one fixed smooth metric with an eigenfunction sequence of unbounded nodal-measure ratio. A second fixed metric on $S^2\times T^2$ has the same property. In both cases the eigenvalues tend to infinity and the intrinsic nodal measures are finite.

Yau's nodal upper-bound conjecture predicts that the nodal measure of an eigenfunction is bounded by a constant times the square root of its eigenvalue. The formalized counterexample fixes one smooth metric on $S^4\times S^1$ and a sequence of nonzero smooth eigenfunctions whose positive eigenvalues tend to infinity, while the intrinsic four-dimensional nodal measures divided by the square roots of the eigenvalues tend to infinity. The metric is fixed throughout the sequence.
