有限 p 群的 chromatic Smith 不动点问题

The chromatic Smith fixed-point problem for finite ppp-groups

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

对于有限 ppp 群 GGG 的每个子群 HHH，确定了从几何 HHH 不动点到几何 GGG 不动点的最优 chromatic 损失。

在每个非负高度，该损失等于从 HHH 到 GGG、各商均为循环群的最短次正规链的长度。

每个商无论其阶数是多少都只计一次，而且有限谱体现了这一界的最优性。

英文原文

Determines the optimal chromatic loss from geometric HHH-fixed points to geometric GGG-fixed points for every subgroup HHH of a finite ppp-group GGG.

At every nonnegative height, the loss equals the shortest subnormal-chain length from HHH to GGG with cyclic quotients.

Each quotient counts once regardless of order, and finite spectra witness sharpness.

论文

  1. 循环长度与取不动点时的 chromatic 损失

    Cyclic length and chromatic fixed-point loss

    摘要

    对有限 p 群 G 及其子群 H，我们证明，取不动点时的最优 chromatic 损失等于从 H 到 G 的、各商群均为循环群的次正规链的最短长度。

    该等式在每个素数和每个非负高度处成立，从而肯定地解决 Kuhn 和 Lloyd 所提出的等式问题。

    英文原文

    For a finite p-group G and a subgroup H, we prove that the optimal chromatic fixed-point loss equals the shortest length of a subnormal chain from H to G with cyclic quotients.

    The equality holds at every prime and every nonnegative height, resolving positively the equality proposed by Kuhn and Lloyd.
