Bernoulli 卷积的算术分类

Arithmetic classification of Bernoulli convolutions

对于每个 λ∈(0,1)\lambda\in(0,1)λ∈(0,1)，利用显式有限代数单位集合所给出的一个无限单侧逼近条件，对无偏 Bernoulli 卷积的奇异性与绝对连续性进行了分类。

Classifies singular and absolutely continuous unbiased Bernoulli convolutions for every λ∈(0,1)\lambda\in(0,1)λ∈(0,1) by an infinite, one-sided approximation condition using explicit finite sets of algebraic units.

论文

  1. Bernoulli 卷积的算术分类及非 Pisot 情形的奇异性

    Arithmetic classification and non-Pisot singularity for Bernoulli convolutions

    摘要

    对于每个参数 λ∈(0,1)\lambda\in(0,1)λ∈(0,1)，我们给出无偏 Bernoulli 卷积为奇异或绝对连续的算术分类。

    该判据通过由显式定义的有限代数单位集进行的单侧逼近来表述；它是一个无穷逼近条件，而非一个有限的成员资格判定算法。

    我们还在 Pisot 数倒数之外建立了奇异的例子：(1,2)(1,2)(1,2) 中每个四次 Salem 数的倒数处都具有奇异性，并且一个显式 31 次多项式的某个指定非 Pisot 根的倒数处也具有奇异性。

    英文原文

    We give an arithmetic classification of singular and absolutely continuous unbiased Bernoulli convolutions for every parameter λ∈(0,1)\lambda\in(0,1)λ∈(0,1).

    The criterion is expressed through one-sided approximation by explicitly defined finite sets of algebraic units; it is an infinite approximation condition, not a finite membership algorithm.

    We also establish singular examples beyond reciprocals of Pisot numbers: singularity holds at the reciprocal of every quartic Salem number in (1,2)(1,2)(1,2) and at the reciprocal of a specified non-Pisot root of an explicit degree-31 polynomial.
