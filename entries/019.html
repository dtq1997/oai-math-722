p 进截面猜想

The ppp-adic section conjecture

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

证明了 Qp\mathbb Q_pQp​ 的有限扩张上每条亏格至少为二的光滑、固有、几何连通曲线，其有理点与完整算术 étale 基本群的截面的共轭类一一对应。

还证明了 Q\mathbb QQ 上亏格至少为二的模曲线 X0(N)X_0(N)X0​(N) 和 X1(N)X_1(N)X1​(N) 的 Grothendieck 截面猜想。

英文原文

Proves that rational points on every smooth proper geometrically connected curve of genus at least two over a finite extension of Qp\mathbb Q_pQp​ correspond bijectively to conjugacy classes of sections of its full arithmetic étale fundamental group.

It also proves Grothendieck's section conjecture over Q\mathbb QQ for the modular curves X0(N)X_0(N)X0​(N) and X1(N)X_1(N)X1​(N) of genus at least two.

论文

  1. 具有指定外部覆盖片的 étale 覆盖

    Étale covers with a prescribed exterior sheet

    摘要

    设 X 是一个 p 进局部域的代数闭包上的光滑固有双曲曲线。

    给定有限多个互不相交的小开圆盘，我们构造一个连通的有限 étale 覆盖，其中有一层同构于这些圆盘的整个外部，并且在每个圆盘上方的每个连通分支上，覆盖次数都能被 p 整除。

    英文原文

    Let X be a smooth proper hyperbolic curve over an algebraic closure of a p-adic local field.

    Given finitely many disjoint small open disks, we construct one connected finite étale cover with a sheet isomorphic to their entire exterior and with degree divisible by p on every connected component above each disk.

  2. p 进截面猜想

    The p-adic section conjecture

    摘要

    在 ℚp 的每个有限扩张上，我们对亏格至少为二的光滑固有几何连通曲线证明了局部 p 进截面猜想。

    结合已有的有限下降定理，对于数域上亏格至少为二的光滑固有几何连通曲线，当其有限覆盖下降点集等于其有理点集时，这也给出了整体截面猜想；这包括 ℚ 上亏格至少为二的 X0(N)X_0(N)X0​(N) 和 X1(N)X_1(N)X1​(N)。

    英文原文

    We prove the local p-adic section conjecture for smooth proper geometrically connected curves of genus at least two, over every finite extension of ℚp.

    Combined with established finite-descent theorems, this also yields the global section conjecture for smooth proper geometrically connected curves of genus at least two over number fields when their finite-cover descent locus equals their rational points; this includes X0(N)X_0(N)X0​(N) and X1(N)X_1(N)X1​(N) of genus at least two over ℚ.
