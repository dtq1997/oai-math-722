Kaplansky 拟迹猜想反例

A counterexample to Kaplansky's quasitrace conjecture

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

通过构造一个可分含幺复 C∗C^*C∗ 代数，容许归一化的 222 拟迹但所有这些拟迹均不可加，否定了 Kaplansky 拟迹猜想。

由此可知，两个含幺、单且稳定有限的 C∗C^*C∗ 代数的最小张量积可以是 properly infinite 的，其中一个因子为 Cr∗(F2)C_r^*(\mathbb F_2)Cr∗​(F2​)。

英文原文

Disproves Kaplansky's quasitrace conjecture by constructing a separable unital complex C∗C^*C∗-algebra admitting normalized 222-quasitraces, all of which are nonadditive.

As a consequence, two unital simple stably finite C∗C^*C∗-algebras can have a properly infinite minimal tensor product, with one factor Cr∗(F2)C_r^*(\mathbb F_2)Cr∗​(F2​).

# Kaplansky's quasitrace conjecture and failure of tensor-product stable finiteness

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [A counterexample to Kaplansky's quasitrace conjecture and failure of tensor-product stable finiteness](../../preprints/A-counterexample-to-Kaplanskys-quasitrace-conjecture-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

Kaplansky's quasitrace conjecture predicts that every $2$-quasitrace on a $C^*$-algebra is a trace. The formalization constructs a separable $C^*$-algebra with normalized $2$-quasitraces and fixed positive contractions $a,b$ for which every such quasitrace satisfies $\mathrm{Re}(\tau(a+b)-\tau(a)-\tau(b))\ge1/144$. Thus none is additive on this pair.

The formalization also gives simple stably finite $C^*$-algebras whose spatial tensor product is properly infinite, a separable stably finite algebra with no tracial state, and a tensor-product example in which normalized $2$-quasitraces are lost. These are the stable-finiteness consequences named in the paper's title.
