    摘要

    存在绝对常数 ε > 0 和 C，使得对于每个充分大的偶数 n，平面中每个具有该点数且无三点共线的点集，都至多具有 Cn4/3−εCn^{4/3-\varepsilon}Cn4/3−ε 个无序平分点对。

    这相较于平面平分线的经典 O(n4/3)O(n^{4/3})O(n4/3) 界给出了幂次上的改进。

    该证明是非定量的，不提供显式常数。

    英文原文

    There are absolute constants ε > 0 and C such that every sufficiently large even n-point set in the plane with no three collinear has at most Cn4/3−εCn^{4/3-\varepsilon}Cn4/3−ε unordered halving pairs.

    This gives a power saving over the classical O(n4/3)O(n^{4/3})O(n4/3) bound for planar halving lines.

    The proof is nonquantitative and does not supply explicit constants.

# Power savings for planar halving lines and <i>k</i>-sets

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [A power saving for planar halving lines](../../preprints/A-power-saving-for-planar-halving-lines-September-25-2026/main.pdf)

## Scope

A halving pair in an even planar point set is a pair whose line leaves equally many remaining points on each side. The formalization proves that some absolute $\varepsilon>0$ and $C$ bound the number of halving pairs by $Cn^{4/3-\varepsilon}$ for every sufficiently large even $n$ and every $n$-point set with no three collinear. It also proves a bound of the same form for all level-switch counts under the additional generic-position assumptions in the statement. The constants are existential.
