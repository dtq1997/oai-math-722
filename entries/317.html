    Thomason Model Structures in Every Strict Higher Dimension

    摘要

    我们证明了 Ara 和 Maltsiniotis 的高维 Thomason 模型结构猜想。

    对每个 1≤n≤∞1\le n\le\infty1≤n≤∞，小严格球状 n 范畴组成的范畴容许一个固有组合模型结构，且该结构与单纯集 Quillen 等价。

    其弱等价和纤维化由两次扩张的 Street 神经 Ex2Nn\mathrm{Ex}^2N_nEx2Nn​ 检测，Quillen 等价由 cnSd2⊣Ex2Nnc_n\mathrm{Sd}^2\dashv\mathrm{Ex}^2N_ncn​Sd2⊣Ex2Nn​ 给出。

    因此，严格高阶范畴在每个正有限维数以及维数 ω 中，都给出空间同伦理论的模型。

    英文原文

    We prove the higher-dimensional Thomason model-structure conjecture of Ara and Maltsiniotis.

    For every 1≤n≤∞1\le n\le\infty1≤n≤∞, the category of small strict globular n-categories admits a proper combinatorial model structure that is Quillen equivalent to simplicial sets.

    Its weak equivalences and fibrations are detected by the twice-extended Street nerve Ex2Nn\mathrm{Ex}^2N_nEx2Nn​, and the Quillen equivalence is given by cnSd2⊣Ex2Nnc_n\mathrm{Sd}^2\dashv\mathrm{Ex}^2N_ncn​Sd2⊣Ex2Nn​.

    Thus strict higher categories model the homotopy theory of spaces in every positive finite dimension and in dimension ω.

# Thomason model structures in all strict higher dimensions

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Thomason Model Structures in Every Strict Higher Dimension](../../preprints/Thomason-Model-Structures-in-Every-Strict-Higher-Dimension-September-25-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The formalization proves the higher-dimensional Thomason model-structure theorem for small strict globular $n$-categories in every positive finite dimension and in dimension $\omega$. Each category has the stated proper combinatorial model structure, Quillen equivalent to simplicial sets through the twice-subdivided categorification and twice-extended Street nerve adjunction.

The selected statements also identify weak equivalences by the Street nerve in all these dimensions. Thus the constructions model the homotopy theory of spaces throughout the stated range.
