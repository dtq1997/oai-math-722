Katok 熵刚性猜想

Katok's entropy rigidity conjecture

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

对维数至少为三、截面曲率严格为负的闭连通 Riemann 流形，证明了 Katok 的熵刚性猜想：归一化的 Liouville 测度使测地流的熵达到最大，当且仅当该度量局部对称。

英文原文

Proves Katok's entropy rigidity conjecture for closed connected Riemannian manifolds of dimension at least three with strictly negative sectional curvature: normalized Liouville measure maximizes entropy for the geodesic flow if and only if the metric is locally symmetric.

OpenAI · overview.tex

论文

  1. 负曲率下的熵相等与局部对称性

    Entropy equality and local symmetry in negative curvature

    摘要

    对于每个维数至少为三、具有严格负截面曲率的闭连通光滑 Riemann 流形，我们证明，归一化的 Liouville 测度是单位速率测地流的最大熵测度，当且仅当该度量局部对称。

    这在这些维数中肯定地解决了 Katok 的熵刚性猜想，其中包括任意尺度下的所有秩一对称类型。

    英文原文

    For every closed connected smooth Riemannian manifold of dimension at least three with strictly negative sectional curvature, we prove that normalized Liouville measure has maximal entropy for the unit-speed geodesic flow if and only if the metric is locally symmetric.

    This resolves Katok's entropy rigidity conjecture positively in these dimensions, including all rank-one symmetric types at arbitrary scale.
