弱无序随机正则图的 GOE 普适性

GOE universality for random regular graphs with weak disorder

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

对每个固定度数d≥3d\ge3d≥3，均匀简单随机ddd-正则图的邻接矩阵特征值在谱内部的点过程收敛到高斯正交系综分布，三正则图也包括在内。

在充分弱且固定的独立同分布均匀对角无序下，在严格位于未加无序时的谱边缘内部的各个紧谱带上，同样的固定能量普适性仍然成立。

英文原文

For every fixed degree d≥3d\ge3d≥3, the bulk adjacency-eigenvalue point process of a uniform simple random ddd-regular graph converges to the Gaussian orthogonal ensemble law, including for cubic graphs.

The same fixed-energy universality persists under sufficiently weak fixed iid uniform diagonal disorder, throughout compact bands strictly inside the clean spectral edges.

论文

  1. 随机正则图上弱 Anderson 无序的固定能量普适性

    Fixed-energy universality for weak Anderson disorder on random regular graphs

    摘要

    对于每个固定度数 d ≥ 3，我们证明了均匀随机的简单有标号 d-正则图的邻接矩阵在加入独立且均匀分布的对角无序后具有固定能量 GOE 普适性。

    无序强度为正且充分小，并在图增长时保持固定。

    对于无序为零时的谱带的某个紧子区间内的每个固定能量，以相应无穷树算子的态密度（其值为正）进行重标度后，完整的微观特征值点过程收敛于 GOE 体内过程。

    所允许的无序强度依赖于度数以及到无序为零时的谱边缘的距离。

    英文原文

    For every fixed degree d ≥ 3, we prove fixed-energy GOE universality for the adjacency matrix of a uniformly random simple labelled d-regular graph with independent uniform diagonal disorder.

    The disorder strength is positive, sufficiently small, and fixed as the graph grows.

    At each fixed energy in a compact subinterval of the clean spectral band, the full microscopic eigenvalue point process converges to the GOE bulk process after rescaling by the positive density of states of the corresponding infinite-tree operator.

    The permitted disorder strength depends on the degree and the distance from the clean spectral edges.

  2. 固定度数随机正则图的 GOE 谱内部普适性

    GOE bulk universality for fixed-degree random regular graphs

    摘要

    我们证明了随机正则图的固定度数体内普适性猜想。

    对于每个固定整数 d ≥ 3 以及开 Kesten–McKay 谱体内的每个固定能量，均匀简单有标号 d-正则图的邻接矩阵的展平特征值点过程收敛于 GOE 体内过程。

    该收敛沿所有容许的图规模成立，无需附加条件化。

    英文原文

    We prove the fixed-degree bulk-universality conjecture for random regular graphs.

    For every fixed integer d ≥ 3 and every fixed energy in the open Kesten–McKay bulk, the unfolded eigenvalue point process of the adjacency matrix of a uniform simple labelled d-regular graph converges to the GOE bulk process.

    The convergence holds along all admissible graph sizes, without additional conditioning.
