矩阵乘积的齐次深度五复杂度

Sharp homogeneous depth-five complexity of matrix products

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

在每个特征为零的域上，nnn 个相互独立的 n×nn\times nn×n 变量矩阵的乘积的 (1,1)(1,1)(1,1) 元，在齐次五层和积电路中需要 nΘ(n)n^{\Theta(\sqrt n)}nΘ(n​) 个门。

这一精确量级的界允许共享门，也允许底层线性形式涉及所有变量。

英文原文

Over every characteristic-zero field, the (1,1)(1,1)(1,1) entry of a product of nnn independent n×nn\times nn×n variable matrices requires nΘ(n)n^{\Theta(\sqrt n)}nΘ(n​) gates in homogeneous depth-five sum–product circuits.

This sharp bound allows shared gates and bottom linear forms involving all variables.

论文

  1. 迭代矩阵乘法的深度五齐次电路下界

    Homogeneous depth-five lower bounds for iterated matrix multiplication

    摘要

    令 IMMn,n\mathop{\mathrm{IMM}}\nolimits _{n,n}IMMn,n​ 为 n 个相互独立的 n×nn\times nn×n 变量矩阵之乘积的 (1,1)(1,1)(1,1) 条目。

    在每个特征为零的域上，对于所有充分大的 n，每个计算 IMMn,n\mathop{\mathrm{IMM}}\nolimits _{n,n}IMMn,n​ 的语法齐次 ΣΠΣΠΣ\Sigma\Pi\Sigma\Pi\SigmaΣΠΣΠΣ 电路都至少有 nn/400n^{\sqrt n/400}nn​/400 个门，其中阈值为与域无关的绝对常数。

    底层线性形式可以具有任意支撑，且允许任意有限扇入、扇出和门共享。

    在每个域上，当 n ≥ 2 时，分块展开给出至多具有 nn+4n^{\sqrt n+4}nn​+4 个门的此类电路。

    因此，特征为零的域上的门复杂度为 nΘ(n)n^{\Theta(\sqrt n)}nΘ(n​)。

    英文原文

    Let IMMn,n\mathop{\mathrm{IMM}}\nolimits _{n,n}IMMn,n​ be the (1,1)(1,1)(1,1) entry of a product of n independent n×nn\times nn×n matrices of variables.

    Over every field of characteristic zero, every syntactically homogeneous ΣΠΣΠΣ\Sigma\Pi\Sigma\Pi\SigmaΣΠΣΠΣ circuit computing IMMn,n\mathop{\mathrm{IMM}}\nolimits _{n,n}IMMn,n​ has at least nn/400n^{\sqrt n/400}nn​/400 gates for all sufficiently large n, with an absolute threshold independent of the field.

    Bottom linear forms may have arbitrary support, and arbitrary finite fan-in, fan-out, and gate sharing are allowed.

    Over every field, a block expansion gives such circuits with at most nn+4n^{\sqrt n+4}nn​+4 gates for n ≥ 2.

    Thus the gate complexity over characteristic-zero fields is nΘ(n)n^{\Theta(\sqrt n)}nΘ(n​).

# Homogeneous depth-five lower bounds for iterated matrix multiplication

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Homogeneous depth-five lower bounds for iterated matrix multiplication](../../preprints/Homogeneous-depth-five-lower-bounds-for-iterated-matrix-multiplication-September-25-2026/Homogeneous-depth-five-lower-bounds-for-iterated-matrix-multiplication-September-25-2026.pdf)

## Scope

Let $\mathrm{IMM}_{n,n}$ be the $(1,1)$ entry of the product of $n$ independent $n\times n$ variable matrices. The formalization proves that, over every characteristic-zero field, syntactically homogeneous depth-five $\Sigma\Pi\Sigma\Pi\Sigma$ circuits computing this polynomial require at least $n^{\sqrt n/400}$ gates for all sufficiently large $n$, with a threshold independent of the field. Arbitrary bottom support, finite fan-in and fan-out, and sharing are allowed.

It also gives, over every field and for $n\ge2$, circuits with at most $n^{\sqrt n+4}$ gates. These are the lower and upper bounds selected from the paper.
