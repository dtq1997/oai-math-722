三态及四态重构的精确阈值

Sharp three- and four-state reconstruction thresholds

OpenAI · 2026-10-063 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

对于正则树（d≥2d\ge2d≥2）和可观测的 Poisson 树（平均值分别满足d>1d>1d>1和d>0d>0d>0）上的三态对称广播和四态铁磁广播，证明精确的重构阈值为dλ2>1d\lambda^2>1dλ2>1，且等号成立时不可重构，其中 Poisson 情形的优势取平均时不以存活为条件。

三态定理允许λ\lambdaλ取正负两种符号，并给出对称三社群随机块模型的精确弱恢复阈值。

英文原文

Proves the exact reconstruction threshold dλ2>1d\lambda^2>1dλ2>1, with nonreconstruction at equality, for three-state symmetric and four-state ferromagnetic broadcasting on regular trees (d≥2d\ge2d≥2) and observed Poisson trees (mean d>1d>1d>1 and d>0d>0d>0, respectively), with Poisson advantage averaged without conditioning on survival.

The three-state theorem allows both signs of λ\lambdaλ and gives the exact weak-recovery threshold for the symmetric three-community stochastic block model.

  1. 铁磁四态 Potts 模型的重构阈值

    The Reconstruction Threshold for the Ferromagnetic Four-State Potts Model

    摘要

    我们对每棵满足 d ≥ 2 的正则 d 叉树，以及每棵均值为 d > 0 的 Poisson Galton–Watson 树，建立了铁磁四态 Potts 广播模型的精确 Kesten–Stigum 重构阈值。

    重构恰在 dλ2>1d\lambda^2\gt 1dλ2>1 时发生；我们证明了在阈值处及其以下均不可重构，包括等号情形。

    在 Poisson 模型中，整棵树均被观测，且重构优势在不以存活为条件的情况下取平均。

    证明使用了对多项式不等式的可复现精确算术验证。

    英文原文

    We establish the exact Kesten–Stigum reconstruction threshold for the ferromagnetic four-state Potts broadcast model on every regular d-ary tree with d ≥ 2 and every Poisson Galton–Watson tree of mean d > 0.

    Reconstruction occurs exactly when dλ2>1d\lambda^2\gt 1dλ2>1; we prove nonreconstruction at and below the threshold, including equality.

    In the Poisson model the whole tree is observed and the reconstruction advantage is averaged without conditioning on survival.

    The proof uses reproducible exact-arithmetic verification of polynomial inequalities.

  2. 树上四态 Potts 重构的容量判据

    A Capacity Criterion for Four-State Potts Reconstruction on Trees

    摘要

    对于满足 0<λ<10\lt \lambda\lt 10<λ<1 的铁磁四态广播模型，我们证明了，在度数有界的确定性有根树上，重构恰在该树以 λ−2∣e∣\lambda^{-2|e|}λ−2∣e∣ 为边电阻的 L3 容量为正时发生。

    这给出了一个不要求树满足正则性或增长率假设的精确判据，包括指数临界边界处。

    英文原文

    For the ferromagnetic four-state broadcast model with 0<λ<10\lt \lambda\lt 10<λ<1, we prove that reconstruction on a bounded-degree deterministic rooted tree occurs exactly when its L3 capacity with edge resistances λ−2∣e∣\lambda^{-2|e|}λ−2∣e∣ is positive.

    This gives an exact criterion without regularity or growth-rate assumptions on the tree, including at the exponential critical boundary.

  3. 三态对称信道的精确重构阈值

    The exact reconstruction threshold for the three-state symmetric channel

    摘要

    我们确定了对称三态广播过程在每棵正则 b 叉树（b ≥ 2）以及每棵被观测的、均值为 d > 1 的 Poisson Galton–Watson 树上的精确重构阈值。

    重构恰在 dλ2>1d\lambda^2\gt 1dλ2>1 时发生，其中正则模型中 d = b；对于信道参数的任一符号，等号处均不可重构。

    Poisson 模型的优势对树和自旋取平均，而不以存活为条件。

    这解决了所有度数下的三态正则树预测。

    将 Poisson 定理与已知的从树到图的结果及算法结果相结合，可得对称三社群稀疏随机块模型的精确弱恢复阈值，其中标签独立且均匀，社群内与社群间的速率固定为 a,b>0a,b\gt 0a,b>0，平均度为 (a+2b)/3>1(a+2b)/3\gt 1(a+2b)/3>1：恰在 (a−b)2>3(a+2b)(a-b)^2\gt 3(a+2b)(a−b)2>3(a+2b) 时可实现恢复。

    高于该阈值时，可在 O(nlog⁡n)O(n\log n)O(nlogn) 时间内实现恢复；在该阈值处或低于该阈值时，弱恢复在信息论意义下不可能。

    英文原文

    We determine the exact reconstruction threshold for the symmetric three-state broadcast process on every regular b-ary tree, b ≥ 2, and every observed Poisson Galton–Watson tree of mean d > 1.

    Reconstruction occurs exactly when dλ2>1d\lambda^2\gt 1dλ2>1, with d = b in the regular model; there is non-reconstruction at equality for either sign of the channel parameter.

    The Poisson advantage is averaged over trees and spins without conditioning on survival.

    This resolves the all-degree three-state regular-tree prediction.

    Combining the Poisson theorem with known tree-to-graph and algorithmic results gives the exact weak-recovery threshold for the symmetric three-community sparse stochastic block model with independent uniform labels, fixed within- and between-community rates a,b>0a,b\gt 0a,b>0, and mean degree (a+2b)/3>1(a+2b)/3\gt 1(a+2b)/3>1: recovery is possible exactly when (a−b)2>3(a+2b)(a-b)^2\gt 3(a+2b)(a−b)2>3(a+2b).

    Above this threshold it is achievable in O(nlog⁡n)O(n\log n)O(nlogn) time; at or below it, weak recovery is information-theoretically impossible.

# Exact three- and four-state reconstruction thresholds and four-state tree capacity

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [The exact reconstruction threshold for the three-state symmetric channel](../../preprints/The-exact-reconstruction-threshold-for-the-three-state-symmetric-channel-September-25-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The formalization proves the supercritical direction of reconstruction for the symmetric three-state broadcast channel, whose parameter satisfies $-1/2\le\lambda\le1$. On a regular $b$-ary tree, reconstruction holds when $b\lambda^2>1$; on an observed Poisson Galton–Watson tree of mean $d$, it holds when $d\lambda^2>1$. In each case the root-estimation advantage converges to a positive limit, with the Poisson advantage averaged over trees and spins.

The selected statements cover reconstruction above the Kesten–Stigum threshold. Non-reconstruction at or below the threshold and the stochastic-block-model consequences in the paper are outside them.
