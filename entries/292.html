Kirchberg 范数超幂嵌入问题反例

A counterexample to Kirchberg's norm-ultrapower embedding problem

首发 · 2026-10-061 篇论文全部形式化

显式构造了一个可分含幺全群 C∗C^*C∗ 代数，对于自然数上的任意自由超滤，它都不能保幺嵌入任意固定的非零含幺核 C∗C^*C∗ 代数的范数超幂。

取目标为 O2\mathcal O_2O2​，就对 Kirchberg 的范数超幂嵌入问题给出了否定答案。

英文原文

Constructs an explicit separable unital full group C∗C^*C∗-algebra that cannot embed unitally into the norm ultrapower of any fixed nonzero unital nuclear C∗C^*C∗-algebra, for any free ultrafilter on the natural numbers.

Taking the target to be O2\mathcal O_2O2​ answers Kirchberg's norm-ultrapower embedding problem negatively.

    An explicit obstruction to nuclear norm-ultrapower embeddings

    摘要

    我们对 Kirchberg 的范数超幂嵌入问题给出否定回答。

    我们构造一个显式的可分有单位元满群 C∗ 代数，使得对任意非零有单位元核 C∗ 代数 B 以及 ℕ 上的任意自由超滤子 ω，它都不容许到 Bω 的保单位元嵌入。

    特别地，它不能保单位元地嵌入 O2ω\mathcal O_2^\omegaO2ω​。

    英文原文

    We give a negative answer to Kirchberg's norm-ultrapower embedding problem.

    We construct an explicit separable unital full group C∗-algebra that admits no unital embedding into Bω, for any nonzero unital nuclear C∗-algebra B and any free ultrafilter ω on ℕ.

    In particular, it does not embed unitally into O2ω\mathcal O_2^\omegaO2ω​.

# Kirchberg's $`\mathcal O_2`$ norm-ultrapower embedding problem

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [An explicit obstruction to nuclear norm-ultrapower embeddings](../../preprints/An-explicit-obstruction-to-nuclear-norm-ultrapower-embeddings-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

Kirchberg's norm-ultrapower embedding problem asks whether separable $C^*$-algebras embed into norm ultrapowers of nuclear algebras. The formalization constructs an explicit separable unital full group $C^*$-algebra that has no unital embedding into $B^\omega$ for any nonzero unital nuclear $C^*$-algebra $B$ and any free ultrafilter $\omega$ on $\mathbb N$. In particular, it does not embed unitally into an ultrapower of the Cuntz algebra $\mathcal O_2$.
