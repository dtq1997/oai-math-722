三角 Hilbert 变换的极大与变差估计

Maximal and variational bounds for the triangular Hilbert transform

首发 · 2026-10-063 篇论文部分形式化

在复值函数情形下，对每个 r>2r>2r>2，证明了从 L3(R2)×L3(R2)L^3(\mathbb R^2)\times L^3(\mathbb R^2)L3(R2)×L3(R2) 到 L3/2(R2)L^{3/2}(\mathbb R^2)L3/2(R2) 的极大估计和环状 rrr-变差估计。

极大估计控制硬截断的两个端点，并给出几乎处处收敛和范数收敛。

与第三个输入配对，便解决了对称 L3×L3×L3L^3\times L^3\times L^3L3×L3×L3 点处的三角 Hilbert 变换估计。

英文原文

Proves maximal and annular rrr-variation bounds, for every r>2r>2r>2, from complex L3(R2)×L3(R2)L^3(\mathbb R^2)\times L^3(\mathbb R^2)L3(R2)×L3(R2) to L3/2(R2)L^{3/2}(\mathbb R^2)L3/2(R2).

The maximal estimate controls both hard truncation endpoints and gives almost-everywhere and norm convergence.

Pairing with a third input settles the triangular Hilbert transform estimate at the symmetric L3×L3×L3L^3\times L^3\times L^3L3×L3×L3 point.

  1. 三角 Hilbert 变换在对称点处的环形变差

    Annular variation of the triangular Hilbert transform at the symmetric point

    摘要

    对于每个 r > 2，我们证明了三角 Hilbert 变换从复值 L3×L3L^3\times L^3L3×L3 到 L3/2 的环形 r-变差估计。

    这些分割可以依赖于输出点，并遍历所有正尺度。

    该估计给出双端点极大界，以及在几乎处处和范数意义下的联合主值，并解决了对称标量三角 Hilbert 变换问题。

    英文原文

    We prove the annular r-variation estimate for the triangular Hilbert transform from complex L3×L3L^3\times L^3L3×L3 to L3/2 for every r > 2.

    The partitions may depend on the output point and range over all positive scales.

    The estimate yields the two-endpoint maximal bound and joint almost-everywhere and norm principal values, and resolves the symmetric scalar triangular Hilbert transform problem.

  2. 二进三角 Hilbert 形式的 L³ 界

    An L³ bound for the dyadic triangular Hilbert form

    摘要

    我们对于不受限制的实值输入，证明了二进三角 Hilbert 形式的一致 L3×L3×L3L^3\times L^3\times L^3L3×L3×L3 估计。

    在任意有限尺度集合上，各局部贡献的绝对值之和至多为输入范数乘积的四十倍。

    特别地，该界允许模至多为一的系数在各个可容许区间三元组之间独立变化。

    英文原文

    We prove a uniform L3×L3×L3L^3\times L^3\times L^3L3×L3×L3 estimate for the dyadic triangular Hilbert form with unrestricted real inputs.

    The sum of the absolute local contributions over any finite set of scales is bounded by forty times the product of the input norms.

    In particular, the bound allows coefficients of modulus at most one to vary independently among admissible interval triples.

  3. 对称点处的极大三角 Hilbert 变换

    The maximal triangular Hilbert transform at the symmetric point

    已列入形式化清单
    摘要

    对于 L3(R2)L^3(\mathbb R^2)L3(R2) 中任意复值输入，我们证明了三角 Hilbert 变换的逐点极大 L3×L3→L3/2L^3\times L^3\to L^{3/2}L3×L3→L3/2 估计，其中上确界同时对两个硬截断端点取。

    当下端点趋于零、上端点趋于无穷时，该估计给出了几乎处处意义和 L3/2 意义下的联合收敛。

    这也证明了所猜测的对称点处标量估计。

    英文原文

    For arbitrary complex inputs in L3(R2)L^3(\mathbb R^2)L3(R2), we prove the pointwise maximal L3×L3→L3/2L^3\times L^3\to L^{3/2}L3×L3→L3/2 estimate for the triangular Hilbert transform, with the supremum over both hard truncation endpoints.

    The estimate yields joint almost-everywhere and L3/2 convergence as the lower endpoint tends to zero and the upper endpoint tends to infinity.

    This also proves the conjectured scalar estimate at the symmetric point.

# Annular variation and dyadic absolute bounds for the triangular Hilbert transform

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [The maximal triangular Hilbert transform at the symmetric point](../../preprints/The-maximal-triangular-Hilbert-transform-at-the-symmetric-point-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The formalized result proves the symmetric maximal estimate for the triangular Hilbert transform: for arbitrary complex $F,G\in L^3(\mathbb R^2)$, the $L^{3/2}$ norm of the supremum over all finite hard-truncation intervals is at most $C\|F\|_3\|G\|_3$ for one absolute constant $C$. It also establishes a common full-measure set on which the truncated integrals are defined and almost-everywhere measurability of the maximal output.
