强薄生成树的确定性构造

Deterministic construction of strong thin spanning trees

首发 · 2026-10-062 篇论文全部形式化

以构造性方式解决了强薄树猜想。

每个至少有两个顶点的有限无自环 kkk-边连通多重图都有一棵生成树，使该树在每个割中包含的边都至多占该割全部边的通用比例 C/kC/kC/k。

即使平行边的重数采用二进制编码，也能用确定性算法在多项式时间内找到这样一棵树。

英文原文

Resolves the strong thin-tree conjecture constructively.

Every finite loopless kkk-edge-connected multigraph on at least two vertices has a spanning tree containing at most a universal C/kC/kC/k fraction of the edges of every cut.

Such a tree can be found deterministically in polynomial time, even with binary-encoded parallel-edge multiplicities.

  1. 强薄树猜想

    The strong thin tree conjecture

    已列入形式化清单
    摘要

    我们证明，每个至少具有两个顶点的有限无自环 k 边连通多重图，其中 k ≥ 1，都有一棵生成树，它与每个割的交集大小至多为该割大小的 C/kC/kC/k 倍，其中 C 是一个普适常数。

    这解决了强薄树猜想。

    英文原文

    We prove that every finite loopless k-edge-connected multigraph on at least two vertices, with k ≥ 1, has a spanning tree meeting each cut in at most C/kC/kC/k times the size of the cut, where C is a universal constant.

    This resolves the strong thin tree conjecture.

  2. 强薄树的多项式时间构造

    A polynomial-time construction of strong thin trees

    摘要

    我们给出强薄树的一个确定性多项式时间构造。

    给定一个至少具有一个顶点的有限 k 边连通无自环多重图，该算法构造一棵生成树，它在每个割中所含的边数至多占该割边数的 C/kC/kC/k，其中 C 是一个普适常数。

    运行时间关于二进制输入长度为多项式，包括平行边重数以二进制编码的情形。

    英文原文

    We give a deterministic polynomial-time construction of strong thin trees.

    Given a finite k-edge-connected loopless multigraph on at least one vertex, the algorithm constructs a spanning tree meeting every cut in at most a C/kC/kC/k fraction of its edges, for a universal constant C.

    The running time is polynomial in the binary input length, including when parallel-edge multiplicities are encoded in binary.

# Deterministic construction of strong thin spanning trees

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [The strong thin tree conjecture](../../preprints/The-strong-thin-tree-conjecture-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)
- [A polynomial-time construction of strong thin trees](../../preprints/A-polynomial-time-construction-of-strong-thin-trees-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The strong thin-tree conjecture asks for spanning trees that cross every cut sparsely relative to the original graph. The formalized result gives one absolute constant $C>0$ such that every finite loopless $k$-edge-connected multigraph with at least two vertices has a spanning tree crossing each nontrivial cut at most $(C/k)$ times the original cut size. Parallel edges remain distinct. No numerical value of $C$ or construction algorithm is asserted.

The strong thin-tree problem asks for a spanning tree that crosses every cut sparsely relative to the original graph. The formalization gives one deterministic polynomial-time algorithm and an absolute constant $C>0$ such that a finite $k$-edge-connected loopless multigraph yields a spanning tree using at most a $C/k$ fraction of the edges of every cut. The input may encode parallel-edge multiplicities in binary, and the running time is polynomial in that binary input length.
