超立方体 Ramsey 猜想

The hypercube Ramsey conjecture

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

解决了 Burr–Erdős 超立方体 Ramsey 猜想：nnn 维立方体的二色 Ramsey 数为 Θ(2n)\Theta(2^n)Θ(2n)。

因此，顶点数为该立方体顶点数某个通用常数倍的完全图，其每种红蓝染色都包含该立方体的一个单色副本。

英文原文

Resolves the Burr–Erdős hypercube Ramsey conjecture: the two-color Ramsey number of the nnn-dimensional cube is Θ(2n)\Theta(2^n)Θ(2n).

Thus every red-blue coloring of a complete graph on a universal constant times the cube's number of vertices contains a monochromatic copy of the cube.

OpenAI · overview.tex

论文

  1. 超立方体 Ramsey 数具有线性量级

    The hypercube Ramsey number has linear order

    摘要

    我们证明，n 维二元立方体的双色 Ramsey 数至多为 C2nC2^nC2n，其中 C 是一个绝对常数。

    这肯定地解决了 Burr 和 Erdős 的超立方体 Ramsey 猜想。

    英文原文

    We prove that the two-color Ramsey number of the n-dimensional binary cube is at most C2nC2^nC2n, where C is an absolute constant.

    This resolves positively the hypercube Ramsey conjecture of Burr and Erdős.
