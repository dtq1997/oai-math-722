每个正则 RCD 点的双 Lipschitz 坐标

Bi-Lipschitz coordinates at every regular RCD point

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

证明了对 K∈RK\in\mathbb RK∈R 和整数 n≥2n\ge2n≥2，非塌缩 RCD⁡(K,n)\operatorname{RCD}(K,n)RCD(K,n) 空间的每个正则点都有一个与 Rn\mathbb R^nRn 的开子集双 Lipschitz 等价的开邻域。

正则性要求所有带基点的切空间都是欧氏的，参照测度恰为 Hn\mathcal H^nHn，且该坐标图以一个依赖于该点的有限常数比较环境距离。

英文原文

Proves that every regular point of a noncollapsed RCD⁡(K,n)\operatorname{RCD}(K,n)RCD(K,n) space, for K∈RK\in\mathbb RK∈R and integer n≥2n\ge2n≥2, has an open neighborhood bi-Lipschitz to an open subset of Rn\mathbb R^nRn.

Regularity requires all pointed tangents to be Euclidean, the reference measure is exactly Hn\mathcal H^nHn, and the chart compares ambient distances with a point-dependent finite constant.

论文

  1. 非塌缩 RCD 空间正则点处的双 Lipschitz 坐标

    Bi-Lipschitz Coordinates at Regular Points of Noncollapsed RCD Spaces

    摘要

    我们解决了非塌缩情形下正则点的双 Lipschitz 猜想。

    对于每个整数 n ≥ 2 和每个实数 K，非塌缩 RCD(K,n)\mathrm{RCD}(K,n)RCD(K,n) 空间的每个正则点都有一个开邻域，与 ℝn 的某个开子集双 Lipschitz 同胚。

    双 Lipschitz 常数仅依赖于 n，且该邻域使用环境距离的限制。

    英文原文

    We resolve the regular-point bi-Lipschitz conjecture in the noncollapsed setting.

    For every integer n ≥ 2 and every real K, every regular point of a noncollapsed RCD(K,n)\mathrm{RCD}(K,n)RCD(K,n) space has an open neighborhood bi-Lipschitz homeomorphic to an open subset of ℝn.

    The bi-Lipschitz constant depends only on n, and the neighborhood uses the restricted ambient distance.
