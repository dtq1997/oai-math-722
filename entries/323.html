可分商问题的相对独立性

Relative independence of the separable quotient problem

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

在可测基数存在的一致性假设下，确立了可分商问题相对于 ZFC 的独立性。

“每个无限维 Banach 空间都有可分的无限维商空间”这一断言可以对所有实和复 Banach 空间成立，而连续统假设则在这两个域上均给出反例。

英文原文

Establishes, relative to the consistency of a measurable cardinal, that the separable quotient problem is independent of ZFC.

The assertion that every infinite-dimensional Banach space has a separable infinite-dimensional quotient can hold for all real and complex Banach spaces, whereas the continuum hypothesis yields counterexamples over both fields.

    摘要

    可分商问题询问，是否每个无限维 Banach 空间都有一个可分无限维商空间。

    我们证明，相对于 ZFC 加上一个可测基数的一致性，这一命题独立于 ZFC。

    该结论在实数域与复数域上均成立。

    对于范数密度为 ℵ1 的空间，我们得到仅相对于 ZFC 自身一致性的独立性结果。

    英文原文

    The separable quotient problem asks whether every infinite-dimensional Banach space has a separable infinite-dimensional quotient.

    We prove that this statement is independent of ZFC, relative to the consistency of ZFC with a measurable cardinal.

    This holds over both the real and complex fields.

    For spaces of norm density ℵ1, we obtain independence relative to the consistency of ZFC alone.

# Independence of the separable quotient problem

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Relative independence of the separable quotient problem](../../preprints/Relative-independence-of-the-separable-quotient-problem-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The separable quotient problem asks whether every infinite-dimensional Banach space has an infinite-dimensional separable quotient. The linked formalization proves the negative direction under the continuum hypothesis: over both the real and complex fields, there is an infinite-dimensional Banach space admitting no bounded linear surjection onto an infinite-dimensional separable Banach space.

This is the conditional counterexample direction. The positive consistency direction and the paper's full relative-independence conclusions are outside the selected statement.
