  1. 准 Riemann 猜想：无零点半平面 ℜs>7/8\Re s\gt 7/8ℜs>7/8

    The Quasi-Riemann Hypothesis: A Zero-Free Half-Plane ℜs>7/8\Re s\gt 7/8ℜs>7/8

    主结果已列入形式化清单
    摘要

    我们证明，Q(−3)\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})Q(−3​) 上所有有限阶 Hecke L 函数以及所有 Dirichlet L 函数在半平面 ℜs>7/8\Re s\gt 7/8ℜs>7/8 内均无零点，同时允许 s = 1 处的主极点。

    特别地，Riemann zeta 函数在该半平面内无零点，从而证明了准 Riemann 猜想。

    英文原文

    We prove that all finite-order Hecke L-functions over Q(−3)\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})Q(−3​) and all Dirichlet L-functions are zero-free in the half-plane ℜs>7/8\Re s\gt 7/8ℜs>7/8, with the principal pole at s = 1 allowed.

    In particular, the Riemann zeta function is zero-free in this half-plane, proving the quasi-Riemann hypothesis.

  2. 准 Riemann 猜想（11/12 界的另一种证明）

    The Quasi-Riemann Hypothesis (alternate 11/12 proof)

    摘要

    我们证明每个 Dirichlet L 函数，包括 Riemann zeta 函数，在半平面 Res>11/12\mathop{\mathrm{Re}}\nolimits s\gt 11/12Res>11/12 内都没有零点，由此确立了准 Riemann 猜想。

    更一般地，我们证明，K=Q(−3)K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})K=Q(−3​) 上每个有限阶 Hecke L 函数都具有相同的无零点半平面。

    特别地，这排除了 Landau–Siegel 零点的存在。

    英文原文

    We establish the quasi-Riemann hypothesis by proving that every Dirichlet L-function, including Riemann's zeta function, has no zeros in the half-plane Res>11/12\mathop{\mathrm{Re}}\nolimits s\gt 11/12Res>11/12.

    More generally, we prove the same zero-free half-plane for every finite-order Hecke L-function over K=Q(−3)K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})K=Q(−3​).

    In particular, this rules out the existence of Landau–Siegel zeros.

  3. 一致排除 Landau–Siegel 零点

    Uniform exclusion of Landau–Siegel zeros

    主结果已列入形式化清单
    摘要

    我们证明了对 Landau–Siegel 零点的一致排除结论。

    存在一个绝对常数 c > 0，使得导子为 q ≥ 3 的每个本原、非主、实 Dirichlet L 函数的每个实零点 β∈(0,1)\beta\in(0,1)β∈(0,1) 都满足 (1−β)log⁡q≥c(1-\beta)\log q\ge c(1−β)logq≥c。

    英文原文

    We prove the uniform exclusion of Landau–Siegel zeros.

    There is an absolute constant c > 0 such that every real zero β∈(0,1)\beta\in(0,1)β∈(0,1) of every primitive nonprincipal real Dirichlet L-function of conductor q ≥ 3 satisfies (1−β)log⁡q≥c(1-\beta)\log q\ge c(1−β)logq≥c.

# The quasi-Riemann hypothesis

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [The Quasi-Riemann Hypothesis: A Zero-Free Half-Plane $\Re(s)>7/8$](../../preprints/The-Quasi-Riemann-Hypothesis-September-30-2026/paper.pdf)
- [Uniform exclusion of Landau–Siegel zeros](../../preprints/Uniform-exclusion-of-Landau-Siegel-zeros-October-1-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The quasi-Riemann hypothesis asks for a fixed zero-free half-plane $\Re s>\theta$ with $\theta<1$. The formalization gives $\theta=7/8$ for the Riemann zeta function and every Dirichlet $L$-function, uniformly over all positive moduli and all characters. It also establishes the same bound for finite-order Hecke $L$-functions over $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})$.

The principal-character poles at $s=1$ are excluded in the Dirichlet and Hecke statements. The paper's later applications are not included.

The formalized result gives a uniform logarithmic exclusion region for Landau–Siegel zeros. There is one constant $c>0$ such that every primitive nonprincipal real Dirichlet character of conductor $q\ge3$ and every real zero $0<\beta<1$ of its $L$-function satisfy $1-\beta\ge c/\log q$.

Both character parities are included. No explicit value of $c$ is given. This excludes real zeros in $1-c/\log q<\beta<1$, but does not rule out real zeros elsewhere in $(0,1)$.
