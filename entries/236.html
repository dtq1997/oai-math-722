树上自由 Ising 态的 IID 因子阈值

The factor-of-IID threshold for free Ising states on trees

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

确定了无限ddd-正则树上的自由零场铁磁 Ising 态何时是独立顶点标签的因子：对于d≥3d\ge3d≥3和β≥0\beta\ge0β≥0，恰好在tanh⁡β≤(d−1)−1/2\tanh\beta\le(d-1)^{-1/2}tanhβ≤(d−1)−1/2时成立，包括等号情形。

该构造不使用根，并且对每个固定的树自同构几乎必然等变，从而解决 Lyons 问题的铁磁情形。

英文原文

Determines when the free zero-field ferromagnetic Ising state on the infinite ddd-regular tree is a factor of independent vertex labels: exactly when tanh⁡β≤(d−1)−1/2\tanh\beta\le(d-1)^{-1/2}tanhβ≤(d−1)−1/2, including equality, for d≥3d\ge3d≥3 and β≥0\beta\ge0β≥0.

The construction uses no root and is almost surely equivariant for each fixed tree automorphism, resolving the ferromagnetic case of Lyons's question.

    The sharp factor-of-IID threshold for the free Ising model on regular trees

    主结果已列入形式化清单
    摘要

    对每个整数 d ≥ 3 和逆温度 β ≥ 0，无限 d-正则树上的自由边界零场铁磁 Ising 测度是 IID 的因子，当且仅当 tanh⁡β≤(d−1)−1/2\tanh\beta\le(d-1)^{-1/2}tanhβ≤(d−1)−1/2。

    我们证明了包含等号情形的正向蕴含，解决了 Nam、Sly 和 Zhang 的猜想；严格反向结论是已知的。

    可以选择自旋因子，使它在每个标号输入上都与每个树自同构交换。

    英文原文

    For every integer d ≥ 3 and inverse temperature β ≥ 0, the free zero-field ferromagnetic Ising measure on the infinite d-regular tree is a factor of IID exactly when tanh⁡β≤(d−1)−1/2\tanh\beta\le(d-1)^{-1/2}tanhβ≤(d−1)−1/2.

    We prove the positive implication, including equality, resolving the conjecture of Nam, Sly and Zhang; the strict converse is known.

    The spin factor can be chosen to commute with every tree automorphism on every label input.

# The exact factor-of-IID threshold for free Ising spins on trees

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [The sharp factor-of-IID threshold for the free Ising model on regular trees](../../preprints/The-sharp-factor-of-IID-threshold-for-the-free-Ising-model-on-regular-trees-September-26-2026/article.pdf)

## Scope

The formalized result determines the factor-of-IID threshold for the free zero-field Ising law on the infinite $d$-regular tree. For every $d\ge3$ and $\beta\ge0$, the law is a factor of IID exactly when $\tanh\beta\le1/\sqrt{d-1}$, including equality. The factor uses independent uniform vertex labels and is almost surely equivariant for each fixed tree automorphism. Finitary coding and coding-radius bounds are not included.
