光滑半正性下的反典范非消失

Anticanonical nonvanishing under smooth semipositivity

OpenAI · 2026-10-067 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

若 XXX 是光滑连通复射影簇，且 −KX-K_X−KX​ 具有曲率非负的光滑 Hermitian 度量，则存在某个 m>0m>0m>0，使得 H0(X,−mKX)≠0H^0(X,-mK_X)\ne0H0(X,−mKX​)=0。

因此，在任意维数下，光滑半正性都保证反典范丛的某个正张量幂具有非零截面。

英文原文

If XXX is a smooth connected complex projective variety and −KX-K_X−KX​ admits a smooth Hermitian metric with nonnegative curvature, then H0(X,−mKX)≠0H^0(X,-mK_X)\ne0H0(X,−mKX​)=0 for some m>0m>0m>0.

Thus smooth semipositivity forces a nonzero section of a positive tensor power of the anticanonical bundle in every dimension.

    Every smooth connected projective complex variety with smoothly semipositive anticanonical bundle has a nonzero section of some positive anticanonical power.

  2. 不变反典范指标与扭曲微分形式的转化

    Invariant anticanonical indices and conversion of twisted differentials

    摘要

    对于紧环面在紧复流形上的全纯作用，我们证明，自然线性化的反典范幂的不变 Euler 特征在一个 divisible progression 上是多项式，并在指数为零处取其实际值。

    独立地，在反典范丛光滑半正的光滑射影簇上，我们从一个无界的有效扭转序列中去掉一个固定的伪有效误差项。

    这些结果将不变上同调转化为扭曲微分形式，并通过在转化前将这些形式下降，证明了反典范丛光滑半正的光滑射影簇上的反典范非消失性。

    英文原文

    For a holomorphic action of a compact torus on a compact complex manifold, we prove that the invariant Euler characteristic of naturally linearized anticanonical powers is polynomial on a divisible progression, with its actual value at exponent zero.

    Independently, on a smooth projective variety with smoothly semipositive anticanonical bundle, we remove a fixed pseudoeffective error from an unbounded sequence of effective twists.

    These results convert invariant cohomology into twisted differential forms and prove anticanonical nonvanishing on smooth projective varieties with smoothly semipositive anticanonical bundle, by descending the forms before conversion.

  3. klt 对和环面商上的有界反典范度量

    Bounded anticanonical metrics on klt pairs and torus quotients

    摘要

    设 (W,D)(W,D)(W,D) 是带有有效有理边界的射影 ℚ-factorial klt 对。

    我们证明，若除子 −(KW+D)-(K_W+D)−(KW​+D) 为 nef，其到某个解消的拉回具有权重局部有界的半正度量，且该解消的结构层 Euler 特征非零，则该除子的某个正 Cartier 倍数具有非零截面。

    因此，反典范丛光滑半正的光滑有理连通射影簇，对每个代数环面作用都有一个非零的自然不变反典范多重截面。

    英文原文

    Let (W,D)(W,D)(W,D) be a projective ℚ-factorial klt pair with effective rational boundary.

    We prove that a positive Cartier multiple of −(KW+D)-(K_W+D)−(KW​+D) has a nonzero section if this divisor is nef, its pullback to a resolution admits a semipositive metric with locally bounded weights, and the resolution has nonzero structure-sheaf Euler characteristic.

    Consequently a smooth rationally connected projective variety with smoothly semipositive anticanonical bundle has a nonzero naturally invariant anticanonical plurisection for every algebraic torus action.

  4. 上同调传递与等变反典范截面

    Cohomological transfer and equivariant anticanonical sections

    摘要

    设 X 是反典范丛光滑半正的光滑复射影簇。

    对于该线丛的任意环面线性化，我们表明，在无界的一列次数中，减去同一个固定的伪有效误差项后存在不变截面，可以推出在某个正的无扭曲次数中存在不变截面。

    结合自然不变指标和compact-monodromy 结构，我们推出，每个这样的 X 的反典范丛的某个正次幂都存在非零截面。

    英文原文

    Let X be a smooth projective complex variety with smoothly semipositive anticanonical bundle.

    For any torus linearization of that bundle, we show that invariant sections in unbounded degrees with one fixed negative pseudoeffective error yield an invariant section in a positive untwisted degree.

    Combining the natural invariant index with compact-monodromy structure, we deduce that every such X has a nonzero section of some positive anticanonical power.

  5. 度量下降与保秩收缩

    Metric descent and rank-preserving contractions

    摘要

    从光滑复射影簇上的光滑半正反典范度量出发，我们利用正规等维环面型模型，以及指数为零处 full generic adjoint rank 为一这一条件，在光滑底空间的修正反典范线丛上构造无权可积半正度量。

    显式的有理边界修正拉回后是一个例外除子。

    在没有正次数全纯形式的簇上，双度量传递从有限体积的对数反典范度量以及具有有界半正权重的目标线丛，给出带有指定边界极点的截面。

    特别地，对于反典范丛光滑半正且没有上述形式的光滑复射影簇，每个配备自然线性化的环面作用都使某个正反典范倍数具有非零不变截面。

    在这类簇上，一般截面和伴随秩假设给出了不变纤维化的收缩，并保持这两个条件，除非已经存在不变整体截面。

    英文原文

    From a smooth semipositive anticanonical metric on a smooth projective complex variety, we construct an unweighted integrable semipositive metric on a corrected anticanonical line of a smooth base, using a normal equidimensional toroidal model and full generic adjoint rank one at exponent zero.

    The explicit rational boundary correction pulls back to an exceptional divisor.

    On varieties without positive-degree holomorphic forms, two-metric transfer gives sections with prescribed boundary poles from a finite-volume log-anticanonical metric and a target line with bounded semipositive weights.

    In particular, a smooth projective complex variety with a smoothly semipositive anticanonical bundle and no such forms has a nonzero invariant section of a positive anticanonical multiple for every torus action with its natural linearization.

    On this class of varieties, generic section and adjoint-rank hypotheses give contractions of invariant fibrations that preserve both conditions unless an invariant global section already exists.

    Integrable metrics and effectivity with controlled boundary

    摘要

    设 Y 光滑且射影，C 是有效整数系数除子。

    若 −KY+C-K_Y+C−KY​+C 存在具有整体严格曲率下界的度量，且 C 的典范截面对该度量局部平方可积，则每个伪有效有理除子在添加支撑于 C 上的有效修正后都变成有理有效的。

    对于具有光滑半正的 L=−KXL=-K_XL=−KX​ 的光滑射影 X，如果在无界的正指数处存在 mjL−Pm_jL-Pmj​L−P 的截面，其中 P 是任意固定的伪有效 Cartier 误差项，则可得到 L 的某个正倍数的截面。

    英文原文

    Let Y be smooth projective and let C be an effective integral divisor.

    If −KY+C-K_Y+C−KY​+C admits a metric with a global strict curvature lower bound for which the canonical section of C is locally square integrable, every pseudoeffective rational divisor becomes rationally effective after an effective correction supported on C.

    For a smooth projective X with smoothly semipositive L=−KXL=-K_XL=−KX​, sections of mjL−Pm_jL-Pmj​L−P at unbounded positive exponents, with any fixed pseudoeffective Cartier error P, yield a section of a positive multiple of L.
