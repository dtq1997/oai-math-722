四维量子场论中从尺度对称性到共形对称性的提升

Scale-to-conformal enhancement in four-dimensional QFT

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

在所述有界局部网与场重构假设下，证明了对于具有离散、有下界且重数有限的标度谱、有限标度支集以及物理局部标度流的四维酉正能理论，标度对称性蕴含局部共形对称性。

应力张量存在一个无迹的改进形式，并保持时空荷不变。

结论涉及局部 Ward 恒等式，而非整个网上的全局共形作用。

英文原文

Under the stated bounded-local-net and field-reconstruction hypotheses, proves that scale symmetry implies local conformal symmetry for four-dimensional unitary positive-energy theories with a discrete bounded-below scaling spectrum of finite multiplicity, finite scaling support and a physical local scale current.

The stress tensor has a traceless improvement with unchanged spacetime charges.

The conclusion concerns local Ward identities, not a global conformal action on the whole net.

论文

  1. 操作框架下四维量子场论的尺度对称性与共形对称性

    Scale and conformal symmetry in four-dimensional operational quantum field theory

    摘要

    我们证明，对一类具有 Poincaré 不变及尺度不变真空的四维酉正能量量子场论，尺度对称性蕴含局部共形对称性。

    这一类理论的标度维数谱离散、有下界且重数有限，每个原始场都具有有限的标度支集。

    原始场及其伴随在一个因果对易的有界局部网中具有相容的 affiliated 实现，而一个物理的局部伸缩流通过其局部 Ward 恒等式生成尺度变换。

    在重构场的联合乘积以及在同一网中的相容 affiliated 实现得到确立后，这一操作框架允许严格局域化的重构场。

    在这些假设下，应力张量容许一个对称、守恒、无迹的改进，且平移荷和 Lorentz 荷保持不变。

    相应的共形流在物理场代数上满足未破缺的局部 Ward 恒等式。

    这一结论是局部的；将其积分为整个有界网上的全局共形作用，仍是一个独立问题。

    英文原文

    We prove that scale symmetry implies local conformal symmetry for a class of four-dimensional unitary, positive-energy quantum field theories with a Poincaré- and scale-invariant vacuum.

    The class has a discrete, bounded-below spectrum of scaling dimensions with finite multiplicities, and each original field has finite scaling support.

    The original fields and their adjoints have compatible affiliated realizations in a causally commuting bounded local net, and a physical local dilatation current generates scale transformations through its local Ward identities.

    The operational framework admits sharply localized reconstructed fields after their joint products and compatible affiliated realizations in the same net have been established.

    Under these hypotheses, the stress tensor admits a symmetric, conserved, traceless improvement with unchanged translation and Lorentz charges.

    The corresponding conformal currents satisfy unbroken local Ward identities on the physical field algebra.

    The conclusion is local; integration to a global conformal action on the whole bounded net remains a separate question.
