圆盘嵌入与 Wall 流形猜想反例

Counterexamples to disk embedding and Wall's manifold conjecture

首发 · 2026-10-063 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

不受限制的四维圆盘嵌入猜想不成立：带标架的代数对偶球面不足以保证得到彼此不交、局部平坦的张成圆盘。

特别地，自由群 F2F_2F2​ 在 Freedman–Quinn 意义下不是好群。

还构造了一个有限展示的四维整系数 Poincaré 对偶群，它具有有限分类空间，却不能实现为闭的非球面拓扑四维流形的基本群，从而否定了 Wall 猜想。

英文原文

The unrestricted four-dimensional disk-embedding conjecture fails: framed algebraic dual spheres do not suffice to obtain disjoint locally flat spanning disks.

In particular, the free group F2F_2F2​ is not good in the sense of Freedman–Quinn.

Also constructs a finitely presented integral Poincaré duality group of dimension four with a finite classifying space but no realization as the fundamental group of a closed aspherical topological four-manifold, disproving Wall's conjecture.

  1. 四维圆盘嵌入的纯边界障碍

    A boundary-only obstruction to four-dimensional disk embedding

    摘要

    我们反驳不对基本群作假设的四维圆盘嵌入猜想，即使不预先指定同伦类或输出标架也是如此。

    我们构造一个紧致定向光滑四维流形，其中包含有限个带有加标架的代数对偶球面的圆盘映射，而它们的边界圆不能围成互不相交的局部平坦圆盘。

    因此，二生成元自由群在 Freedman–Quinn 意义下不是好群，任何包含它作为子群的群也都不是好群。

    英文原文

    We disprove the four-dimensional disc embedding conjecture without a fundamental-group hypothesis, even when no homotopy classes or output framings are prescribed.

    We construct a compact oriented smooth four-manifold containing finitely many disc maps with framed algebraic dual spheres whose boundary circles bound no disjoint locally flat discs.

    Consequently, the free group on two generators is not good in the sense of Freedman–Quinn, and neither is any group containing it as a subgroup.

  2. 四维圆盘嵌入的带标记张量障碍

    A marked tensor obstruction to four-dimensional disk embedding

    摘要

    我们反驳不受限制的四维圆盘嵌入猜想。

    我们在一个紧致定向光滑四维流形中构造浸入圆盘及带标架的代数对偶球面，它们满足通常的等变相交条件和约化自相交条件，但不存在保留边界映射及诱导法标架的、两两不交且局部平坦的替代圆盘。

    即使不预先指定替代圆盘的相对同伦类，这一障碍仍然成立。

    英文原文

    We disprove the unrestricted four-dimensional disk-embedding conjecture.

    We construct immersed disks in a compact oriented smooth four-manifold with framed algebraic dual spheres satisfying the usual equivariant intersection and reduced self-intersection conditions, but with no pairwise disjoint locally flat replacements that preserve the boundary maps and induced normal framings.

    The obstruction holds even when the replacement disks' relative homotopy classes are not prescribed.

  3. 没有非球面流形模型的 PD4 群

    A PD4 group without an aspherical manifold model

    摘要

    我们构造一个有限展示的四维整系数 Poincaré 对偶群，它具有有限分类空间，却不是任何闭非球面拓扑四维流形的基本群。

    这对 Wall 的流形实现问题在四维情形下给出否定回答。

    英文原文

    We construct a finitely presented integral Poincaré duality group of dimension four that has a finite classifying space but is not the fundamental group of any closed aspherical topological four-manifold.

    This gives a negative answer to Wall's manifold-realization question in dimension four.
