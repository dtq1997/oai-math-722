混合变换的逐点多重遍历平均

Pointwise multiple ergodic averages for mixing transformations

OpenAI · 2026-10-064 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

对于可逆、混合的保概率变换，证明了每个有限长度的consecutive 多重遍历平均都几乎处处收敛。

对于每个固定的有界函数元组，当平均长度沿所有正整数趋于无穷时，极限为这些函数各自积分的乘积。

不需要混合速率假设，也不需要概率空间为标准空间。

英文原文

Proves almost-everywhere convergence of consecutive multiple ergodic averages of every finite length for invertible mixing probability-preserving transformations.

For each fixed tuple of bounded functions, the limit is the product of their integrals, along all positive averaging lengths.

No mixing rate or standardness assumption on the probability space is required.

  1. 混合变换的逐点多重遍历平均

    Pointwise Multiple Ergodic Averages for Mixing Transformations

    摘要

    设 T 是一个可逆、混合且保概率的变换。

    对于每个整数 n ≥ 2 及每个固定的有界可测函数元组，我们证明，当平均长度沿所有正整数趋于无穷时，长度为 n 的连续多重遍历平均几乎处处收敛于各积分的乘积。

    概率空间不必是标准空间，也不需要任何混合速率条件。

    英文原文

    Let T be an invertible mixing probability-preserving transformation.

    For every integer n ≥ 2 and every fixed tuple of bounded measurable functions, we prove that the consecutive multiple ergodic averages of length n converge almost everywhere to the product of the integrals, as the averaging length tends to infinity through all positive integers.

    The probability space need not be standard, and no rate of mixing is required.

  2. 混合变换四重遍历平均的逐点收敛性

    Pointwise convergence of fourfold ergodic averages for mixing transformations

    摘要

    我们证明，对于每个可逆、双向可测、混合且保概率的变换，以及每个固定选择的有界可测输入，沿时间 n,2n,3n,4nn,2n,3n,4nn,2n,3n,4n 的四重遍历平均都几乎处处收敛于各积分的乘积。

    该收敛对沿所有正整数取值的平均长度成立。

    这里不假设任何定量的混合速率，且概率空间不必是标准空间。

    英文原文

    We prove that fourfold ergodic averages along the times n,2n,3n,4nn,2n,3n,4nn,2n,3n,4n converge almost everywhere to the product of the integrals for every invertible, bimeasurable, mixing probability-preserving transformation and every fixed choice of bounded measurable inputs.

    Convergence holds along all positive integer averaging lengths.

    No quantitative mixing rate is assumed, and the probability space need not be standard.

  3. 具有互异整数斜率的三重遍历平均

    Triple ergodic averages with distinct integer slopes

    摘要

    对于每个可逆、混合且保概率的变换，有界可测函数沿任意三个两两不同的非零整数斜率的三重遍历平均，都几乎处处收敛于它们各自积分的乘积。

    这些斜率可以为正，也可以为负。

    英文原文

    For every invertible mixing probability-preserving transformation, triple ergodic averages of bounded measurable functions along any three pairwise distinct nonzero integer slopes converge almost everywhere to the product of their integrals.

    The slopes may be positive or negative.
