基于布尔预言机的多项式时间常数误差酉合成

Polynomial-time, constant-error unitary synthesis from a Boolean oracle

首发 · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

解决了常数误差的 Aaronson–Kuperberg 酉合成问题：存在一个一致的多项式规模量子预言机电路，使得对每个 nnn 量子比特的酉信道，都可选择适合该目标的布尔预言机，使该电路在钻石范数误差 1/21/21/2 内逼近该信道。

门数、量子比特数、预言机调用次数以及查询长度均有多项式上界。

该预言机依赖于目标，其真值表可以不受限制；文中并未声称存在高效的经典构造。

英文原文

Solves the constant-error Aaronson–Kuperberg unitary synthesis problem: a uniform polynomial-size quantum oracle circuit approximates every nnn-qubit unitary channel within diamond-norm error 1/21/21/2, after a suitable Boolean oracle is chosen.

Gates, qubits, oracle calls and query length are polynomially bounded.

The target-dependent oracle may have an unrestricted truth table; its efficient classical construction is not asserted.

论文

  1. 通过布尔预言机实现多项式时间酉算子合成

    Polynomial-Time Unitary Synthesis from a Boolean Oracle

    摘要

    我们对 Aaronson–Kuperberg 酉算子合成问题的常数误差版本给出肯定回答。

    对每个 n，仅由 n 在多项式时间内生成的一个量子预言机电路，在选定适当的布尔预言机后，能够逼近每个 n 量子比特酉算子所对应的信道，使完整钻石范数误差至多为 1/2。

    该电路使用固定门 H,T,T†,CNOTH,T,T^\dagger,\mathrm{CNOT}H,T,T†,CNOT，其量子比特数、基本门数及预言机调用次数均为多项式数量，且预言机查询的长度为多项式量级。

    预言机可以依赖于目标酉算子；该定理未给出构造这一预言机的高效经典程序。

    英文原文

    We give a positive answer to the constant-error formulation of the Aaronson–Kuperberg unitary synthesis problem.

    For every n, a quantum oracle circuit generated in polynomial time from n alone can approximate the channel of every n-qubit unitary to full diamond-norm error at most 1/2, after a suitable Boolean oracle is chosen.

    Using the fixed gates H,T,T†,CNOTH,T,T^\dagger,\mathrm{CNOT}H,T,T†,CNOT, the circuit has polynomially many qubits, elementary gates, and oracle calls, and its oracle queries have polynomial length.

    The oracle may depend on the target unitary; the theorem does not give an efficient classical procedure for constructing it.
