度量 Blaschke 猜想

The metric Blaschke conjecture

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

证明了度量 Blaschke 猜想：每个正维数的闭连通 Riemann 流形，若其单射半径等于直径，则在相差一个尺度因子的意义下，是标准的紧秩一对称空间。

Proves the metric Blaschke conjecture: every closed connected Riemannian manifold of positive dimension whose injectivity radius equals its diameter is, up to scaling, a standard compact rank-one symmetric space.

OpenAI · overview.tex

论文

  1. 度量 Blaschke 定理

    The metric Blaschke theorem

    摘要

    我们证明了度量 Blaschke 猜想：每个整体单射半径等于直径的正维连通闭光滑 Riemann 流形，在相差一个尺度因子的意义下，都是一个标准的紧秩一对称空间。

    英文原文

    We prove the metric Blaschke conjecture: every connected closed smooth Riemannian manifold of positive dimension whose global injectivity radius equals its diameter is, up to scale, a standard compact rank-one symmetric space.
