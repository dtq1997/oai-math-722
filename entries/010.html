素数 2 处的 Fontaine–Mazur 模性

Fontaine–Mazur modularity at the prime 222 and 222-adic pro-modularity

OpenAI · 2026-10-063 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

GQG_{\mathbb Q}GQ​ 的每个连续、奇、绝对不可约的二维 222 进表示，若仅在有限多个素数处可能分歧，则都出现在某个奇数驯水平的完备 Hecke 代数中。

还证明了满足上述有限性条件、在 222 处为 de Rham 且 Hodge–Tate 权互异的不可约奇表示，在相差 Tate 扭曲的意义下具有经典模性，从而在不附加剩余表示限制的情况下解决 Fontaine–Mazur 猜想的二进情形。

英文原文

Every continuous odd absolutely irreducible two-dimensional 222-adic representation of GQG_{\mathbb Q}GQ​ unramified outside finitely many primes occurs in a completed Hecke algebra at some odd tame level.

Also proves classical modularity up to Tate twist for irreducible odd representations with these finiteness conditions that are de Rham at 222 with distinct Hodge–Tate weights, resolving the dyadic Fontaine–Mazur case without residual restrictions.

  1. 素数二处无附加限制的 pro-模性

    Unrestricted pro-modularity at the prime two

    摘要

    GQG_{\mathbb Q}GQ​ 的每个连续、奇、绝对不可约的二维 2 进表示，只要在有限多个有限素点之外非分歧，就在某个奇的驯水平下的完整完备 Hecke 代数中出现。

    该水平可以包含辅助的驯素数，且该结果涵盖标量及可约的剩余表示。

    这是一个关于在完备 Hecke 代数中出现的结果，无须 de Rham 假设；它并不声称经典模性。

    英文原文

    Every continuous, odd, absolutely irreducible two-dimensional 2-adic representation of GQG_{\mathbb Q}GQ​ that is unramified outside finitely many finite primes occurs in the full completed Hecke algebra at some odd tame level.

    The level may contain auxiliary tame primes, and scalar and reducible residual representations are included.

    This is a completed-Hecke occurrence result, with no de Rham hypothesis; it does not assert classical modularity.

  2. 奇数水平下二进 Hecke 代数的维数

    The Dimension of the Two-Adic Hecke Algebra at Odd Level

    摘要

    对于每个奇正整数 N，水平为 Γ1(N)\Gamma_1(N)Γ1​(N) 的完整二进 Hecke 代数的每个不可约分支的 Krull 维数均为四。

    这证明了 Emerton 维数猜想中 p = 2 的情形，包括所有剩余分支。

    英文原文

    For every odd positive integer N, every irreducible component of the full two-adic Hecke algebra of level Γ1(N)\Gamma_1(N)Γ1​(N) has Krull dimension four.

    This proves the p = 2 case of Emerton's dimension conjecture, including all residual components.

  3. 素数 2 处的 Fontaine–Mazur 模性

    Fontaine–Mazur modularity at the prime 2

    摘要

    我们证明，GQG_{\mathbb Q}GQ​ 的每个连续、不可约、奇的二维 2 进表示，只要在有限多个素数之外非分歧，且在 2 处为 de Rham 并具有互异的 Hodge–Tate 权，就在相差 Tate 扭曲的意义下具有模性。

    这解决了 ℚ 上在 2 处的奇、正则二维 Fontaine–Mazur 猜想，包括所有剩余表示。

    英文原文

    We prove that every continuous, irreducible, odd two-dimensional 2-adic representation of GQG_{\mathbb Q}GQ​, unramified outside finitely many primes and de Rham at 2 with distinct Hodge–Tate weights, is modular up to Tate twist.

    This resolves the odd, regular two-dimensional Fontaine–Mazur conjecture over ℚ at 2, including all residual representations.
