088 · 凸几何与度量几何

Petty 投影体体积与单纯形反例

Petty's projection-volume conjecture and simplex counterexamples

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

在剩余维数 n≥4n\ge4n≥4 下证明了 Petty 投影体体积猜想：在凸体体积固定时，椭球是投影体体积的唯一极小者。

还确立了完整的 Lutwak–Petty 投影不等式。

与之相反，在每个充分大的维数下，单纯形的乘积所具有的归一化投影体体积，比 Brannen 所提出的单纯形最大值大一个指数因子。

英文原文

Proves Petty's projection-volume conjecture in the remaining dimensions n≥4n\ge4n≥4: ellipsoids uniquely minimize projection-body volume at fixed body volume.

Also establishes the full Lutwak–Petty projection inequalities.

In contrast, products of simplices exceed Brannen's proposed simplex maximum for normalized projection-body volume by an exponential factor in every sufficiently large dimension.

    Petty’s projection-volume conjecture in dimensions at least four

    主结果已列入形式化清单
    摘要

    我们证明，在每个至少为四的维数中，在体积固定的凸体之间，椭球唯一地使投影体的体积达到最小。

    英文原文

    We prove that ellipsoids uniquely minimize the volume of the projection body among convex bodies of fixed volume in every dimension at least four.

    This proves Petty's projection-volume conjecture in these dimensions.

  2. 投影体体积的单纯形最大性断言的乘积反例

    A product counterexample to the simplex maximum for projection-body volume

    主结果已列入形式化清单
    摘要

    两个十维单纯形的乘积，其归一化投影体体积大于二十维单纯形的归一化投影体体积。

    这给出了 Brannen 所提出的单纯形最大性断言的一个反例。

    英文原文

    The product of two ten-dimensional simplices has larger normalized projection-body volume than a twenty-dimensional simplex.

    This gives a counterexample to Brannen's proposed simplex maximum.

# Sharp projection-body inequalities and a counterexample to simplex maximization

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Petty’s projection-volume conjecture in dimensions at least four](../../preprints/Pettys-projection-volume-conjecture-in-dimensions-at-least-four-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)
- [A product counterexample to the simplex maximum for projection-body volume](../../preprints/A-product-counterexample-to-the-simplex-maximum-for-projection-body-volume-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

Petty's projection-volume conjecture predicts

$\displaystyle \frac{|\Pi K|}{|K|^{n-1}}\ge \kappa_{n-1}^{n}\kappa_n^{2-n},$

with equality exactly for ellipsoids; here $\kappa_j$ is the volume of the Euclidean unit ball in dimension $j$. The formalization establishes this for every convex body $K\subset\mathbb R^n$ and every $n\ge4$.

Other inequalities in the projection-body family are not included.

Brannen's simplex-maximization conjecture predicts that a simplex maximizes normalized projection-body volume $|\Pi K|/|K|^{n-1}$ in dimension $n$. The formalized counterexample is the product of two ten-dimensional simplices. Its normalized projection volume, divided by that of a twenty-dimensional simplex, is

$\displaystyle \frac{22{,}355{,}476}{22{,}020{,}096}>1.$

Thus the proposed maximum fails in dimension $20$. The paper's exponential-factor result for every sufficiently large dimension is not included.
