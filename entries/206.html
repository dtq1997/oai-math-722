有限格表示的反例与不可判定性

Finite lattice representation: counterexamples and undecidability

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

某些有限格不是任何有限代数的同余格，从而否定地回答了有限格表示问题。

此外，不存在算法能够判定一个有限格是否具有这样的表示，或它是否为某个有限群的完整子群区间。

英文原文

Some finite lattices are not congruence lattices of any finite algebra, answering the finite lattice representation problem negatively.

Moreover, no algorithm decides whether a finite lattice has such a representation, or whether it is a full subgroup interval of a finite group.

    Finite congruence lattices: characterization and undecidability

    摘要

    对于能够作为有限代数的完整同余格出现的有限非空格，我们给出了一个明确的着色图刻画，并证明判定这一表示性质是不可判定的。

    特别地，有限格表示问题的答案是否定的。

    我们还证明了识别有限群中的完整子群区间是不可判定的。

    英文原文

    We give an explicit colored-graph characterization of the finite nonempty lattices that occur as full congruence lattices of finite algebras, and prove that deciding this representation property is undecidable.

    In particular, the finite lattice representation problem has a negative answer.

    We also prove that recognition of full subgroup intervals in finite groups is undecidable.

# Finite lattice representation and undecidability

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Finite congruence lattices: characterization and undecidability](../../preprints/Finite-Congruence-Lattices-Characterization-and-Undecidability-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The finite lattice representation problem asks which finite lattices occur as the full congruence lattice of a finite algebra. The formalization proves the paper's colored-graph characterization: a finite nonempty lattice is representable exactly when it admits the specified finite nonempty graph witness, whose edge colors encode the required congruence relations. This selected theorem is the equivalence with the graph criterion; the paper's undecidability and subgroup-interval conclusions are outside it.
