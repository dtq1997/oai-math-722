Quillen 猜想的有理同调形式

Quillen's conjecture in rational homology

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

对每个有限群和每个素数，证明了 Quillen 猜想的有理同调形式。

如果 GGG 的最大正规 ppp 子群是平凡的，那么非平凡初等交换 ppp 子群构成的偏序集具有非零的增广约化有理同调，因此不可缩。

英文原文

Proves the rational-homology form of Quillen's conjecture for every finite group and every prime.

If the largest normal ppp-subgroup of GGG is trivial, the poset of nontrivial elementary abelian ppp-subgroups has nonzero augmented reduced rational homology and is therefore not contractible.

    Rational homology and Quillen's conjecture

    摘要

    我们对所有有限群及所有素数证明了 Quillen 猜想。

    更准确地说，若有限群 G 的最大正规 p 子群 Op(G)O_p(G)Op​(G) 平凡，则 G 的非平凡初等交换 p 子群组成的偏序集具有非零的增广约化有理同调。

    这确立了该猜想更强的有理同调形式。

    英文原文

    We prove Quillen's conjecture for all finite groups and all primes.

    More precisely, if a finite group G has trivial largest normal p-subgroup Op(G)O_p(G)Op​(G), then the poset of nontrivial elementary abelian p-subgroups of G has nonzero augmented reduced rational homology.

    This establishes the stronger rational-homology form of the conjecture.
