素域上的确定性多项式分解

Deterministic polynomial factorization over prime fields

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

给出了一种一致的确定性算法，它能将素域 Fp\mathbb F_pFp​ 上每个以稠密形式表示的非零 nnn 次多项式完全分解，包括各因子的重数，所需比特复杂度为关于 (n+1)log⁡p(n+1)\log p(n+1)logp 的多项式。

素数以二进制形式给定。

不需要随机性、整数分解预言机、原根预言机或 GRH 假设。

英文原文

Gives a uniform deterministic algorithm that completely factors every nonzero dense degree-nnn polynomial over a prime field Fp\mathbb F_pFp​, including multiplicities, in bit complexity polynomial in (n+1)log⁡p(n+1)\log p(n+1)logp.

The prime is supplied in binary.

No randomness, integer-factorization or primitive-root oracle, or GRH assumption is required.

论文

  1. 素域上多项式的确定性因式分解

    Deterministic Polynomial Factorization over Prime Fields

    摘要

    我们给出一个在素域上进行完全因式分解的一致确定性多项式时间算法。

    对于以二进制表示的素数 p，以及以稠密系数表给出的非零多项式 f∈Fp[x]f\in\mathbf F_p[x]f∈Fp​[x]，该算法计算其不可约因子及其重数，所用比特运算次数关于 (deg⁡f+1)log⁡p(\deg f+1)\log p(degf+1)logp 为多项式。

    证明使用姊妹论文 *Primitive roots for every admissible integer base* 中的一致 Hecke 无零点定理。

    英文原文

    We give a uniform deterministic polynomial-time algorithm for complete factorization over prime fields.

    For a prime p in binary and a nonzero polynomial f∈Fp[x]f\in\mathbf F_p[x]f∈Fp​[x] given by its dense coefficient list, the algorithm computes the irreducible factors and their multiplicities using a number of bit operations polynomial in (deg⁡f+1)log⁡p(\deg f+1)\log p(degf+1)logp.

    The proof uses the uniform Hecke zero-free theorem from the companion paper *Primitive roots for every admissible integer base*.
