Kirchberg–Rørdam 的特征标判据

The Kirchberg–Rørdam character criterion

首发 · 2026-10-061 篇论文部分形式化

对每个自由超滤，一个非零含幺可分复 C∗C^*C∗ 代数是 Jiang–Su 稳定的，当且仅当其范数中心序列代数没有特征标。

这回答了 Kirchberg–Rørdam 的特征标问题。

此外，每个这样的无特征标代数的无限最小张量幂都是 Jiang–Su 稳定的，从而回答了 Dadarlat–Toms 的问题。

英文原文

A nonzero unital separable complex C∗C^*C∗-algebra is Jiang–Su stable exactly when its norm central-sequence algebra has no characters, for every free ultrafilter.

This answers the Kirchberg–Rørdam character question.

Also, the infinite minimal tensor power of every such algebra without characters is Jiang–Su stable, answering the Dadarlat–Toms question.

    摘要

    我们证明 Kirchberg–Rørdam 特征标判据：对 ℕ 上的每个自由超滤子，一个非零有单位元可分复 C∗ 代数吸收 Jiang–Su 代数，当且仅当其范数中心序列代数没有特征标。

    我们还证明，每个没有特征标的非零有单位元可分复 C∗ 代数的无限最小张量幂都是 Z\mathcal ZZ 稳定的，并包含一个 Z\mathcal ZZ 的保单位元副本。

    英文原文

    We prove the Kirchberg–Rørdam character criterion: for every free ultrafilter on ℕ, a nonzero unital separable complex C∗-algebra absorbs the Jiang–Su algebra if and only if its norm central-sequence algebra has no characters.

    We also prove that the infinite minimal tensor power of every nonzero unital separable complex C∗-algebra without characters is Z\mathcal ZZ-stable and contains a unital copy of Z\mathcal ZZ.

# The Kirchberg–Rørdam character criterion and infinite tensor-power Jiang–Su stability

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [The Kirchberg–Rørdam character criterion](../../preprints/The-Kirchberg-Rordam-character-criterion-September-25-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The Kirchberg–Rørdam criterion relates Jiang–Su absorption to characters of the central-sequence algebra. The formalized result proves that, for every nonzero unital separable complex $C^*$-algebra $A$ and every free ultrafilter on $\mathbb N$, the norm central-sequence algebra has no nonzero character exactly when $A\cong A\otimes_{\min}\mathcal Z$. No nuclearity, simplicity, trace, or comparison hypothesis is imposed.
