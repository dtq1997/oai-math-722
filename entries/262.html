Sharp one-dimensional Lieb–Thirring inequalities

对1/2<γ<3/21/2<\gamma<3/21/2<γ<3/2和满足∫tr⁡(Wγ+1/2)<∞\int\operatorname{tr}(W^{\gamma+1/2})<\infty∫tr(Wγ+1/2)<∞的任意有限阶矩阵势W≥0W\ge0W≥0，证明最优的一维 Lieb–Thirring 不等式：最优常数等于标量单束缚态的值，与矩阵大小无关。

Proves the sharp one-dimensional Lieb–Thirring inequality for 1/2<γ<3/21/2<\gamma<3/21/2<γ<3/2 and arbitrary finite-matrix potentials W≥0W\ge0W≥0 with ∫tr⁡(Wγ+1/2)<∞\int\operatorname{tr}(W^{\gamma+1/2})<\infty∫tr(Wγ+1/2)<∞: the optimal constant is the scalar one-bound-state value, independent of matrix size.

  2. 矩阵势的一维 Lieb–Thirring 最优不等式

    Sharp one-dimensional Lieb–Thirring inequalities for matrix potentials

    摘要

    我们证明了每个有限矩阵大小和每个指数 1/2<γ<3/21/2\lt \gamma\lt 3/21/2<γ<3/2 的最优一维 Lieb–Thirring 不等式。

    最优常数就是标量单束缚态常数，与矩阵大小无关。

    对于每个满足 ∫Rtr(Wγ+1/2)<∞\int_\mathbb R\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}\nolimits (W^{\gamma+1/2})\lt \infty∫R​tr(Wγ+1/2)<∞ 的可测 Hermitian 半正定势 W，该不等式给出了全部负特征值矩之和的界。

    这些矩阵的秩可以任意，且不同点处的矩阵不必交换。

    英文原文

    We prove the sharp one-dimensional Lieb–Thirring inequality for every finite matrix size and every exponent 1/2<γ<3/21/2\lt \gamma\lt 3/21/2<γ<3/2.

    The optimal constant is the scalar one-bound-state constant, independently of the matrix size.

    The inequality bounds the full sum of negative eigenvalue moments for every measurable Hermitian positive semidefinite potential W satisfying ∫Rtr(Wγ+1/2)<∞\int_\mathbb R\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}\nolimits (W^{\gamma+1/2})\lt \infty∫R​tr(Wγ+1/2)<∞.

    The matrices may have arbitrary rank and need not commute at different points.

# Sharp finite-matrix Lieb–Thirring inequalities and all equality cases

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Sharp one-dimensional Lieb–Thirring constants](../../preprints/Sharp-One-Dimensional-Lieb-Thirring-Constants-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The formalized result determines the sharp one-dimensional Lieb–Thirring constant for $1/2<\gamma<3/2$. For every nonnegative $W\in L^{\gamma+1/2}(\mathbb R)$, it bounds the full negative-eigenvalue moment of $-d^2/dx^2-W$ by the one-bound-state constant times the potential integral. This constant is optimal and is attained by $(r+1)\mathrm{sech}^2(rx)$, where $r=(\gamma-1/2)^{-1}$. No finite spectral cutoff is imposed.
