Chai 不变理想猜想

Chai's invariant-ideal conjecture

首发 · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

对于有限剩余域上的 Lubin–Tate 形变环，在每个素数与每个正高度 nnn 处证明了 Chai 不变理想猜想。

通过 Barthel–Heard–Naumann 给出的蕴含关系，这证明了 Hovey–Strickland 猜想：可对偶的 K(n)K(n)K(n) 局部谱恰有 n+2n+2n+2 个厚张量理想，其 Balmer 谱是一条由 n+1n+1n+1 个点组成的链。

英文原文

Proves Chai's invariant-ideal conjecture for Lubin–Tate deformation rings over finite residue fields, at every prime and positive height nnn.

Through the implication of Barthel–Heard–Naumann, this proves the Hovey–Strickland conjecture: dualizable K(n)K(n)K(n)-local spectra have exactly n+2n+2n+2 thick tensor ideals, and their Balmer spectrum is a chain of n+1n+1n+1 points.

OpenAI · overview.tex

论文

  1. 稳定子轨道与可对偶化 K(n) 局部谱的厚张量理想

    Stabilizer orbits and thick tensor ideals of dualizable K(n)-local spectra

    摘要

    对每个素数及每个正高度，我们分类了 Lubin–Tate 形变环中在某个开 Morava 稳定子子群作用下不变的素理想与根理想。

    这证明了 Chai 的不变理想猜想的标准有限剩余域版本。

    这也证明了 Hovey–Strickland 猜想：可对偶化的 K(n)K(n)K(n) 局部范畴恰有 n+2n+2n+2 个厚张量理想，其 Balmer 谱是一条含 n+1n+1n+1 个点的链。

    英文原文

    We classify the prime and radical ideals of the Lubin–Tate deformation ring invariant under an open Morava stabilizer subgroup, at every prime and positive height.

    This proves Chai's invariant-ideal conjecture in the standard finite-residue-field formulation.

    It also proves the Hovey–Strickland conjecture: the dualizable K(n)K(n)K(n)-local category has exactly n+2n+2n+2 thick tensor ideals, and its Balmer spectrum is a chain of n+1n+1n+1 points.
