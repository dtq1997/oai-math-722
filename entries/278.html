Kohn–Sham 系综表示失败

Failure of Kohn–Sham ensemble representation

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

构造了一个具有两个电荷相等且为正整数的原子核的三电子 Coulomb 分子，其绝对基态密度无法由 L3/2(R3)+L∞(R3)L^{3/2}(\mathbb R^3)+L^\infty(\mathbb R^3)L3/2(R3)+L∞(R3) 中的单个实值、与自旋无关的局部势给出非相互作用基态系综表示。

这否定了该势类中的 Kohn–Sham 系综可表示性；所需核电荷以非数值方式指定。

英文原文

Constructs a three-electron Coulomb molecule with two equal positive-integer-charge nuclei whose absolute ground-state density has no noninteracting ground-state ensemble representation by a single real spin-independent local potential in L3/2(R3)+L∞(R3)L^{3/2}(\mathbb R^3)+L^\infty(\mathbb R^3)L3/2(R3)+L∞(R3).

This disproves Kohn–Sham ensemble representability for that potential class; the required nuclear charge is specified nonnumerically.

论文

  1. 不能由 Kohn-Sham 系综表示的 Coulomb 基态密度

    A Coulomb ground-state density without Kohn-Sham ensemble representation

    摘要

    我们构造一个有限的三电子 Coulomb 分子，其对自旋求和后的基态密度，无法由处于任何单一的、属于 L3/2(R3)+L∞(R3)L^{3/2}(\mathbf R^3)+L^\infty(\mathbf R^3)L3/2(R3)+L∞(R3) 的实值且自旋无关局部势中的无相互作用电子的任何基态系综复现。

    该分子具有两个相等的正整数核电荷，它们由一个精确、有限、非数值的公式指定。

    英文原文

    We construct a finite three-electron Coulomb molecule whose spin-summed ground-state density cannot be reproduced by any ground-state ensemble of noninteracting electrons in a single real, spin-independent local potential in L3/2(R3)+L∞(R3)L^{3/2}(\mathbf R^3)+L^\infty(\mathbf R^3)L3/2(R3)+L∞(R3).

    The molecule has two equal positive integer nuclear charges, specified by an exact finite nonnumerical formula.
