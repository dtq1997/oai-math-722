四维 Singer 猜想

The four-dimensional Singer conjecture

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

证明了每个闭、连通、非球面拓扑四维流形的万有覆盖，其 L2L^2L2 Betti 数在二次以外均为零。

更一般地，对于每个形式维数为四的有限、连通、非球面整系数 Poincaré 复形，同样的结论成立，从而在这一更广的类别中证明了四维 Singer 猜想。

英文原文

Proves that the L2L^2L2-Betti numbers of the universal cover of every closed connected aspherical topological four-manifold vanish outside degree two.

More generally, the same conclusion holds for every finite connected aspherical integral Poincaré complex of formal dimension four, proving the four-dimensional Singer conjecture in this wider class.

论文

  1. 四维 Singer 猜想

    The Singer conjecture in dimension four

    摘要

    我们证明四维 Singer 猜想：闭、连通、非球面拓扑四维流形的万有覆盖的 L2 Betti 数在二次以外均为零。

    更一般地，对于形式维数为四的有限连通非球面整系数 Poincaré 复形，包括不可定向者，同样的消失结论也成立。

    英文原文

    We prove the four-dimensional Singer conjecture: the L2-Betti numbers of the universal cover of a closed connected aspherical topological four-manifold vanish outside degree two.

    More generally, the same vanishing holds for finite connected aspherical integral Poincaré complexes of formal dimension four, including nonorientable ones.
