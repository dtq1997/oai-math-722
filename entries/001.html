Milne 的有理性猜想

Milne's rationality conjecture

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

证明了具有良约化的 Q‾\overline{\mathbb Q}Q​ 上阿贝尔簇的 Milne 有理性猜想：特化后的 Hodge 类与互补维数的除子乘积配对所得为有理数，且与所选的上同调理论无关。

结合第 032 组的结果，对每个剩余特征 ppp，每个特化后的 Hodge 类都可由同一个有理系数代数闭链同时在所有素于 ppp 的实现及晶体实现中表示。

英文原文

Proves Milne's rationality conjecture for abelian varieties over Q‾\overline{\mathbb Q}Q​ with good reduction: specialized Hodge classes pair rationally with complementary divisor products, independently of cohomology theory.

Together with result 032, every specialized Hodge class is represented by a single rational algebraic cycle simultaneously in all prime-to-ppp and crystalline realizations, for every residue characteristic ppp.

论文

  1. 阿贝尔簇的 Milne 有理性猜想

    Milne's rationality conjecture for abelian varieties

    摘要

    我们证明了阿贝尔簇的 Milne 有理性猜想，包括剩余特征为 2 的情形。

    经过良约化后，一个有理 Hodge 类与约化上任意互补维数的除子类乘积的配对，在每个素于 p 的实现以及晶体上同调中都是同一个有理数。

    利用 CM 阿贝尔簇的 Hodge 定理，我们还证明，每个这样的特化 Hodge 类在所有这些实现中都由同一个有理系数代数闭链表示。

    英文原文

    We prove Milne's rationality conjecture for abelian varieties, including residue characteristic 2.

    After good reduction, the pairing of a rational Hodge class with any complementary product of divisor classes on the reduction is the same rational number in every prime-to-p realization and in crystalline cohomology.

    Using the Hodge theorem for CM abelian varieties, we also show that every such specialized Hodge class is represented by a single rational algebraic cycle in all these realizations.
