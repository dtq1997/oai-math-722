035 · 代数几何与复几何

特征 p>3p>3p>3 下数值维数为一的三维对数丰沛性

Threefold log abundance in numerical dimension one in characteristic p>3p>3p>3

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

对于特征为 p>3p>3p>3 的代数闭域上的射影对数典范三维对，若边界为有效有理除子，且 Q\mathbb QQ-Cartier 伴随除子为 nef、数值维数为一，则证明对数丰沛性成立。

该伴随除子是半丰沛的，无须要求原簇具有终端奇点或为 Q\mathbb QQ-factorial。

英文原文

Proves log abundance for projective log canonical threefold pairs over algebraically closed fields of characteristic p>3p>3p>3 when the effective boundary is rational and the Q\mathbb QQ-Cartier adjoint is nef of numerical dimension one.

The adjoint is semiample, without requiring the original variety to be terminal or Q\mathbb QQ-factorial.

论文

  1. 正特征下三维簇在数值维数为一时的对数丰沛性

    Log abundance in numerical dimension one for threefolds in positive characteristic

    摘要

    我们证明了特征为 p > 3 的代数闭域上三维簇的对数丰沛性中数值维数为一的情形。

    若 (X,B)(X,B)(X,B) 是一个具有有效有理边界的射影对数典范三维对，且 KX+BK_X+BKX​+B 为 ℚ-Cartier、nef、数值维数为一，则 KX+BK_X+BKX​+B 是半丰沛的。

    既不要求 X 具有终端奇点，也不要求其为 ℚ-factorial。

    英文原文

    We prove the numerical-dimension-one case of log abundance for threefolds over algebraically closed fields of characteristic p > 3.

    If (X,B)(X,B)(X,B) is a projective log canonical threefold pair with effective rational boundary, and KX+BK_X+BKX​+B is ℚ-Cartier, nef, and of numerical dimension one, then KX+BK_X+BKX​+B is semiample.

    Neither terminality nor ℚ-factoriality of X is required.

  2. 正特征下具有终端奇点的三维簇在数值维数为一时的丰沛性

    Abundance in numerical dimension one for terminal threefolds in positive characteristic

    摘要

    设 X 是特征为 p > 3 的代数闭域上具有终端奇点的射影 ℚ-factorial 三维簇。

    我们证明了丰沛性中数值维数为一的情形：若 KX 为 nef 且满足 ν(KX)=1\nu(K_X)=1ν(KX​)=1，则 KX 半丰沛，并且 κ(X,KX)=1\kappa(X,K_X)=1κ(X,KX​)=1。

    英文原文

    Let X be a projective ℚ-factorial terminal threefold over an algebraically closed field of characteristic p > 3.

    We prove the numerical-dimension-one case of abundance: if KX is nef with ν(KX)=1\nu(K_X)=1ν(KX​)=1, then KX is semiample and κ(X,KX)=1\kappa(X,K_X)=1κ(X,KX​)=1.
