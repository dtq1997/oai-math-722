Solomon–Yau 最小体积猜想

The Solomon–Yau least-volume conjecture

首发 · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

对圆球面的极小超曲面，证明了 Solomon–Yau 最小体积猜想。

对每个 m≥2m\ge2m≥2，一个到单位球面 Sm+1S^{m+1}Sm+1 的闭连通极小浸入，若其像不是全测地的，则计入覆盖重数后的体积至少为体积最小的极小 Clifford 乘积的体积。

英文原文

Proves the Solomon–Yau least-volume conjecture for minimal hypersurfaces of round spheres.

For every m≥2m\ge2m≥2, a closed connected minimal immersion into the unit sphere Sm+1S^{m+1}Sm+1 with non-totally-geodesic image has volume at least that of the smallest minimal Clifford product, counting covering multiplicity.

论文

  1. Solomon–Yau 最小体积定理

    The Solomon–Yau least-volume theorem

    摘要

    我们证明了单位圆球面中极小超曲面的 Solomon–Yau 最小体积猜想：在每个维数 m ≥ 2 中，一个像非全测地的闭连通极小浸入，其体积至少等于体积最小的 m 维极小 Clifford 乘积的体积。

    体积是在定义域上测量的，因此计入覆盖重数。

    英文原文

    We prove the Solomon–Yau least-volume conjecture for minimal hypersurfaces of unit round spheres: in every dimension m ≥ 2, a closed connected minimal immersion with non-totally-geodesic image has volume at least the smallest m-dimensional minimal Clifford product.

    Volume is measured on the domain, so covering multiplicities are included.
