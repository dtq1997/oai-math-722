开 Galois 同态的 Uchida 猜想

Uchida's conjecture for open Galois homomorphisms

首发 · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

证明了 Uchida 猜想：数域的两个可解闭 Galois 扩张（允许为无限扩张）的 Galois 群之间，每个连续开同态都来自唯一的反向等变域嵌入。

不需要对核作限制，也不需要另加分圆相容性假设。

英文原文

Proves Uchida's conjecture: every continuous open homomorphism between Galois groups of possibly infinite solvably closed Galois extensions of number fields comes from a unique equivariant field embedding in the opposite direction.

No restriction on the kernel or separate cyclotomic-compatibility assumption is needed.

OpenAI · overview.tex

论文

  1. 整体可解闭 Galois 群的开同态

    Open Homomorphisms of Global Solvably Closed Galois Groups

    摘要

    我们证明了 Uchida 关于 Galois 群的开同态的猜想。

    数域的可解闭 Galois 扩张的 Galois 群之间，每个连续开同态都由反方向上唯一的等变域嵌入诱导。

    无须对核施加限制，也无须额外的相容性假设。

    英文原文

    We prove Uchida's conjecture on open homomorphisms of Galois groups.

    Every continuous open homomorphism between Galois groups of solvably closed Galois extensions of number fields is induced by a unique equivariant field embedding in the opposite direction.

    No restriction on the kernel or additional compatibility hypothesis is required.
