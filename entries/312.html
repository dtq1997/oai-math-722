    摘要

    我们对 Ara–Henry 约定下的每个 Grothendieck coherator 证明 Grothendieck 同伦假说：它的弱球状无穷群胚恢复空间的同伦理论。

    我们还解决 Henry 的推出猜想，证明初等扩张保持胞腔无穷群胚的分支以及所有同伦群。

    英文原文

    We prove the Grothendieck homotopy hypothesis for every Grothendieck coherator in the Ara–Henry convention: its weak globular infinity-groupoids recover the homotopy theory of spaces.

    We also resolve Henry's pushout conjecture, showing that elementary expansions preserve components and all homotopy groups of cellular infinity-groupoids.

# The Grothendieck homotopy hypothesis

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [The Grothendieck homotopy hypothesis via elementary expansions](../../preprints/The-Grothendieck-homotopy-hypothesis-via-elementary-expansions-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The Grothendieck homotopy hypothesis asks whether algebraic $\infty$-groupoids recover the homotopy theory of spaces. The formalized result is the elementary-expansion theorem used in this approach: for every Grothendieck coherator in the Ara–Henry convention and every boundary-cellular model, attaching an $(n+1)$-disk along its source $n$-face induces a weak equivalence.

The later semi-model structure and full comparison with the homotopy theory of spaces are not included.
