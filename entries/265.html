二维有谱隙系统的面积律

The two-dimensional gapped area law

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

仅利用一致的全系统谱隙和局部相互作用的界，证明任意有限的方格诱导区域上有限程 Hamiltonian 的唯一基态满足熵面积律。

在开边界L×LL\times LL×L正方形上，具有一致谱隙的最近邻基态还容许投影纠缠对态近似，其键维数为多项式量级，且全局向量误差至多为L−1L^{-1}L−1。

英文原文

Proves an entropy area law for unique ground states of finite-range Hamiltonians on arbitrary finite induced square-lattice domains, using only a uniform full-system spectral gap and bounds on the local interactions.

On open L×LL\times LL×L squares, uniformly gapped nearest-neighbor ground states also admit projected entangled-pair state approximations with polynomial bond dimension and global vector error at most L−1L^{-1}L−1.

论文

  1. 由全局谱隙推出的二维面积律

    A two-dimensional area law from a global spectral gap

    摘要

    我们证明了方格的任意有限诱导子图上的有限程 Hamilton 算子的唯一基态所满足的熵面积律。

    只需完整 Hamilton 算子的谱隙下界，以及局部维数、相互作用范围和相互作用强度的固定界。

    对于每个格点集合，其纠缠熵以穿过其边界的边数的一个常数倍为界，与区域的大小和形状无关。

    英文原文

    We prove an entropy area law for the unique ground state of a finite-range Hamiltonian on any finite induced subgraph of the square lattice.

    A lower bound on the spectral gap of the full Hamiltonian and fixed bounds on the local dimension, interaction range, and interaction strength suffice.

    For every set of sites, its entanglement entropy is bounded by a constant times the number of edges crossing its boundary, independently of the size and shape of the domain.

  2. 有能隙方格基态的多项式键维数 PEPS 近似

    Polynomial PEPS approximation of gapped square-grid ground states

    摘要

    我们证明，L×LL\times LL×L 方格上具有一致谱隙的最近邻 Hamilton 算子的唯一基态，具有一个投影纠缠对态近似，其键维数关于 L 为多项式，且归一化后的全局向量误差至多为 L−1。

    仅假设完整 Hamilton 算子的谱隙。

    该结果是一个存在性定理，其中的常数对于局部维数、相互作用强度和谱隙固定的 Hamilton 算子一致成立。

    英文原文

    We prove that the unique ground state of a uniformly gapped nearest-neighbor Hamiltonian on an L×LL\times LL×L square lattice admits a projected entangled-pair state approximation with bond dimension polynomial in L and global vector error at most L−1 after normalization.

    Only the gap of the full Hamiltonian is assumed.

    The result is an existence theorem, with constants uniform over Hamiltonians of fixed local dimension, interaction strength, and gap.
