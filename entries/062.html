LeBrun–Salamon 与射影接触分类

The LeBrun–Salamon conjecture and projective contact classification

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

证明了 LeBrun–Salamon 猜想：每个实维至少为八的闭连通正 quaternionic-Kähler 流形，都与紧对称 Wolf 空间位似。

还证明了接触 Fano 流形的齐性，并分类复维至少为三的光滑连通复射影接触流形：当 b2=1b_2=1b2​=1 时，它是带典范接触结构的伴随簇；当 b2≥2b_2\ge2b2​≥2 时，其底层流形为 P(T∗Z)\mathbb P(T^*Z)P(T∗Z)，其中 ZZZ 是光滑射影簇。

英文原文

Proves the LeBrun–Salamon conjecture: every closed connected positive quaternionic-Kähler manifold of real dimension at least eight is homothetic to a compact symmetric Wolf space.

It also proves contact-Fano homogeneity and classifies smooth connected complex projective contact manifolds of complex dimension at least three: those with b2=1b_2=1b2​=1 are adjoint varieties with their canonical contact structures, while those with b2≥2b_2\ge2b2​≥2 have underlying manifold P(T∗Z)\mathbb P(T^*Z)P(T∗Z) for a smooth projective variety ZZZ.

论文

  1. 接触 Fano 流形与 LeBrun–Salamon 猜想

    Contact Fano manifolds and the LeBrun–Salamon conjecture

    摘要

    我们肯定地解决了接触 Fano 齐性猜想和 Riemann 几何中的 LeBrun–Salamon 猜想。

    每个复维数至少为三的光滑连通复射影接触 Fano 流形，连同其给定的接触分布，都接触同构于某个复单 Lie 代数的伴随簇。

    因此，每个实维数为 4m≥84m\geq84m≥8 的闭连通光滑正四元数 Kähler 流形都与一个紧对称 Wolf 空间位似。

    英文原文

    We resolve the contact-Fano homogeneity conjecture and the Riemannian LeBrun–Salamon conjecture positively.

    Every smooth connected complex projective contact Fano manifold of complex dimension at least three, with its given contact distribution, is contact-isomorphic to the adjoint variety of a simple complex Lie algebra.

    Consequently, every closed connected smooth positive quaternionic-Kähler manifold of real dimension 4m≥84m\geq84m≥8 is homothetic to a compact symmetric Wolf space.
