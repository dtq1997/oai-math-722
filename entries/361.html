Yau 调和维数界反例

Counterexamples to Yau's harmonic dimension bound

首发 · 2026-10-062 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

否定了 Yau 对非负 Ricci 曲率流形上具有整数阶增长的调和函数所提出的欧氏维数界。

对每个充分大的整数 kkk，R3\mathbb R^3R3 上存在一个完备光滑度量，其上至少有 (k+2)2(k+2)^2(k+2)2 个增长阶至多为 kkk 的线性无关调和函数，超过了欧氏情形的数目 (k+1)2(k+1)^2(k+1)2。

该度量可以依赖于 kkk。

英文原文

Disproves Yau's proposed Euclidean dimension bound for harmonic functions of integer growth on manifolds with nonnegative Ricci curvature.

For every sufficiently large integer kkk, a complete smooth metric on R3\mathbb R^3R3 has at least (k+2)2(k+2)^2(k+2)2 independent harmonic functions of growth at most kkk, exceeding the Euclidean count (k+1)2(k+1)^2(k+1)2.

The metric may depend on kkk.

  1. 整数阶增长调和函数维数比较的一个反例

    A counterexample to integer-degree harmonic dimension comparison

    已列入形式化清单
    摘要

    对于某个偶数 n ≥ 8 和整数 k ≥ 2，我们在 ℝn 上构造了一个具有非负 Ricci 曲率的完备光滑度量，其逐点多项式增长次数至多为 k 的实调和函数空间的维数，大于欧氏空间中相应调和多项式空间的维数。

    该度量在原点附近是欧氏的，渐近体积比严格介于零和一之间，且在无穷远处的切锥不唯一。

    这否定地回答了 Yau 维数比较问题的整数次数形式。

    英文原文

    For some even n ≥ 8 and integer k ≥ 2, we construct a complete smooth metric on ℝn with nonnegative Ricci curvature whose space of real harmonic functions of pointwise polynomial growth at most k has dimension larger than the Euclidean harmonic-polynomial dimension.

    The metric is Euclidean near the origin, has asymptotic volume ratio strictly between zero and one, and has nonunique tangent cones at infinity.

    This answers the integer-degree form of Yau's dimension comparison question in the negative.

  2. 整数阶增长调和函数维数比较的一个三维反例

    A Three-Dimensional Counterexample to Integer-Degree Harmonic Dimension Comparison

    摘要

    对于每个 4/9<v<14/9\lt v\lt 14/9<v<1 和 1<c<9v/41\lt c\lt 9v/41<c<9v/4，以及所有充分大的整数 k，ℝ3 上都存在一个完备光滑度量，它具有非负 Ricci 曲率、渐近体积比 v，以及至少 c(k+1)2c(k+1)^2c(k+1)2 个线性无关的、逐点增长次数至多为 k 的实调和函数。

    这在三维中否定地回答了 Yau 的整数次数维数比较问题，且相对于欧氏计数有一个固定因子的超出量。

    对于每个 1<c<9/41\lt c\lt 9/41<c<9/4，这些度量在全局双 Lipschitz 距离下可以选得任意接近欧氏度量。

    该度量可以依赖于 k。

    英文原文

    For every 4/9<v<14/9\lt v\lt 14/9<v<1 and 1<c<9v/41\lt c\lt 9v/41<c<9v/4, all sufficiently large integers k admit a complete smooth metric on ℝ3 with nonnegative Ricci curvature, asymptotic volume ratio v, and at least c(k+1)2c(k+1)^2c(k+1)2 linearly independent real harmonic functions of pointwise growth at most k.

    This answers Yau's integer-degree dimension comparison question negatively in dimension three, with a fixed-factor excess over the Euclidean count.

    For each 1<c<9/41\lt c\lt 9/41<c<9/4, these metrics can be chosen arbitrarily close to the Euclidean metric in global bi-Lipschitz distance.

    The metric may depend on k.

# Failure of integer-degree harmonic dimension comparison

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [A counterexample to integer-degree harmonic dimension comparison](../../preprints/A-counterexample-to-integer-degree-harmonic-dimension-comparison-September-25-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The formalized result disproves the proposed Euclidean upper comparison for dimensions of harmonic functions with integer polynomial growth. It constructs one complete smooth metric with nonnegative Ricci curvature on an even-dimensional Euclidean space, Euclidean near the origin and with asymptotic volume ratio strictly between zero and one, admitting more independent harmonic functions of the prescribed growth degree than the Euclidean count. The construction uses dimension $16$ and degree $50000$. Separate tangent-cone and cone-spectrum conclusions are not included.
