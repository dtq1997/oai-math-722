广义振幅阻尼的经典容量

The classical capacity of generalized amplitude damping

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

确定了每个量子比特广义振幅阻尼信道在所有阻尼参数和热参数下的无辅助经典容量。

一个显式的单变量优化给出了该容量，并可通过独立的双态信号系综配合集体解码达到。

与任意有限维量子信道取张量积时，Holevo 容量、最小输出熵和正则化经典容量均具有可加性。

英文原文

Determines the unassisted classical capacity of every qubit generalized amplitude-damping channel, including all damping and thermal parameters.

An explicit one-variable optimization gives the capacity, attained by independent two-state signal ensembles with collective decoding.

Holevo capacity, minimum output entropy and regularized classical capacity are additive when tensoring with any finite-dimensional quantum channel.

    Classical capacity and entropy inequalities for generalized amplitude damping

    主结果已列入形式化清单
    摘要

    我们对所有阻尼强度和热占据数，确定每个量子比特广义振幅阻尼信道的无辅助经典容量。

    它等于单次 Holevo 容量，并由一个单变量最大值给出。

    一个二元纯态系综达到单次使用时的最优值，其独立乘积则在每个块长度上达到最优值。

    我们还证明，单次 Holevo 容量、最小输出熵以及正则化的无辅助经典容量，在与每个有限维完全正保迹信道作张量积时均具有可加性。

    英文原文

    We determine the unassisted classical capacity of every qubit generalized amplitude-damping channel, for all damping strengths and thermal occupations.

    It equals the one-shot Holevo capacity and is given by a one-variable maximum.

    A binary pure-state ensemble attains the one-use optimum, and its independent products attain the optimum at every block length.

    We also prove that one-shot Holevo capacity, minimum output entropy, and regularized unassisted classical capacity are additive under tensor product with every finite-dimensional completely positive trace-preserving partner channel.

# Classical capacity of generalized amplitude damping

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Classical capacity and entropy inequalities for generalized amplitude damping](../../preprints/Classical-capacity-and-entropy-inequalities-for-generalized-amplitude-damping-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The formalized result determines the unassisted classical capacity of every generalized amplitude-damping channel with parameters in $[0,1]^2$. Its unrestricted Holevo information is additive across every number of repeated uses, equals an attained scalar maximum, and agrees with the operational capacity per use. Every maximizing equiprobable phase-pair product ensemble attains the corresponding block value. Additivity with an arbitrary different partner channel and decoding by separate measurements are not included.
