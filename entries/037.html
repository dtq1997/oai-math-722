普通二重点的体积间隙

The sharp ordinary-double-point volume gap

首发 · 2026-10-062 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

证明了普通二重点的体积间隙猜想：每个维数为 n≥2n\ge2n≥2、边界为零的奇异复代数 klt 芽，其归一化体积至多为 2(n−1)n2(n-1)^n2(n−1)n。

等号成立当且仅当该芽解析同构于普通二重点。

英文原文

Proves the ordinary-double-point volume-gap conjecture: every singular complex algebraic klt germ of dimension n≥2n\ge2n≥2, with zero boundary, has normalized volume at most 2(n−1)n2(n-1)^n2(n−1)n.

Equality holds precisely for an analytic ordinary double point.

  1. 所有维数中的普通二重点间隙

    The ordinary-double-point gap in every dimension

    摘要

    我们证明了所有维数中边界为零的复代数 klt 芽的普通二重点间隙猜想：奇异的 n 维芽的归一化体积至多为 2(n−1)n2(n-1)^n2(n−1)n，且等号成立当且仅当它在解析意义下是普通二重点。

    英文原文

    We prove the ordinary-double-point gap conjecture for boundary-zero complex algebraic klt germs in every dimension: a singular n-dimensional germ has normalized volume at most 2(n−1)n2(n-1)^n2(n−1)n, with equality precisely at an analytic ordinary double point.

    The normalized-volume gap in dimension four

    摘要

    我们证明，每个边界为零的奇异复代数 klt 四维芽的归一化体积至多为 162，且等号成立当且仅当它在解析意义下是普通二重点。

    英文原文

    We prove that every singular complex algebraic klt fourfold germ with zero boundary has normalized volume at most 162, with equality precisely for an analytic ordinary double point.

    This resolves the ordinary-double-point volume-gap conjecture in dimension four.
