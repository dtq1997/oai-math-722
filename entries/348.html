非负曲率 Einstein 四流形与拓扑间隙

Nonnegatively curved Einstein four-manifolds and a topological gap

OpenAI · 2026-10-063 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

分类了 Einstein 常数为正、截面曲率非负的闭连通 Einstein 四维流形：在相差一个尺度因子的意义下，它们的万有 Riemann 覆盖是圆 S4S^4S4、带 Fubini–Study 度量的 CP2\mathbb{CP}^2CP2，或两个相同圆二球面的乘积。

若一个闭单连通非负曲率四维流形的无迹 Ricci 曲率的尺度不变 L2L^2L2 范数低于某个普适的正常数，则它与上述流形之一微分同胚。

英文原文

Classifies closed connected Einstein four-manifolds with positive Einstein constant and nonnegative sectional curvature: up to scaling, their universal Riemannian covers are the round S4S^4S4, Fubini–Study CP2\mathbb{CP}^2CP2, or a product of equal round two-spheres.

A closed simply connected nonnegatively curved four-manifold is diffeomorphic to one of these whenever the scale-invariant L2L^2L2 norm of its trace-free Ricci curvature lies below a universal positive constant.

  1. Einstein 四维流形的零曲率平面刚性

    Zero-Plane Rigidity for Einstein Four-Manifolds

    摘要

    我们证明，一个具有正 Einstein 常数、非负截面曲率以及一个零曲率二平面的闭 Einstein 四维流形，其 Riemann 万有覆盖等距于两个圆二维球面的乘积。

    在归一化 Ric=3g\mathop{\mathrm{Ric}}\nolimits =3gRic=3g 下，两个球面的半径都为 1/31/\sqrt31/3​。

    证明将两个 Weyl 曲率块的耦合估计推广到截面曲率锥的边界，并确定了其等号情形。

    英文原文

    We prove that a closed Einstein four-manifold with positive Einstein constant, nonnegative sectional curvature, and a zero-curvature plane has universal Riemannian cover isometric to a product of two round two-spheres.

    Under the normalization Ric=3g\mathop{\mathrm{Ric}}\nolimits =3gRic=3g, both spheres have radius 1/31/\sqrt31/3​.

    The proof extends coupled estimates for the two Weyl curvature blocks to the boundary of the sectional-curvature cone and determines their equality case.

  2. 非负曲率四维流形的 L² Einstein 间隙

    An L² Einstein Gap for Nonnegatively Curved Four-Manifolds

    摘要

    我们证明，对于具有非负截面曲率的单连通闭四维流形，其无迹 Ricci 张量存在一个普适的、尺度不变的 L2 间隙。

    如果无迹 Ricci 能量充分小，则该流形微分同胚于 S4、CP2\mathbb{CP}^2CP2 或 S2×S2S^2\times S^2S2×S2。

    证明使用了配套的零平面刚性定理所提供的正 Einstein 常数、非负曲率四维流形的分类，我们将其明确陈述为结果的分类前提。

    无需额外的曲率、体积、直径、单射半径或 Sobolev 界。

    结论涉及光滑流形，而非原始度量的等距性。

    英文原文

    We prove a universal, scale-invariant L2 gap for the trace-free Ricci tensor on simply connected closed four-manifolds with nonnegative sectional curvature.

    If the trace-free Ricci energy is sufficiently small, the manifold is diffeomorphic to S4, CP2\mathbb{CP}^2CP2, or S2×S2S^2\times S^2S2×S2.

    The proof uses the classification of positive-Einstein, nonnegatively curved four-manifolds supplied by the companion zero-plane rigidity theorem, stated explicitly as the classification premise of our result.

    No auxiliary curvature, volume, diameter, injectivity-radius, or Sobolev bound is required.

    The conclusion concerns the smooth manifold, not an isometry of the original metric.

  3. 正曲率 Einstein 四维流形

    Positively curved Einstein four-manifolds

    摘要

    我们证明了具有严格正截面曲率的连通光滑闭 Einstein 四维流形的分类猜想。

    在相差正尺度变换和等距映射的意义下，每个这样的流形都是圆四维球面、带 Fubini–Study 度量的复射影平面，或带圆度量的实四维射影空间。

    无需可定向性假设。

    英文原文

    We prove the classification conjecture for connected smooth closed Einstein four-manifolds with strictly positive sectional curvature.

    Up to positive scaling and isometry, every such manifold is the round four-sphere, the complex projective plane with its Fubini–Study metric, or real projective four-space with its round metric.

    No orientability assumption is needed.

# Nonnegative-curvature Einstein classification and an <i>L</i><sup>2</sup> topological gap

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Positively curved Einstein four-manifolds](../../preprints/Positively-curved-Einstein-four-manifolds-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The formalization classifies connected smooth closed Einstein four-manifolds with strictly positive sectional curvature. Up to positive scaling and isometry, every such manifold is the round four-sphere, the complex projective plane with its Fubini–Study metric, or round real projective four-space. No orientability hypothesis is required. In the oriented case, only the sphere and complex projective plane occur.
