    The higher-dimensional Erdős distinct-distances conjecture

    摘要

    对于每个固定整数 d ≥ 3，我们证明，ℝd 中每个由 n ≥ 2 个不同点组成的集合都确定了至少 cdn2/dc_d n^{2/d}cd​n2/d 个不同的距离，其中 cd>0c_d\gt 0cd​>0 仅依赖于 d。

    这肯定地解决了高维 Erdős 不同距离猜想。

    英文原文

    For every fixed integer d ≥ 3, we prove that every set of n ≥ 2 distinct points in ℝd determines at least cdn2/dc_d n^{2/d}cd​n2/d distinct distances, where cd>0c_d\gt 0cd​>0 depends only on d.

    This resolves the higher-dimensional Erdős distinct-distances conjecture positively.
