超 Kähler SYZ 与射影空间底

Hyperkähler SYZ and projective-space bases

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

证明了强形式的超 Kähler SYZ 猜想：在紧不可约全纯辛 Kähler 流形上，每个第一 Chern 类非零、nef 且各向同性的全纯线丛都是半丰沛的。

还证明，在任意维数和形变类型下，每个底为正规射影簇的射影 Lagrangian 纤维化，其底都是射影空间。

英文原文

Proves the strong hyperkähler SYZ conjecture: every holomorphic line bundle with nonzero nef isotropic first Chern class on a compact irreducible holomorphic symplectic Kähler manifold is semiample.

It also proves that every projective Lagrangian fibration with normal projective base has projective space as its base, in every dimension and deformation type.

  1. Lagrangian 纤维化的射影空间底空间

    Projective-space bases of Lagrangian fibrations

    摘要

    我们证明，从紧不可约全纯辛 Kähler 流形出发的射影 Lagrangian 纤维化的正规射影底空间是射影空间。

    这解决了所有维数和形变类型中此类纤维化的射影空间底空间猜想。

    英文原文

    We prove that the normal projective base of a projective Lagrangian fibration from a compact irreducible holomorphic symplectic Kähler manifold is projective space.

    This resolves the projective-space base conjecture for such fibrations in every dimension and deformation type.
