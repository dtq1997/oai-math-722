随机删除三角形的精确渐近常数

The sharp constant in random triangle removal

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

从有 nnn 个顶点的完全图出发，反复均匀随机选取一个尚存的三角形并删除。

终止时的边数渐近于 n3/2/(22)n^{3/2}/(2\sqrt2)n3/2/(22​)，且按 n3/2n^{3/2}n3/2 归一化后依均方收敛。

这证明了 Joos–Kühn 精确常数猜想的三角形情形。

英文原文

Starting from the complete graph on nnn vertices, repeatedly delete a uniformly chosen remaining triangle.

The terminal edge count is asymptotic to n3/2/(22)n^{3/2}/(2\sqrt2)n3/2/(22​), with mean-square convergence after normalization by n3/2n^{3/2}n3/2.

This proves the triangle case of the Joos–Kühn sharp-constant conjecture.

  1. 随机三角形移除过程终止时剩余边数的精确渐近

    The sharp terminal leave in random triangle removal

    摘要

    从具有 n 个顶点的完全图出发，反复均匀选取一个尚存的三角形，并删除它的三条边。

    我们证明，终止时剩余的边数除以 n3/2 后，在 L2 中收敛于 1/(22)1/(2\sqrt2)1/(22​)。

    这证明了 Joos 和 Kühn 的精确常数猜想的三角形情形。

    特别地，依概率收敛以及归一化期望也具有相同的极限。

    英文原文

    Starting from the complete graph on n vertices, repeatedly remove the three edges of a uniformly chosen remaining triangle.

    We prove that the number of edges left at termination, divided by n3/2, converges in L2 to 1/(22)1/(2\sqrt2)1/(22​).

    This proves the triangle case of the sharp-constant conjecture of Joos and Kühn.

    In particular, the same limit holds in probability and for the normalized expectation.

# The sharp terminal leave in random triangle removal

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [The sharp terminal leave in random triangle removal](../../preprints/The-Sharp-Terminal-Leave-in-Random-Triangle-Removal-September-25-2026/The-Sharp-Terminal-Leave-in-Random-Triangle-Removal-September-25-2026.pdf)

## Scope

Start with the complete graph on $n$ vertices and repeatedly delete the three edges of a uniformly chosen remaining triangle. The formalization proves that the number of edges at termination, divided by $n^{3/2}$, converges in $L^2$ to $1/(2\sqrt2)$. It also states convergence in probability and convergence of the normalized expectation to the same constant. This is the triangle case of the sharp terminal-leave conjecture of Joos and Kühn.
