一般图的近线性时间精确匹配

Almost-linear-time exact matching in general graphs

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

给出了一种随机化算法，能在 (n+m)1+o(1)(n+m)^{1+o(1)}(n+m)1+o(1) 字操作时间内找出任意简单无向图的精确最大基数匹配，成功概率至少为 2/32/32/3。

这一时间界在每一条计算路径上都成立。

同样的保证也适用于寻找具有预先指定的容许顶点度数的生成子图，或判定这样的子图不存在。

英文原文

Gives a randomized algorithm finding an exact maximum-cardinality matching in any simple undirected graph in (n+m)1+o(1)(n+m)^{1+o(1)}(n+m)1+o(1) word time, with success probability at least 2/32/32/3.

The time bound holds on every computation path.

The same guarantees apply to finding a spanning subgraph with prescribed admissible vertex degrees, or deciding that none exists.

论文

  1. 一般图最大基数匹配的近线性时间算法

    Almost-Linear-Time Maximum-Cardinality Matching in General Graphs

    摘要

    我们证明，具有 n 个顶点和 m 条边的简单无向图中的最大基数匹配，可以由同一个一致的随机算法在 (n+m)1+o(1)(n+m)^{1+o(1)}(n+m)1+o(1) 时间内找到。

    该界在字长为对数级的模型中的每条计算路径上都成立，且算法以至少 2/3 的概率返回一个显式最大匹配。

    一个显式归约对下列任务给出相同的时间和概率保证：判断简单母图是否有一个顶点度数为指定合法值的生成子图，以及在其存在时找到一个这样的子图。

    英文原文

    We prove that maximum-cardinality matching in a simple undirected graph with n vertices and m edges can be found by one uniform randomized algorithm in (n+m)1+o(1)(n+m)^{1+o(1)}(n+m)1+o(1) time.

    The bound holds on every computation path in a logarithmic-word model, and the algorithm returns an explicit maximum matching with probability at least 2/3.

    An explicit reduction gives the same time and probability guarantees for deciding whether a simple host graph has a spanning subgraph with prescribed valid vertex degrees, and for finding one when it exists.
