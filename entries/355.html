355 · 微分几何

曲面首次奇性处的切流唯一性

Unique tangent flows at the first surface singularity

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

对 R3\mathbb R^3R3 中光滑紧连通、无边界的嵌入曲面，证明了首次奇异时刻的切流唯一性。

在每个奇点处，所有固定中心的向后切流，在原有环境坐标中、每个负时间处作为面积测度都相同，无须平均曲率凸性，也无须预先指定切模型。

英文原文

Proves the first-singular-time case of tangent-flow uniqueness for smooth compact connected embedded surfaces without boundary in R3\mathbb R^3R3.

At every singular point, all fixed-center backward tangent flows agree as area measures at every negative time in the original ambient coordinates, without mean-convexity or a prescribed tangent model.

论文

  1. 嵌入曲面平均曲率流首次奇异时刻切流的唯一性

    Uniqueness of tangent flows at the first singular time of embedded surface mean-curvature flow

    摘要

    我们证明，对于 ℝ3 中一个光滑紧连通无边界嵌入曲面的平均曲率流，在其首次奇异时刻的每个奇异点处，所有固定中心的重标度都在紧的负时间区间上局部光滑收敛到同一个重数为一的位似自收缩流。

    极限在原始环境坐标中是唯一的，包括其位置和各轴。

    无需平均曲率凸性假设，也无需预先指定切流模型。

    英文原文

    We prove that, at each singular point of the first singular time of the mean-curvature flow of a smooth compact connected embedded surface without boundary in ℝ3, all fixed-center rescalings converge locally smoothly on compact negative-time intervals to one multiplicity-one homothetic self-shrinker flow.

    The limit is unique in the original ambient coordinates, including its position and axes.

    No mean-convexity assumption or prescribed tangent model is required.
