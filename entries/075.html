L ln L 尺度的 Fourier 收敛

The Llog⁡LL\log LLlogL Fourier-convergence conjecture

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

证明了 Llog⁡L(T)L\log L(\mathbb T)LlogL(T) 中每个复值函数的普通对称 Fourier 部分和沿整个序列几乎处处收敛。

这解决了 Llog⁡LL\log LLlogL 尺度上的经典充分性猜想。

英文原文

Proves that the ordinary symmetric Fourier partial sums of every complex-valued function in Llog⁡L(T)L\log L(\mathbb T)LlogL(T) converge almost everywhere along the full sequence.

This resolves the classical sufficiency conjecture at the Llog⁡LL\log LLlogL scale.

  1. L log L 中几乎处处的 Fourier 收敛

    Almost-everywhere Fourier convergence in L log L

    摘要

    我们证明，圆周上 Llog⁡LL\log LLlogL 中每个复值函数的通常对称 Fourier 部分和，沿完整序列几乎处处收敛到该函数。

    这确立了经典的 Llog⁡LL\log LLlogL 充分性猜想。

    英文原文

    We prove that the ordinary symmetric Fourier partial sums of every complex-valued function in Llog⁡LL\log LLlogL on the circle converge almost everywhere to the function along the full sequence.

    This establishes the classical Llog⁡LL\log LLlogL sufficiency conjecture.
