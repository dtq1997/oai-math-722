超平坦实 Littlewood 多项式

Ultraflat real Littlewood polynomials

OpenAI · 2026-10-063 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

对每个充分大的整数长度 NNN，构造了具有 NNN 个取值于 {−1,1}\{-1,1\}{−1,1} 的连续系数的多项式，其模在整个单位圆上一致地为 (1+o(1))N(1+o(1))\sqrt N(1+o(1))N​。

因此，实 Littlewood 多项式是超平坦的，包括在实端点处。

它们的二元 merit factor 趋于无穷，从而否定了 Turyn 的 merit factor 有界性猜想。

英文原文

Constructs polynomials with NNN consecutive coefficients in {−1,1}\{-1,1\}{−1,1} whose modulus is (1+o(1))N(1+o(1))\sqrt N(1+o(1))N​ uniformly on the entire unit circle, for every sufficiently large integer length NNN.

Thus real Littlewood polynomials are ultraflat, including at the real endpoints.

Their binary merit factors tend to infinity, disproving Turyn's bounded-merit-factor conjecture.

  1. 超平坦实 Littlewood 多项式

    Ultraflat real Littlewood polynomials

    摘要

    对于每个 ε∈(0,1)\varepsilon\in(0,1)ε∈(0,1) 和每个充分大的整数 N，都存在长度为 N、系数属于 {−1,1}\{-1,1\}{−1,1} 的多项式，其模在单位圆周上处处介于 (1−ε)N(1-\varepsilon)\sqrt N(1−ε)N​ 和 (1+ε)N(1+\varepsilon)\sqrt N(1+ε)N​ 之间。

    因此，实 Littlewood 多项式可以在每个充分大的整数长度上达到超平坦。

    可以对每个长度分别选取符号。

    英文原文

    For every ε∈(0,1)\varepsilon\in(0,1)ε∈(0,1) and every sufficiently large integer N, there is a polynomial of length N with coefficients in {−1,1}\{-1,1\}{−1,1} whose modulus lies between (1−ε)N(1-\varepsilon)\sqrt N(1−ε)N​ and (1+ε)N(1+\varepsilon)\sqrt N(1+ε)N​ everywhere on the unit circle.

    Thus real Littlewood polynomials can be ultraflat through every sufficiently large integer length.

    The signs may be chosen separately at each length.

  3. 实 Littlewood 多项式最大模的渐近最小值

    Asymptotically minimal maxima of real Littlewood polynomials

    主结果已列入形式化清单
    摘要

    我们证明，当 N 遍历所有整数趋于无穷时，连续幂次的 N 个实系数均取值于 {−1,1}\{-1,1\}{−1,1} 的多项式，在单位圆周上的最大模的最小可能值为 (1+o(1))N(1+o(1))\sqrt N(1+o(1))N​。

    这否定了 Erdős 的固定相对间隙猜想的实符号版本。

    由此，当长度遍历所有整数趋于无穷时，长度为 N 的最大二元 merit factor 也趋于无穷，从而否定了 Turyn 猜想。

    英文原文

    We prove that the minimum possible maximum modulus on the unit circle of a polynomial with N consecutive real coefficients in {−1,1}\{-1,1\}{−1,1} is (1+o(1))N(1+o(1))\sqrt N(1+o(1))N​, as N tends to infinity through all integers.

    This disproves the real-sign analogue of Erdős's fixed relative-gap conjecture.

    As a consequence, the largest binary merit factor at length N tends to infinity through all integer lengths, disproving Turyn's conjecture.

# Real ultraflat Littlewood polynomials and unbounded binary merit factors

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Asymptotically minimal maxima of real Littlewood polynomials](../../preprints/Asymptotically-minimal-maxima-of-real-Littlewood-polynomials-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The formalization gives real Littlewood polynomials of every sufficiently large length whose maximum modulus on the unit circle is at most $(1+\eta)\sqrt N$ for any fixed $\eta>0$. Thus the smallest possible maximum is asymptotically minimal through all integer lengths.

A further statement chooses one family of real sign polynomials for all lengths such that, for every fixed finite $p>0$, the $L^p$ mean of $\bigl||P_N(z)|/\sqrt N-1\bigr|$ on the unit circle tends to zero. The results are existential and do not give an effective convergence rate or a signing algorithm.
