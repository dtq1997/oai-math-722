ℓ1\ell_1ℓ1​ 的度量 Markov 余型为二

Metric Markov cotype two for ℓ1\ell_1ℓ1​

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

证明了实 ℓ1\ell_1ℓ1​ 具有度量 Markov 余型二，从而回答了 Mendel 和 Naor 的问题。

因此，从实 Hilbert 空间的任意子集到 ℓ1\ell_1ℓ1​ 的每个 Lipschitz 映射都可以延拓到整个空间，其 Lipschitz 常数只损失一个普适的乘性因子，从而解决了以该空间为目标的 Ball 延拓问题。

英文原文

Proves that real ℓ1\ell_1ℓ1​ has metric Markov cotype two, answering Mendel and Naor's question.

Consequently, every Lipschitz map from an arbitrary subset of a real Hilbert space into ℓ1\ell_1ℓ1​ extends to the whole space with a universal multiplicative loss in its Lipschitz constant, resolving Ball's extension problem for this target.

论文

  1. ℓ1 的度量 Markov 余型为二

    Metric Markov Cotype Two of ℓ1

    摘要

    我们证明，实 Banach 空间 ℓ1 具有度量 Markov 余型二，从而回答了 Mendel 和 Naor 的一个问题。

    由此，从实 Hilbert 空间任意子集到 ℓ1 的每个 Lipschitz 映射都可以延拓到整个 Hilbert 空间，且其 Lipschitz 常数只损失一个普适的乘法因子，从而解决了以此为目标空间的 Ball 延拓问题。

    英文原文

    We prove that the real Banach space ℓ1 has metric Markov cotype two, answering a question of Mendel and Naor.

    As a consequence, every Lipschitz map from an arbitrary subset of a real Hilbert space into ℓ1 extends to the whole Hilbert space with a universal multiplicative loss in its Lipschitz constant, resolving Ball's extension problem for this target.
