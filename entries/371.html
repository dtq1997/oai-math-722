散焦 Schrödinger 方程的稳定爆破

Stable blowup for a defocusing Schrödinger equation

首发 · 2026-10-061 篇论文全部形式化

对某个充分大的奇数非线性幂次，构造了 Hk(T12)H^k(\mathbb T^{12})Hk(T12) 中一个非空的初始数据开集，其中 k>8k>8k>8，这些初始数据对应的标量散焦非线性 Schrödinger 方程的解在有限时间内爆破。

因此，在这一超临界情形下，有限时间爆破在 Sobolev 扰动下是稳定的。

英文原文

For a sufficiently large odd nonlinearity power, constructs a nonempty open set of initial data in Hk(T12)H^k(\mathbb T^{12})Hk(T12), with k>8k>8k>8, whose solutions of the scalar defocusing nonlinear Schrödinger equation blow up in finite time.

Thus finite-time blowup is stable under Sobolev perturbations in this supercritical regime.

论文

  1. 环面上超临界散焦 Schrödinger 方程的稳定自相似爆破

    Stable self-similar blowup for a supercritical defocusing Schrödinger equation on the torus

    摘要

    我们证明，十二维环面上的一个超临界散焦非线性 Schrödinger 方程存在稳定的自相似有限时间爆破。

    对于一个充分大的奇数幂指数，产生爆破的初值包含一个高阶 Sobolev 空间中的非空开集。

    由此，具有任意高 Sobolev 正则性的高斯 Fourier 初值可以以正概率发生有限时间爆破。

    英文原文

    We prove stable self-similar finite-time blowup for a supercritical defocusing nonlinear Schrödinger equation on the twelve-dimensional torus.

    For a sufficiently large odd power, the blowup initial data contain a nonempty open set in a high Sobolev space.

    As a consequence, Gaussian Fourier initial data of arbitrarily high Sobolev regularity can have positive probability of finite-time blowup.

# Stable blowup for the defocusing Schrödinger equation

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Stable self-similar blowup for a supercritical defocusing Schrödinger equation on the torus](../../preprints/Stable-Self-Similar-Blowup-for-a-Supercritical-Defocusing-Schrodinger-Equation-on-the-Torus-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The formalization constructs stable self-similar finite-time blowup for defocusing nonlinear Schrödinger equations on the twelve-dimensional torus. For every prescribed lower bound on the power, it selects an odd power at least that large and a Sobolev index $k>8$ for which a nonempty open set of $H^k$ initial data has the stated classical blowup behavior. The selected quantifier gives arbitrarily large odd powers, rather than every sufficiently large odd power.

For each such construction, Gaussian Fourier data with decay exponent $\alpha>k+6$ assign positive probability to the blowup set, and almost-sure global existence fails. The exponent $\alpha$ may be arbitrarily large.
