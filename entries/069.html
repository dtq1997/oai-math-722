069 · 代数几何与复几何

非有理水平的量子几何 Langlands

Quantum geometric Langlands at irrational level

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

对每个连通单复代数群、每条光滑射影连通复曲线以及每个平移后的水平 c∈C∖Qc\in\mathbb C\setminus\mathbb Qc∈C∖Q，证明了非分歧 de Rham 量子几何 Langlands 等价。

它将该群及其 Langlands 对偶的扭曲 DDD-模的整个导出范畴等同起来，并保留所有全局形式和连通分支。

英文原文

Proves the unramified de Rham quantum geometric Langlands equivalence for every connected simple complex algebraic group on every smooth projective connected complex curve, at every shifted level c∈C∖Qc\in\mathbb C\setminus\mathbb Qc∈C∖Q.

It identifies the full derived categories of twisted DDD-modules for the group and its Langlands dual, retaining all global forms and connected components.

    Global quantum geometric Langlands at irrational level

    摘要

    我们证明了每个连通复单代数群 G 和每个水平 c∈C∖Qc\in\mathbb C\setminus\mathbb Qc∈C∖Q 的非分歧量子几何 Langlands 等价，包括非实水平。

    对于每条光滑射影连通复曲线 X，它将 BunG(X)\mathop{\mathrm{Bun}}\nolimits _G(X)BunG​(X) 和 BunG∨(X)\mathop{\mathrm{Bun}}\nolimits _{G^\vee}(X)BunG∨​(X) 上分别处于对偶平移水平 c 和 −1/(rc)-1/(rc)−1/(rc) 的完整扭曲 D-模范畴对应起来，其中 r 是 lacing number。

    该等价采用给定的整体形式，并包含所有连通分支。

    英文原文

    We prove the unramified quantum geometric Langlands equivalence for every connected simple complex algebraic group G and every level c∈C∖Qc\in\mathbb C\setminus\mathbb Qc∈C∖Q, including non-real levels.

    For every smooth projective connected complex curve X, it identifies the full twisted D-module categories on BunG(X)\mathop{\mathrm{Bun}}\nolimits _G(X)BunG​(X) and BunG∨(X)\mathop{\mathrm{Bun}}\nolimits _{G^\vee}(X)BunG∨​(X) at the dual shifted levels c and −1/(rc)-1/(rc)−1/(rc), where r is the lacing number.

    The equivalence uses the given global forms and includes all connected components.
