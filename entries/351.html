标量曲率检测四维 Ricci 流奇性

Scalar curvature detects four-dimensional Ricci-flow singularities

首发 · 2026-10-063 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

证明了闭四维流形上的光滑 Ricci 流，只要标量曲率在趋近某个有限时刻时保持一致有界，就能延拓越过该时刻。

一个更高维的反例在其有限的最大存在时刻具有有界的标量曲率，但完整曲率无界，从而否定了不加限制的标量曲率延拓猜想。

英文原文

Proves that a smooth Ricci flow on a closed four-manifold extends past any finite time at which scalar curvature remains uniformly bounded.

A higher-dimensional counterexample has bounded scalar curvature but unbounded full curvature at its finite maximal time, disproving the unrestricted scalar-curvature extension conjecture.

  1. 闭流形上标量曲率有界而曲率在有限时间内爆破的 Ricci 流

    A closed Ricci flow with bounded scalar curvature and finite-time curvature blowup

    摘要

    我们否定了标量曲率延拓猜想在不加限制、涵盖所有维数时的形式。

    在充分高的维数中，我们在一个闭流形上构造了 Ricci 流，其标量曲率始终一致有界，而完整曲率在一个有限的最大存在时刻发散。

    在某个固定的充分高维数中，这些例子的曲率爆破具有双侧幂律界，且指数可以任意大。

    英文原文

    We disprove the scalar-curvature extension conjecture in its unrestricted all-dimensions form.

    In sufficiently high dimension, we construct a Ricci flow on a closed manifold whose scalar curvature remains uniformly bounded while full curvature diverges at a finite maximal time.

    In one fixed sufficiently high dimension, the examples have two-sided power-law curvature blowup with arbitrarily large exponents.

  2. 有界标量曲率与四维 Ricci 流的光滑延拓

    Bounded scalar curvature and smooth extension of four-dimensional Ricci flow

    摘要

    我们证明，闭实四维流形上的光滑 Ricci 流，可以在同一流形上延拓经过每一个标量曲率始终一致有界的有限时刻。

    这解决了四维的标量曲率延拓问题。

    英文原文

    We prove that a smooth Ricci flow on a closed real four-manifold extends on the same manifold through every finite time at which its scalar curvature remains uniformly bounded.

    This resolves the scalar-curvature extension problem in dimension four.

  3. 退化的 Ricci 平坦空间树上的路径选择与椭圆不等式

    Path selection and an elliptic inequality on degenerating Ricci-flat trees

    摘要

    我们对一个由四维 Ricci 平坦空间组成的固定有限树上的重整化 Einstein–Hilbert 泛函，证明了一个序列式椭圆不等式。

    当连接长度趋于无穷且尺度中性的加权 Ricci 误差 M 趋于零时，该泛函满足 E=o(M)E=o(M)E=o(M)。

    根端的衰减指数大于一，且每个参与连接的商群都非平凡。

    我们还证明了用于获得这一估计的渐近展开多变量路径选择定理。

    英文原文

    We prove a sequential elliptic inequality for the renormalized Einstein–Hilbert functional on a fixed finite tree of four-dimensional Ricci-flat spaces.

    As the joining lengths diverge and the scale-neutral weighted Ricci error M tends to zero, the functional satisfies E=o(M)E=o(M)E=o(M).

    The root end has decay exponent greater than one, and every joined quotient group is nontrivial.

    We also prove the multivariable path-selection theorem for asymptotic expansions used to obtain this estimate.
