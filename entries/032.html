032 · 代数几何与复几何

CM 阿贝尔簇及 K3 乘积的有理 Hodge 猜想

The rational Hodge conjecture for CM abelian varieties and products of K3 surfaces

OpenAI · 2026-10-068 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

在每个维数和余维数下，证明了所有复 CM 阿贝尔簇的有理 Hodge 猜想。

由 Milne 的定理，这还给出有限域上所有阿贝尔簇的 Tate 猜想，以及任意特征下阿贝尔簇的 Hodge 标准猜想。

配套结果证明了射影复 K3 曲面的任意乘积的有理 Hodge 猜想，以及每个此类曲面的 Kuga–Satake 对应的代数性。

英文原文

Proves the rational Hodge conjecture for every complex CM abelian variety, in every dimension and codimension.

Through Milne's theorems, this also gives the Tate conjecture for all abelian varieties over finite fields and the Hodge standard conjecture for abelian varieties in every characteristic.

Companion results prove rational Hodge for arbitrary products of projective complex K3 surfaces and algebraicity of the Kuga–Satake correspondence for every such surface.

论文

  1. K3 曲面乘积的有理 Hodge 猜想

    The rational Hodge conjecture for products of K3 surfaces

    摘要

    我们证明了任意有限个射影复 K3 曲面的乘积的有理 Hodge 猜想：每个有理 Hodge 类都是代数的。

    各因子可以互不相同，也可以重复，且对它们的 Picard 数、周期或自同态域没有限制。

    英文原文

    We prove the rational Hodge conjecture for every finite product of projective complex K3 surfaces: every rational Hodge class is algebraic.

    The factors may be distinct or repeated, with no restrictions on their Picard numbers, periods, or endomorphism fields.

  2. K3 曲面的 Kuga–Satake 对应的代数性

    Algebraicity of Kuga–Satake Correspondences for K3 Surfaces

    摘要

    我们证明，每个光滑射影复 K3 曲面的 Kuga–Satake 对应都是代数的。

    更准确地说，其超越上同调到其 Kuga–Satake 阿贝尔簇的上同调中的指定嵌入，在固定的归一化及完整偶 Clifford 目标下，由一个有理系数代数闭链诱导。

    该结果也适用于同源的 Kuga–Satake 模型及经传递得到的嵌入。

    英文原文

    We prove that the Kuga–Satake correspondence is algebraic for every smooth projective complex K3 surface.

    More precisely, the prescribed embedding of its transcendental cohomology into the cohomology of its Kuga–Satake abelian variety is induced by a rational algebraic cycle, with the fixed normalization and full even-Clifford target.

    The result also holds for isogenous Kuga–Satake models with the transported embedding.

  3. CM 阿贝尔簇的有理 Hodge 猜想

    The rational Hodge conjecture for CM abelian varieties

    摘要

    我们证明了具有复乘的复阿贝尔簇的有理 Hodge 猜想：这种簇上的每个有理 Hodge 类都是代数闭链类的有理线性组合。

    由此，我们得到了 CM 阿贝尔簇的广义 Hodge 猜想、有限域上阿贝尔簇的 Tate 猜想，以及任意特征下阿贝尔簇的 Hodge 标准猜想。

    英文原文

    We prove the rational Hodge conjecture for complex abelian varieties with complex multiplication: every rational Hodge class on such a variety is a rational linear combination of algebraic cycle classes.

    As consequences, we obtain the generalized Hodge conjecture for CM abelian varieties, the Tate conjecture for abelian varieties over finite fields, and the Hodge standard conjecture for abelian varieties in arbitrary characteristic.

  4. K3 二次型条件下的代数 Kuga–Satake 对应与 Hodge 猜想

    Algebraic Kuga–Satake correspondences and Hodge conjectures on a K3 quadratic locus

    摘要

    对于超越二次空间连同其杯积形式能够有理等距嵌入 UQ⊕2⊥⟨−1⟩4\mathbb U_{\mathbb Q}^{\oplus2}\perp\langle-1\rangle^4UQ⊕2​⊥⟨−1⟩4 的射影复 K3 曲面，我们证明了其每个自身幂在每个上同调次数上的有理 Hodge 猜想及广义 Hodge 猜想。

    这包括 P=U⊕D8(−1)⊕D4(−1)P=\mathbb U\oplus D_8(-1)\oplus D_4(-1)P=U⊕D8​(−1)⊕D4​(−1) 时每个丰沛 P 极化的 K3 曲面，并涵盖所有 Picard 数跳跃。

    在这一点集上，我们构造了代数对应，诱导每个指定的标准偶 Clifford Kuga–Satake 张量。

    更一般地，对于任意射影复 K3 曲面，一个精确的标准偶 Clifford Kuga–Satake 张量的代数性，就能推出其每个自身幂上的这两个猜想。

    英文原文

    We prove the rational Hodge and generalized Hodge conjectures in every cohomological degree of every self-power of a projective complex K3 surface whose transcendental quadratic space, with its cup-product form, admits a rational isometric embedding in UQ⊕2⊥⟨−1⟩4\mathbb U_{\mathbb Q}^{\oplus2}\perp\langle-1\rangle^4UQ⊕2​⊥⟨−1⟩4.

    This includes every ample P-polarized K3 surface, including all Picard jumps, for P=U⊕D8(−1)⊕D4(−1)P=\mathbb U\oplus D_8(-1)\oplus D_4(-1)P=U⊕D8​(−1)⊕D4​(−1).

    On this locus we construct algebraic correspondences inducing every prescribed standard even-Clifford Kuga–Satake tensor.

    More generally, for any projective complex K3 surface, algebraicity of one exact standard even-Clifford Kuga–Satake tensor implies both conjectures for every self-power.

  5. 阿贝尔簇幂上的 Weil 类与 Hodge 类

    Weil classes and Hodge classes on abelian powers

    摘要

    对于带有虚二次域作用及相容极化、且其有理同调 Hermitian 形式为符号为 (3,3)(3,3)(3,3) 的双曲形式的复六维阿贝尔簇，我们证明了其每个自身幂在每个余维上的有理 Hodge 猜想。

    对于每个允许虚二次域作用、维数至多为五的复阿贝尔簇，我们也证明了其每个自身幂在每个余维上的有理 Hodge 猜想。

    这两个结果都涵盖非单簇以及具有额外自同态的特殊周期。

    该证明使用了配套的关于 CM 阿贝尔簇有理 Hodge 猜想的定理。

    英文原文

    We prove the rational Hodge conjecture in every codimension on every self-power of a complex abelian sixfold with an imaginary-quadratic action and a compatible polarization whose rational homological Hermitian form is hyperbolic of signature (3,3)(3,3)(3,3).

    We also prove it in every codimension on every self-power of a complex abelian variety of dimension at most five admitting an imaginary-quadratic action.

    Both results include nonsimple varieties and special periods with additional endomorphisms.

    The proof uses the companion theorem on the rational Hodge conjecture for CM abelian varieties.

  6. 阿贝尔覆盖、Gale 对应与幂上的 Hodge 猜想

    Abelian covers, Gale correspondences, and the Hodge conjecture for powers

    摘要

    对于曲线的一个连通阿贝尔覆盖的完整带标记变分中的每个张量 Hodge-generic 点，我们证明了其 Jacobian 的每个自身幂上的有理 Hodge 猜想。

    这对基曲线的每个亏格及每种相容的分歧模式都成立。

    对于任意 CM 阿贝尔簇，在相同的 Hodge-generic 点集上，结论也适用于它与该 Jacobian 的乘积的每个自身幂。

    对于至多由两个相同次数的方程截出的对角完全交的完整光滑带标号族，我们还证明了其甚一般成员的每个自身幂上的该猜想，这对每个维数及每个至少为二的次数都成立。

    英文原文

    We prove the rational Hodge conjecture on every self-power of the Jacobian at each tensor Hodge-generic point of the full marked variation of a connected abelian cover of curves.

    This holds in every base genus and for every compatible branching pattern.

    For any CM abelian variety, the conclusion also holds for every self-power of its product with the Jacobian, on the same Hodge-generic locus.

    We also prove the conjecture on every self-power of a very general member of the full smooth labelled family of diagonal complete intersections cut out by at most two equations of a common degree, in every dimension and every degree at least two.

  7. 分裂八维阿贝尔簇上 Weil 类的代数性

    Algebraicity of Weil classes on split abelian eightfolds

    摘要

    我们证明，Weil 型的分裂八维阿贝尔簇上的每个有理 Weil 类都是代数的。

    该结果对每个虚二次域、每种相容极化类型，以及该分裂族的每个成员都成立，包括具有额外自同态的成员。

    因此，余维为四的完整二维有理 Weil 空间由代数闭链类生成。

    英文原文

    We prove that every rational Weil class on a split abelian eightfold of Weil type is algebraic.

    The result holds for every imaginary quadratic field, every compatible polarization type, and every member of the split family, including those with additional endomorphisms.

    Thus the full two-dimensional rational Weil space in codimension four is generated by algebraic cycle classes.

  8. 代数 Kuga–Satake 对应的条件性归约

    A Conditional Reduction for Algebraic Kuga–Satake Correspondences

    摘要

    对于本原上同调的 Hodge 群为完整正交群的极化 K3 曲面，只要一个代数对应诱导了从该上同调到某个阿贝尔簇第二上同调的非零映射，就足以恢复指定的完整 Kuga–Satake 对应。

    我们利用一个泛有限的曲面覆盖上的全纯一形式，给出了一个等价条件。

    若这一输入在某个极化分支上甚一般地成立，则对于超越部分上标准数据的每种选择，指定的对应在该分支处处都是代数的。

    初始对应的存在性仍是一项假设。

    英文原文

    For a polarized K3 surface whose primitive cohomology has full orthogonal Hodge group, one algebraic correspondence inducing a nonzero map from that cohomology to the second cohomology of an abelian variety suffices to recover the prescribed full Kuga–Satake correspondence.

    We give an equivalent condition using holomorphic one-forms on a generically finite surface cover.

    If this input holds very generally in a polarized component, the prescribed correspondence is algebraic throughout that component, for every choice of standard data on the transcendental part.

    The existence of the initial correspondence remains a hypothesis.
