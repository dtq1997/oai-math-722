自旋一 Haldane 谱隙

The spin-one Haldane gap

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

证明了偶数长度周期环上的纯反铁磁 Heisenberg 链的自旋一 Haldane 谱隙猜想：随着链长增大，谱隙始终具有一致的正下界。

配套论文证明了端点场为 h=3/5h=3/5h=3/5 的奇数长度开链存在谱隙，并给出了由边界选定、拓扑指标为 −1-1−1 的无限体积态。

英文原文

Proves the spin-one Haldane gap conjecture for the pure antiferromagnetic Heisenberg chain on even periodic rings: the spectral gap stays uniformly positive as the chain grows.

A companion establishes a gap for odd open chains with endpoint field h=3/5h=3/5h=3/5 and gives boundary-selected infinite-volume states with topological index −1-1−1.

论文

  1. 周期边界条件下的自旋一 Haldane 能隙

    The periodic spin-one Haldane gap

    摘要

    我们证明，偶数长度周期环上的纯反铁磁自旋一 Heisenberg 链具有一致的正谱隙，从而确立自旋一 Haldane 猜想的正能隙偶数长度周期版本。

    英文原文

    We prove a uniform positive spectral gap for the pure antiferromagnetic spin-one Heisenberg chain on even periodic rings, establishing the positive even-periodic formulation of the spin-one Haldane conjecture.

  2. 边界磁场作用下自旋一 Heisenberg 链的能隙

    A boundary-field gap for the spin-one Heisenberg chain

    摘要

    我们证明，两端施加相同固定磁场的奇数长度开放自旋一反铁磁 Heisenberg 链具有一致谱隙。

    磁场 h=3/5h=3/5h=3/5 使每条具有 2L+12L+12L+1 个格点且 L ≥ 960 的链的谱隙都大于 log⁡(10)/392\log(10)/392log(10)/392。

    随后，Tasaki 的指标定理给出，这些由边界选定的基态的每个子序列局部极限的指标均为 −1。

    英文原文

    We prove a uniform spectral gap for odd open spin-one antiferromagnetic Heisenberg chains with the same fixed magnetic field at both endpoints.

    The field h=3/5h=3/5h=3/5 gives a gap greater than log⁡(10)/392\log(10)/392log(10)/392 on every chain of 2L+12L+12L+1 sites with L ≥ 960.

    Tasaki's index theorem then gives index −1 for every subsequential local limit of these boundary-selected ground states.
