二点 Chowla 与修正的二元 Elliott 猜想

Two-point Chowla and the corrected binary Elliott conjecture

证明了普通平均意义下的二点 Chowla 猜想：沿固定且不成比例的仿射形式，Liouville 相关和满足界 O(X/(log⁡X)c)O(X/(\log X)^c)O(X/(logX)c)，其中 c>0c>0c>0 为绝对常数。

    摘要

    我们证明了普通平均下的二点 Chowla 猜想。

    对于每对固定的不成比例的仿射形式，Liouville 相关和在每个截断位置都有一个对数幂量级的节省，且指数为绝对常数。

    对于模至多为一的复值乘法函数的普通平均，在至少一个原始因子满足一致 nonpretentiousness 条件的假设下，我们还证明了二元修正版 Elliott 猜想。

    这一定性结论在固定剩余类中以及对固定的不成比例的仿射形式均成立。

    英文原文

    We prove the ordinary two-point Chowla conjecture.

    For every fixed pair of nonproportional affine forms, the Liouville correlation has a power-of-logarithm saving at every cutoff, with an absolute exponent.

    We also prove the binary corrected Elliott conjecture for ordinary averages of complex multiplicative functions of modulus at most one, under uniform nonpretentiousness of at least one original factor.

    This qualitative conclusion holds in fixed residue classes and for fixed nonproportional affine forms.

# Ordinary two-point correlations and the corrected Elliott conjecture

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Ordinary two-point correlations of multiplicative functions](../../preprints/Ordinary-two-point-correlations-of-multiplicative-functions-September-24-2026/final.pdf)

## Scope

The formalization proves ordinary two-point cancellation for multiplicative functions. For the Liouville function on every fixed pair of nonproportional affine forms, the correlation sum up to $X$ is $O(X/(\log X)^c)$ for an absolute $c>0$, with the implied constant depending on the forms.

For two one-bounded multiplicative functions, if at least one is uniformly nonpretentious against all Dirichlet-character twists with frequency $|t|\le N$, then their shifted and nonproportional affine correlation sums divided by $N$ tend to zero. These are ordinary averages, with no logarithmic averaging.
