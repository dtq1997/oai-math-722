正曲率曲面的 Fourier 限制

Fourier restriction for positively curved surfaces

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

证明了三维空间中正曲率曲面的对角 Fourier 延拓猜想。

对每个紧致光滑正曲率曲面 Σ⊂R3\Sigma\subset\mathbb R^3Σ⊂R3，包括有边界的曲面，延拓算子对每个 p>3p>3p>3 都是从 Lp(Σ)L^p(\Sigma)Lp(Σ) 到 Lp(R3)L^p(\mathbb R^3)Lp(R3) 的有界算子。

英文原文

Proves the diagonal Fourier extension conjecture for positively curved surfaces in three dimensions.

For every compact smooth positively curved surface Σ⊂R3\Sigma\subset\mathbb R^3Σ⊂R3, including surfaces with boundary, the extension operator is bounded from Lp(Σ)L^p(\Sigma)Lp(Σ) to Lp(R3)L^p(\mathbb R^3)Lp(R3) for every p>3p>3p>3.

论文

  1. 椭圆容量的传播与球面上的 Fourier 限制

    Elliptic capacity propagation and Fourier restriction to the sphere

    摘要

    我们证明了三维空间中球面的有界数据 Fourier 限制猜想：对于每个 p > 3，Fourier 延拓算子都从 L∞(S2)L^\infty(S^2)L∞(S2) 有界映入 Lp(R3)L^p(\mathbb R^3)Lp(R3)。

    这涵盖了猜想的完整开范围，且阈值 p = 3 不能改进。

    英文原文

    We prove the bounded-data Fourier restriction conjecture for the sphere in three dimensions: the Fourier extension operator maps L∞(S2)L^\infty(S^2)L∞(S2) boundedly into Lp(R3)L^p(\mathbb R^3)Lp(R3) for every p > 3.

    This is the full conjectured open range, and the threshold p = 3 is sharp.

  2. 三维空间中正曲率曲面的对角 Fourier 延拓

    Diagonal Fourier extension for positively curved surfaces in three dimensions

    摘要

    我们证明了紧光滑正曲率曲面 Σ⊂R3\Sigma\subset\mathbb R^3Σ⊂R3 的对角 Fourier 延拓猜想，包括具有光滑边界的曲面。

    对于每个 3<p<∞3\lt p\lt \infty3<p<∞，延拓算子都从 Lp(Σ)L^p(\Sigma)Lp(Σ) 有界映入 Lp(R3)L^p(\mathbb R^3)Lp(R3)。

    紧抛物面情形还给出了两个空间维数中自由 Schrödinger 方程的局部光滑化，适用于每个 3<p<∞3\lt p\lt \infty3<p<∞ 和 Sobolev 阶数 s>2−6/ps\gt 2-6/ps>2−6/p。

    英文原文

    We prove the diagonal Fourier extension conjecture for compact smooth positively curved surfaces Σ⊂R3\Sigma\subset\mathbb R^3Σ⊂R3, including surfaces with smooth boundary.

    For every 3<p<∞3\lt p\lt \infty3<p<∞, the extension operator maps Lp(Σ)L^p(\Sigma)Lp(Σ) boundedly into Lp(R3)L^p(\mathbb R^3)Lp(R3).

    The compact paraboloid case also yields free Schrödinger local smoothing in two spatial dimensions for every 3<p<∞3\lt p\lt \infty3<p<∞ and Sobolev order s>2−6/ps\gt 2-6/ps>2−6/p.
