056 · 代数几何与复几何

四维极小模型程序的终止

Termination of fourfold minimal model programs

OpenAI · 2026-10-065 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

对于整体 Weil Q\mathbb QQ-factorial 的紧 Kähler 四维簇，在边界有理、有理且解析 nef 的 b-数据固定、翻转图为射影小双有理图且满足指定丰沛性符号条件时，证明每个存在的广义对数典范翻转序列都终止。

还证明了特征零、带有理边界的射影对数典范四维对的任意允许的极小模型程序均终止。

英文原文

Proves termination of every existing generalized log canonical flip sequence on globally Weil Q\mathbb QQ-factorial compact Kähler fourfolds, with rational boundary, fixed rational analytically nef b-data, and projective small flip diagrams with the prescribed ample signs.

Also proves termination of arbitrary permitted minimal model programs for projective log canonical fourfolds with rational boundary in characteristic zero.

论文

  1. 紧 Kähler 四维簇上广义对数典范翻转的终止性

    Termination of generalized log canonical flips on compact Kähler fourfolds

    摘要

    在正规不可约、整体 Weil ℚ-factorial 的紧 Kähler 四维簇上，每个广义对数典范翻转序列都会终止，只要这些翻转是具有所述相反丰沛符号的射影小态射图。

    边界是有理的，nef b-除子固定，并由某个射影修正上的解析 nef ℚ-Cartier 除子表示。

    无需缩放规则或伪有效性假设。

    该定理讨论已有的翻转序列；它并不声称所有收缩或翻转都存在。

    英文原文

    Every sequence of generalized log canonical flips on a normal irreducible globally Weil ℚ-factorial compact Kähler fourfold terminates, provided the flips are projective small diagrams with the stated opposite ample signs.

    The boundary is rational, and the nef b-divisor is fixed and represented by an analytically nef ℚ-Cartier divisor on a projective modification.

    No scaling rule or pseudo-effectivity assumption is required.

    The theorem concerns existing flip sequences; it does not assert the existence of all contractions or flips.

  2. 紧 Kähler 四维簇上广义典范翻转的终止性

    Termination of generalized-canonical flips on compact Kähler fourfolds

    摘要

    在具有有理边界以及固定的有理解析 nef b-除子的紧 Kähler 四维簇上，当小态射是射影态射时，每个广义典范翻转序列都会终止。

    我们对于边界系数小于一的正规、整体 Weil ℚ-factorial 模型证明这一结论，并允许例外除子的对数差异数等于一。

    无需缩放规则或伪有效性假设。

    该定理适用于具有指定相反丰沛符号的已有射影小态射图。

    英文原文

    Every sequence of generalized-canonical flips on compact Kähler fourfolds with rational boundary and fixed rational analytically nef b-divisor terminates when the small morphisms are projective.

    We prove this for normal globally Weil ℚ-factorial models with boundary coefficients less than one, allowing exceptional log discrepancies equal to one.

    No scaling rule or pseudo-effectivity assumption is required.

    The theorem applies to existing projective small diagrams with the specified opposite ample signs.

  3. 具有有理边界的射影对数典范四维簇的终止性

    Termination for projective log canonical fourfolds with rational boundary

    摘要

    我们证明，特征零代数闭域上具有有理边界的射影对数典范四维簇的每个允许的极小模型纲领都会终止。

    该结果允许在给定模型上选取任意负极端射线并混合进行双有理步骤，不要求伪有效性或初始模型的 ℚ-factorial 性。

    英文原文

    We prove that every permitted minimal model program for a projective log canonical fourfold with rational boundary over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero terminates.

    The result allows arbitrary negative extremal rays and mixed birational steps on the given models, without pseudo-effectivity or initial ℚ-factoriality.

  4. 紧 Kähler 四维簇上有限步的普通极小模型纲领

    Finite ordinary minimal model programs on compact Kähler fourfolds

    摘要

    我们证明，在整体 Weil 除子 ℚ-factorial 范畴中，从带有有效有理边界的紧 Kähler klt 四维对出发的每个极大通常负射线纲领都会终止。

    当伴随除子伪有效时，它终止于 nef 模型，否则终止于射影 Mori 纤维空间。

    英文原文

    We prove that every maximal ordinary negative-ray program starting from a compact Kähler klt fourfold pair with effective rational boundary in the global Weil-divisor ℚ-factorial category terminates.

    It ends at a nef model when the adjoint is pseudo-effective and at a projective Mori fibre space otherwise.

  5. 紧 Kähler 四维簇上有限步的普通极小模型纲领

    Finite ordinary minimal model programs on compact Kähler fourfolds

    摘要

    我们证明，带有有效有理边界和典范有理线丛、整体 Weil-ℚ-factorial 的紧 Kähler klt 四维对存在从该给定对出发的有限通常极小模型纲领。

    当伴随类伪有效时，它终止于 nef 模型，否则终止于 Mori 纤维空间。

    英文原文

    We prove that a globally Weil-ℚ-factorial compact Kähler klt fourfold pair with effective rational boundary and canonical rational line bundle admits a finite ordinary minimal model program starting on the given pair.

    It ends at a nef model when the adjoint class is pseudo-effective and at a Mori fibre space otherwise.
