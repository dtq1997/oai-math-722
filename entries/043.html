固定行列式模空间的 P=W

P=WP=WP=W for fixed-determinant moduli spaces

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

对于亏格至少为二的光滑复射影曲线上的光滑、互素、固定行列式、迹为零的 Higgs 模空间及其特征标簇，证明在合数秩情形下，其全部有理上同调满足 Pk=W2k=W2k+1P_k=W_{2k}=W_{2k+1}Pk​=W2k​=W2k+1​。

这包括变异上同调；结合已知的素数秩定理，便在每个互素秩下建立固定行列式的 P=WP=WP=W 猜想。

英文原文

Proves Pk=W2k=W2k+1P_k=W_{2k}=W_{2k+1}Pk​=W2k​=W2k+1​ on the full rational cohomology of smooth coprime fixed-determinant, trace-free Higgs moduli spaces and their character varieties for composite ranks over smooth projective complex curves of genus at least two.

This includes variant cohomology and, together with the known prime-rank theorems, establishes the fixed-determinant P=WP=WP=W conjecture in every coprime rank.

论文

  1. 固定行列式、合数秩情形中的 P=W

    P=W in composite rank for fixed determinant

    摘要

    对于亏格至少为二的光滑复射影曲线，我们证明了秩为合数、次数与秩互素的固定行列式无迹 Higgs 模空间的全部有理上同调上的 P = W 猜想。

    结合已确立的素数秩情形，这给出了所有秩与次数互素情形下的这一等式。

    英文原文

    We prove the P = W conjecture on the full rational cohomology of fixed-determinant, trace-free Higgs moduli spaces in composite rank and coprime degree, for smooth projective complex curves of genus at least two.

    Together with the established prime-rank cases, this gives the equality in every coprime rank.
