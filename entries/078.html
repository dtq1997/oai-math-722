三维 Bochner–Riesz 猜想

The three-dimensional Bochner–Riesz conjecture

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

在严格范围内解决了三维 Bochner–Riesz 猜想：只要 δ>max⁡{3∣1/p−1/2∣−1/2,0}\delta>\max\{3|1/p-1/2|-1/2,0\}δ>max{3∣1/p−1/2∣−1/2,0}，δ\deltaδ 阶 Bochner–Riesz 乘子对每个 1≤p≤∞1\le p\le\infty1≤p≤∞ 都在 Lp(R3)L^p(\mathbb R^3)Lp(R3) 上有界。

英文原文

Resolves the three-dimensional Bochner–Riesz conjecture in its strict range: the Bochner–Riesz multipliers of order δ\deltaδ are bounded on Lp(R3)L^p(\mathbb R^3)Lp(R3) for every 1≤p≤∞1\le p\le\infty1≤p≤∞ whenever δ>max⁡{3∣1/p−1/2∣−1/2,0}\delta>\max\{3|1/p-1/2|-1/2,0\}δ>max{3∣1/p−1/2∣−1/2,0}.

论文

  1. 三维 Bochner–Riesz 乘子

    Bochner–Riesz multipliers in three dimensions

    摘要

    我们证明了三维 Bochner–Riesz 猜想的严格阶数形式。

    主要结果是每个正阶数的 Bochner–Riesz 乘子在 L3(R3)L^3(\mathbb R^3)L3(R3) 上的有界性。

    随后由插值和对偶性得到猜想的完整严格范围。

    英文原文

    We prove the three-dimensional Bochner–Riesz conjecture in its strict-order formulation.

    The main result is boundedness of the Bochner–Riesz multipliers on L3(R3)L^3(\mathbb R^3)L3(R3) for every positive order.

    Interpolation and duality then give the full conjectured strict range.
