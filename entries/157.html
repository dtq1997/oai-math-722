Hadwiger 与 Colin de Verdière 猜想反例

Counterexamples to the Hadwiger and Colin de Verdière conjectures

OpenAI · 2026-10-063 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

即使对于分数染色也否定了 Hadwiger 猜想：存在任意大的有限简单图，其独立数至多为二且满足 χf(G)>h(G)\chi_f(G)>h(G)χf​(G)>h(G)，其中 h(G)h(G)h(G) 是完全图子式的最大阶数。

还否定了分数 Colin de Verdière 色数界 χf(G)≤μ(G)+1\chi_f(G)\le\mu(G)+1χf​(G)≤μ(G)+1。

在正面结果方面，对于某个普适常数 CCC，每个有限非空图都满足 χlist(G)≤Ch(G)\chi_{\mathrm{list}}(G)\le C h(G)χlist​(G)≤Ch(G)。

英文原文

Disproves Hadwiger's conjecture even for fractional coloring: arbitrarily large finite simple graphs with independence number at most two satisfy χf(G)>h(G)\chi_f(G)>h(G)χf​(G)>h(G), where h(G)h(G)h(G) is the largest clique-minor order.

Also disproves the fractional Colin de Verdière chromatic bound χf(G)≤μ(G)+1\chi_f(G)\le\mu(G)+1χf​(G)≤μ(G)+1.

In the positive direction, every finite nonempty graph satisfies χlist(G)≤Ch(G)\chi_{\mathrm{list}}(G)\le C h(G)χlist​(G)≤Ch(G) for a universal constant CCC.

  1. Hadwiger 猜想的一个反例

    A counterexample to Hadwiger's conjecture

    摘要

    我们通过构造色数超过 Hadwiger 数的任意大的图，否定了 Hadwiger 猜想。

    这些例子的独立数至多为二，甚至其普通分数色数也超过其 Hadwiger 数。

    因此，它们也否定了 Reed 和 Seymour 所讨论的分数染色弱化版本。

    英文原文

    We disprove Hadwiger's conjecture by constructing arbitrarily large graphs whose chromatic number exceeds their Hadwiger number.

    The examples have independence number at most two, and even their ordinary fractional chromatic number exceeds their Hadwiger number.

    Thus they also disprove the fractional-coloring weakening discussed by Reed and Seymour.

    摘要

    我们通过构造色数比 Colin de Verdière 不变量大超过一的图，否定了 Colin de Verdière 色数猜想。

    英文原文

    We disprove the Colin de Verdière chromatic conjecture by constructing graphs whose chromatic number exceeds their Colin de Verdière invariant by more than one.

    The examples have independence number at most two.

    In fact, their ordinary fractional chromatic number also exceeds their Colin de Verdière invariant by more than one.

    A linear list-coloring bound in terms of the Hadwiger number

    摘要

    我们证明，每个有限非空图 G 都满足 χlist(G)≤Ch(G)\chi_{\mathrm{list}}(G)\le C h(G)χlist​(G)≤Ch(G)，其中 C 为一个绝对整数常数，h(G)h(G)h(G) 为完全图子式的最大阶数。

    这肯定地解决了线性列表 Hadwiger 猜想。

    英文原文

    We prove that every finite nonempty graph G satisfies χlist(G)≤Ch(G)\chi_{\mathrm{list}}(G)\le C h(G)χlist​(G)≤Ch(G) for an absolute integer C, where h(G)h(G)h(G) is the largest order of a clique minor.

    This resolves the Linear List Hadwiger conjecture affirmatively.

# Graph coloring, clique minors, and Colin de Verdière invariants

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [A linear list-coloring bound in terms of the Hadwiger number](../../preprints/A-linear-list-coloring-bound-in-terms-of-the-Hadwiger-number-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The Linear List Hadwiger conjecture asks for a universal linear bound on list chromatic number in terms of clique-minor size. The formalization proves that there is one integer $C\ge1$ such that every finite nonempty simple graph $G$ satisfies $\chi_{\mathrm{list}}(G)\le C h(G)$, where $h(G)$ is the largest order of a clique minor. The constant is independent of the graph.
