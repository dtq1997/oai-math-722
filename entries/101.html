101 · 凸几何与度量几何

迷向常数的尖锐单纯形猜想

The sharp simplex conjecture for isotropic constants

首发 · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

证明了在任意维数的凸体中，单纯形是迷向常数的唯一极大者，从而解决了强迷向常数猜想。

还确立了对数凹概率密度的最优熵下界，且恰在单侧指数分布之乘积的可逆仿射像处取等号。

英文原文

Proves that simplices uniquely maximize the isotropic constant among convex bodies in every dimension, resolving the strong isotropic constant conjecture.

Also establishes the sharp entropy lower bound for log-concave probability densities, with equality precisely for invertible affine images of products of one-sided exponential laws.

    我们证明强迷向常数猜想：在每一维中，单纯形是所有凸体中迷向常数的唯一极大者。

    我们还对 ℝm 上的每个对数凹概率密度证明最优熵界 h(f)≥m+12log⁡det⁡Cov(f)h(f)\ge m+\tfrac12\log\det\mathop{\mathrm{Cov}}\nolimits (f)h(f)≥m+21​logdetCov(f)，且等号恰在单侧指数分布的乘积的可逆仿射像处取得。

    英文原文

    We prove the strong isotropic constant conjecture: in each dimension, simplices are the unique maximizers of the isotropic constant among convex bodies.

    We also prove the sharp entropy bound h(f)≥m+12log⁡det⁡Cov(f)h(f)\ge m+\tfrac12\log\det\mathop{\mathrm{Cov}}\nolimits (f)h(f)≥m+21​logdetCov(f) for every log-concave probability density on ℝm, with equality precisely for invertible affine images of products of one-sided exponential laws.
