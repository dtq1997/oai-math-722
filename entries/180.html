Barnette Hamilton 圈猜想

Barnette's Hamiltonian-cycle conjecture

首发 · 2026-10-061 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

证明了每个有限简单三正则二部平面 333-顶点连通图都有 Hamilton 圈，解决了 Barnette 猜想。

等价地，每个有限简单三正则 333-顶点连通二部 Pfaffian 图中的每条三边路径都包含在某个 Hamilton 圈中。

英文原文

Proves that every finite simple cubic bipartite planar 333-vertex-connected graph has a Hamiltonian cycle, resolving Barnette's conjecture.

Equivalently, every three-edge path in a finite simple cubic 333-vertex-connected bipartite Pfaffian graph lies in a Hamiltonian cycle.

# Barnette’s Hamiltonian-cycle conjecture

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Paired states and Hamiltonian cycles in cubic bipartite planar graphs](../../preprints/Paired-states-and-Hamiltonian-cycles-in-cubic-bipartite-planar-graphs-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

Barnette's conjecture states that every finite simple cubic bipartite planar graph that is 3-vertex-connected has a Hamiltonian cycle. The formalization proves this statement for every such graph. The selected result is the existence of a cycle visiting every vertex exactly once.
