阿贝尔簇及 Siegel 三维簇中的 Zilber–Pink

Zilber–Pink in abelian varieties and the Siegel threefold

OpenAI · 2026-10-064 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

证明了 Q‾\overline{\mathbb Q}Q​ 上的阿贝尔 Zilber–Pink 猜想：相对于包含给定不可约子簇的最小挠陪集，该子簇仅有有限多个极大异常子簇。

还证明了 Siegel 三维簇 A2\mathcal A_2A2​ 中定义在 Q‾\overline{\mathbb Q}Q​ 上的 Hodge 一般曲线所满足的完整曲线情形，不附加边界或约化假设。

英文原文

Proves the abelian Zilber–Pink conjecture over Q‾\overline{\mathbb Q}Q​: every irreducible subvariety has finitely many maximal atypical subvarieties relative to its smallest containing torsion coset.

It also proves the full curve case in the Siegel threefold A2\mathcal A_2A2​ for Hodge-generic curves defined over Q‾\overline{\mathbb Q}Q​, without boundary or reduction assumptions.

  1. 阿贝尔 Zilber–Pink 猜想

    The abelian Zilber–Pink conjecture

    摘要

    我们证明了 Q‾\overline{\mathbb Q}Q​ 上的阿贝尔 Zilber–Pink 猜想。

    阿贝尔簇的每个不可约子簇都只有有限多个极大非典型子簇，其中非典型性是在包含原不可约子簇的最小挠陪集中衡量的。

    英文原文

    We prove the abelian Zilber–Pink conjecture over Q‾\overline{\mathbb Q}Q​.

    Every irreducible subvariety of an abelian variety has only finitely many maximal atypical subvarieties, where atypicality is measured inside its smallest containing torsion coset.

  2. A2 中曲线的 Zilber–Pink 猜想的 E×CM 部分

    The E×CM component of Zilber–Pink for curves in A2

    摘要

    我们对 Q‾\overline{\mathbb Q}Q​ 上 A2\mathcal A_2A2​ 中的 Hodge-generic 代数曲线证明了 Zilber–Pink 猜想的 E×CME\times\mathrm{CM}E×CM 部分。

    每条这样的曲线都只包含有限多个点，使得其对应的阿贝尔曲面同源于两个椭圆曲线的乘积，且至少一个因子具有复乘。

    英文原文

    We prove the E×CME\times\mathrm{CM}E×CM component of Zilber–Pink for Hodge-generic algebraic curves in A2\mathcal A_2A2​ over Q‾\overline{\mathbb Q}Q​.

    Each such curve contains only finitely many points whose abelian surface is isogenous to a product of elliptic curves with at least one factor having complex multiplication.

  3. Siegel 三维簇上曲线中的四元数除代数型点

    Quaternionic division points on curves in the Siegel threefold

    摘要

    我们对 Q‾\overline{\mathbb Q}Q​ 上 A2\mathcal A_2A2​ 中的曲线证明了 Zilber–Pink 猜想的四元数除代数部分。

    一条 Hodge-generic 代数曲线只包含有限多个点，使得其完整的几何有理自同态代数是 ℚ 上的不定四元数除代数。

    无须任何边界或约化假设。

    英文原文

    We prove the quaternionic-division component of Zilber–Pink for curves in A2\mathcal A_2A2​ over Q‾\overline{\mathbb Q}Q​.

    A Hodge-generic algebraic curve contains only finitely many points whose full geometric rational endomorphism algebra is an indefinite quaternion division algebra over ℚ.

    No boundary or reduction hypothesis is required.

  4. 椭圆曲线的平方与 A2 中曲线的 Zilber–Pink 猜想

    Elliptic squares and Zilber–Pink for curves in A2

    摘要

    我们证明，Q‾\overline{\mathbb Q}Q​ 上 A2\mathcal A_2A2​ 中每条 Hodge-generic 代数曲线都只包含有限多个点，使得其对应的阿贝尔曲面同源于一条无复乘（CM）椭圆曲线的平方。

    将该结果与配套的 E×CME\times\mathrm{CM}E×CM 及四元数除代数有限性定理相结合，我们证明了 Q‾\overline{\mathbb Q}Q​ 上 A2\mathcal A_2A2​ 中曲线情形的 Zilber–Pink 猜想，而无须边界假设。

    英文原文

    We prove that every Hodge-generic algebraic curve in A2\mathcal A_2A2​ over Q‾\overline{\mathbb Q}Q​ contains only finitely many points whose abelian surface is isogenous to the square of an elliptic curve without complex multiplication (CM).

    Combining this result with the companion E×CME\times\mathrm{CM}E×CM and quaternionic-division finiteness theorems, we prove the curve case of Zilber–Pink in A2\mathcal A_2A2​ over Q‾\overline{\mathbb Q}Q​, without a boundary hypothesis.
