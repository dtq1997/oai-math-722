The optimal randomized–quantum query exponent

  1. 随机查询复杂度与量子查询复杂度之间的近四次分离

    A Nearly Quartic Separation Between Randomized and Quantum Query Complexity

    摘要

    我们构造全定义布尔函数，使其有界错误随机查询复杂度与量子查询复杂度之间具有近四次的分离。

    将这两种复杂度记作 R(f)\mathrm R(f)R(f) 和 Q(f)\mathrm Q(f)Q(f)，这些例子排除了所有满足 α < 4 的普适界 R(f)=O((1+Q(f))α)\mathrm R(f)=O((1+\mathrm Q(f))^\alpha)R(f)=O((1+Q(f))α)。

    因此，已知四次上界具有最优指数，从而反驳了所猜想的三次界。

    两种复杂度均计算最坏情形下的比特查询次数，并要求每个输入上的错误概率至多为 1/3。

    英文原文

    We construct total Boolean functions with a nearly quartic separation between bounded-error randomized and quantum query complexity.

    Writing these complexities as R(f)\mathrm R(f)R(f) and Q(f)\mathrm Q(f)Q(f), the examples rule out every universal bound R(f)=O((1+Q(f))α)\mathrm R(f)=O((1+\mathrm Q(f))^\alpha)R(f)=O((1+Q(f))α) with α < 4.

    Thus the known quartic upper bound has the optimal exponent, disproving the conjectured cubic bound.

    Both complexities count worst-case bit queries with error at most 1/3 on every input.
