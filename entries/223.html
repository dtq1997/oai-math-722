  1. 1 <= q < 4 时方格上的 FK 界面与嵌套环

    Square-lattice FK interfaces and nested loops for 1 <= q < 4

    摘要

    对于每个固定的 1≤q<41\le q\lt 41≤q<4，临界方格随机簇 Dobrushin 界面作为有序曲线收敛于弦 SLEκ(q)\mathop{\mathrm{SLE}}\nolimits _{\kappa(q)}SLEκ(q)​，其中 κ(q)=4π/arccos⁡(−q/2)\kappa(q)=4\pi/\arccos(-\sqrt q/2)κ(q)=4π/arccos(−q​/2)，从而证实了这一参数范围内的 Rohde–Schramm 预测。

    这在每个有界 Jordan 区域中，在带标记边界的一致逼近下成立。

    完整的嵌套平面环族在保留重数和遍历方式的球面匹配拓扑中收敛于全平面 CLEκ(q)\mathop{\mathrm{CLE}}\nolimits _{\kappa(q)}CLEκ(q)​。

    当 q = 1 时，我们得到了具有自由边界边的方格键渗流的 Cardy 公式。

    英文原文

    For every fixed 1≤q<41\le q\lt 41≤q<4, critical square-lattice random-cluster Dobrushin interfaces converge as ordered curves to chordal SLEκ(q)\mathop{\mathrm{SLE}}\nolimits _{\kappa(q)}SLEκ(q)​, where κ(q)=4π/arccos⁡(−q/2)\kappa(q)=4\pi/\arccos(-\sqrt q/2)κ(q)=4π/arccos(−q​/2), confirming the Rohde–Schramm prediction in this parameter range.

    This holds in every bounded Jordan domain under uniform marked boundary approximation.

    The complete nested plane loop collections converge to whole-plane CLEκ(q)\mathop{\mathrm{CLE}}\nolimits _{\kappa(q)}CLEκ(q)​ in a spherical matching topology retaining multiplicities and traversals.

    At q = 1 we obtain Cardy's formula for square-lattice bond percolation with free boundary edges.

  2. 参数小于一时的自对偶随机簇界面

    Self-dual random-cluster interfaces below one

    摘要

    对于每个固定的 0<q<10\lt q\lt 10<q<1，我们证明了方格随机簇模型在其自对偶参数处的 Dobrushin 界面收敛于弦 SLEκ，其中 κ=4π/arccos⁡(−q/2)∈(6,8)\kappa=4\pi/\arccos(-\sqrt q/2)\in(6,8)κ=4π/arccos(−q​/2)∈(6,8)。

    逼近区域是带有不同标记顶点的简单闭合最近邻晶格多边形，其带标记边界参数化一致收敛于某个有界光滑 Jordan 区域的相应参数化。

    沿整个网格序列，收敛在模去递增重新参数化的有向曲线一致度量中成立。

    证明结合了参数小于一时的有限连接比较、不规则铺砌圆盘中的局部化，以及一个确定极限 Loewner 驱动函数的边界可观测量。

    英文原文

    For every fixed 0<q<10\lt q\lt 10<q<1, we prove that the Dobrushin interface of the square-lattice random-cluster model at its self-dual parameter converges to chordal SLEκ, where κ=4π/arccos⁡(−q/2)∈(6,8)\kappa=4\pi/\arccos(-\sqrt q/2)\in(6,8)κ=4π/arccos(−q​/2)∈(6,8).

    The approximating domains are simple closed nearest-neighbor lattice polygons with distinct marked vertices, whose marked boundary parametrizations converge uniformly to those of a bounded smooth Jordan domain.

    Convergence holds along the full mesh sequence in the uniform metric on oriented curves modulo increasing reparametrization.

    The proof combines finite connection comparisons below one, localization in irregular tiled disks, and a boundary observable that determines the limiting Loewner driver.

  3. 弱无序 FK–Ising 界面的淬火 SLE 普适性

    Quenched SLE Universality for Weakly Disordered FK–Ising Interfaces

    摘要

    我们证明了，具有充分弱的、对称的、独立二值键无序的临界 FK–Ising 界面收敛于弦 SLE16/3。

    网格尺度趋于零时，无序强度保持固定，且条件曲线分布的收敛对环境依概率成立。

    英文原文

    We prove that critical FK–Ising interfaces with sufficiently weak, symmetric, independent two-valued bond disorder converge to chordal SLE16/3.

    The disorder strength is fixed as the mesh tends to zero, and convergence of the conditional curve laws holds in probability over the environment.

  4. 热 FK–Ising 界面与有质量 SLE

    Thermal FK–Ising interfaces and massive SLE

    摘要

    对于任一符号的每个固定非零质量，我们证明了有界单连通区域中满足一致角度界条件（uniformly angle-bounded）的等半径晶格上的热 FK–Ising 界面的收敛。

    该极限与晶格及容许的区域逼近无关。

    对于正质量，它是唯一的有质量 SLE16/3_{16/3}16/3​ 分布，其局部有限能量漂移由一个有质量边值问题指定；负质量情形由对偶性和反向得到。

    该定理还允许直径发散的 Carathéodory 逼近。

    英文原文

    We prove convergence of thermal FK–Ising interfaces, for every fixed nonzero mass of either sign, on uniformly angle-bounded isoradial lattices in bounded simply connected domains.

    The limit is independent of the lattice and the admissible domain approximation.

    For positive mass it is the unique massive SLE16/3_{16/3}16/3​ law with locally finite-energy drift prescribed by a massive boundary value problem; negative mass follows by duality and reversal.

    The theorem also allows Carathéodory approximations whose diameters diverge.

  5. 临界方格 FK 界面的自然占据测度

    Natural Occupation Measures for Critical Square-Lattice FK Interfaces

    摘要

    对于每个固定簇权重 1≤q<41\le q\lt 41≤q<4，我们证明了单位正方形中临界方格 Fortuin–Kasteleyn Dobrushin 界面的重标度计数测度，收敛于其 Schramm–Loewner 极限的 Minkowski 含量测度。

    用同一个确定性常数乘以网格尺度的所预测幂次，便得到归一化因子。

    该收敛与有序曲线的收敛联合成立，并包括总质量，从而给出界面总步数的标度极限。

    英文原文

    We prove that the rescaled counting measure of a critical square-lattice Fortuin–Kasteleyn Dobrushin interface in the unit square converges to the Minkowski-content measure of its Schramm–Loewner limit, for every fixed cluster weight 1≤q<41\le q\lt 41≤q<4.

    A single deterministic constant times the predicted power of the mesh gives the normalization.

    Convergence is joint with the ordered curve and includes the total mass, giving the scaling limit of the interface's total number of steps.
