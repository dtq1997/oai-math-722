论文

  1. 六维中的 Campana–Peternell 猜想

    The Campana–Peternell conjecture in dimension six

    摘要

    我们证明，每个切丛为 nef 的光滑连通复射影 Fano 六维簇都是有理齐性的，从而解决了复维数为六时的 Campana–Peternell 猜想。

    由此，若连通紧 Kähler 流形 X 的全纯切丛为 nef 且 dim⁡CX−q~(X)≤6\dim_{\mathbb C}X-\widetilde q(X)\leq6dimC​X−q​(X)≤6，则其通常的万有覆盖为 F×Cq~(X)F\times\mathbb C^{\widetilde q(X)}F×Cq​(X)，其中 F 是有理齐性流形，q~(X)\widetilde q(X)q​(X) 是连通有限 étale 覆盖的最大不规则度。

    英文原文

    We prove that every smooth connected complex projective Fano sixfold with nef tangent bundle is rational homogeneous, resolving the Campana–Peternell conjecture in complex dimension six.

    As a consequence, a connected compact Kähler manifold X with nef holomorphic tangent bundle and dim⁡CX−q~(X)≤6\dim_{\mathbb C}X-\widetilde q(X)\leq6dimC​X−q​(X)≤6 has ordinary universal cover F×Cq~(X)F\times\mathbb C^{\widetilde q(X)}F×Cq​(X), where F is a rational homogeneous manifold and q~(X)\widetilde q(X)q​(X) is the maximal irregularity of a connected finite étale cover.
