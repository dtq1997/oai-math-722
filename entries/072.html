Brennan 猜想与 Kraetzer 预测反例

Brennan's conjecture and a counterexample to Kraetzer's prediction

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

证明了 Brennan 猜想：对从一个单连通平面域到圆盘的每个共形双射 ϕ\phiϕ，当 4/3<s<44/3<s<44/3<s<4 时，∣ϕ′∣s|\phi'|^s∣ϕ′∣s 关于面积测度可积。

最优的普适积分平均恒等式为：当 t≤−2t\le-2t≤−2 时，BS(t)=∣t∣−1B_{\mathcal S}(t)=|t|-1BS​(t)=∣t∣−1。

有界单叶函数的严格界 Bb(−1)<1/4B_b(-1)<1/4Bb​(−1)<1/4 否定了 Kraetzer 在该参数处的预言。

英文原文

Proves Brennan's conjecture: for every conformal bijection ϕ\phiϕ from a simply connected plane domain onto the disk, ∣ϕ′∣s|\phi'|^s∣ϕ′∣s is area-integrable for 4/3<s<44/3<s<44/3<s<4.

The sharp universal integral-means identity is BS(t)=∣t∣−1B_{\mathcal S}(t)=|t|-1BS​(t)=∣t∣−1 for t≤−2t\le-2t≤−2.

A strict bound Bb(−1)<1/4B_b(-1)<1/4Bb​(−1)<1/4 for bounded univalent functions disproves Kraetzer's prediction at that parameter.

# Brennan's conjecture and the integral-means spectrum

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Brennan's conjecture and sharp inverse-square integral means](../../preprints/Brennans-conjecture-and-sharp-inverse-square-integral-means-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)
- [A strict inverse-first-power bound for univalent functions](../../preprints/A-strict-inverse-first-power-bound-for-univalent-functions-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

Brennan's conjecture asserts that $|\phi'|^s$ is area-integrable for $4/3<s<4$ when $\phi$ conformally maps a simply connected plane domain with nontrivial spherical boundary onto the disk. The formalization establishes this interval, along with area integrability of $|f'|^t$ for univalent disk maps when $-2<t<2/3$.

It also gives the uniform inverse-square radial-mean bound with exponent $-1-\varepsilon$ for every $\varepsilon>0$ and the spectrum value $B_{\mathcal S}(-2)=1$. The Koebe map and its inverse give divergence at all four boundary exponents.

Kraetzer's proposed integral-means spectrum predicts $B_b(-1)=1/4$ for bounded univalent maps. The formalized result proves $B_b(-1)<1/4$, contradicting that prediction.

The underlying estimate gives constants $0<\varepsilon<1/4$ and $C<\infty$ such that the normalized circular mean of $|f'|^{-1}$ is at most $C(1-r)^{-1/4+\varepsilon}$ for every normalized univalent disk map and $1/2\le r<1$. The bound requires neither bounded image nor boundary regularity, and no numerical value of $\varepsilon$ is specified.
