Saxl 猜想与普适张量平方

Saxl's conjecture and universal tensor squares

首发 · 2026-10-062 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

证明了 Saxl 猜想：每个阶梯表示的张量平方都包含相应对称群的每个不可约复表示。

更一般地，每个满足 n∉{2,4,9}n\notin\{2,4,9\}n∈/{2,4,9} 的 SnS_nSn​ 都有一个不可约表示，其张量平方包含所有不可约表示。

英文原文

Proves Saxl's conjecture: the tensor square of every staircase representation contains every irreducible complex representation of the corresponding symmetric group.

More generally, every SnS_nSn​ with n∉{2,4,9}n\notin\{2,4,9\}n∈/{2,4,9} has an irreducible representation whose tensor square contains all irreducibles.

  1. 对称群的普适张量平方

    Universal Tensor Squares for Symmetric Groups

    摘要

    对于除 2、4 和 9 以外的每个正整数 n，我们证明了 Sn 存在某个不可约复表示，其张量平方包含每个不可约表示。

    这肯定地解决了对称群的张量平方猜想。

    英文原文

    For every positive integer n other than 2, 4, and 9, we prove that some irreducible complex representation of Sn has a tensor square containing every irreducible representation.

    This resolves the tensor square conjecture for symmetric groups affirmatively.

# Saxl’s conjecture and universal tensor squares

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Universal Tensor Squares for Symmetric Groups](../../preprints/Universal-Tensor-Squares-for-Symmetric-Groups-September-24-2026/main.pdf)
- [A Cyclic Polytabloid Proof of Saxl's Conjecture](../../preprints/A-Cyclic-Polytabloid-Proof-of-Saxls-Conjecture-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The formalization proves the universal tensor-square conjecture for symmetric groups in the stated range. For every positive integer $n\notin\{2,4,9\}$, it constructs an irreducible complex representation of $S_n$ whose tensor square contains every irreducible complex representation of $S_n$. Equivalently, all corresponding Kronecker coefficients are positive. The statement also supplies injective intertwining maps for arbitrary finite-dimensional irreducible representations.

Saxl's conjecture asserts that the tensor square of each staircase Specht module contains every irreducible representation of the corresponding symmetric group. The formalization establishes this for every $m\ge1$: if $\rho_m=(m,m-1,\ldots,1)$, then $g(\rho_m,\rho_m,\mu)>0$ for every partition $\mu$ of $m(m+1)/2$.

The stronger claim that every constituent appears in the orbit span of one prescribed tensor is not included.
