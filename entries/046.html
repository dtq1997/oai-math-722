Shafarevich 全纯凸性反例

Counterexamples to Shafarevich holomorphic convexity

首发 · 2026-10-062 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

构造了一个具有大基本群的光滑复射影四维簇，其万有覆盖不含正维紧解析子簇，却既不是 Stein 的，也不全纯凸。

另一个光滑复射影曲面的例子已在二维否定了不附加限制的 Shafarevich 全纯凸性猜想。

英文原文

Constructs a smooth projective complex fourfold with large fundamental group whose universal cover contains no positive-dimensional compact analytic subvariety but is neither Stein nor holomorphically convex.

A separate smooth projective complex surface already disproves unrestricted Shafarevich holomorphic convexity in dimension two.

  1. 具有大基本群和非 Stein 万有覆盖的射影四维簇

    A projective fourfold with large fundamental group and non-Stein universal cover

    摘要

    我们构造了一个具有大基本群的光滑复射影四维簇，其万有覆盖不是 Stein 的。

    该万有覆盖不含正维紧复解析子簇，因此即使在大基本群假设下，这也否定了 Shafarevich 的全纯凸性猜想。

    英文原文

    We construct a smooth projective complex fourfold with large fundamental group whose universal cover is not Stein.

    The universal cover contains no positive-dimensional compact complex-analytic subvariety, so this disproves Shafarevich's holomorphic-convexity conjecture even under the large-fundamental-group hypothesis.

  2. Shafarevich 全纯凸性猜想的曲面反例

    A surface counterexample to Shafarevich holomorphic convexity

    摘要

    我们构造了一个光滑连通复射影曲面，其万有覆盖不是全纯凸的。

    这否定了复维数为二时的 Shafarevich 全纯凸性猜想。

    英文原文

    We construct a smooth connected projective complex surface whose universal cover is not holomorphically convex.

    This disproves the Shafarevich conjecture on holomorphic convexity in complex dimension two.
