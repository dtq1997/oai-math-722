平稳整 varifold 奇异集维数的最优界

Sharp singular-set bounds for stationary integral varifolds

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

证明了欧氏开集中每个平稳整 mmm 维 varifold 的奇异集的 Hausdorff 维数至多为 m−1m-1m−1，且在每个正维数和余维数中，这一界都是最优的。

在圆球面上，这组结果还证明了几乎处处正则性：奇异集的 mmm 维 Hausdorff 测度为零。

英文原文

Proves that every stationary integral mmm-varifold in a Euclidean open set has singular set of Hausdorff dimension at most m−1m-1m−1, a sharp bound in every positive dimension and codimension.

On round spheres, the family also proves almost-everywhere regularity: the singular set has zero mmm-dimensional Hausdorff measure.

  1. 平稳整 varifold 奇异集的余维一界

    A Codimension-One Bound for the Singular Set of a Stationary Integral Varifold

    摘要

    在每个正维数和正余维数下，欧氏开集中每个平稳整 m 维 varifold 的奇异集的 Hausdorff 维数都至多为 m−1m-1m−1。

    这一最优界证明了 Brena、Decio 和 De Lellis 记载的欧氏奇异集猜想。

    英文原文

    The singular set of every stationary integral m-varifold in an open Euclidean set has Hausdorff dimension at most m−1m-1m−1, in every positive dimension and codimension.

    This sharp bound proves the Euclidean singular-set conjecture recorded by Brena, Decio, and De Lellis.

  2. 平稳整 varifold 的几乎处处正则性

    Almost-everywhere regularity of stationary integral varifolds

    摘要

    我们解决了 Brena、Decio 和 De Lellis 关于欧氏开集中任意正维数和正余维数的平稳整 varifold 的几乎处处正则性猜想。

    奇异集在 varifold 的维数下具有零测度：在几乎每个支撑点附近，该 varifold 都是一个光滑嵌入极小子流形的恒定正整数倍。

    相应的结论在圆球面上也成立。

    英文原文

    We resolve the almost-everywhere regularity conjecture of Brena, Decio, and De Lellis for stationary integral varifolds of arbitrary positive dimension and codimension in Euclidean open sets.

    The singular set has zero measure in the dimension of the varifold: near almost every support point, the varifold is a constant positive integer multiple of a smooth embedded minimal submanifold.

    The corresponding statement also holds on round spheres.
