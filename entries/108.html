积和式与行列式的三次下界

A cubic permanent–determinant lower bound

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

证明了 C\mathbb CC 上 n×nn\times nn×n 积和式的边界行列式复杂度具有 Ω(n3)\Omega(n^3)Ω(n3) 下界。

即使允许取仿射线性矩阵之行列式的逐系数极限，矩阵大小也必须至少为 cn3cn^3cn3，其中 c>0c>0c>0 为绝对常数，且这对所有充分大的 nnn 都成立；因此，同一个界也适用于精确表示。

英文原文

Proves an Ω(n3)\Omega(n^3)Ω(n3) lower bound for the border determinantal complexity of the n×nn\times nn×n permanent over C\mathbb CC.

Even coefficientwise limits of determinants of affine-linear matrices require matrix size at least cn3cn^3cn3, for an absolute c>0c>0c>0 and all sufficiently large nnn; the same bound therefore holds for exact representations.

论文

  1. 积和式的边界行列式复杂度的三次下界

    A cubic lower bound for border determinantal complexity of the permanent

    摘要

    我们证明，m×mm\times mm×m 积和式在复数域上的边界行列式复杂度为 Ω(m3)\Omega(m^3)Ω(m3)，其中允许任意仿射线性行列式表示和逐系数极限。

    该结果还给出精确行列式复杂度以及仿射线性代数分支程序中顶点数和边数的三次下界，其中包括在顶点数或边数预算固定时的逐系数极限。

    英文原文

    We prove that the complex border determinantal complexity of the m×mm\times mm×m permanent is Ω(m3)\Omega(m^3)Ω(m3), allowing arbitrary affine-linear determinant representations and coefficientwise limits.

    It also gives cubic lower bounds for exact determinantal complexity and for the numbers of vertices and edges in affine-linear algebraic branching programs, including coefficientwise limits with a fixed vertex or edge budget.

# A cubic permanent–determinant lower bound

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [A cubic lower bound for border determinantal complexity of the permanent](../../preprints/A-cubic-lower-bound-for-border-determinantal-complexity-of-the-permanent-September-24-2026/A-cubic-lower-bound-for-border-determinantal-complexity-of-the-permanent-September-24-2026.pdf)

## Scope

The formalization proves a cubic lower bound for both exact and border determinantal representations of the complex $m\times m$ permanent. For $m\ge1408$, every affine-linear determinant representation of size $n$, including coefficientwise limits, satisfies $n\ge m^3/(5529600e)$.

A general supporting theorem applies to a polynomial in $d\ge2$ variables whose first nonzero homogeneous Taylor term has degree $r\ge2$ and no nonzero singular zero. Its exact and border determinantal sizes are at least $(r-1)(d-1)/(4e)$. The paper's algebraic-branching-program consequences are outside these statements.
