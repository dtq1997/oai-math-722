有限 Benson–Etingof–Ostrik 猜想

The finite Benson–Etingof–Ostrik conjecture

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

证明了特征为 p>0p>0p>0 的代数闭域 kkk 上每个有限对称张量范畴，都存在一个到高阶 Verlinde 范畴 Verpn\mathrm{Ver}_{p^n}Verpn​ 的 kkk-线性、正合、忠实、强对称幺半纤维函子。

层级可以依赖于该范畴，且该定理包括特征二的情形，从而解决了 Benson–Etingof–Ostrik 猜想的有限情形。

英文原文

Proves that every finite symmetric tensor category over an algebraically closed field kkk of characteristic p>0p>0p>0 admits a kkk-linear exact faithful strong symmetric monoidal fiber functor to a higher Verlinde category Verpn\mathrm{Ver}_{p^n}Verpn​.

The level may depend on the category, and the theorem includes characteristic two, resolving the finite case of the Benson–Etingof–Ostrik conjecture.

论文

  1. 正特征下有限对称张量范畴的纤维函子

    Fiber functors for finite symmetric tensor categories in positive characteristic

    摘要

    我们证明了 Benson–Etingof–Ostrik 猜想的有限情形：特征为 p > 0 的代数闭域 k 上的每个有限对称张量范畴，都存在一个到有限高阶 Verlinde 范畴 Verpn(k)\mathop{\mathrm{Ver}}\nolimits _{p^n}(k)Verpn​(k) 的 k-线性、正合、忠实的强对称幺半函子。

    该结果涵盖特征为二的情形，且层级 n 可以依赖于该范畴。

    英文原文

    We prove the finite case of the Benson–Etingof–Ostrik conjecture: every finite symmetric tensor category over an algebraically closed field k of characteristic p > 0 admits a k-linear exact faithful strong symmetric monoidal functor to a finite higher Verlinde category Verpn(k)\mathop{\mathrm{Ver}}\nolimits _{p^n}(k)Verpn​(k).

    The result includes characteristic two, and the level n may depend on the category.
