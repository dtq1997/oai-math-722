Lipschitz 高度场的高斯与 SLE 极限

Gaussian fields and SLE interfaces for Lipschitz heights

首发 · 2026-10-063 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

对于三角格上的高度模型，证明其在有界光滑单连通区域上的高斯自由场极限：这些模型包括增量为0,±20,\pm20,±2且两段边界弧上的边界值为±1\pm1±1的均匀奇数高度，以及以固定x∈[1/2,1]x\in[1/\sqrt2,1]x∈[1/2​,1]加权的零边界整数 Lipschitz 高度。

均匀实值 Lipschitz 高度也收敛到高斯场；在调定的相反边界值振幅下，其界面收敛到弦状SLE4\mathrm{SLE}_4SLE4​，从而确立 Schramm 关于实值场及其界面的预言。

英文原文

Proves Gaussian free field limits on bounded smooth simply connected domains for triangular-lattice height models: uniform odd heights with increments 0,±20,\pm20,±2 and two-arc boundary values ±1\pm1±1, and zero-boundary integer Lipschitz heights weighted by fixed x∈[1/2,1]x\in[1/\sqrt2,1]x∈[1/2​,1].

Uniform real Lipschitz heights also converge to a Gaussian field; at a tuned opposite-boundary amplitude, their interface converges to chordal SLE4\mathrm{SLE}_4SLE4​, establishing Schramm’s real-field/interface predictions.

  1. 加权整数 Lipschitz 高度的高斯自由场极限

    Gaussian free-field limits of weighted integer Lipschitz heights

    论文已修订官方日志 2026.10.07

    摘要

    我们证明了三角格上加权整数 Lipschitz 高度的高斯自由场标度极限。

    在边界值为零、且每条高度发生变化的边都带有因子 x 的情况下，对于每个固定的 x∈[1/2,1]x\in[1/\sqrt2,1]x∈[1/2​,1]，该场除以一个仅依赖于 x 的正常数后，收敛于零 Dirichlet 高斯自由场。

    在从内部作晶格逼近且边界参数化一致收敛的条件下，该收敛在每个有界 C2 Jordan 区域上作为随机广义函数成立。

    这涵盖了均匀高度模型以及所预测的临界端点。

    英文原文

    We prove a Gaussian free field scaling limit for weighted integer Lipschitz heights on the triangular lattice.

    With zero boundary values and a factor x for each edge on which the height changes, the field converges after division by a positive constant depending only on x to the zero-Dirichlet Gaussian free field, for every fixed x∈[1/2,1]x\in[1/\sqrt2,1]x∈[1/2​,1].

    The convergence holds as a random distribution on every bounded C2 Jordan domain under inside lattice approximations with uniformly convergent boundary parametrizations.

    This includes the uniform height model and the predicted critical endpoint.

  2. 具有两弧边界数据的整数 Lipschitz 高度的高斯自由场极限

    The Gaussian free field limit of integer Lipschitz heights with two-arc boundary data

    论文已修订官方日志 2026.10.07

    摘要

    我们证明了，在光滑单连通区域的三角格逼近上，相邻高度之差为零或二、两段边界弧上的边界值分别为 +1 和 −1 的均匀奇整数高度函数，在中心化后收敛于 Dirichlet 高斯自由场的一个普适倍数。

    这解决了 Schramm 问题 2.2 中关于场的部分。

    我们给出了归一化的一个绝对收敛的有限体积公式。

    证明结合了反射正性、一个谱求和规则、边界比较以及高斯矩恒等式。

    英文原文

    We prove that the centered uniform odd integer height function on triangular-lattice approximations of a smooth simply connected domain, with neighboring differences zero or two and boundary values +1 and −1 on two arcs, converges to a universal multiple of the Dirichlet Gaussian free field.

    This resolves the field part of Schramm's Problem 2.2.

    We give an absolutely convergent finite-volume formula for the normalization.

    The proof combines reflection positivity, a spectral sum rule, and boundary comparison with Gaussian moment identities.

  3. 三角格上均匀抽样的实值 Lipschitz 曲面

    Uniform real Lipschitz surfaces on the triangular lattice

    论文已修订官方日志 2026.10.07

    摘要

    我们证明了三角格上均匀抽样的实值最近邻 Lipschitz 高度的高斯自由场与 SLE4_44​ 标度极限，从而解决了 Schramm 问题 2.3。

    在光滑单连通区域的逼近上，中心化高度场作为随机广义函数收敛于 Dirichlet 高斯自由场的一个倍数。

    在某个经调定的两弧边界幅度下，零高度界面在曲线的一致距离下收敛于弦 SLE4_44​。

    我们以一个隐式的平稳切向通量系数确定了场方差与边界高度之间的关系。

    英文原文

    We prove the Gaussian free field and SLE4_44​ scaling limits for uniformly sampled real nearest-neighbor Lipschitz heights on the triangular lattice, resolving Schramm's Problem 2.3.

    On approximations of smooth simply connected domains, the centered height field converges as a random distribution to a multiple of the Dirichlet Gaussian free field.

    At one tuned two-arc boundary amplitude, the zero-height interface converges in uniform curve distance to chordal SLE4_44​.

    We identify the relation between the field variance and the boundary height in terms of an implicit stationary tangent-flux coefficient.

我们证明了三角格上加权整数 Lipschitz 高度的高斯自由场标度极限。

在边界值为零、且每条高度发生变化的边都带有因子 x 的情况下，对于每个固定的 x∈[1/2,1]x\in[1/\sqrt2,1]x∈[1/2​,1]，该场除以一个仅依赖于 x 的正常数后，收敛于零 Dirichlet 高斯自由场。

在从内部作晶格逼近且边界参数化一致收敛的条件下，该收敛在每个有界 C2 Jordan 区域上作为随机广义函数成立。

这涵盖了均匀高度模型以及所预测的临界端点。

英文原文

We prove a Gaussian free field scaling limit for weighted integer Lipschitz heights on the triangular lattice.

With zero boundary values and a factor x for each edge on which the height changes, the field converges after division by a positive constant depending only on x to the zero-Dirichlet Gaussian free field, for every fixed x∈[1/2,1]x\in[1/\sqrt2,1]x∈[1/2​,1].

The convergence holds as a random distribution on every bounded C2 Jordan domain under inside lattice approximations with uniformly convergent boundary parametrizations.

This includes the uniform height model and the predicted critical endpoint.

The proof combines reflection positivity, a spectral sum rule, and boundary comparison with Gaussian moment identities.

We prove the Gaussian free field and SLE4_44​ scaling limits for uniformly sampled real nearest-neighbor Lipschitz heights on the triangular lattice, resolving Schramm's Problem 2.3.

At one tuned two-arc boundary amplitude, the zero-height interface converges in uniform curve distance to chordal SLE4_44​.

We identify the relation between the field variance and the boundary height in terms of an implicit stationary tangent-flux coefficient.
