单关系群的 Gersten 猜想

Gersten's conjecture for one-relator groups

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

证明了 Gersten 猜想：每个有限生成单关系群，只要不含任何满足m,n≠0m,n\ne0m,n=0的 Baumslag–Solitar 子群BS(m,n)\mathrm{BS}(m,n)BS(m,n)，就是字双曲群。

还证明每个字双曲单关系群都是 virtually compact special 的。

英文原文

Proves Gersten's conjecture: every finitely generated one-relator group containing no Baumslag–Solitar subgroup BS(m,n)\mathrm{BS}(m,n)BS(m,n), with m,n≠0m,n\ne0m,n=0, is word-hyperbolic.

It also proves that every word-hyperbolic one-relator group is virtually compact special.

OpenAI · overview.tex

论文

  1. 不含 Baumslag–Solitar 子群的单关系群是双曲群

    Baumslag-Solitar-free one-relator groups are hyperbolic

    摘要

    我们证明了 Gersten 猜想：每个有限生成单关系群，只要不包含任何同构于非零整数 m、n 所对应的 Baumslag–Solitar 群 BS(m,n)\mathop{\mathrm{BS}}\nolimits (m,n)BS(m,n) 的子群，就是字双曲的。

    英文原文

    We prove Gersten's conjecture: every finitely generated one-relator group containing no subgroup isomorphic to a Baumslag–Solitar group BS(m,n)\mathop{\mathrm{BS}}\nolimits (m,n)BS(m,n), for nonzero integers m, n, is word-hyperbolic.

  2. 双曲单关系群的几乎紧致特殊性

    Virtual compact specialness of hyperbolic one-relator groups

    摘要

    我们证明，每个字双曲单关系群都是几乎紧致特殊的。

    结合 Kielak–Linton 的工作，这解决了 Wise 关于双曲单关系群几乎为 free-by-cyclic 群的猜想：每个这样的群都几乎为 free-by-cyclic 群，其中允许自由核的秩为无穷。

    英文原文

    We prove that every word-hyperbolic one-relator group is virtually compact special.

    Combined with the work of Kielak–Linton, this resolves Wise's virtual free-by-cyclic conjecture for hyperbolic one-relator groups: every such group is virtually free-by-cyclic, with the free kernel allowed to have infinite rank.
