负 Kähler 曲率却无有界全纯坐标

Negative Kähler curvature without bounded holomorphic coordinates

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

在 C3\mathbb C^3C3 中构造了一个可缩域，它具有负夹逼曲率的完备 Kähler 度量，却没有有界全纯坐标，从而否定了这一情形下的有界域单值化。

一个更高维的例子具有至多为 −1-1−1 的截面曲率，且有界全纯函数只有常函数；其曲率没有下界。

英文原文

Constructs a contractible domain in C3\mathbb C^3C3 with a complete negatively pinched Kähler metric but no bounded holomorphic coordinates, disproving bounded-domain uniformization in this setting.

A higher-dimensional example has sectional curvature at most −1-1−1 and only constant bounded holomorphic functions; its curvature is not bounded below.

论文

  1. 一个具有负曲率双侧夹逼却不存在有界全纯坐标的复三维 Kähler 流形

    A negatively pinched Kähler threefold without bounded holomorphic coordinates

    主结果已列入形式化清单
    摘要

    我们在复维数三中构造了一个可缩域，它具有完备 Kähler 度量，且其实截面曲率介于两个有限负常数之间。

    它不容许到 ℂ3 的、Jacobian 处处非零的有界全纯映射，因此不双全纯于任何有界域。

    这否定地回答了负曲率双侧夹逼下的 Kähler 单值化问题。

    英文原文

    We construct a contractible domain in complex dimension three with a complete Kähler metric whose real sectional curvatures lie between two finite negative constants.

    It admits no bounded holomorphic map to ℂ3 with nowhere-vanishing Jacobian and is therefore not biholomorphic to a bounded domain.

    This gives a negative answer to the negatively pinched Kähler uniformization question.

  2. 单侧负截面曲率与全纯 Liouville 性质

    One-sided negative sectional curvature and the holomorphic Liouville property

    摘要

    在某个固定的充分大的有限复维数 m 中，我们构造了 ℂm 中一个与 R2m\mathbb R^{2m}R2m 微分同胚的域，它具有实截面曲率至多为 −1 的完备 Kähler 度量，且所有有界全纯函数都是常数。

    其截面曲率没有下界。

    这一构造否定地回答了单侧曲率界下的有界全纯函数问题。

    英文原文

    We construct, in some sufficiently large fixed finite complex dimension m, a domain in ℂm diffeomorphic to R2m\mathbb R^{2m}R2m that admits a complete Kähler metric with real sectional curvature at most −1 and has only constant bounded holomorphic functions.

    Its sectional curvatures are unbounded below.

    The construction gives a negative answer to the one-sided bounded-holomorphic-function question.

# Negative Kähler curvature without bounded holomorphic coordinates

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [A negatively pinched Kähler threefold without bounded holomorphic coordinates](../../preprints/A-negatively-pinched-Kahler-threefold-without-bounded-holomorphic-coordinates-September-25-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The formalized result constructs a nonempty contractible complex threefold with a complete Kähler metric whose real sectional curvatures lie between two fixed negative constants. Nevertheless, it has no system of bounded holomorphic coordinates and is not biholomorphic to a bounded domain. The metric is obtained from an explicitly controlled potential. This is the negatively pinched construction; the different companion with only one-sided curvature control is separate.
