英文原文

Gives a deterministic length-nnn discrete Fourier transform algorithm using O(n(log⁡n)1−δ)O(n(\log n)^{1-\delta})O(n(logn)1−δ) operations for every nnn, with explicit δ=10−13\delta=10^{-13}δ=10−13.

  1. 精确离散 Fourier 变换的显式幂次节省

    An explicit power saving for the exact discrete Fourier transform

    摘要

    我们给出一个确定性算法，对每个长度 n，在 O(n(log⁡n)1−10−13)O(n(\log n)^{1-10^{-13}})O(n(logn)1−10−13) 次运算内计算离散 Fourier 变换。

    该模型使用精确复数算术、不受限制的系数、指定的 Fourier 单位根以及单位代价的对数大小索引；标量准备和数组组织的代价也计入其中。

    英文原文

    We give a deterministic algorithm that computes the discrete Fourier transform at every length n in O(n(log⁡n)1−10−13)O(n(\log n)^{1-10^{-13}})O(n(logn)1−10−13) operations.

    The model uses exact complex arithmetic, unrestricted coefficients, specified Fourier roots, and unit-cost logarithmic-size indexing; scalar preparation and array organization are included.

  2. 有限张量运算节省与精确 Fourier 电路

    Finite tensor savings and exact Fourier circuits

    主结果已列入形式化清单
    摘要

    沿一个无界的长度序列，我们构造大小为 o(nlog⁡n)o(n\log n)o(nlogn) 的精确非一致 Fourier 电路，并计入每次加法、减法和标量乘法。

    这否定了不受限制的复线性电路模型中的 Ω(nlog⁡n)\Omega(n\log n)Ω(nlogn) 下界。

    该构造利用有限张量的运算节省：当允许在调用之间无代价地使用可逆单项映射时，某个可逆非单项复矩阵的张量幂，可以用比相同坐标上的标准张量轴算法更少的矩阵调用次数来计算。

    英文原文

    We construct exact nonuniform Fourier circuits of size o(nlog⁡n)o(n\log n)o(nlogn) along an unbounded sequence of lengths, counting every addition, subtraction, and scalar multiplication.

    This refutes the Ω(nlog⁡n)\Omega(n\log n)Ω(nlogn) lower bound in the unrestricted complex linear-circuit model.

    The construction uses a finite tensor saving: a tensor power of some invertible nonmonomial complex matrix can be computed with fewer matrix calls than the standard tensor-axis algorithm on the same coordinates, when invertible monomial maps are allowed freely between calls.

# Exact Fourier transforms below $`n\log n`$

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Finite tensor savings and exact Fourier circuits](../../preprints/Finite-tensor-savings-and-exact-Fourier-circuits-September-25-2026/main.pdf)

## Scope

The formalized result gives exact discrete Fourier transforms with arbitrarily small normalized circuit cost along an unbounded sequence of lengths. For every $c>0$ and every cutoff $N_0\ge2$, some $n\ge N_0$ has a circuit computing the unnormalized DFT with fewer than $cn\log_2 n$ gates. Addition, subtraction, and multiplication by a predetermined complex scalar each cost one gate; diagonal scalings are charged. The result is subsequential, with no all-length, bounded-coefficient, conditioning, or bit-complexity claim.
