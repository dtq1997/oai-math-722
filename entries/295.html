Kadison–Ringrose 上同调猜想

The Kadison–Ringrose cohomology conjecture

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

证明了复 von Neumann 代数上每个以该代数自身为系数、次数至少为二的有界 Hochschild 上闭链，都可写成某个有界上链的上边界。

等价地，所有高次有界 Hochschild 上同调群都为零，从而解决了 Kadison–Ringrose 猜想。

英文原文

Proves that every bounded Hochschild cocycle of degree at least two on a complex von Neumann algebra, with coefficients in the algebra itself, has a bounded primitive.

Equivalently, all higher bounded Hochschild cohomology groups vanish, resolving the Kadison–Ringrose conjecture.

OpenAI · overview.tex

论文

  1. 高次有界 Hochschild 上同调的消失

    Vanishing of higher bounded Hochschild cohomology

    摘要

    我们证明，复 von Neumann 代数上每个取值于该代数自身、次数至少为二的有界 Hochschild 余循环，都具有有界原像。

    结合已经确立的一次内导子定理，这肯定地解决了 Kadison–Ringrose 上同调猜想。

    英文原文

    We prove that every bounded Hochschild cocycle of degree at least two on a complex von Neumann algebra, with values in the algebra itself, has a bounded primitive.

    Together with the established degree-one inner-derivation theorem, this resolves the Kadison–Ringrose cohomology conjecture positively.

# The Kadison–Ringrose cohomology conjecture

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Vanishing of higher bounded Hochschild cohomology](../../preprints/Vanishing-of-higher-bounded-Hochschild-cohomology-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The formalization proves vanishing of bounded Hochschild cohomology in every degree at least two for a complex von Neumann algebra with coefficients in itself. Every bounded multilinear cocycle of such a degree is the Hochschild differential of a bounded multilinear cochain one degree lower. No separability or type restriction is imposed. The degree-one inner-derivation theorem is outside this selected statement.
