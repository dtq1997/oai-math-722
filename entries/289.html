强 Kadison–Kastler 猜想

The strong Kadison–Kastler conjecture

OpenAI · 2026-10-063 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

证明了同一 Hilbert 空间上充分接近的含幺 von Neumann 代数可由任意接近恒等算子的酉算子共轭，且单位球之间算子范数距离的容许阈值是普适的。

反例表明，对单侧近包含，接近恒等的共轭结论不成立，而且任意接近的范数可分 C∗C^*C∗ 代数也未必在环境算子代数中酉共轭。

英文原文

Proves that sufficiently close unital von Neumann algebras on the same Hilbert space are conjugate by a unitary arbitrarily close to the identity, with a universal tolerance in the operator-norm distance between unit balls.

Counterexamples show that near-identity conjugacy fails for one-sided near inclusions, and that arbitrarily close norm-separable C∗C^*C∗-algebras need not be ambiently unitarily conjugate.

  1. 普适的强 Kadison–Kastler 稳定性

    Universal strong Kadison–Kastler stability

    摘要

    我们证明，充分接近的有单位元 von Neumann 代数可由一个任意接近恒等算子的酉算子共轭。

    允许的误差只依赖于预先指定的该酉算子与恒等算子之间的距离，并且对所有代数、表示和 Hilbert 空间一致。

    这解决了强 Kadison–Kastler 猜想。

    英文原文

    We prove that sufficiently close unital von Neumann algebras are conjugate by a unitary arbitrarily close to the identity.

    The tolerance depends only on the prescribed distance of that unitary from the identity, uniformly over all algebras, representations, and Hilbert spaces.

    This resolves the strong Kadison–Kastler conjecture.

  2. 无法由接近恒等的酉算子实现空间嵌入的 von Neumann 代数近包含

    Near Inclusions of von Neumann Algebras Without Small Spatial Embeddings

    摘要

    我们对 von Neumann 代数单侧近包含的不受限制的小空间嵌入问题给出否定回答。

    在可分复 Hilbert 空间上，我们构造具有相同单位元的有单位元 von Neumann 代数对，使其单侧间隙趋于零。

    从源代数到目标代数的空间嵌入存在，但实现这些嵌入的每个酉算子与恒等算子之间的距离都保持在某个固定正下界之上。

    因此，该障碍涉及的是接近恒等算子的实现酉算子，而非空间嵌入的存在性。

    英文原文

    We give a negative answer to the unrestricted small-spatial-embedding problem for one-sided near inclusions of von Neumann algebras.

    On separable complex Hilbert spaces, we construct pairs of unital von Neumann algebras with common identity whose one-sided gaps tend to zero.

    Spatial embeddings of the source into the target exist, but every implementing unitary stays a fixed positive distance from the identity.

    The obstruction therefore concerns small implementing unitaries, rather than the existence of spatial embeddings.

  3. 相近却不空间共轭的可分 C* 代数

    Close Separable C*-Algebras Without Spatial Conjugacy

    摘要

    我们反驳 Kadison–Kastler 猜想的可分 C∗ 代数空间形式。

    对每个 ε > 0，我们在同一可分复 Hilbert 空间上构造有单位元、范数可分的 C∗ 代数，使它们具有相同单位元，Kadison–Kastler 距离小于 ε，但不能由任何酉算子共轭。

    这两个代数具有相同的 von Neumann 闭包。

    英文原文

    We disprove the separable C∗-algebraic spatial form of the Kadison–Kastler conjecture.

    For every ε > 0, we construct unital, norm-separable C∗-algebras on a common separable complex Hilbert space, with the same identity and Kadison–Kastler distance less than ε, that are not conjugate by any unitary.

    The two algebras have the same von Neumann closure.

# Strong Kadison–Kastler stability and its spatial boundaries

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Universal strong Kadison–Kastler stability](../../preprints/Universal-strong-Kadison-Kastler-stability-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The formalization proves the strong Kadison–Kastler conjecture uniformly. For every $\varepsilon>0$, there is a $\delta>0$ such that any two unital von Neumann algebras on the same complex Hilbert space at Kadison–Kastler distance below $\delta$ are conjugate by a unitary $u$ with $\lVert u-1\rVert<\varepsilon$. The tolerance depends only on $\varepsilon$, uniformly over the algebras, their representations, and the Hilbert space.
