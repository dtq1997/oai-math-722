Arnold 不动点猜想强形式反例

Counterexamples to strong forms of Arnold's fixed-point conjecture

OpenAI · 2026-10-065 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

否定了非退化 Hamiltonian 不动点数的稳定 Morse 下界：在实维数为 222222 的单连通闭 Kähler 流形上，不动点数低于稳定 Morse 数的差额可以无界。

另有一个复三维二次超曲面上的 Hamiltonian 微分同胚恰有三个不动点，而其上每个光滑函数都必须至少具有四个临界点。

英文原文

Disproves stable-Morse lower bounds for nondegenerate Hamiltonian fixed points: on simply connected closed Kähler manifolds of real dimension 222222, the deficit below the stable Morse number is unbounded.

A separate Hamiltonian diffeomorphism of the complex quadric threefold has exactly three fixed points, fewer than the four critical points required of every smooth function.

  1. 二十二维中少于稳定 Morse 数的 Hamilton 不动点

    Hamiltonian Fixed Points Below the Stable Morse Number in Dimension Twenty-Two

    摘要

    我们用实维数固定为二十二的例子，否定了非退化 Hamilton 不动点数的稳定 Morse 下界。

    对于每个整数 m ≥ 1，我们都构造了一个稳定 Morse 数为 80+1968m80+1968m80+1968m 的单连通闭 Kähler 流形，以及一个恰有 80+1952m80+1952m80+1952m 个不动点的 Hamilton 微分同胚，其中所有不动点均非退化，且轨道环均可缩。

    因此，缺额没有上界，且不动点数至多为稳定 Morse 数的 127/128。

    英文原文

    We disprove the stable Morse lower bound for nondegenerate Hamiltonian fixed points with examples in fixed real dimension twenty-two.

    For every integer m ≥ 1, we construct a simply connected closed Kähler manifold with stable Morse number 80+1968m80+1968m80+1968m and a Hamiltonian diffeomorphism with exactly 80+1952m80+1952m80+1952m fixed points, all nondegenerate and with contractible orbit loops.

    The deficit is therefore unbounded, and the fixed-point count is at most 127/128 of the stable Morse number.

  2. 低于稳定 Morse 数的循环整系数 Floer 下界的最优性

    Sharpness of the Cyclic Integral Floer Bound Below the Stable Morse Number

    摘要

    我们构造了一个实维数为 3332、最小 Chern 数为一的单连通闭 Kähler 流形，其 Hamilton 不动点数达到循环整系数 Floer 下界，同时又小于稳定 Morse 数。

    该 Hamilton 微分同胚恰有 1 872 2321\,872\,2321872232 个不动点，所有不动点均非退化且轨道环均可缩，而稳定 Morse 数为 1 872 2641\,872\,2641872264。

    因此，即使循环整系数下界能够达到，较强的稳定 Morse 下界仍会恰好差 32 而失效。

    英文原文

    We construct a simply connected closed Kähler manifold of real dimension 3332 and minimal Chern number one whose Hamiltonian fixed-point count attains the cyclic integral Floer bound while falling below the stable Morse number.

    The Hamiltonian diffeomorphism has exactly 1 872 2321\,872\,2321872232 fixed points, all nondegenerate and with contractible orbit loops, whereas the stable Morse number is 1 872 2641\,872\,2641872264.

    Thus the stronger stable Morse bound fails by exactly 32, even when the cyclic integral bound is sharp.

  3. 少于稳定 Morse 数的 Hamilton 不动点

    Hamiltonian Fixed Points Below the Stable Morse Number

    摘要

    我们构造了一个单连通闭 Kähler 流形，其不动点数比稳定 Morse 数少十六，从而否定了非退化 Hamilton 不动点数的稳定 Morse 下界。

    所有对应的周期轨道都是可缩的。

    该构造将一个 Hamilton 对合与整系数同调在两个不同素数处的挠性结合起来；其证明使用有限维 Morse 理论和复爆破。

    英文原文

    We disprove the stable Morse lower bound for nondegenerate Hamiltonian fixed points by constructing a simply connected closed Kähler manifold with sixteen fewer fixed points than its stable Morse number.

    All the corresponding periodic orbits are contractible.

    The construction combines a Hamiltonian involution with integral homology torsion at two different primes; its proof uses finite-dimensional Morse theory and complex blowups.

  4. Morse 数形式的 Arnold 下界的一个非退化反例

    A nondegenerate counterexample to the Morse-number Arnold bound

    摘要

    我们在一个闭辛十二维流形上构造了一个光滑的周期为一的 Hamilton 函数，其时间一映射的不动点数小于该流形的通常 Morse 数。

    每个不动点均非退化，且具有可缩的 Hamilton 轨迹。

    这否定了 Arnold 猜想的 Morse 数形式。

    在十二维例子中，缺额可以任意大。

    英文原文

    We construct a smooth one-periodic Hamiltonian on a closed symplectic twelve-manifold whose time-one map has fewer fixed points than the manifold's ordinary Morse number.

    Every fixed point is nondegenerate and has a contractible Hamiltonian trajectory.

    This disproves the Morse-number form of the Arnold conjecture.

    The deficit can be arbitrarily large among twelve-dimensional examples.

  5. 带辛结构的复三维二次超曲面上的三个不动点

    Three fixed points on the symplectic quadric threefold

    主结果已列入形式化清单
    摘要

    我们构造了复二次超曲面三维簇的一个光滑 Hamilton 微分同胚，它恰有三个不动点，其中至少一个是退化的。

    该流形的临界数和包含单位元的有理系数杯积长度都为四。

    因此，这个例子否定了 Arnold 猜想在不加限制情形下的临界数形式和有理系数杯积长度形式。

    英文原文

    We construct a smooth Hamiltonian diffeomorphism of the complex quadric threefold with exactly three fixed points, at least one of which is degenerate.

    The critical number and the unit-inclusive rational cup length of this manifold are both four.

    Thus the example disproves the unrestricted critical-number and rational cup-length forms of the Arnold conjecture.

# Counterexamples to stable-Morse and strong Arnold fixed-point bounds

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Three fixed points on the symplectic quadric threefold](../../preprints/A-degenerate-counterexample-to-the-critical-number-Arnold-bound-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The critical-number form of Arnold's fixed-point conjecture predicts at least as many fixed points as the minimum number of critical points of a smooth function. The formalized counterexample is a Hamiltonian diffeomorphism of the complex quadric threefold with exactly three fixed points, while every smooth function on that manifold has at least four critical points. At least one fixed point is degenerate. The separate nondegenerate Morse-number construction is not included.
