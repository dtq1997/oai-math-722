Campana's orbifold Iitaka conjecture and logarithmic subadditivity

证明了带有有理简单正规交叉边界的光滑 Fujiki C\mathcal CC 类流形的 Campana 轨形 Iitaka 次可加性猜想。

对于光滑复拟射影簇之间的射影纤维化 f:U→Vf:U\to Vf:U→V，若其纤维连通、一般纤维为 FFF 且 κˉ(V)≥0\bar\kappa(V)\ge0κˉ(V)≥0，则证明 Popa 不等式 κˉ(U)≥κ(F)+max⁡{κˉ(V),Var⁡(f)}\bar\kappa(U)\ge\kappa(F)+\max\{\bar\kappa(V),\operatorname{Var}(f)\}κˉ(U)≥κ(F)+max{κˉ(V),Var(f)}，其中变异度衡量的是整个几何泛纤维的变化。

  1. 轨形与对数 Iitaka 次可加性

    Orbifold and logarithmic Iitaka subadditivity

    摘要

    对于 Fujiki 类 C\mathcal CC 中的紧流形上的有理简单正规交叉边界，我们证明了 Campana 的轨形 Iitaka 次可加性猜想，包括系数为一的情形。

    普通次可加性和对数次可加性随之成立。

    英文原文

    We prove Campana's orbifold Iitaka subadditivity conjecture for rational simple normal crossing boundaries on compact manifolds in Fujiki class C\mathcal CC, including coefficient one.

    Ordinary and logarithmic subadditivity follow.

  2. 对数 Kodaira 维数与整个纤维的变异度

    Logarithmic Kodaira dimension and whole-fiber variation

    摘要

    对于光滑复拟射影簇之间纤维连通的射影满态射，当基底的对数 Kodaira 维数非负时，我们证明了对数 Iitaka–Viehweg 不等式。

    变异度衡量的是整个几何泛纤维的双有理定义域。

    这肯定地解决了 Popa 的对数变异度猜想。

    英文原文

    We prove the logarithmic Iitaka–Viehweg inequality for projective surjective morphisms with connected fibers between smooth complex quasi-projective varieties whose base has nonnegative logarithmic Kodaira dimension.

    The variation measures the birational field of definition of the whole geometric generic fiber.

    This resolves Popa's logarithmic variation conjecture positively.

# Iitaka subadditivity, variation, and logarithmic additivity

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [The reverse logarithmic Kodaira inequality and additivity](../../preprints/The-reverse-logarithmic-Kodaira-inequality-and-additivity-September-26-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The paper studies logarithmic Kodaira additivity for connected-fiber morphisms of smooth projective reduced simple-normal-crossing pairs that are smooth on all boundary strata away from the base boundary. The linked formalization proves the negative-fiber branch: for a very general base point, if the logarithmic Kodaira dimension of the fiber is $-\infty$, then the total logarithmic Kodaira dimension equals the sum of the base and fiber dimensions and is $-\infty$. Every positive-degree logarithmic pluriform section on the total space then vanishes.

The finite-dimension and negative-base branches of the paper's additivity theorem are outside this selected statement.
