极小整数群作用的 Phillips–Toms 公式

The Phillips–Toms formula for minimal integer actions

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

对于无限紧致可度量空间 XXX 的每个极小同胚 hhh，C(X)⋊hZC(X)\rtimes_h\mathbb ZC(X)⋊h​Z 的比较半径等于 12mdim(X,h)\tfrac12\mathrm{mdim}(X,h)21​mdim(X,h)，也包括取无限值的情形。

平均维数为零与小边界性质、Jiang–Su 稳定性及有限核维数等价；在这种情况下，核维数至多为一。

英文原文

For every minimal homeomorphism hhh of an infinite compact metrizable space XXX, the radius of comparison of C(X)⋊hZC(X)\rtimes_h\mathbb ZC(X)⋊h​Z equals 12mdim(X,h)\tfrac12\mathrm{mdim}(X,h)21​mdim(X,h), including infinite values.

Zero mean dimension is equivalent to the small boundary property, Jiang–Su stability and finite nuclear dimension; in this case nuclear dimension is at most one.

论文

  1. 复配边理论中的滤过乘积与保边界压缩

    Filtered products and boundary-preserving compression in complex cobordism

    摘要

    我们证明，在充分大的 smash 幂中，若到某个有限带基点子复形的限制在 MUMUMU 意义下为零，则它在受限制因子占某个固定正比例的各乘积的整个并集上稳定零伦。

    这一相干消失定理给出每个紧可度量输入空间上的立方体值映射的保边界压缩。

    当环面槽位丛连续嵌入一个秩小于源丛秩两倍的平凡丛时，该压缩在任意大的幂中，将正比例的槽位置于各自边界上，并精确固定每个原始边界槽位。

    等号情形的一个例子表明，这个严格的秩不等式不能去掉。

    英文原文

    We prove that, in sufficiently large smash powers, an MUMUMU-null restriction to a finite pointed subcomplex becomes stably null on the entire union of products with a fixed positive proportion of restricted factors.

    This coherent vanishing theorem yields boundary-preserving compression of cube-valued maps on every compact metrizable input space.

    When the torus slot bundle embeds continuously into a trivial bundle of rank less than twice the source rank, the compression places a positive proportion of slots on their boundaries in arbitrarily large powers and fixes every original boundary slot exactly.

    An example at equality shows that the strict rank inequality cannot be removed.

  2. 比较半径等于平均维数的一半

    Radius of comparison equals half the mean dimension

    摘要

    我们证明 Phillips–Toms 猜想的整数群作用情形：对无限紧可度量空间的每个极小同胚，其交叉积的比较半径等于其平均维数的一半，包括两者同为无穷的情形。

    对这些系统，零平均维数、小边界性质、交叉积的 Jiang–Su 稳定性以及交叉积的有限核维数这四个条件彼此等价；在此情形下，其核维数至多为一。

    英文原文

    We prove the integer-action case of the Phillips–Toms conjecture: for every minimal homeomorphism of an infinite compact metrizable space, the radius of comparison of its crossed product equals one half of its mean dimension, including equality at infinity.

    For these systems, zero mean dimension and the small boundary property are equivalent to Jiang–Su stability and to finite nuclear dimension of the crossed product; in this case its nuclear dimension is at most one.
