球面及三流形的无穷多闭测地线

Infinitely many closed geodesics on spheres and three-manifolds

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

证明了 SnS^nSn 上的每个光滑 Riemann 度量，在 n≥2n\ge2n≥2 时，都有无穷多条像两两不同的素闭测地线。

同一结论也对每个容许有限光滑球面覆盖的闭流形，以及每个闭三维流形成立，无须定向性或非退化性限制。

英文原文

Proves that every smooth Riemannian metric on SnS^nSn, n≥2n\ge2n≥2, has infinitely many prime closed geodesics with pairwise distinct images.

The same conclusion holds on every closed manifold admitting a finite smooth spherical cover and on every closed three-manifold, without orientability or nondegeneracy restrictions.

论文

  1. 每个带 Riemann 度量的球面上都有无穷多个闭测地线像

    Infinitely many closed geodesic images on every Riemannian sphere

    摘要

    我们解决了闭测地线无穷性问题的球面情形：标准球面 Sn（n ≥ 2）上的每个光滑 Riemann 度量都有无穷多条像两两不同的素闭测地线。

    二维球面的情形是经典结果；高维结果包括退化度量。

    利用有限覆盖以及 Perelman 和 Rademacher–Taimanov 的定理，我们还对非空闭光滑三维流形上的每个光滑 Riemann 度量得到了相同结论。

    英文原文

    We resolve the sphere case of the closed-geodesic infinitude problem: every smooth Riemannian metric on the standard sphere Sn, n ≥ 2, has infinitely many prime closed geodesics with pairwise distinct images.

    The two-sphere case is classical; the result in higher dimensions includes degenerate metrics.

    Using finite covers and theorems of Perelman and Rademacher–Taimanov, we also obtain the same conclusion for every smooth Riemannian metric on a nonempty closed smooth three-manifold.
