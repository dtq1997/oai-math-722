Razak–Jacelon 稳定化后的迹锥分类

Classification by trace cones after Razak–Jacelon stabilization

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

利用全体扩展下半连续迹权所构成的拓扑锥，对与 Razak–Jacelon 代数及紧算子代数取张量积后的可分核复 C∗C^*C∗ 代数进行了分类。

这回答了 Robert 的迹锥问题，其中包括具有任意理想结构以及有限和无限子商的代数。

英文原文

Classifies separable nuclear complex C∗C^*C∗-algebras after tensoring with the Razak–Jacelon algebra and the compact operators, using their full topological cones of extended lower-semicontinuous tracial weights.

This answers Robert’s trace-cone question, including algebras with arbitrary ideal structure and both finite and infinite subquotients.

OpenAI · overview.tex

论文

  1. 迹锥对 Razak–Jacelon 稳定化的分类

    The trace cone classifies Razak–Jacelon stabilizations

    摘要

    我们证明，所有扩展下半连续迹权组成的典范拓扑锥，决定了可分核 C∗ 代数与 Razak–Jacelon 代数及紧算子代数取张量积后的代数，从而肯定地回答了 Robert 的迹锥分类问题。

    该同构实现预先指定的锥映射，允许任意理想结构，且无须对各权取有限值的定义域作稠密性假设。

    英文原文

    We prove that the canonical topological cone of all extended lower-semicontinuous tracial weights determines a separable nuclear C∗-algebra after tensoring with the Razak–Jacelon algebra and the compact operators, answering Robert's trace-cone classification question positively.

    The isomorphism realizes the prescribed cone map, with arbitrary ideal structure and without a density assumption on the finite domains of the weights.
