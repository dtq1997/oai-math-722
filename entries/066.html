Fano 收缩的有界 klt 补

Bounded klt complements for Fano contractions

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

对于反对数典范除子为 nef 的复 Fano 型 ϵ\epsilonϵ-lc 对，证明了 Shokurov 有界 klt 补猜想的有限有理系数形式。

对于任意特征零代数闭域上的 ϵ\epsilonϵ-lc Fano 收缩，在每个底点附近给出 klt 补，其指标仅由维数及正有理数 ϵ\epsilonϵ 控制。

英文原文

Proves the finite-rational-coefficient form of Shokurov’s bounded-klt-complement conjecture for ϵ\epsilonϵ-lc complex Fano-type pairs with nef anti-log-canonical divisor.

For ϵ\epsilonϵ-lc Fano contractions over any algebraically closed characteristic-zero field, it gives klt complements near every base point, with index bounded only by dimension and positive rational ϵ\epsilonϵ.

论文

  1. Fano 收缩的有界 klt 补

    Bounded klt complements for Fano contractions

    摘要

    对于固定的维数 d 和正有理数 ϵ，我们证明，特征零代数闭域上的每个 ϵ-对数典范 Fano 收缩，在底空间的每个闭点附近都存在一个 klt 补，其指标仅由 d 和 ϵ 控制。

    在 ℂ 上，我们还对于反对数典范除子为 nef、系数属于某个固定有限有理数集的 Fano 型对，得到了单调 klt 补。

    这证明了 Shokurov 有界 klt 补猜想的有限有理系数形式。

    英文原文

    For fixed dimension d and positive rational ϵ, we prove that every ϵ-log-canonical Fano contraction over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero admits, near each closed base point, a klt complement of index bounded only by d and ϵ.

    Over ℂ, we also obtain monotone klt complements for Fano type pairs with nef anti-log-canonical divisor and coefficients in a fixed finite rational set.

    This proves the finite-rational-coefficient form of Shokurov's bounded-klt-complement conjecture.

  2. Fano 型收缩的一致 Cartier 截面

    Uniform Cartier sections for Fano type contractions

    摘要

    我们证明了 Birkar 和 Shokurov 的 Cartier 除子猜想，分别涵盖特征零时的有理边界和 ℂ 上的实边界。

    对于底空间为正维、负对数典范类为 nef 的 ϵ-lc Fano 型收缩，可以选取一个经过任意指定底空间点的 Cartier 除子，使其拉回的对数典范阈值具有仅依赖于维数和 ϵ 的下界。

    英文原文

    We prove the Cartier-divisor conjecture of Birkar and Shokurov for rational boundaries in characteristic zero and for real boundaries over ℂ.

    For an ϵ-lc Fano type contraction with positive-dimensional base and nef negative log canonical class, a Cartier divisor through any prescribed base point can be chosen with pullback log canonical threshold bounded below in terms of the dimension and ϵ alone.
