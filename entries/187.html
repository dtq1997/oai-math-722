Snaky 的 21 步 Maker 策略

Snaky in 21 Maker moves

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

解决了 Snaky 成形问题：在起初为空的无限方格棋盘上，Maker 能强制在自己的 21 步之内形成六格 Snaky 形状。

Maker 先手，每位玩家每回合占据一个空格，该形状的平移、旋转和反射都算获胜。

英文原文

Settles the Snaky achievement problem: Maker can force the six-cell Snaky shape within 21 of its own moves on the initially empty infinite square board.

Maker moves first, each player claims one free cell per turn, and translations, rotations and reflections count as wins.

  1. Maker 在 21 次落子内完成 Snaky

    Snaky in 21 Maker moves

    摘要

    我们证明，在初始为空的无穷方格棋盘上，面对 Breaker 的任意合法走法，Maker 都能在实际进行的 21 次 Maker 落子之内完成 Snaky 六连方。

    同样的界在一个 17×1717\times1717×17 方形棋盘上也成立；事实上，Maker 可以将自己占据的格子限制在一个固定的 251 格棋盘内。

    英文原文

    We prove that Maker can achieve the Snaky hexomino within 21 actual Maker moves against arbitrary legal Breaker play on the initially empty infinite square board.

    The same bound holds on a 17×1717\times1717×17 square; in fact, Maker can confine its claims to a fixed 251-cell board.

# Snaky in 21 Maker moves

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Snaky in 21 Maker moves](../../preprints/Snaky-in-21-Maker-moves-September-25-2026/article.pdf)

## Scope

In the Snaky Maker–Breaker game, the players alternately claim cells of $\mathbb Z^2$, and Maker seeks a translated, rotated, or reflected copy of the six-cell Snaky shape. The formalization gives a legal strategy that wins within $21$ actual Maker moves against every legal Breaker play from the empty infinite board. The selected theorem checks disjointness and the move counts throughout play; it does not impose the paper's smaller finite-board restriction.

The linked supplements reconstruct the older $35$-move appendix's finite recursive certificate and prove four conditional winning templates from partial positions, assuming the required Maker cells are present and Breaker avoids the corresponding finite envelope.
