Voronoi 渗流的临界与近临界普适性

Critical and near-critical universality for Voronoi percolation

OpenAI · 2026-10-063 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

证明了每个有界 Jordan 四边形中退火临界 Poisson–Voronoi 穿越概率的 Cardy 公式。

将每个模型按其自身单位正方形中的枢轴点数期望归一化后，有理多边形 quads的条件联合近临界穿越阈值分布，关于环境依概率收敛到三角格参考分布。

这就建立了穿越阈值的淬火近临界普适性。

英文原文

Proves Cardy's formula for annealed critical Poisson–Voronoi crossing probabilities in every bounded Jordan quadrilateral.

With each model normalized by its own expected unit-square pivotal count, the conditional joint near-critical crossing-threshold laws for rational polygonal quads converge in environment probability to the triangular-lattice reference law.

This establishes quenched near-critical universality for crossing thresholds.

  1. 从临界穿越到 Voronoi 渗流的淬火近临界普适性

    From critical crossings to quenched near-critical universality in Voronoi percolation

    摘要

    以临界 Poisson–Voronoi 渗流的 Cardy 穿越公式作为输入结果，我们证明了在单调耦合下，有理多边形四边区域的近临界穿越阈值存在普适联合极限。

    以 Poisson 剖分为条件，阈值分布对剖分依概率收敛于与三角格情形相同的分布。

    每个模型均以其自身单位正方形穿越事件的颜色枢纽点数的期望进行归一化。

    英文原文

    Taking Cardy's crossing formula for critical Poisson–Voronoi percolation as an input, we prove a universal joint limit for the near-critical crossing thresholds of rational polygonal quadrilaterals under the monotone coupling.

    Conditional on the Poisson tessellation, the threshold law converges in probability over tessellations to the same law as on the triangular lattice.

    Each model is normalized by its own expected number of color-pivotal sites for a unit-square crossing.

  2. 由 Cardy 公式得到的 Voronoi 渗流关键点振幅

    A Pivotal Amplitude for Voronoi Percolation from Cardy's Formula

    摘要

    以每个有界 Jordan 四边形中临界平面 Poisson–Voronoi 渗流的 Cardy 共形穿越公式作为输入结果，我们证明了单位正方形穿越事件的颜色枢纽胞腔数的期望，渐近等于一个正常数乘以 ε−3/4\varepsilon ^{-3/4}ε−3/4，其中点强度为 ε−2\varepsilon ^{-2}ε−2。

    不要求 Cardy 公式中的收敛具有任何速率。

    英文原文

    Taking Cardy's conformal crossing formula for critical planar Poisson–Voronoi percolation in every bounded Jordan quadrilateral as an input, we prove that the expected number of color-pivotal cells for a unit-square crossing is asymptotic to a positive constant times ε−3/4\varepsilon ^{-3/4}ε−3/4, where the point intensity is ε−2\varepsilon ^{-2}ε−2.

    No rate of convergence in Cardy's formula is required.

  3. 临界 Poisson–Voronoi 渗流的 Cardy 公式

    Cardy’s formula for critical Poisson–Voronoi percolation

    摘要

    我们证明了每个有界 Jordan 四边形中临界平面 Poisson–Voronoi 渗流的退火穿越概率满足 Cardy 公式。

    这证明了该模型共形不变性猜想的退火穿越概率形式。

    英文原文

    We prove Cardy's formula for annealed crossing probabilities in critical planar Poisson–Voronoi percolation in every bounded Jordan quadrilateral.

    This proves the annealed crossing-probability form of the conformal-invariance conjecture for this model.
