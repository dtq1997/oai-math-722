受限几何 Langlands、Ramanujan 与 Arthur 参数

Restricted geometric Langlands, generic Ramanujan and Arthur parameters

OpenAI · 2026-10-068 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

在所列的四个 Lie 理论特征假设下，证明了 F‾q\overline{\mathbb F}_qFq​ 上光滑射影连通曲线的连通约化群所对应的受限几何 Langlands 等价。

在任意特征为 p>0p>0p>0 的代数闭域上，若另假设 ppp 对该群是非常好的素数且 p∤∣WG∣p\nmid |W_G|p∤∣WG​∣，则同样的结论成立。

在整体函数域上，对于分裂、伴随型、绝对单的例外群的整体 generic 尖点表示，证明其在每个地方都满足 Ramanujan 猜想，不限制特征或分歧深度；对于具有 generic 非分歧分量的分裂、伴随型、绝对单群的尖点表示，则证明其在每个非分歧地方都满足该猜想。

假设有限水平的 Ramanujan–Arthur 分解成立，则对分裂连通半单群，在有限全水平下构造实际出现的尖点 excursion 参数的整体 Arthur 增强；在整个 Weil 群（包括惯性群）上作对角特化，即恢复给定参数。

英文原文

Proves the restricted geometric Langlands equivalence for connected reductive groups on smooth projective connected curves over F‾q\overline{\mathbb F}_qFq​ under the four stated Lie-theoretic characteristic hypotheses.

Over arbitrary algebraically closed fields of characteristic p>0p>0p>0, the same conclusion holds assuming additionally that ppp is very good for the group and p∤∣WG∣p\nmid |W_G|p∤∣WG​∣.

Over global function fields, proves Ramanujan at every place for globally generic cuspidal representations of split adjoint absolutely simple exceptional groups, without characteristic or ramification-depth restrictions, and at every unramified place for cuspidal representations of split adjoint absolutely simple groups with a generic unramified component.

Assuming the finite-level Ramanujan–Arthur decomposition, constructs global Arthur enhancements of occurring cuspidal excursion parameters for split connected semisimple groups at full finite level, recovering the given parameters by diagonal specialization on the entire Weil group, including inertia.

论文

  1. 尖点 excursion 参数的整体 Arthur 增强

    Global Arthur Enhancements of Cuspidal Excursion Parameters

    摘要

    在定理 1.1 所述有限水平 Ramanujan–Arthur 分解的假设下，对于整体函数域上的分裂连通半单群，我们为在指定有限全水平上出现的每个尖点 excursion 参数构造一个整体 Arthur 增强。

    一个代数 SL2 与一个和它交换的 Weil 中心化子映射，通过在整个 Weil 群上作对角特化恢复给定参数，其中包括惯性群。

    该结果并不声称椭圆性、Arthur 包分类或重数公式。

    英文原文

    Under the finite-level Ramanujan–Arthur decomposition stated in Theorem 1.1, we construct a global Arthur enhancement for every occurring cuspidal excursion parameter at a specified full finite level for a split connected semisimple group over a global function field.

    A single algebraic SL2 and a commuting Weil centralizer map recover the given parameter by diagonal specialization on the entire Weil group, including inertia.

    The result does not assert ellipticity, Arthur-packet classification, or a multiplicity formula.

  2. 典范非分歧 Arthur 滤过的有理性

    Rationality of the Canonical Unramified Arthur Filtration

    摘要

    在受限几何 Langlands 理论的特征假设下，我们证明，对于分裂连通半单群，有限支撑非分歧自守函数上的典范 Arthur 滤过在 ℚ 上有定义。

    该结果涵盖非尖点部分和每个闭的不变幂零支撑，从而确立了这一情形下 Gaitsgory–Lafforgue–Raskin 的有理性猜想。

    英文原文

    Under the characteristic hypotheses of restricted geometric Langlands theory, we prove that the canonical Arthur filtration on finitely supported unramified automorphic functions is defined over ℚ for split connected semisimple groups.

    The result includes the noncuspidal part and every closed invariant nilpotent support, establishing the rationality conjecture of Gaitsgory–Lafforgue–Raskin in this setting.

  3. 有限全水平下尖点函数的 Ramanujan–Arthur 分解

    Ramanujan-Arthur Decompositions of Cuspidal Functions at Full Finite Level

    摘要

    我们证明，整体函数域上的分裂半单群的尖点自守函数具有有理分解和 Q‾ℓ\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ellQ​ℓ​ 分解，其分量为由对偶群的幂零轨道索引的子空间。

    这些分解在每个有限全水平上都成立，并允许任意除子重数。

    每个直和项在每个非分歧地方和每个复嵌入处都由同一个轨道控制。

    在平凡水平下，这证明了 Gaitsgory–Lafforgue–Raskin 的猜想 3.4.5 和 3.4.6。

    对于分裂、连通、伴随、绝对单群，我们还证明，若一个尖点自守表示有一个 generic 的非分歧局部分量，则它在每个非分歧地方都是缓增的。

    因此，这一范围内的整体 generic 尖点表示满足广义 Ramanujan 猜想的非分歧部分。

    英文原文

    We prove rational and Q‾ℓ\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ellQ​ℓ​ decompositions of cuspidal automorphic functions for split semisimple groups over global function fields into subspaces indexed by nilpotent orbits of the dual group.

    The decompositions hold at every full finite level, with arbitrary divisor multiplicities.

    Each summand is governed by the same orbit at every unramified place and every complex embedding.

    At trivial level this proves Conjectures 3.4.5 and 3.4.6 of Gaitsgory–Lafforgue–Raskin.

    For split connected adjoint absolutely simple groups, we also prove that a cuspidal automorphic representation with one generic unramified local component is tempered at every unramified place.

    Thus globally generic cuspidal representations in this scope satisfy the unramified part of the generalized Ramanujan conjecture.

  4. 例外群的整体 generic 表示在分歧地方的缓增性

    Temperedness at ramified places for globally generic exceptional groups

    摘要

    我们对整体函数域上的分裂连通伴随例外群的整体 generic 尖点自守表示证明了广义 Ramanujan 猜想。

    每个局部分量都是缓增的，且对特征或分歧深度没有限制。

    该证明将配套论文中的非分歧 Ramanujan 定理推广到了所有分歧地方。

    英文原文

    We prove the generalized Ramanujan conjecture for globally generic cuspidal automorphic representations of split connected adjoint exceptional groups over global function fields.

    Every local component is tempered, without restrictions on characteristic or ramification depth.

    The proof extends the unramified Ramanujan theorem of a companion paper to all ramified places.

  5. 正特征下的受限几何 Langlands 等价

    The Restricted Geometric Langlands Equivalence in Positive Characteristic

    摘要

    对于特征 p > 0 下的光滑射影连通曲线及连通约化群，在 ℓ ≠ p 时，我们在两种情形下证明了 Q‾ℓ\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ellQ​ℓ​ 线性的受限几何 Langlands 等价。

    在 F‾q\overline{\mathbb F}_qFq​ 上，我们假设下文所述受限理论的四个特征条件成立。

    在任意代数闭域上，我们假设这些条件成立，并进一步假设 p 是 very good 素数且不整除 Weyl 群的阶。

    这证明了这些情形下 Gaitsgory–Raskin 的全支撑猜想 [[12, Conjecture 1.3.10]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.02237v1)。

    英文原文

    We prove the Q‾ℓ\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ellQ​ℓ​-linear restricted geometric Langlands equivalence for smooth projective connected curves and connected reductive groups in characteristic p > 0, with ℓ ≠ p, in two regimes.

    Over F‾q\overline{\mathbb F}_qFq​, we assume the four characteristic conditions of the restricted theory stated below.

    Over arbitrary algebraically closed fields, we assume these conditions and additionally that p is very good and does not divide the Weyl-group order.

    This proves Gaitsgory–Raskin's full-support conjecture [[12, Conjecture 1.3.10]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.02237v1) in these regimes.

  6. 正特征下可构造的驯 Hecke 特征层

    Constructible tame Hecke eigensheaves in positive characteristic

    摘要

    在有限域的代数闭包上一条亏格至少为二的光滑射影曲线上，我们构造了非零的局部可构造反常 Hecke 特征层，并在一个标记点处带有 Borel 水平结构。

    其参数是一个 Zariski 稠密的几何 ℓ 进局部系统，具有驯的正则幺幂单值性。

    该群为单且单连通的，并满足四个明确的 Lie 理论特征假设。

    这些特征层具有抛物幂零奇异支撑，其特征同构与张量积、置换和融合相容。

    英文原文

    We construct nonzero locally constructible perverse Hecke eigensheaves with Borel level at one marked point on a smooth projective curve of genus at least two over an algebraic closure of a finite field.

    The parameter is a Zariski-dense geometric ℓ-adic local system with tame regular-unipotent monodromy.

    The group is simple and simply connected and satisfies four explicit Lie-theoretic characteristic hypotheses.

    The eigensheaves have parabolic nilpotent singular support, and their eigenisomorphisms are compatible with tensor products, permutations, and fusion.

  7. 具有多个标记点的驯 Hecke 特征层

    Tame Hecke Eigensheaves with Several Marked Points

    摘要

    对于特征为 p > n 的 SLn，在有限域的代数闭包上一条亏格至少为二的光滑射影曲线上，我们构造了非零的局部可构造反常 Hecke 特征层，并在两个或更多标记点处带有 Borel 水平结构。

    其参数是一个 Zariski 稠密的几何 ℓ 进 PGLn 局部系统，具有幺幂驯单值性；其幂零对数可以具有任意 Jordan 型，包括零。

    这些特征层具有抛物幂零奇异支撑，且其特征同构保留完整的张量和融合结构。

    英文原文

    For SLn in characteristic p > n, we construct nonzero locally constructible perverse Hecke eigensheaves with Borel level at two or more marked points on a smooth projective curve of genus at least two over an algebraic closure of a finite field.

    The parameter is a Zariski-dense geometric ℓ-adic PGLn-local system with unipotent tame monodromy; its nilpotent logarithms may have any Jordan type, including zero.

    The eigensheaves have parabolic nilpotent singular support, and their eigenisomorphisms retain the full tensor and fusion structure.

  8. 驯 Hecke 特征层上的 Frobenius 结构

    Frobenius Structures on Tame Hecke Eigensheaves

    摘要

    经过常数域的一个有限扩张后，我们为 SLn 构造了非零的反常 Weil Hecke 特征层，并在一个标记点处带有 Borel 水平结构。

    其参数是特征为 p > n 的有限域上一条亏格至少为二、去掉一个点的曲线上的算术 PGLn 局部系统，该局部系统几何稠密，并具有驯的正则幺幂单值性。

    完整的多腿特征结构与 Frobenius 相容，并具有指定的算术特征值，且几何层具有抛物幂零奇异支撑。

    英文原文

    We construct nonzero perverse Weil Hecke eigensheaves for SLn with Borel level at one marked point, after a finite extension of the field of constants.

    The parameter is a geometrically dense arithmetic PGLn-local system with tame regular-unipotent monodromy on a once-punctured curve of genus at least two over a finite field of characteristic p > n.

    The full multi-leg eigenstructure is Frobenius compatible with the prescribed arithmetic eigenvalue, and the geometric sheaf has parabolic nilpotent singular support.
