无半正定提升的双曲锥

Hyperbolicity cones without semidefinite lifts

首发 · 2026-10-063 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

否定了投影 Lax 猜想：存在不是 spectrahedral shadow 的双曲锥。

无论使用多少辅助变量或何种实系数，这些例子都不具有精确的有限仿射半正定提升。

这也否定了声称每个双曲锥都是 spectrahedral 的广义 Lax 猜想。

英文原文

Disproves the Projected Lax conjecture: some hyperbolicity cones are not spectrahedral shadows.

The examples admit no exact finite affine semidefinite lift, regardless of the number of auxiliary variables or the real coefficients used.

This also disproves the generalized Lax conjecture that every hyperbolicity cone is spectrahedral.

  1. 不存在半正定提升的双曲锥

    Hyperbolicity Cones Without Semidefinite Lifts

    摘要

    我们证明，并非每个双曲锥都是 spectrahedral shadow：某些闭双曲锥不存在有限仿射半正定提升，即使允许任意实系数和任意有限多个辅助变量也不例外。

    这否定了投影 Lax 猜想，因而也否定了广义 Lax 猜想。

    英文原文

    We prove that not every hyperbolicity cone is a spectrahedral shadow: some closed hyperbolicity cones admit no finite affine semidefinite lift, even with arbitrary real coefficients and any finite number of auxiliary variables.

    This disproves the Projected Lax Conjecture and hence the generalized Lax conjecture.

  2. 一个非谱面体双曲锥

    A nonspectrahedral hyperbolicity cone

    已列入形式化清单
    摘要

    我们构造一个具有 23 个实变量的 16 次齐次多项式，其双曲锥不能由有限齐次实对称线性矩阵不等式表示。

    英文原文

  3. 一个非谱面体双曲锥的精确半正定提升

    An Exact Semidefinite Lift of a Nonspectrahedral Hyperbolicity Cone

    摘要

    我们为配套论文中定义的那个显式的、具有二十三个变量的非谱面体双曲锥构造一个精确半正定提升。

    该提升是一个大小为 100、带有 307 个辅助变量的齐次实对称矩阵束，表示整个闭锥，包括 X 奇异的每一点。

    因此，尽管该锥在其原坐标中没有半正定表示，但允许辅助变量时就存在这样的表示。

    我们不声称所给出的大小是最小的。

    英文原文

    We construct an exact semidefinite lift of the explicit nonspectrahedral hyperbolicity cone in twenty-three variables defined in the companion paper.

    The lift is a homogeneous real symmetric pencil of size 100 with 307 auxiliary variables and represents the entire closed cone, including every point with singular X.

    Thus, although this cone has no semidefinite representation in its original coordinates, it admits one when auxiliary variables are allowed.

    The stated sizes are not claimed to be minimal.

# Hyperbolicity cones without semidefinite lifts

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [A nonspectrahedral hyperbolicity cone](../../preprints/A-Nonspectrahedral-Hyperbolicity-Cone-September-24-2026/nonspectrahedral-hyperbolicity-cone.pdf)

## Scope

The generalized Lax conjecture predicts that every hyperbolicity cone is spectrahedral. The formalized counterexample is an explicit homogeneous polynomial of degree $20$ in $23$ real variables. It is hyperbolic, but its closed hyperbolicity cone cannot be represented as the positive-semidefinite region of any finite real symmetric linear matrix pencil, in any positive matrix size.
