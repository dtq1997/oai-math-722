Shareshian–Wachs 的 e 正性猜想

The Shareshian–Wachs eee-positivity conjecture

首发 · 2026-10-061 篇论文全部形式化

解决了 Shareshian–Wachs 猜想中的初等正性部分：每个自然单位区间图的色拟对称函数在初等对称函数基下的系数都属于 N[q]\mathbb N[q]N[q]。

这些系数计数的是一类有明确描述的排列，从而给出了正性的组合解释。

英文原文

Resolves the elementary-positivity part of the Shareshian–Wachs conjecture: the chromatic quasisymmetric function of every natural unit interval graph has elementary-basis coefficients in N[q]\mathbb N[q]N[q].

The coefficients count explicitly described permutations, giving a combinatorial explanation of positivity.

  1. 色拟对称函数在初等对称函数基下的正性

    Elementary positivity of chromatic quasisymmetric functions

    摘要

    我们证明，每个自然单位区间图的色拟对称函数在 N[q]\mathbb N[q]N[q] 上都是初等正的。

    这解决了 Shareshian–Wachs 猜想中的初等正性部分。

    英文原文

    We prove that the chromatic quasisymmetric function of every natural unit interval graph is elementary-positive over N[q]\mathbb N[q]N[q].

    This resolves the elementary-positivity part of the Shareshian–Wachs conjecture.

# Shareshian–Wachs elementary positivity

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Elementary positivity of chromatic quasisymmetric functions](../../preprints/Elementary-Positivity-of-Chromatic-Quasisymmetric-Functions-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The elementary-positivity part of the Shareshian–Wachs conjecture asks whether the chromatic quasisymmetric function of every natural unit interval graph has nonnegative coefficients in the elementary basis. The formalization proves this over $\mathbb N[q]$. It constructs an explicit elementary-basis expansion indexed by the graph's permitted nondescent permutations, with each term weighted by a nonnegative power of $q$ determined by its graph inversions. The expansion holds for every finite number of color variables.
