谱标量曲率与余维二宽度

Spectral scalar curvature and codimension-two width

OpenAI · 2026-10-063 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

每个完备连通光滑无边界的 nnn 维流形，在 n≥3n\ge3n≥3 且以二次型不等式的意义满足 −4Δ+Scal≥1-4\Delta+\mathrm{Scal}\ge1−4Δ+Scal≥1 时，都容许一个到维数至多为 n−2n-2n−2 的单纯复形的连续映射，其每个完整纤维在原有度量下的直径都有仅依赖于 nnn 的上界。

这将 Gromov 的宽度结论加强到了谱标量曲率情形。

对每个 n≥2n\ge2n≥2，闭正标量曲率流形的万有覆盖的连续宏观维数也至多为 n−2n-2n−2。

英文原文

Every complete connected smooth boundaryless nnn-manifold, n≥3n\ge3n≥3, satisfying −4Δ+Scal≥1-4\Delta+\mathrm{Scal}\ge1−4Δ+Scal≥1 as a quadratic-form inequality admits a continuous map to a simplicial complex of dimension at most n−2n-2n−2 whose entire fibers have diameter bounded only by nnn in the original metric.

This strengthens Gromov's width conclusion to spectral scalar curvature.

Universal covers of closed positive-scalar-curvature manifolds also have continuous macroscopic dimension at most n−2n-2n−2 for every n≥2n\ge2n≥2.

论文

  1. 谱标量曲率与一致 Urysohn 宽度

    Spectral scalar curvature and uniform Urysohn width

    摘要

    对于每个 n ≥ 4，若一个完备连通光滑无边界 Riemann n 维流形以二次型不等式的意义满足 −4Δ+Scal≥1-4\Delta+\mathop{\mathrm{Scal}}\nolimits \ge1−4Δ+Scal≥1，则它具有到一个维数至多为 n−2n-2n−2 的单纯复形的连续映射，其每个完整纤维的直径都有仅依赖于 n 的上界。

    该界是在原始度量下测量的。

    这将一致 Urysohn 宽度定理从逐点的标量曲率下界推广到了谱下界。

    英文原文

    For every n ≥ 4, a complete connected smooth Riemannian n-manifold without boundary satisfying −4Δ+Scal≥1-4\Delta+\mathop{\mathrm{Scal}}\nolimits \ge1−4Δ+Scal≥1 as a quadratic-form inequality admits a continuous map to a simplicial complex of dimension at most n−2n-2n−2 whose entire fibers have diameter bounded only in terms of n.

    The bound is measured in the original metric.

    This extends the uniform Urysohn width theorem from a pointwise scalar-curvature lower bound to a spectral lower bound.

  2. 三维中的谱标量曲率与 Urysohn 宽度

    Spectral scalar curvature and Urysohn width in dimension three

    摘要

    每个以二次型不等式的意义满足 −4Δ+Scal≥λ>0-4\Delta+\mathop{\mathrm{Scal}}\nolimits \ge\lambda\gt 0−4Δ+Scal≥λ>0 的连通完备光滑无边界三维 Riemann 流形，都具有到一个图的连续映射，其每个完整纤维在原始度量下的直径至多为 500/λ500/\sqrt\lambda500/λ​。

    无需可定向性、自旋结构、紧致性或有界几何假设。

    英文原文

    Every connected complete smooth Riemannian three-manifold without boundary satisfying −4Δ+Scal≥λ>0-4\Delta+\mathop{\mathrm{Scal}}\nolimits \ge\lambda\gt 0−4Δ+Scal≥λ>0 as a quadratic-form inequality admits a continuous map to a graph whose entire fibers have diameter at most 500/λ500/\sqrt\lambda500/λ​ in the original metric.

    No orientability, spin, compactness, or bounded-geometry assumption is required.

  3. 正标量曲率与一致的余维二宽度

    Positive scalar curvature and uniform codimension-two width

    摘要

    我们在每个维数 n ≥ 4 中证明了 Gromov 标量曲率猜想的定量连续形式。

    每个标量曲率至少为一的完备连通光滑无边界 n 维流形，都具有到一个维数至多为 n−2n-2n−2 的单纯复形的连续映射，其每个完整纤维的直径都有仅依赖于 n 的上界。

    我们还在每个维数 n ≥ 2 中，对具有正标量曲率的闭流形的万有覆盖得到了连续的宏观维数结论。

    英文原文

    We prove the quantitative continuous form of Gromov's scalar-curvature conjecture in every dimension n ≥ 4.

    Every complete connected smooth boundaryless n-manifold with scalar curvature at least one admits a continuous map to a simplicial complex of dimension at most n−2n-2n−2 whose entire fibers have diameter bounded only in terms of n.

    We also obtain the continuous macroscopic-dimension conclusion for universal covers of closed manifolds with positive scalar curvature in every dimension n ≥ 2.
