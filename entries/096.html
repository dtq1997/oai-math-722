高斯螺旋桨猜想

The Gaussian propeller conjecture

首发 · 2026-10-061 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

证明了任意有限可测分割的高斯一阶矩的平方和至多为 9/(8π)9/(8\pi)9/(8π)。

在至少二维的空间中，将三个角度为 2π/32\pi/32π/3 的平面扇形沿正交方向延伸，即可达到该界。

结合单独的唯一博弈定理，这证明了对固定的 k≥3k\ge3k≥3，在有理、中心化的半正定输入上，以单位矩阵为目标的核聚类若要改进损失因子 (8π/9)(1−1/k)(8\pi/9)(1-1/k)(8π/9)(1−1/k)，则是 NP-难的。

英文原文

Proves that the sum of squared Gaussian first moments of any finite measurable partition is at most 9/(8π)9/(8\pi)9/(8π).

In dimension at least two, three planar sectors of angle 2π/32\pi/32π/3, extended orthogonally, attain the bound.

Combined with the separate Unique Games theorem, this proves NP-hardness of improving the loss factor (8π/9)(1−1/k)(8\pi/9)(1-1/k)(8π/9)(1−1/k) for identity-target kernel clustering with fixed k≥3k\ge3k≥3 on rational centered positive semidefinite inputs.

    摘要

    我们证明高斯螺旋桨猜想：对于欧氏空间的每个有限可测划分，其各部分的高斯一阶矩的长度平方之和至多为 9/(8π)9/(8\pi)9/(8π)。

    当维数至少为二且划分至少有三个部分时，将三个角度为 2π/32\pi/32π/3 的平面扇形沿正交的欧氏因子延拓，便可达到该界。

    英文原文

    We prove the Gaussian propeller conjecture: for every finite measurable partition of a Euclidean space, the sum of the squared lengths of its Gaussian first moments is at most 9/(8π)9/(8\pi)9/(8π).

    For dimension at least two and at least three cells, three planar sectors of angle 2π/32\pi/32π/3, extended by an orthogonal Euclidean factor, attain the bound.

# The Gaussian propeller conjecture in every dimension

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [The Gaussian propeller bound in every dimension](../../preprints/The-Gaussian-Propeller-Bound-in-Every-Dimension-September-24-2026/main.pdf)

## Scope

The Gaussian propeller problem asks how large the sum of squared Gaussian first moments can be over a partition. The formalized result proves the sharp bound $9/(8\pi)$ for every positive dimension and every positive number of labelled cells, allowing empty cells and arbitrary masses. In dimension at least two with at least three cells, three planar sectors of angle $120^\circ$, extended in orthogonal directions, attain equality. The separate Gaussian-maxima and kernel-clustering consequences are not included.
