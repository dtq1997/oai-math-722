034 · 代数几何与复几何

对数丰沛性与有效 Iitaka 纤维化

Log abundance and effective Iitaka fibrations

OpenAI · 2026-10-0614 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

利用对数 Iitaka 次可加性，证明了任意维数、带有效有理边界的正规紧 Kähler 对数典范对的对数丰沛性：解析意义下 nef 的 Q\mathbb QQ-Cartier 伴随除子是半丰沛的。

还证明了每个特征零代数闭域上的射影对数丰沛性，以及该情形下 Kodaira 维数非负的光滑射影簇的有效 Iitaka 纤维化猜想。

进一步的结果解决了这些域上连通射影半对数典范对数 Calabi–Yau 对的有限有理系数指标猜想：固定维数及边界系数所属的有限有理数集后，存在不依赖于分支个数的一致指标。

英文原文

Using logarithmic Iitaka subadditivity, proves log abundance in every dimension for normal compact Kähler log canonical pairs with effective rational boundary: an analytically nef Q\mathbb QQ-Cartier adjoint is semiample.

It also proves projective log abundance over every algebraically closed field of characteristic zero and the effective Iitaka fibration conjecture for smooth projective varieties of nonnegative Kodaira dimension in that setting.

A further result resolves the finite-rational-coefficient index conjecture for connected projective semi-log-canonical log Calabi–Yau pairs over such fields in each fixed dimension and for each fixed finite set of rational boundary coefficients, with a uniform index independent of the number of components.

  1. 对数 Iitaka 次可加性假设下紧致 Kähler 空间的对数丰沛性

    Log abundance for compact Kähler spaces under logarithmic Iitaka subadditivity

    摘要

    假设对光滑射影复簇之间纤维连通、带有相容的约化简单正规交叉边界的满态射，对数 Iitaka 次可加性成立。

    我们证明了每个维数下正规不可约紧致 Kähler 空间的对数丰沛性：对于具有有效有理边界且 KX+ΔK_X+\DeltaKX​+Δ 为 ℚ-Cartier 的对数典范对 (X,Δ)(X,\Delta)(X,Δ)，KX+ΔK_X+\DeltaKX​+Δ 的解析 nef 性蕴含半丰沛性。

    英文原文

    Assume logarithmic Iitaka subadditivity for surjective morphisms with connected fibers between smooth projective complex varieties with compatible reduced simple normal crossing boundaries.

    We prove log abundance for normal irreducible compact Kähler spaces in every dimension: for a log canonical pair (X,Δ)(X,\Delta)(X,Δ) with effective rational boundary and KX+ΔK_X+\DeltaKX​+Δ ℚ-Cartier, analytic nefness of KX+ΔK_X+\DeltaKX​+Δ implies semiampleness.

  2. 半对数典范的对数 Calabi–Yau 对的一致指标

    Uniform indices for semi-log-canonical log Calabi–Yau pairs

    摘要

    在特征为零的代数闭域上，对于每个固定的至少为四的维数，我们证明了连通射影半对数典范的对数 Calabi–Yau 对的一致指标定理。

    当边界系数属于一个固定的有限有理数集时，对数典范除子的同一个倍数是 Cartier 的且线性平凡。

    这个倍数只依赖于维数和系数集，不依赖于不可约分支的数量。

    结合维数至多为三时已有的定理，这解决了有限有理系数的半对数典范指标猜想。

    英文原文

    We prove a uniform index theorem for connected projective semi-log-canonical log Calabi–Yau pairs in every fixed dimension at least four over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero.

    For boundary coefficients in a fixed finite rational set, a single multiple of the log canonical divisor is Cartier and linearly trivial.

    The multiple depends only on the dimension and coefficient set, not on the number of irreducible components.

    Together with the established theorem in dimensions at most three, this resolves the finite-rational-coefficient semi-log-canonical index conjecture.

  3. 紧致 Kähler 四维簇良极小模型存在性的条件性结果

    Conditional good minimal models for compact Kähler fourfolds

    摘要

    假设轨形 Iitaka 次可加性、指定的伪有效四维极小模型纲领，以及 Kodaira 维数非负的 nef 四维伴随除子的丰沛性成立，我们证明了整体强 ℚ-factorial 的紧致 Kähler klt 四维对的良极小模型存在性，其中边界为有效有理边界，实际的 ℚ-Cartier 伴随除子在解析意义下伪有效。

    额外的一步是非消失性。

    我们通过纤维化论证证明它；在代数维数为零时，则通过奇异度量、全纯叶状结构和从约化边界作延拓来证明。

    证明中使用的射影丰沛性论证已完整收入文中。

    英文原文

    Assuming orbifold Iitaka subadditivity, the specified pseudo-effective fourfold minimal model program, and abundance for nef fourfold adjoints of nonnegative Kodaira dimension, we prove the existence of good minimal models for globally strongly ℚ-factorial compact Kähler klt fourfold pairs with effective rational boundary and analytically pseudo-effective actual ℚ-Cartier adjoint.

    The additional step is nonvanishing.

    We prove it by fibration arguments and, in algebraic dimension zero, by singular metrics, holomorphic foliations, and extension from a reduced boundary.

    The projective abundance argument used in the proof is included in full.

  4. 特征为零时的对数丰沛性

    Log abundance in characteristic zero

    摘要

    我们证明了特征为零的代数闭域上每个维数下有理边界的对数丰沛性猜想：具有有效有理边界的射影对数典范对上的每个 nef ℚ-Cartier 对数典范除子都是半丰沛的。

    在 ℂ 上，该证明还确立了每个维数下光滑射影簇的典范非消失性。

    英文原文

    We prove the rational-boundary log abundance conjecture in every dimension over algebraically closed fields of characteristic zero: every nef ℚ-Cartier log canonical divisor on a projective log canonical pair with effective rational boundary is semiample.

    Over ℂ, the proof also establishes canonical nonvanishing for smooth projective varieties in every dimension.

  5. nef 伴随除子的极小度量与边界取内点时的单射性

    Minimal metrics and interior injectivity for nef adjoints

    摘要

    设 (H,Θ)(H,\Theta)(H,Θ) 是一个射影复 klt 对，具有有效有理边界和 nef ℚ-Cartier 伴随除子。

    在任意射影对数消解上，拉回的伴随除子存在极小半正度量，且这些度量处处具有零 Lelong 数。

    在光滑射影复簇上，当两个端点线丛都带有零 Lelong 数的半正度量时，我们还对具有简单正规交叉支撑的有理内部边界证明了一个 H1 单射性定理。

    英文原文

    Let (H,Θ)(H,\Theta)(H,Θ) be a projective complex klt pair with effective rational boundary and nef ℚ-Cartier adjoint.

    On any projective log resolution, minimal semipositive metrics on the pulled-back adjoint exist and have zero Lelong numbers everywhere.

    On smooth projective complex varieties, we also prove an H1-injectivity theorem for rational interior boundaries with simple-normal-crossing support when both endpoint bundles carry zero-Lelong semipositive metrics.

  6. 利用极小度量与移动射流证明四维非消失性

    Fourfold nonvanishing by minimal metrics and moving jets

    摘要

    我们证明了光滑连通射影复四维簇的典范非消失性：若 KX 伪有效，则存在某个正整数 m 使得 H0(X,mKX)≠0H^0(X,mK_X)\ne0H0(X,mKX​)=0。

    英文原文

    We prove canonical nonvanishing for smooth connected projective complex fourfolds: if KX is pseudo-effective, then H0(X,mKX)≠0H^0(X,mK_X)\ne0H0(X,mKX​)=0 for some positive integer m.

  7. 从伴随除子的约化支撑提升截面

    Lifting sections from the reduced support of an adjoint

    摘要

    对于特征为零的代数闭域上具有有效有理边界的射影 ℚ-factorial dlt 对，我们证明，只要伴随除子的一个非零有效 Cartier 倍数支撑在系数为一的边界上，并且限制到其整个约化支撑上是半丰沛线丛，则该伴随除子的 Iitaka 维数为正。

    这给出了 ℂ 上维数至多为四时非消失性成立之后的对数丰沛性。

    英文原文

    For a projective ℚ-factorial dlt pair with effective rational boundary over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, we prove that the adjoint has positive Iitaka dimension whenever a nonzero effective Cartier multiple is supported on the coefficient-one boundary and restricts to a semiample line bundle on its whole reduced support.

    This gives log abundance after nonvanishing in dimension at most four over ℂ.

  8. Schnell 纤维空间与良典范模型

    Schnell fiber spaces and good canonical models

    摘要

    对于光滑连通射影复簇 X 和有效 Cartier 除子 D，只要存在某个正整数 m0 使得 m0KX−Dm_0K_X-Dm0​KX​−D 伪有效，我们就证明了 Campana–Peternell 不等式 κ(X)≥κ(D)\kappa(X)\geq\kappa(D)κ(X)≥κ(D)。

    We prove the Campana–Peternell inequality κ(X)≥κ(D)\kappa(X)\geq\kappa(D)κ(X)≥κ(D) for a smooth connected projective complex variety X and an effective Cartier divisor D whenever m0KX−Dm_0K_X-Dm0​KX​−D is pseudo-effective for some positive integer m0.

  9. 一致对数 Iitaka 纤维化与模除子分母的有界性

    Uniform log Iitaka fibrations and bounded moduli denominators

    摘要

    对于特征为零的代数闭域上固定维数为 d ≥ 5 的正规射影对数典范对，若有效边界的系数属于一个固定的有限有理数集，我们证明，只要 ℚ-Cartier 对数典范除子的 Kodaira 维数非负，同一个完全取整多重典范线性系就生成完整的 Iitaka 域。

    其次数只依赖于维数和系数集。

    对于本文在 ℂ 上的归一化典范丛公式，我们还用维数和系数集，给出了光滑射影确定模型上模除子的 Cartier 分母的界。

    英文原文

    For normal projective log canonical pairs over algebraically closed fields of characteristic zero, of fixed dimension d ≥ 5 and with effective boundary coefficients in a fixed finite rational set, we prove that one complete rounded pluricanonical system generates the full Iitaka field whenever the ℚ-Cartier log canonical divisor has nonnegative Kodaira dimension.

    Its degree depends only on the dimension and coefficient set.

    For the paper's normalized canonical bundle formulae over ℂ, we also bound the Cartier denominators of the moduli divisors on smooth projective determining models in terms of the dimension and coefficient set.

  10. 一致多重典范 Iitaka 纤维化

    Uniform Pluricanonical Iitaka Fibrations

    摘要

    我们证明了特征为零时的有效 Iitaka 纤维化猜想。

    对于每个维数，都存在同一个多重典范次数，定义代数闭域上该维数且 Kodaira 维数非负的每个光滑整射影簇的 Iitaka 纤维化。

    相应截面生成完整的 Iitaka 函数域。

    英文原文

    We prove the effective Iitaka fibration conjecture in characteristic zero.

    For each dimension, one pluricanonical degree defines the Iitaka fibration of every smooth integral projective variety of that dimension and nonnegative Kodaira dimension over an algebraically closed field.

    The associated sections generate the full Iitaka function field.

  11. 对数 Calabi–Yau 纤维化的相对分母与有效线性系

    Relative denominators and effective systems for log Calabi-Yau fibrations

    摘要

    对于从维数至多为四的射影对数典范复对到正维基底的对数 Calabi–Yau 纤维化，若边界系数属于一个固定的有限有理数集，我们证明了分母及有效线性系的一致界。

    这些界给出了统一的平凡化次数和模 b-除子的统一 b-Cartier 倍数。

    当基底除子为大除子时，一个统一的完全取整伴随线性系的截面之比生成完整的基底函数域。

    英文原文

    We prove uniform denominator and effective-system bounds for log Calabi-Yau fibrations from projective log canonical complex pairs of dimension at most four onto positive-dimensional bases, with boundary coefficients in a fixed finite rational set.

    The bounds give a uniform trivializing degree and a uniform b-Cartier multiple of the moduli b-divisor.

    When the base divisor is big, a uniform complete rounded adjoint system has section ratios generating the full base function field.

  12. 对数 Calabi–Yau 对的算术 Stein 次数界

    Arithmetic Stein-degree bounds for log Calabi–Yau pairs

    摘要

    固定 d ≥ 1 和 t > 0。

    设 (X,B)(X,B)(X,B) 是特征为零的域 k 上维数为 d 的普通射影对数典范 ℚ-对，其中 X 正规且整，H0(X,OX)=kH^0(X,\mathcal O_X)=kH0(X,OX​)=k，B 有效，且 KX+B∼Q0K_X+B\sim_{\mathbb Q}0KX​+B∼Q​0。

    我们证明，B 的每个系数至少为 t 的素分支 S 都满足 [kS:k]≤N(d,t)[k_S:k]\leq N(d,t)[kS​:k]≤N(d,t)，其中 kS 是 k 在 k(S)k(S)k(S) 中的相对代数闭包。

    这也给出了 S 在 k 上的 Stein 次数的界，从而证明了 Birkar 的 Stein 次数猜想对普通 ℚ-对的收缩到一点表述。

    英文原文

    Fix d ≥ 1 and t > 0.

    Let (X,B)(X,B)(X,B) be an ordinary projective log canonical ℚ-pair of dimension d over a characteristic-zero field k, with X normal and integral, H0(X,OX)=kH^0(X,\mathcal O_X)=kH0(X,OX​)=k, B effective, and KX+B∼Q0K_X+B\sim_{\mathbb Q}0KX​+B∼Q​0.

    We prove that every prime component S of B with coefficient at least t satisfies [kS:k]≤N(d,t)[k_S:k]\leq N(d,t)[kS​:k]≤N(d,t), where kS is the relative algebraic closure of k in k(S)k(S)k(S).

    This also bounds the Stein degree of S over k, proving the contraction-to-a-point formulation of Birkar's Stein-degree conjecture for ordinary ℚ-pairs.

  13. 四维簇的一致有效对数 Iitaka 纤维化

    Uniform effective log Iitaka fibrations for fourfolds

    摘要

    我们证明了四维有效对数 Iitaka 猜想的有限有理系数情形。

    对于正规射影对数典范复四维簇，若边界系数属于一个固定的有限有理数集，且有理 Cartier 伴随除子伪有效，则一个统一次数使得完全取整自反线性系非空，且其截面之比生成完整的 Iitaka 域。

    对于典范除子有理平凡的射影 klt 复四维簇，我们还证明了典范指标的一致界。

    英文原文

    We prove the finite-rational-coefficient case of the effective log Iitaka conjecture in dimension four.

    For normal projective log canonical complex fourfolds with boundary coefficients in a fixed finite rational set and pseudo-effective rational Cartier adjoint, one uniform degree makes the complete rounded reflexive system nonempty and its section ratios generate the full Iitaka field.

    We also prove a uniform canonical index bound for projective klt complex fourfolds with rationally trivial canonical divisor.

  14. 紧致 Kähler 四维簇在非消失性成立后的丰沛性

    Abundance after nonvanishing for compact Kähler fourfolds

    摘要

    对于正规连通紧致 Kähler klt 四维簇，我们证明，只要其实际的 ℚ-Cartier 伴随除子 KX+ΔK_X+\DeltaKX​+Δ 在解析意义下为 nef，且某个正 Cartier 倍数具有非零截面，则该伴随除子是半丰沛的。

    边界有效且有理；该四维簇无须是射影的或 ℚ-factorial 的。

    在 Iitaka 维数为零时，一个正 Cartier 倍数是平凡的全纯线丛。

    英文原文

    We prove semiampleness of the actual ℚ-Cartier adjoint KX+ΔK_X+\DeltaKX​+Δ of a normal connected compact Kähler klt fourfold whenever it is analytically nef and some positive Cartier multiple has a nonzero section.

    The boundary is effective and rational; the fourfold need not be projective or ℚ-factorial.

    In Iitaka dimension zero, a positive Cartier multiple is the trivial holomorphic line bundle.
