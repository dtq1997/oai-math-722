对数丰沛性与有效 Iitaka 纤维化

Log abundance and effective Iitaka fibrations

OpenAI · 2026-10-0614 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

利用对数 Iitaka 次可加性，证明了任意维数、带有效有理边界的正规紧 Kähler 对数典范对的对数丰沛性：解析意义下 nef 的 Q\mathbb QQ-Cartier 伴随除子是半丰沛的。

还证明了每个特征零代数闭域上的射影对数丰沛性，以及该情形下 Kodaira 维数非负的光滑射影簇的有效 Iitaka 纤维化猜想。

进一步的结果解决了这些域上连通射影半对数典范对数 Calabi–Yau 对的有限有理系数指标猜想：固定维数及边界系数所属的有限有理数集后，存在不依赖于分支个数的一致指标。

英文原文

Using logarithmic Iitaka subadditivity, proves log abundance in every dimension for normal compact Kähler log canonical pairs with effective rational boundary: an analytically nef Q\mathbb QQ-Cartier adjoint is semiample.

It also proves projective log abundance over every algebraically closed field of characteristic zero and the effective Iitaka fibration conjecture for smooth projective varieties of nonnegative Kodaira dimension in that setting.

A further result resolves the finite-rational-coefficient index conjecture for connected projective semi-log-canonical log Calabi–Yau pairs over such fields in each fixed dimension and for each fixed finite set of rational boundary coefficients, with a uniform index independent of the number of components.

    Log abundance for compact Kähler spaces under logarithmic Iitaka subadditivity

    摘要

    假设对光滑射影复簇之间纤维连通、带有相容的约化简单正规交叉边界的满态射，对数 Iitaka 次可加性成立。

    我们证明了每个维数下正规不可约紧致 Kähler 空间的对数丰沛性：对于具有有效有理边界且 KX+ΔK_X+\DeltaKX​+Δ 为 ℚ-Cartier 的对数典范对 (X,Δ)(X,\Delta)(X,Δ)，KX+ΔK_X+\DeltaKX​+Δ 的解析 nef 性蕴含半丰沛性。

    英文原文

    Assume logarithmic Iitaka subadditivity for surjective morphisms with connected fibers between smooth projective complex varieties with compatible reduced simple normal crossing boundaries.

    We prove log abundance for normal irreducible compact Kähler spaces in every dimension: for a log canonical pair (X,Δ)(X,\Delta)(X,Δ) with effective rational boundary and KX+ΔK_X+\DeltaKX​+Δ ℚ-Cartier, analytic nefness of KX+ΔK_X+\DeltaKX​+Δ implies semiampleness.

  2. 半对数典范的对数 Calabi–Yau 对的一致指标

    Uniform indices for semi-log-canonical log Calabi–Yau pairs

    摘要

    在特征为零的代数闭域上，对于每个固定的至少为四的维数，我们证明了连通射影半对数典范的对数 Calabi–Yau 对的一致指标定理。

    当边界系数属于一个固定的有限有理数集时，对数典范除子的同一个倍数是 Cartier 的且线性平凡。

    这个倍数只依赖于维数和系数集，不依赖于不可约分支的数量。

    结合维数至多为三时已有的定理，这解决了有限有理系数的半对数典范指标猜想。

    英文原文

    We prove a uniform index theorem for connected projective semi-log-canonical log Calabi–Yau pairs in every fixed dimension at least four over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero.

    For boundary coefficients in a fixed finite rational set, a single multiple of the log canonical divisor is Cartier and linearly trivial.

    The multiple depends only on the dimension and coefficient set, not on the number of irreducible components.

    Together with the established theorem in dimensions at most three, this resolves the finite-rational-coefficient semi-log-canonical index conjecture.

    摘要

    假设轨形 Iitaka 次可加性、指定的伪有效四维极小模型纲领，以及 Kodaira 维数非负的 nef 四维伴随除子的丰沛性成立，我们证明了整体强 ℚ-factorial 的紧致 Kähler klt 四维对的良极小模型存在性，其中边界为有效有理边界，实际的 ℚ-Cartier 伴随除子在解析意义下伪有效。

    额外的一步是非消失性。

    我们通过纤维化论证证明它；在代数维数为零时，则通过奇异度量、全纯叶状结构和从约化边界作延拓来证明。

    Assuming orbifold Iitaka subadditivity, the specified pseudo-effective fourfold minimal model program, and abundance for nef fourfold adjoints of nonnegative Kodaira dimension, we prove the existence of good minimal models for globally strongly ℚ-factorial compact Kähler klt fourfold pairs with effective rational boundary and analytically pseudo-effective actual ℚ-Cartier adjoint.

    The additional step is nonvanishing.

    We prove it by fibration arguments and, in algebraic dimension zero, by singular metrics, holomorphic foliations, and extension from a reduced boundary.

    The projective abundance argument used in the proof is included in full.

  14. 紧致 Kähler 四维簇在非消失性成立后的丰沛性

    Abundance after nonvanishing for compact Kähler fourfolds

    摘要

    对于正规连通紧致 Kähler klt 四维簇，我们证明，只要其实际的 ℚ-Cartier 伴随除子 KX+ΔK_X+\DeltaKX​+Δ 在解析意义下为 nef，且某个正 Cartier 倍数具有非零截面，则该伴随除子是半丰沛的。

    边界有效且有理；该四维簇无须是射影的或 ℚ-factorial 的。

    在 Iitaka 维数为零时，一个正 Cartier 倍数是平凡的全纯线丛。

    英文原文

    We prove semiampleness of the actual ℚ-Cartier adjoint KX+ΔK_X+\DeltaKX​+Δ of a normal connected compact Kähler klt fourfold whenever it is analytically nef and some positive Cartier multiple has a nonzero section.

    The boundary is effective and rational; the fourfold need not be projective or ℚ-factorial.

    In Iitaka dimension zero, a positive Cartier multiple is the trivial holomorphic line bundle.
