Friedgut–Kalai 图与超图阈值猜想

The Friedgut–Kalai graph and hypergraph threshold conjectures

首发 · 2026-10-062 篇论文部分形式化

证明了关于图和固定一致度超图的 Friedgut–Kalai 阈值宽度猜想。

对固定的 0<ε<1/20<\varepsilon<1/20<ε<1/2，每个非平凡、递增且在顶点重新标号下不变的性质，其概率从 ε\varepsilonε 上升到 1−ε1-\varepsilon1−ε 所需的宽度，对图为 O((log⁡n)−2)O((\log n)^{-2})O((logn)−2)，对满足 r≥3r\ge3r≥3 的 rrr-一致超图为 Or((log⁡n)−r/(r−1))O_r((\log n)^{-r/(r-1)})Or​((logn)−r/(r−1))。

英文原文

Proves the Friedgut–Kalai threshold-width conjectures for graphs and fixed-uniformity hypergraphs.

For fixed 0<ε<1/20<\varepsilon<1/20<ε<1/2, every nontrivial increasing relabeling-invariant property crosses from probability ε\varepsilonε to 1−ε1-\varepsilon1−ε within width O((log⁡n)−2)O((\log n)^{-2})O((logn)−2) for graphs and Or((log⁡n)−r/(r−1))O_r((\log n)^{-r/(r-1)})Or​((logn)−r/(r−1)) for rrr-uniform hypergraphs, r≥3r\ge3r≥3.

The hypergraph influence bound also applies to nonmonotone properties.

  1. 超图性质的影响量的一致界

    A uniform influence bound for hypergraph properties

    摘要

    对于每个固定整数 r ≥ 3，我们证明，具有 n 个顶点的简单 r 一致超图的每个在重新标号下不变的布尔性质都满足 Varp(f)≤CrIp(f)/(log⁡n)r/(r−1)\mathop{\mathrm{Var}}\nolimits _p(f)\le C_r I_p(f)/(\log n)^{r/(r-1)}Varp​(f)≤Cr​Ip​(f)/(logn)r/(r−1)。

    该常数仅依赖于 r，并且该界对所有 0<p<10\lt p\lt 10<p<1 一致成立，无须单调性假设。

    对于递增性质，它给出了指数为 r/(r−1)r/(r-1)r/(r−1) 的相应阈值宽度界，从而证明了 Friedgut 和 Kalai 的超图阈值宽度猜想。

    英文原文

    For every fixed integer r ≥ 3, we prove that every relabeling-invariant Boolean property of simple r-uniform hypergraphs on n vertices satisfies Varp(f)≤CrIp(f)/(log⁡n)r/(r−1)\mathop{\mathrm{Var}}\nolimits _p(f)\le C_r I_p(f)/(\log n)^{r/(r-1)}Varp​(f)≤Cr​Ip​(f)/(logn)r/(r−1).

    The constant depends only on r, and the bound holds uniformly for all 0<p<10\lt p\lt 10<p<1 without a monotonicity assumption.

    For increasing properties, it gives the corresponding threshold-width bound with exponent r/(r−1)r/(r-1)r/(r−1), proving the hypergraph threshold-width conjecture of Friedgut and Kalai.

  2. 单调图性质的锐阈值界

    A Sharp Threshold Bound for Monotone Graph Properties

    摘要

    我们证明了 Friedgut–Kalai 锐阈值（sharp-threshold）猜想。

    对于每个整数 n ≥ 2、每个由 n 个顶点的图组成且在所有顶点置换下不变的非平凡递增族，以及每个 0<ε<1/20\lt \varepsilon\lt 1/20<ε<1/2，使该族概率分别等于 ε 和 1−ε1-\varepsilon1−ε 的边概率相差至多 Clog⁡(1/(2ε))/(log⁡n)2C\log(1/(2\varepsilon))/(\log n)^2Clog(1/(2ε))/(logn)2，其中 C 是一个普适常数。

    英文原文

    We prove the Friedgut–Kalai sharp-threshold conjecture.

    For every integer n ≥ 2, every nontrivial increasing family of graphs on n vertices invariant under all vertex permutations, and every 0<ε<1/20\lt \varepsilon\lt 1/20<ε<1/2, the edge probabilities at which its probability equals ε and 1−ε1-\varepsilon1−ε differ by at most Clog⁡(1/(2ε))/(log⁡n)2C\log(1/(2\varepsilon))/(\log n)^2Clog(1/(2ε))/(logn)2, for a universal constant C.

# Uniform influence and sharp thresholds for graph and hypergraph properties

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [A Sharp Threshold Bound for Monotone Graph Properties](../../preprints/A-Sharp-Threshold-Bound-for-Monotone-Graph-Properties-September-25-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The Friedgut–Kalai sharp-threshold conjecture concerns how quickly a nontrivial increasing graph property appears in the independent-edge random graph. For every $n\ge2$, every such property invariant under vertex relabeling, and $0<\varepsilon<1/2$, the formalization proves that the edge-probability interval between probabilities $\varepsilon$ and $1-\varepsilon$ has width at most $2^{19}\log(1/(2\varepsilon))/(\log n)^2$. The constant is uniform over the graph property, $n$, and $\varepsilon$.
