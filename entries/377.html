    Uniform Interior C1,αC^{1,\alpha}C1,α Estimates for Infinity-Harmonic Functions

    摘要

    我们在每个维数 d ≥ 3 中，对有界无穷调和函数证明了一致的内部 C1,αdC^{1,\alpha_d}C1,αd​ 估计。

    对于某个仅依赖于维数的 αd∈(0,1/3]\alpha_d\in(0,1/3]αd​∈(0,1/3]，梯度在 B1/2 上的上确界范数和 αd-Hölder 半范数，都以一个依赖于维数的常数乘以函数在 B1 上的振幅为上界。

    该指数不是显式的，且不作任何端点或边界正则性的断言。

    特别地，当 d ≥ 3 时，无穷调和函数在 ℝd 的每个开子集上都是局部 C1 的。

    英文原文

    We prove a uniform interior C1,αdC^{1,\alpha_d}C1,αd​ estimate for bounded infinity-harmonic functions in every dimension d ≥ 3.

    For some αd∈(0,1/3]\alpha_d\in(0,1/3]αd​∈(0,1/3] depending only on the dimension, the gradient's supremum norm and αd-Hölder seminorm on B1/2 are bounded by a dimension-dependent constant times the oscillation on B1.

    The exponent is not explicit, and no endpoint or boundary regularity claim is made.

    In particular, infinity-harmonic functions are locally C1 on every open subset of ℝd, for d ≥ 3.
