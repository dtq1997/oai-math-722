Selmer 余秩为零或一时的完整 BSD 公式

The full Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer formula in Selmer coranks zero and one

OpenAI · 2026-10-063 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

对于 Q\mathbb QQ 上的任意椭圆曲线，若存在素数 qqq，使其完整的 qqq 幂 Selmer 群的余秩为零或一，则证明完整的 Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer 首项公式成立，其中包括 Tate–Shafarevich 群的有限性。

结合第 006 组的结果，可得 Q\mathbb QQ 上每条椭圆曲线都有密度为一的二次扭曲满足完整的 BSD 公式。

英文原文

Proves the full Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer leading-term formula for every elliptic curve over Q\mathbb QQ whose full qqq-power Selmer group has corank zero or one for some prime qqq, including finiteness of the Tate–Shafarevich group.

With result 006, this gives full BSD for a density-one set of quadratic twists of every elliptic curve over Q\mathbb QQ.

论文

  1. 由低 Selmer 余秩导出的精确 Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer 公式

    Exact Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer Formula from Low Selmer Corank

    摘要

    对于 ℚ 上的每条椭圆曲线，只要其完整的 q 幂 Selmer 群在某个素数 q 处的余秩为零或一，我们就证明了完整的 Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer 首项公式。

    解析秩和 Mordell–Weil 秩都等于该余秩，且 Tate–Shafarevich 群有限。

    该公式包含所有素因子，无须对约化、有理挠点、同源、复乘或剩余 Galois 表示施加额外假设。

    英文原文

    We prove the full Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer leading-term formula for every elliptic curve over ℚ whose full q-power Selmer group has corank zero or one at some prime q.

    The analytic and Mordell–Weil ranks equal that corank, and the Tate–Shafarevich group is finite.

    The formula includes all prime factors and requires no additional hypotheses on reduction, rational torsion, isogenies, complex multiplication, or residual Galois representations.

  2. 椭圆曲线在每个素数处的 Selmer 逆命题

    The Selmer converse for elliptic curves at every prime

    摘要

    我们对 ℚ 上的每条椭圆曲线和每个素数 p 证明了余秩为零和一时的 Selmer 逆命题：若完整的 p 幂 Selmer 群的 ℤp 余秩为 r∈{0,1}r\in\{0,1\}r∈{0,1}，则解析秩和 Mordell–Weil 秩都等于 r，且整个 Tate–Shafarevich 群有限。

    作为在加性约化素数 3 处的应用，我们证明，对于每个素数 ℓ≡4,7,8(mod9)\ell\equiv4,7,8\pmod9ℓ≡4,7,8(mod9)，三次曲线 X3+Y3=ℓZ3X^3+Y^3=\ell Z^3X3+Y3=ℓZ3 的解析秩和 Mordell–Weil 秩均为一，且 Tate–Shafarevich 群有限。

    特别地，每个这样的 ℓ 都是两个有理数的立方之和。

    英文原文

    We prove the Selmer converse in coranks zero and one for every elliptic curve over ℚ and every prime p: if the full p-power Selmer group has ℤp-corank r∈{0,1}r\in\{0,1\}r∈{0,1}, then the analytic and Mordell–Weil ranks both equal r, and the entire Tate–Shafarevich group is finite.

    As an application at the additive prime 3, we prove that for every prime ℓ≡4,7,8(mod9)\ell\equiv4,7,8\pmod9ℓ≡4,7,8(mod9), the cubic X3+Y3=ℓZ3X^3+Y^3=\ell Z^3X3+Y3=ℓZ3 has analytic and Mordell–Weil rank one and finite Tate–Shafarevich group.

    In particular, every such ℓ is a sum of two rational cubes.

  3. Selmer 余秩至多为一时 Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer 公式的二主部分

    The two-primary Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer formula in Selmer corank at most one

    摘要

    对于有理数域上的每条椭圆曲线，只要其二幂 Selmer 群的余秩至多为一，我们就证明了 Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer 首项公式的二主部分。

    在这一范围内，代数秩、解析秩和 Selmer 余秩相等，且 Tate–Shafarevich 群有限。

    结合二次扭曲的 Selmer 分布，这对每条固定曲线的带符号无平方因子扭曲中按绝对值排序、密度为一的集合给出了精确的二主部分公式；共同的秩为零或一，且每个值的密度都是二分之一。

    英文原文

    We prove the two-primary Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer leading-term formula for every elliptic curve over the rationals whose two-power Selmer group has corank at most one.

    In this range, the algebraic rank, analytic rank, and Selmer corank are equal, and the Tate–Shafarevich group is finite.

    Combined with the quadratic-twist Selmer distribution, this gives the exact two-primary formula for a density-one set of signed squarefree twists of each fixed curve, ordered by absolute value; the common rank is zero or one, with each value having density one half.
