半平面双二聚体的 CLE₄ 猜想

The double-dimer CLE4\mathrm{CLE}_4CLE4​ conjecture in the half-plane

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

解决了双二聚体标度极限猜想的半平面 Temperleyan 形式：由 Temperleyan 方格的两个独立二聚体覆盖形成的完整环系综收敛到嵌套CLE4\mathrm{CLE}_4CLE4​。

这种收敛将每条宏观环作为无参数曲线逐一匹配，从而把环的可观测量的收敛提升为环本身的收敛。

英文原文

Resolves the half-plane Temperleyan form of the double-dimer scaling-limit conjecture: the complete loop ensemble formed by two independent dimer coverings of the Temperleyan square lattice converges to nested CLE4\mathrm{CLE}_4CLE4​.

Convergence matches every macroscopic loop as an unparametrized curve, upgrading convergence of loop observables to convergence of the loops themselves.

论文

  1. 半平面双二聚体的曲线标度极限

    The curve scaling limit of half-plane double dimers

    摘要

    我们证明了上半平面中 Temperleyan 方格的完整双二聚体环系综收敛于嵌套 CLE4。

    该收敛沿完整的网格极限成立，并将每个宏观环作为无参数曲线进行匹配。

    这解决了双二聚体 CLE4 标度极限猜想的半平面 Temperleyan 形式。

    英文原文

    We prove that the complete double-dimer loop ensemble for the Temperleyan square lattice in the upper half-plane converges to nested CLE4.

    The convergence holds along the full mesh limit and matches every macroscopic loop as an unparametrized curve.

    This resolves the half-plane Temperleyan form of the double-dimer CLE4 scaling-limit conjecture.
