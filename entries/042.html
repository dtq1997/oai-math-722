042 · 代数几何与复几何

K3 曲面与紧复曲面的 Oka 分类

Oka classification for K3 surfaces and other compact complex surfaces

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

证明了每个复 K3 曲面都具有 Oka 性质，包括非射影曲面。

更一般地，每个 Kodaira 维数为零的连通极小紧复曲面都具有 Oka 性质；VII 类连通极小紧复曲面具有 Oka 性质，当且仅当它是 Hopf 曲面或 Enoki 曲面。

英文原文

Proves that every complex K3 surface is Oka, including nonprojective surfaces.

More generally, every connected minimal compact complex surface of Kodaira dimension zero is Oka; a connected minimal compact complex surface of class VII is Oka exactly when it is a Hopf or Enoki surface.

OpenAI · overview.tex

论文

  1. 每个复 K3 曲面都是 Oka 曲面

    Every complex K3 surface is Oka

    摘要

    我们证明，每个复 K3 曲面 X 都是 Oka 的，从而解决了 K3 Oka 猜想。

    等价地，对每个 m ≥ 1，从 ℂm 中紧凸集的某个邻域到 X 的每个全纯映射，都可由整映射 Cm→X\mathbb C^m\to XCm→X 在该紧凸集上一致逼近。

    英文原文

    We prove that every complex K3 surface X is Oka, resolving the K3 Oka conjecture.

    Equivalently, for every m ≥ 1, every holomorphic map to X from a neighborhood of a compact convex set in ℂm can be approximated uniformly on that set by entire maps Cm→X\mathbb C^m\to XCm→X.
