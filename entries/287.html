    An isomorphism of the free group factors

    摘要

    我们证明 L(F2)L(\mathbb F_2)L(F2​) 与 L(F3)L(\mathbb F_3)L(F3​) 作为带迹 von Neumann 代数同构，从而肯定地解决自由群因子的同构问题。

    随后，经典的自由群因子二择一定理推出，所有插值自由群因子，包括 L(F∞)L(\mathbb F_\infty)L(F∞​)，均彼此同构，且基本群为 R>0\mathbb R_{\gt 0}R>0​。

    英文原文

    We solve the free group factor isomorphism problem affirmatively by proving that L(F2)L(\mathbb F_2)L(F2​) and L(F3)L(\mathbb F_3)L(F3​) are isomorphic as tracial von Neumann algebras.

    The classical free group factor alternative then implies that all interpolated free group factors, including L(F∞)L(\mathbb F_\infty)L(F∞​), are isomorphic and have fundamental group R>0\mathbb R_{\gt 0}R>0​.

# Isomorphism of the free group factors

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [An isomorphism of the free group factors](../../preprints/An-isomorphism-of-the-free-group-factors-September-23-2026/An-isomorphism-of-the-free-group-factors-September-23-2026.pdf)

## Scope

The free group factor problem asks whether the von Neumann algebras of free groups of different ranks are isomorphic. The formalization proves that interpolated free group factors with any parameters $r,s>1$, including the infinite parameter, are normally trace-preservingly isomorphic. The paper's fundamental-group conclusion is a further consequence rather than a separate selected statement here.
