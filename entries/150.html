无理三角形台球的弱混合

Weak mixing of irrational triangular billiards

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

证明了每个至少有一个角与 π\piπ 之比为无理数的非退化欧几里得三角形，其台球流相对于归一化面积测度与均匀方向测度的乘积都是弱混合的。

这将整个无理角类上的遍历性加强为弱混合性，且不要求一般性假设或 Diophantine 条件。

英文原文

Proves that the billiard flow in every nondegenerate Euclidean triangle with at least one angle irrational relative to π\piπ is weakly mixing for normalized area times uniform direction.

This strengthens ergodicity on the entire irrational-angle class, with no genericity or Diophantine restrictions.

  1. 含无理角的三角形台球的弱混合性

    Weak mixing of triangular billiards with an irrational angle

    摘要

    我们证明，在每个至少有一个内角与 π 之比为无理数的非退化欧氏三角形中，单位速度台球流相对于归一化 Liouville 测度都是弱混合的。

    等价地，该流与自身的乘积是遍历的。

    英文原文

    We prove that the unit-speed billiard flow in every nondegenerate Euclidean triangle with at least one angle irrational relative to π is weakly mixing for normalized Liouville measure.

    Equivalently, the product of the flow with itself is ergodic.

    摘要

    我们证明，在每个至少有一个内角与 π 之比为无理数的非退化欧氏三角形中，单位速度台球流相对于归一化面积测度与均匀角度测度的乘积都是遍历的。

    We prove that the unit-speed billiard flow in every nondegenerate Euclidean triangle with at least one angle irrational relative to π is ergodic for normalized area times uniform angular measure.

    No genericity or Diophantine condition is required.

    The flow is considered outside the null set of trajectories that hit a vertex.

# Weak mixing of triangular billiards with an irrational angle

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Ergodicity of triangular billiards with an irrational angle](../../preprints/Ergodicity-of-triangular-billiards-with-an-irrational-angle-September-25-2026/Ergodicity-of-triangular-billiards-with-an-irrational-angle-September-25-2026.pdf)

## Scope

The formalization proves that the unit-speed billiard flow in every nondegenerate Euclidean triangle with at least one angle irrational relative to $\pi$ is ergodic for normalized area times uniform angular measure. It constructs the flow outside the null set of exceptional trajectories, proves uniqueness of the flight chain there, and establishes measure preservation and the flow law almost everywhere. No genericity or Diophantine condition on the irrational angle is assumed.
