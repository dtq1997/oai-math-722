Zariski 重数猜想反例

Counterexamples to Zariski's multiplicity conjecture

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

通过构造在环境空间中同胚、但重数不同的约化全纯超曲面芽，否定 Zariski 重数猜想。

这些例子包括 C4\mathbb C^4C4 中具有孤立临界点、重数分别为四和五的超曲面。

英文原文

Disproves Zariski's multiplicity conjecture by constructing reduced holomorphic hypersurface germs that are ambiently homeomorphic but have different multiplicities.

The examples include hypersurfaces in C4\mathbb C^4C4 with isolated critical points and multiplicities four and five.

  1. 在环境空间中同胚、重数分别为二和三的孤立超曲面奇点

    Ambiently homeomorphic isolated hypersurfaces of multiplicities two and three

    摘要

    我们对嵌入 Zariski 重数猜想给出了否定回答。

    我们构造了两个在环境空间中同胚的约化超曲面芽，但其重数分别为二和三。

    二者都具有孤立奇点，并位于同一个维数为八的倍数的复仿射空间中。

    它们的定义函数芽也拓扑右等价，而且这些相同的例子也对环境拓扑等价意义下的 Arnold 余秩问题给出了否定回答。

    英文原文

    We give a negative answer to the embedded Zariski multiplicity conjecture.

    We construct two reduced hypersurface germs that are ambiently homeomorphic but have multiplicities two and three.

    Both have isolated singularities and lie in a common complex affine space of dimension divisible by eight.

    Their defining function germs are also topologically right equivalent, and the same examples answer Arnold's corank problem negatively for ambient topological equivalence.

  2. ℂ⁴ 中在环境空间内同胚、重数分别为四和五的孤立超曲面奇点芽

    Ambiently homeomorphic isolated hypersurface germs in ℂ⁴ with multiplicities four and five

    摘要

    我们对四个复变量情形的 Zariski 重数问题给出了否定回答。

    我们构造了两个约化的收敛全纯函数芽，它们具有孤立临界点，重数分别为四和五，而其零点集芽在环境空间中同胚。

    英文原文

    We give a negative answer to Zariski's multiplicity question in four complex variables.

    We construct two reduced convergent holomorphic function germs with isolated critical points and multiplicities four and five whose zero-set germs are ambiently homeomorphic.
