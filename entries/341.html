Donaldson hypersymplectic 形变猜想

Donaldson's hypersymplectic deformation conjecture

首发 · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

以保持上同调的形式证明了 Donaldson 的 hypersymplectic 形变猜想。

闭连通定向四维流形上每个由光滑闭二形式组成、并经 ∫ωi∧ωj=δij\int\omega_i\wedge\omega_j=\delta_{ij}∫ωi​∧ωj​=δij​ 归一化的正三元组，都能通过正的闭三元组形变为一个超 Kähler 三元组，同时保持全部三个上同调类。

任意正三元组都可以先通过常系数线性变换归一化。

英文原文

Proves Donaldson's hypersymplectic deformation conjecture in a cohomology-preserving form.

Every positive triple of smooth closed two-forms on a closed connected oriented four-manifold, normalized by ∫ωi∧ωj=δij\int\omega_i\wedge\omega_j=\delta_{ij}∫ωi​∧ωj​=δij​, deforms through positive closed triples to a hyperkähler triple while preserving all three cohomology classes.

Any positive triple can first be normalized by a constant linear change.

    Deforming hypersymplectic four-manifolds to hyperkähler triples

    论文已修订官方日志 2026.10.07

    摘要

    在闭连通定向四维流形上，每个由闭二形式组成的光滑归一化正三元组，都可以在各个上同调类保持固定的条件下光滑形变为一个超 Kähler 三元组。

    这解决了 Donaldson 的 hypersymplectic 形变猜想。

    英文原文

    Every smooth normalized positive triple of closed two-forms on a closed connected oriented four-manifold admits a smooth deformation, with each cohomology class fixed, to a hyperkähler triple.

    This resolves Donaldson's hypersymplectic deformation conjecture.
