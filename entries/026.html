  1. 大素数间隙的正下密度

    Positive lower density of large prime gaps

    摘要

    对于每个固定的 C > 0，我们证明，有正比例的相邻素数间隙超过 Clog⁡pC\log pClogp，其中 p 是较小的素数。

    对于素数序列的每个充分长的初始段，该比例都有一个下界，其常数依赖于 C。

    由此可知，pn/np_n/npn​/n 增大的那些指标具有正的下渐近密度，从而回答了 Erdős 和 Prachar 的一个问题。

    英文原文

    For every fixed C > 0, we prove that a positive proportion of consecutive prime gaps exceed Clog⁡pC\log pClogp, where p is the smaller prime.

    The proportion is bounded below for every sufficiently large initial segment of the prime sequence, with a constant depending on C.

    It follows that the indices at which pn/np_n/npn​/n increases have positive lower asymptotic density, answering a question of Erdős and Prachar.

# Positive lower density of large prime gaps

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Positive lower density of large prime gaps](../../preprints/Positive-lower-density-of-large-prime-gaps-September-25-2026/main.pdf)

## Scope

The formalized supplement proves that the indices $n$ for which $p_n/n<p_{n+1}/(n+1)$ have positive lower asymptotic density, where $p_n$ is the $n$th prime. Thus the normalized prime sequence has a positive-density set of increases. This is the prime-ratio corollary associated with the paper's theorem on a positive lower density of large prime gaps.
