  1. SLE 的精确 Hausdorff 规范函数

    An exact Hausdorff gauge for SLE

    摘要

    对于每个 0<κ<80\lt \kappa\lt 80<κ<8，我们构造了一个确定性的 Hausdorff 规范函数，它几乎必然为弦 Schramm–Loewner 演化的每个非平凡的正时间紧致段赋予正且有限的测度。

    这回答了该参数范围内 Schramm 的 Hausdorff 测度存在性问题。

    整条迹在每个有界盒子中的规范测度具有有限期望。

    英文原文

    For each 0<κ<80\lt \kappa\lt 80<κ<8, we construct a deterministic Hausdorff gauge that almost surely assigns positive finite measure to every nontrivial compact positive-time segment of chordal Schramm–Loewner evolution.

    This answers Schramm's Hausdorff-measure existence problem in this parameter range.

    The entire trace has finite expected gauge measure in each bounded box.

  2. SLE 的显式精确 Hausdorff 规范函数

    An explicit exact Hausdorff gauge for SLE

    摘要

    对于每个固定的 0<κ<80\lt \kappa\lt 80<κ<8，令 d=1+κ/8d=1+\kappa/8d=1+κ/8。

    在充分小的半径处由 h(r)=rd(log⁡log⁡(1/r))(2−d)/2h(r)=r^d(\log\log(1/r))^{(2-d)/2}h(r)=rd(loglog(1/r))(2−d)/2 给出的规范函数 几乎必然为弦 SLEκ 的每个非平凡的正时间紧致段赋予正且有限的 Hausdorff 测度。

    这给出了该参数范围内 Schramm 的 Hausdorff 测度问题的显式解答。

    整条迹在每个有界圆盘中的测度具有有限期望。

    Schramm 所提出的指数为一的迭代对数规范函数，在任何这样的段上都不产生 sigma-有限测度。

    英文原文

    For each fixed 0<κ<80\lt \kappa\lt 80<κ<8, let d=1+κ/8d=1+\kappa/8d=1+κ/8.

    The gauge h(r)=rd(log⁡log⁡(1/r))(2−d)/2h(r)=r^d(\log\log(1/r))^{(2-d)/2}h(r)=rd(loglog(1/r))(2−d)/2 at sufficiently small radii almost surely gives positive finite Hausdorff measure to every nontrivial positive-time compact segment of chordal SLEκ.

    This gives an explicit solution to Schramm's Hausdorff-measure problem in this parameter range.

    The entire trace has finite expected measure in every bounded disk.

    The exponent-one iterated-logarithm gauge suggested by Schramm is not sigma-finite on any such segment.

# Exact Hausdorff gauges for SLE

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [An exact Hausdorff gauge for SLE: A moment-integral and finite-batch construction](../../preprints/An-exact-Hausdorff-gauge-for-SLE-September-25-2026/An-exact-Hausdorff-gauge-for-SLE-September-25-2026.pdf)
- [An explicit exact Hausdorff gauge for SLE: A regular formula from quantitative tails and dense visits](../../preprints/An-explicit-exact-Hausdorff-gauge-for-SLE-September-26-2026/An-explicit-exact-Hausdorff-gauge-for-SLE-September-26-2026.pdf)

## Scope

For $0<\kappa<8$, put $d=1+\kappa/8$. The formalization proves that every continuous nondecreasing Hausdorff gauge agreeing with $h(r)=r^d(\log\log(1/r))^{(2-d)/2}$ at sufficiently small positive radii almost surely assigns positive measure to every nontrivial compact positive-time segment of ordinary chordal $\mathrm{SLE}_\kappa$.
