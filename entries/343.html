Sharp symplectic ball-packing criteria in higher dimensions

英文原文

Resolves the Siegel–Yao conjecture for arbitrary capacities in every dimension 2n≥62n\ge62n≥6.

Finitely many closed symplectic balls of capacities R1,…,RkR_1,\ldots,R_kR1​,…,Rk​ embed disjointly into an open ball of capacity RRR exactly when ∑iRin<Rn\sum_iR_i^n<R^n∑i​Rin​<Rn and Ri+Rj<RR_i+R_j<RRi​+Rj​<R for every distinct pair i,ji,ji,j.

    摘要

    我们证明，对于所有整数 n ≥ 3 和 k ≥ 1，以及所有正实容量 R1,…,RkR_1,\ldots,R_kR1​,…,Rk​，具有这些容量的闭标准辛 2n2n2n 维球可以彼此不交地嵌入一个容量为 R > 0 的球的内部，当且仅当 ∑i=1kRin<Rn,Ri+Rj<R(i≠j).\displaystyle \sum_{i=1}^k R_i^n\lt R^n, \qquad R_i+R_j\lt R\quad(i\ne j).i=1∑k​Rin​<Rn,Ri​+Rj​<R(i=j).

    容量等于欧氏半径平方的 π 倍，且每个嵌入都定义在其闭源球的一个邻域上。

    这证明了 Siegel 和 Yao 的猜想 A。

    英文原文

    We prove that, for all integers n ≥ 3 and k ≥ 1 and all positive real capacities R1,…,RkR_1,\ldots,R_kR1​,…,Rk​, the closed standard symplectic 2n2n2n-balls of these capacities embed disjointly into the interior of a ball of capacity R > 0 if and only if ∑i=1kRin<Rn,Ri+Rj<R(i≠j).\displaystyle \sum_{i=1}^k R_i^n\lt R^n, \qquad R_i+R_j\lt R\quad(i\ne j).i=1∑k​Rin​<Rn,Ri​+Rj​<R(i=j).

    Capacity is π times the squared Euclidean radius, and each embedding is defined on a neighborhood of its closed source ball.

    This proves Siegel and Yao's Conjecture A.

# Symplectic ball packing in higher dimensions

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Symplectic Ball Packings in Higher Dimensions](../../preprints/Symplectic-Ball-Packings-in-Higher-Dimensions-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The formalization proves Siegel and Yao's ball-packing criterion in every symplectic dimension $2n$ with $n\ge3$. For $k\ge1$ closed standard balls of positive capacities $R_1,\ldots,R_k$, disjoint symplectic embeddings into the interior of a ball of capacity $R$ exist exactly when
$\sum_i R_i^n<R^n$ and $R_i+R_j<R$ for all $i\ne j$.
Capacity is $\pi$ times squared Euclidean radius, and each embedding is defined on a neighborhood of its closed source ball. The linked statements include both the full equivalence and the necessity direction.
