  1. 1≤p≤2 时三维中的强微分同胚逼近

    Strong diffeomorphic approximation in three dimensions for 1≤p≤2

    摘要

    我们解决了 1≤p≤21\le p\le21≤p≤2 时的三维 Ball–Evans 逼近问题。

    ℝ3 中任意有界域之间的每个 W1,pW^{1,p}W1,p 同胚，都是映满同一目标域的光滑微分同胚的强 W1,pW^{1,p}W1,p 极限。

    英文原文

    We resolve the three-dimensional Ball–Evans approximation problem for 1≤p≤21\le p\le21≤p≤2.

    Every W1,pW^{1,p}W1,p homeomorphism between arbitrary bounded domains in ℝ3 is a strong W1,pW^{1,p}W1,p limit of smooth diffeomorphisms onto the same target.

  2. p>2 时三维中的强微分同胚逼近

    Strong diffeomorphic approximation in three dimensions for p>2

    我们对每个有限的 p > 2 解决了三维 Ball–Evans 逼近问题。

    ℝ3 中任意有界域之间的每个 W1,pW^{1,p}W1,p 同胚，都可以在 W1,pW^{1,p}W1,p 中由映满同一目标域的光滑微分同胚强逼近。

    英文原文

    We resolve the three-dimensional Ball–Evans approximation problem for every finite p > 2.

    Every W1,pW^{1,p}W1,p homeomorphism between arbitrary bounded domains in ℝ3 can be approximated strongly in W1,pW^{1,p}W1,p by smooth diffeomorphisms onto the same target.
