对称随机符号矩阵奇异概率的精确指数衰减率

Sharp singularity rates for symmetric sign matrices

首发 · 2026-10-062 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

确定了对角线及其上方元素相互独立的对称随机符号矩阵的奇异概率的精确指数衰减率。

均匀符号给出Pr⁡(det⁡An=0)=(1/2+o(1))n\Pr(\det A_n=0)=(1/2+o(1))^nPr(detAn​=0)=(1/2+o(1))n；对于固定偏置p∈(0,1)∖{1/2}p\in(0,1)\setminus\{1/2\}p∈(0,1)∖{1/2}，该概率的衰减率为(p2+(1−p)2+o(1))n(p^2+(1-p)^2+o(1))^n(p2+(1−p)2+o(1))n。

在有偏情形中，相同行达到这一衰减率。

英文原文

Determines the sharp exponential singularity rate of symmetric random sign matrices with independent entries on and above the diagonal.

Uniform signs give Pr⁡(det⁡An=0)=(1/2+o(1))n\Pr(\det A_n=0)=(1/2+o(1))^nPr(detAn​=0)=(1/2+o(1))n; for fixed bias p∈(0,1)∖{1/2}p\in(0,1)\setminus\{1/2\}p∈(0,1)∖{1/2}, the rate is (p2+(1−p)2+o(1))n(p^2+(1-p)^2+o(1))^n(p2+(1−p)2+o(1))n.

In the biased case, agreeing rows attain this rate.

  1. 对称 Bernoulli 矩阵奇异概率的精确指数衰减率

    The sharp exponential rate of singularity for symmetric Bernoulli matrices

    摘要

    设 An 为一个对称 n×nn\times nn×n 矩阵，其对角线及其上方的元素是独立且均匀的正负号。

    我们证明 Pr⁡(det⁡An=0)=(12+o(1))n.\displaystyle \Pr(\det A_n=0)=\left(\frac12+o(1)\right)^n.Pr(detAn​=0)=(21​+o(1))n.。

    英文原文

    Let An be a symmetric n×nn\times nn×n matrix whose entries on and above the diagonal are independent uniform signs.

    We prove Pr⁡(det⁡An=0)=(12+o(1))n.\displaystyle \Pr(\det A_n=0)=\left(\frac12+o(1)\right)^n.Pr(detAn​=0)=(21​+o(1))n.

  2. 有偏对称符号矩阵奇异概率的精确衰减率

    The sharp singularity rate for biased symmetric sign matrices

    摘要

    设 An 为一个对称随机矩阵，其对角线及其上方的元素是独立的正负号，以固定概率 p∈(0,1)∖{1/2}p\in(0,1)\setminus\{1/2\}p∈(0,1)∖{1/2} 等于 1。

    我们证明 P(det⁡An=0)=(p2+(1−p)2+o(1))n\mathbb P(\det A_n=0)=(p^2+(1-p)^2+o(1))^nP(detAn​=0)=(p2+(1−p)2+o(1))n。

    两行相同这一事件达到了该速率。

    英文原文

    Let An be a symmetric random matrix whose entries on and above the diagonal are independent signs, equal to 1 with fixed probability p∈(0,1)∖{1/2}p\in(0,1)\setminus\{1/2\}p∈(0,1)∖{1/2}.

    We prove that P(det⁡An=0)=(p2+(1−p)2+o(1))n\mathbb P(\det A_n=0)=(p^2+(1-p)^2+o(1))^nP(detAn​=0)=(p2+(1−p)2+o(1))n.

    The rate is attained by the event that two rows agree.
