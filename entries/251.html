可均性、可酉化性与 Ulam 稳定性

Amenability, unitarizability, and Ulam stability

首发 · 2026-10-062 篇论文部分形式化

对所有离散群解决 Dixmier 问题：可均性等价于每个一致有界的 Hilbert 空间表示都与某个酉表示相似。

对可数离散群，可均性还等价于强 Ulam 稳定性：足够精确的酉近似表示，在算子范数下一致接近于同一个可能无限维的 Hilbert 空间上的真正表示。

英文原文

Resolves Dixmier's problem for all discrete groups: amenability is equivalent to every uniformly bounded Hilbert-space representation being similar to a unitary representation.

For countable discrete groups, amenability is also equivalent to strong Ulam stability: sufficiently accurate unitary approximate representations are uniformly close in operator norm to genuine representations on the same, possibly infinite-dimensional, Hilbert space.

  1. 离散群的可酉化性蕴含可均性

    Unitarizability implies amenability for discrete groups

    已列入形式化清单
    摘要

    我们证明，一个离散群是可均的，当且仅当它在复 Hilbert 空间上的每个一致有界表示都相似于一个酉表示。

    这对离散群肯定地解决了 Dixmier 的可酉化问题。

    英文原文

    We prove that a discrete group is amenable if and only if every uniformly bounded representation on a complex Hilbert space is similar to a unitary representation.

    This resolves Dixmier's unitarizability problem affirmatively for discrete groups.

  2. 强 Ulam 稳定性刻画可均性

    Strong Ulam Stability Characterizes Amenability

    摘要

    一个可数离散群是可均的，当且仅当它是强 Ulam 稳定的：在任意复 Hilbert 空间上，每个充分精确的酉几乎表示都在算子范数下一致接近同一空间上的一个真正的表示。

    我们证明了 Kazhdan 可均稳定性定理的逆命题，回答了 Burger、Ozawa 和 Thom 的问题。

    将无限维 Hilbert 空间包括在内是必要的。

    英文原文

    A countable discrete group is amenable if and only if it is strongly Ulam stable: every sufficiently accurate unitary almost representation, on any complex Hilbert space, is uniformly close in operator norm to a genuine representation on the same space.

    We prove the converse to Kazhdan's amenable stability theorem, answering the question of Burger, Ozawa, and Thom.

    The inclusion of infinite-dimensional Hilbert spaces is essential.

# Amenability, unitarizability, and strong Ulam stability

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Unitarizability implies amenability for discrete groups](../../preprints/Unitarizability-Implies-Amenability-for-Countable-Groups-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

Dixmier's unitarizability problem asks whether a discrete group is amenable exactly when every uniformly bounded Hilbert-space representation is similar to a unitary one. The formalization establishes this equivalence for every discrete group.

For every nonamenable group and every $\varepsilon>0$, it also gives a nonunitarizable representation with uniform operator bound at most $1+\varepsilon$. The Hilbert space can be chosen separable for countable groups; a separate linked statement records a separable witness with uniform bound $101$ in that case.
