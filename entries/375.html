八维 De Giorgi 猜想

De Giorgi's conjecture in dimension eight

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

在最优的八维端点证明了 De Giorgi 猜想：方程 Δu=u3−u\Delta u=u^3-uΔu=u3−u 的每个沿某个方向严格递增的整 C2C^2C2 解 u:R8→(−1,1)u:\mathbb R^8\to(-1,1)u:R8→(−1,1)，都只依赖于一个线性坐标。

一个更强的定理将所有稳定整解 v:R7→[−1,1]v:\mathbb R^7\to[-1,1]v:R7→[−1,1] 分类为常值势阱解或平面过渡解，无须能量增长假设。

英文原文

Proves De Giorgi's conjecture at its sharp dimension-eight endpoint: every entire C2C^2C2 solution u:R8→(−1,1)u:\mathbb R^8\to(-1,1)u:R8→(−1,1) of Δu=u3−u\Delta u=u^3-uΔu=u3−u that is strictly increasing in one direction depends on only one linear coordinate.

A stronger theorem classifies all stable entire solutions v:R7→[−1,1]v:\mathbb R^7\to[-1,1]v:R7→[−1,1] as constant wells or planar transitions, without an energy-growth assumption.

  1. 八维 De Giorgi 猜想的肯定解答

    A positive resolution of De Giorgi's conjecture in dimension eight

    摘要

    我们在八维中肯定地解决了 De Giorgi 猜想：Δu=u3−u\Delta u=u^3-uΔu=u3−u 的每个具有某个处处为正的方向导数的 C2 整解 u:R8→(−1,1)u:\mathbb R^8\to(-1,1)u:R8→(−1,1)，都是一个平面异宿解。

    我们证明，该方程的每个稳定解 v:R7→[−1,1]v:\mathbb R^7\to[-1,1]v:R7→[−1,1] 都是一个取值于势阱的常数解或平面异宿解，无需能量增长假设。

    英文原文

    We resolve De Giorgi's conjecture positively in dimension eight: every entire C2 solution u:R8→(−1,1)u:\mathbb R^8\to(-1,1)u:R8→(−1,1) of Δu=u3−u\Delta u=u^3-uΔu=u3−u with an everywhere positive directional derivative is a planar heteroclinic.

    We prove that every stable solution v:R7→[−1,1]v:\mathbb R^7\to[-1,1]v:R7→[−1,1] of this equation is a constant well or a planar heteroclinic, without an energy-growth assumption.
