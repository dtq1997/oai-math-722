286 · 算子代数

格群 von Neumann 代数的算术刚性

Arithmetic rigidity of lattice von Neumann algebras

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

分类了以下两类因子之间的有限指标双模：一类是与特征零局部域上具有性质 (T)(T)(T) 的格可公度的 ICC 群的标量扭曲群因子，另一类是任意 ICC 群因子。

每个这样的双模都是若干模型的有限直和的一个直和项，这些模型来自有限指数子群之间的同构及有限维射影表示。

该分类还在所述稳定等价意义下恢复了群、标量上闭链及放大尺度。

英文原文

Classifies finite-index bimodules between scalar-twisted group factors of ICC groups commensurable with property-(T)(T)(T) lattices over characteristic-zero local fields and arbitrary ICC group factors.

Every such bimodule is a summand of finite sums of models arising from finite-index subgroup isomorphisms and finite-dimensional projective representations.

The classification also recovers the group, scalar cocycle and amplification scale up to the stated stable equivalence.

OpenAI · overview.tex

论文

  1. 局部域上格的扭曲有限对应的算术性

    Arithmeticity of twisted finite correspondences for lattices over local fields

    摘要

    我们证明，对于与特征零局部域上的具有性质 (T)(T)(T) 的格可公度的 ICC 群，其任意标量扭曲群因子与任意可数 ICC 群的扭曲群因子之间的双有限对应满足算术穷尽性。

    每个这样的对应，都是由实际的有限指数子群同构以及匹配余循环之比的有限维射影表示所构造的模型的某个有限直和的闭直和项。

    证明涵盖可约乘积、实位点与有限位点的混合，以及四元数和 Cayley 秩一因子。

    英文原文

    We prove arithmetic exhaustion for bifinite correspondences between arbitrarily scalar-twisted group factors of ICC groups commensurable with property-(T)(T)(T) lattices over characteristic-zero local fields and twisted group factors of arbitrary countable ICC groups.

    Every such correspondence is a closed summand of a finite direct sum of models obtained from actual finite-index subgroup isomorphisms and finite-dimensional projective representations of the matched cocycle ratio.

    The proof includes reducible products, mixed real and finite places, and quaternionic and Cayley rank-one factors.

  2. 算术范畴与格因子的稳定恢复

    The arithmetic category and stable recovery of lattice factors

    摘要

    对于与特征零局部域上具有性质 (T)(T)(T) 的格可公度的 ICC 群的标量扭曲群因子，我们证明稳定典范恢复定理。

    每个给定的、到任意可数无限 ICC 群的标量扭曲因子的稳定同构，都恢复出群、在归一化余链变换意义下的余循环，以及相等的放大尺度，并在给定的矩阵角代数中给出实现该同构的部分等距算子。

    我们对任意可数 ICC 群计算算术对应子范畴，包括全部有界映射、直和项、共轭及 Connes 融合，并将其与姊妹论文中的算术穷尽定理结合。

    乘法还恢复任意有限指标的相邻因子，并给出匹配余循环之比所满足的精确射影条件。

    英文原文

    We prove stable canonical recovery for scalar-twisted group factors of ICC groups commensurable with property-(T)(T)(T) lattices over characteristic-zero local fields.

    Every specified stable isomorphism to a scalar-twisted factor of any countably infinite ICC group recovers the group, the cocycle up to a normalized cochain, and equal amplification scales, with an implementing partial isometry in the given matrix corners.

    We compute the arithmetic correspondence subcategory for arbitrary countable ICC groups, including all bounded maps, summands, conjugates, and Connes fusion, and combine it with the companion's arithmetic exhaustion theorem.

    Multiplication also recovers arbitrary finite-index factor neighbors, with the exact projective condition on the matched cocycle ratio.
