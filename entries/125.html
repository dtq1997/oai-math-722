  1. 度量 k 中位数问题的单指数恢复与有界价格严格性

    Single-exponential recovery and bounded-price strictness for metric k-median

    主结果已列入形式化清单
    摘要

    我们给出度量 k 中位数问题的一个精确预算恢复算法，其对如下比较簇数目的依赖为单指数：这些比较簇在给定锚点解中缺少准确且互不相同的代理。

    在直径多项式有界的正整数度量上，如果总代理误差充分小，且这类簇的数目为对数级，就能以任意高的成功概率在多项式时间内得到 (1+2/e+ε)(1+2/e+\varepsilon)(1+2/e+ε) 近似。

    我们还对对数盈余构造的一次相容执行证明有界价格严格性（bounded-price strictness）。

    恢复论证和支付论证合在一起，在任意有限有理度量上给出随机 (2−σ)(2-\sigma)(2−σ) 近似，其中 σ > 0 是绝对常数；该近似既以高概率成立，也在期望意义下成立，并且每个输出都至多开设 k 个设施。

    英文原文

    We give an exact-budget recovery algorithm for metric k-median with single-exponential dependence on the number of comparison clusters without accurate, distinct proxies in a supplied anchor solution.

    On positive integral metrics of polynomially bounded diameter, a sufficiently small total proxy error and logarithmically many such clusters yield a (1+2/e+ε)(1+2/e+\varepsilon)(1+2/e+ε) approximation in polynomial time with arbitrarily high success probability.

    We also prove bounded-price strictness for one compatible execution of the logarithmic-surplus construction.

    Together the recovery and payment arguments give a randomized (2−σ)(2-\sigma)(2−σ) approximation, for an absolute σ > 0, on arbitrary finite rational metrics, both with high probability and in expectation, while opening at most k facilities on every output.

# The metric <i>k</i>-median approximation threshold and recovery

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Single-exponential recovery and bounded-price strictness for metric $k$-median](../../preprints/Single-Exponential-Recovery-and-Bounded-Price-Strictness-for-Metric-k-Median-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)
- [The approximation threshold for metric $k$-median](../../preprints/The-Approximation-Threshold-for-Metric-k-Median-September-24-2026/main.pdf)

## Scope

The formalization gives an exact-budget recovery algorithm for metric $k$-median on the stated polynomially bounded integral metrics. Suppose a supplied anchor represents all but logarithmically many comparison clusters by distinct proxies, with total proxy cost at most the comparison cost plus a sufficiently small relative error. The algorithm always opens at most $k$ facilities and, with any requested confidence, attains a $(1+2/e+\varepsilon)$ factor relative to those comparison centers in polynomial time.

The linked final approximation result applies to arbitrary finite rational metrics. For one absolute $\sigma>0$, a polynomial-time fair-bit algorithm always returns a feasible solution, has expected cost at most $(2-\sigma)\mathrm{OPT}$, and attains the same factor with arbitrarily high polynomial confidence.

The formalization gives, for every fixed $\varepsilon>0$, a deterministic polynomial-time $(1+2/e+\varepsilon)$-approximation for metric $k$-median on finite rational metrics with specified candidate facilities. Every output is a nonempty subset of the candidate facilities and opens at most $k$ of them.

Assuming $P\ne NP$, it also proves that the infimum of all polynomial-time approximation factors in this model is exactly $1+2/e$.
