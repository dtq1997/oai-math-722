055 · 代数几何与复几何

Toda 的 Gepner 猜想与大体积稳定性

Toda's Gepner conjecture and large-volume stability

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

对每个光滑复五次三维簇，构造具有指定相位平移 2/52/52/5 的数值 Bridgeland 稳定性条件，从而证明 Toda 的 Gepner 猜想。

还在典范丛平凡的所有光滑复射影三维簇上，对每个充分大的体积构造数值 Bridgeland 稳定性条件，并给出精确的普通中心荷及含 Todd 类平方根的中心荷。

英文原文

Proves Toda's Gepner conjecture for every smooth complex quintic threefold, constructing a numerical Bridgeland stability condition with the prescribed phase shift 2/52/52/5.

Also constructs numerical Bridgeland stability conditions at every sufficiently large volume on all smooth projective complex threefolds with trivial canonical bundle, with the exact ordinary and square-root-Todd central charges.

OpenAI · overview.tex

论文

  1. 每个光滑五次三维簇上的 Gepner 稳定性条件

    A Gepner stability condition on every smooth quintic threefold

    摘要

    我们证明了 Toda 的归一化五次 Gepner 猜想。

    在每个光滑复五次三维簇上，我们构造了一个数值 Bridgeland 稳定性条件，使得先与超平面线丛作张量积、再在结构层处作球面扭转的操作将相位增加 2/5。

    英文原文

    We prove Toda's normalized quintic Gepner conjecture.

    On every smooth complex quintic threefold, we construct a numerical Bridgeland stability condition for which tensoring by the hyperplane bundle, followed by the spherical twist at the structure sheaf, increases phase by 2/5.

  2. 典范丛平凡的三维簇上预先指定的大体积中心荷

    Prescribed large-volume charges on threefolds with trivial canonical bundle

    摘要

    设 X 是典范丛平凡的光滑复射影三维簇。

    我们在每个充分大的体积处构造了数值 Bridgeland 稳定性条件，其中心荷精确为通常中心荷及Todd 平方根中心荷。

    同一个体积阈值适用于实扭转和丰沛方向的一个开集。

    所得稳定性条件在完整的数值 Grothendieck 群上具有支撑性质，并且点层是稳定的。

    此外，在每个光滑复射影三维簇上，我们证明了沿固定极化、超过一个对对象和扭转都一致的体积阈值时成立的强 tilt 不等式。

    英文原文

    Let X be a smooth projective complex threefold with trivial canonical bundle.

    We construct numerical Bridgeland stability conditions with the exact ordinary and square-root-Todd central charges at every sufficiently large volume.

    One volume threshold works on an open set of real twists and ample directions.

    The resulting stability conditions have the support property on the full numerical Grothendieck group and stable point sheaves.

    Separately, on every smooth projective complex threefold we prove a strong tilt inequality above a volume threshold uniform in the object and twist along a fixed polarization.
