Baum–Connes 与 Kadison–Kaplansky 反例

Counterexamples to Baum–Connes and Kadison–Kaplansky

OpenAI · 2026-10-063 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

通过装配映射的有理单射性失效，以及另一个满射性失效的结果，否定了无系数的约化 Baum–Connes 猜想。

一个有限生成的无挠例子体现了单射性的失效。

另外，一个无挠群的约化群 C∗C^*C∗ 代数中存在非平凡投影，从而否定了 Kadison–Kaplansky 猜想。

英文原文

Disproves the coefficient-free reduced Baum–Connes conjecture through a failure of rational injectivity and a separate failure of surjectivity of assembly.

A finitely generated torsion-free example witnesses the injectivity failure.

Separately, a torsion-free group has a nontrivial projection in its reduced group C∗C^*C∗-algebra, disproving the Kadison–Kaplansky conjecture.

  1. 约化 Baum–Connes 单射性的无挠反例

    A torsion-free counterexample to reduced Baum–Connes injectivity

    摘要

    我们反驳了无挠群的无系数约化 Baum–Connes 装配映射的有理单射性。

    具体而言，我们构造一个有限生成的无挠离散群，其零次装配映射的核中含有一个无限阶类。

    英文原文

    We disprove rational injectivity of the coefficient-free reduced Baum–Connes assembly map for torsion-free groups.

    Specifically, we construct a finitely generated torsion-free discrete group whose degree-zero assembly map has a kernel class of infinite order.

  2. Kadison–Kaplansky 投影猜想的无挠反例

    A torsion-free counterexample to the Kadison–Kaplansky projection conjecture

    摘要

    我们构造一个有限生成的无挠离散群，其约化群 C∗ 代数包含不同于零和单位元的投影。

    这反驳了 Kadison–Kaplansky 投影猜想。

    英文原文

    We construct a finitely generated torsion-free discrete group whose reduced group C∗-algebra contains a projection other than zero and the identity.

    This disproves the Kadison–Kaplansky projection conjecture.

  3. 约化 Baum–Connes 猜想的无理迹反例

    An irrational-trace counterexample to reduced Baum–Connes

    摘要

    我们构造一个有限生成的离散群，其约化群 C∗ 代数含有一个典范迹为无理数的投影。

    它的 K0 类不在无系数约化 Baum–Connes 装配映射的像中。

    这反驳了可数离散群的无系数约化 Baum–Connes 猜想。

    英文原文

    We construct a finitely generated discrete group whose reduced group C∗-algebra contains a projection of irrational canonical trace.

    Its K0-class lies outside the image of the coefficient-free reduced Baum–Connes assembly map.

    This disproves the coefficient-free reduced Baum–Connes conjecture for countable discrete groups.
