曲线特征标簇上的整点稠密性

Integral density on curve character varieties

首发 · 2026-10-061 篇论文部分形式化

解决了 Litt 的整点稠密性问题在曲线上行列式为一的情形。

对于每条光滑连通复代数曲线及每个秩，都存在一个数域，使该曲线的 SLr\mathrm{SL}_rSLr​ 特征标簇在此数域的完整整数环上的整点，在其每个分支中 Zariski 稠密。

允许指定拟幺幂的边界共轭类，包括非半单的共轭类。

英文原文

Resolves the determinant-one curve case of Litt's integral-density question.

For every smooth connected complex algebraic curve and every rank, integral points become Zariski dense in every component of its SLr\mathrm{SL}_rSLr​ character variety over the full ring of integers of one number field.

Prescribed quasi-unipotent boundary conjugacy classes are allowed, including nonsemisimple classes.

  1. 曲线的特征标簇上的整点

    Integral points on character varieties of curves

    已列入形式化清单
    摘要

    我们证明了光滑复曲线在每个秩下的 SLr 特征标簇上整点的潜在 Zariski 稠密性。

    该结果允许指定拟幺幂边界单值性，包括精确指定的非半单共轭类，并且在同一个有限扩张的完整整数环上，对每个分支都成立。

    这里，整性是在所在的特征标簇中衡量的，而精确的边界条件则施加于复表示。

    英文原文

    We prove potential Zariski density of integral points on SLr-character varieties of smooth complex curves in every rank.

    The result allows prescribed quasi-unipotent boundary monodromy, including exact nonsemisimple conjugacy classes, and holds on every component over the full ring of integers of one finite extension.

    Here integrality is measured in the ambient character variety, while the exact boundary conditions are imposed on the complex representation.

# Potential integral density on curve character varieties

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Integral points on character varieties of curves](../../preprints/Integral-points-on-character-varieties-of-curves-September-25-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The integral-density question asks whether integral points become Zariski dense in every component of a curve character variety after one finite extension of the number field. The linked formalization proves a supporting compatibility result for its marked-surface construction. Given a proper diagram with finitely many vertices, a chosen seam with maximal adjacent facet rank and a positive-genus parent, puncture data, an existing solution over a field, and a chosen boundary occurrence, the produced marked values satisfy every seam equation.

This is conditional compatibility of the produced solution. Potential integral density, including the prescribed-boundary and all-component conclusions, remains outside the selected statement.
