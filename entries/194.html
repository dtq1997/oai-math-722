Lech's multiplicity conjecture

对非零 Noether 局部环之间的每个平坦局部同态，证明了 e(R)≤e(S)e(R)\le e(S)e(R)≤e(S)，其中 eee 为 Hilbert–Samuel 重数。

英文原文

Proves e(R)≤e(S)e(R)\le e(S)e(R)≤e(S) for every flat local homomorphism of nonzero Noetherian local rings, where eee is Hilbert–Samuel multiplicity.

This resolves Lech's conjecture in every dimension and characteristic.

    摘要

    我们证明了 Lech 的重数猜想：在非零 Noether 局部环之间的平坦局部同态下，Hilbert–Samuel 重数不会减小。

    该结果在任意维数中都成立，对剩余域或特征均无限制。

    英文原文

    We prove Lech's multiplicity conjecture: Hilbert–Samuel multiplicity cannot decrease under a flat local homomorphism of nonzero Noetherian local rings.

    The result holds in arbitrary dimension, with no restrictions on the residue fields or characteristics.

# Lech’s multiplicity conjecture

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Lech's multiplicity conjecture](../../preprints/Lechs-multiplicity-conjecture-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

Lech's multiplicity conjecture compares Hilbert–Samuel multiplicities across flat local maps. The linked formalization covers a supporting characteristic-$p$ comparison over a complete Noetherian local domain $D$: for a complex satisfying the stated short-complex and finite-length homology conditions, its Frobenius multiplicity sequence converges to its Dutta multiplicity, and the Hilbert–Samuel multiplicity of $D$ is at most that Dutta multiplicity.

This selected statement is the complete-domain Dutta comparison. The paper's full flat-local result in arbitrary characteristic is outside it.
