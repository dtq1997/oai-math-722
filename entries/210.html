Foulkes 六次情形与二次稳定化

Foulkes’ conjecture for sixth powers and quadratic stabilization

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

证明了 Foulkes 猜想的六次幂情形：对每个 b≥6b\ge6b≥6 和有限维复向量空间 VVV，Sym⁡6(Sym⁡bV)\operatorname{Sym}^6(\operatorname{Sym}^bV)Sym6(SymbV) 都等变嵌入 Sym⁡b(Sym⁡6V)\operatorname{Sym}^b(\operatorname{Sym}^6V)Symb(Sym6V)。

更一般地，当 a≥2a\ge2a≥2 且 b≥a(a−1)b\ge a(a-1)b≥a(a−1) 时，典范乘法映射 Sym⁡b(Sym⁡aV)→Sym⁡a(Sym⁡bV)\operatorname{Sym}^b(\operatorname{Sym}^aV)\to\operatorname{Sym}^a(\operatorname{Sym}^bV)Symb(SymaV)→Syma(SymbV) 是满射，从而得到与维数无关的二次稳定化。

英文原文

Proves the sixth case of Foulkes’ conjecture: Sym⁡6(Sym⁡bV)\operatorname{Sym}^6(\operatorname{Sym}^bV)Sym6(SymbV) embeds equivariantly in Sym⁡b(Sym⁡6V)\operatorname{Sym}^b(\operatorname{Sym}^6V)Symb(Sym6V) for every b≥6b\ge6b≥6 and finite-dimensional complex VVV.

More generally, the canonical multiplication map Sym⁡b(Sym⁡aV)→Sym⁡a(Sym⁡bV)\operatorname{Sym}^b(\operatorname{Sym}^aV)\to\operatorname{Sym}^a(\operatorname{Sym}^bV)Symb(SymaV)→Syma(SymbV) is surjective for a≥2a\ge2a≥2 and b≥a(a−1)b\ge a(a-1)b≥a(a−1), giving dimension-independent quadratic stabilization.

# Foulkes' conjecture for sixth powers and quadratic stabilization

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Quadratic stabilization of the canonical Foulkes–Howe map](../../preprints/Quadratic-Stabilization-of-the-Canonical-Foulkes-Howe-Map-September-25-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The formalized result proves surjectivity of the canonical averaged Foulkes–Howe map $\mathrm{Sym}^b(\mathrm{Sym}^a\,V)\to\mathrm{Sym}^a(\mathrm{Sym}^b\,V)$ for every finite-dimensional complex vector space, $a\ge2$, and $b\ge a(a-1)$. It also covers the bijective $a=1$ case, the vanishing-on-products consequence, and the corresponding equivariant embedding in the reverse direction. The sixth-power specialization is covered for $b\ge30$; the companion's full range $b\ge6$ is not included.
