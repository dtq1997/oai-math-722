  1. 邻近 Lagrangian 猜想的一个反例

    A counterexample to the nearby Lagrangian conjecture

    摘要

    我们否定了不加限制的邻近 Lagrangian 猜想。

    对于某个充分大的偶整数 N，我们在 T∗(S9×SN−1)T^*(S^9\times S^{N-1})T∗(S9×SN−1) 中构造了一个闭的、正合的光滑嵌入 Lagrangian 子流形，它与底空间微分同胚，但不与零截面 Hamilton 同痕。

    英文原文

    We disprove the unrestricted nearby Lagrangian conjecture.

    For some sufficiently large even integer N, we construct a closed exact smoothly embedded Lagrangian in T∗(S9×SN−1)T^*(S^9\times S^{N-1})T∗(S9×SN−1) that is diffeomorphic to the base but is not Hamiltonian isotopic to the zero section.
