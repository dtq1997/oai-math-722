Shelah 最终范畴性猜想

Shelah's eventual categoricity conjecture

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

在 ZFC 中证明 Shelah 最终范畴性猜想：对 Löwenheim–Skolem 数的每个界，存在一个统一阈值，使得一个抽象初等类在该阈值以上某个基数处具有范畴性，就蕴含它在同一尾段的所有基数处均具有范畴性。

范畴性指在给定基数下同构意义上的唯一性。

在连续统假设下，一个已被提出的特定 Hanf 阈值未必足够。

英文原文

Proves Shelah's eventual categoricity conjecture in ZFC: for each bound on the Löwenheim–Skolem number, a uniform threshold makes categoricity of an abstract elementary class in one cardinal above that threshold imply categoricity throughout the same tail.

Categoricity means uniqueness up to isomorphism at a given cardinality.

Under the continuum hypothesis, a proposed specific Hanf threshold need not suffice.

  1. 连续统假设对指定范畴性阈值的阻碍

    A CH obstruction to a prescribed categoricity threshold

    主结果已列入形式化清单
    摘要

    假设连续统假设成立，我们构造了一个 Löwenheim–Skolem 数为 ℵ0 的抽象初等类，它在每个充分大的基数上都是范畴的，但在基数 ℶω2\beth_{\omega_2}ℶω2​​ 上至少有两个不同构的模型。

    因此，范畴性不能向下转移到所提出的界 ℶ(2ℵ0)+\beth_{(2^{\aleph_0})^+}ℶ(2ℵ0​)+​，该界在 CH 下等于 ℶω2\beth_{\omega_2}ℶω2​​。

    从而，如果 ZFC 是相容的，那么 Shelah 范畴性猜想的指定阈值形式在 ZFC 中不可证。

    英文原文

    Assuming the continuum hypothesis, we construct an abstract elementary class with Löwenheim–Skolem number ℵ0 that is categorical in every sufficiently large cardinal but has at least two nonisomorphic models of cardinality ℶω2\beth_{\omega_2}ℶω2​​.

    Thus categoricity does not transfer down to the proposed bound ℶ(2ℵ0)+\beth_{(2^{\aleph_0})^+}ℶ(2ℵ0​)+​, which equals ℶω2\beth_{\omega_2}ℶω2​​ under CH.

    Consequently, if ZFC is consistent, the prescribed-threshold form of Shelah's categoricity conjecture is not provable in ZFC.

  2. 抽象初等类的最终范畴性

    Eventual categoricity for abstract elementary classes

    摘要

    我们在 ZFC 中证明了抽象初等类的 Shelah 最终范畴性猜想。

    对于 Löwenheim–Skolem 数的每个无穷界，都存在一个一致阈值，使得在任意一个不小于该阈值的基数上的范畴性，都蕴含在每个不小于同一阈值的基数上的范畴性。

    英文原文

    We prove Shelah's eventual categoricity conjecture for abstract elementary classes in ZFC.

    For each infinite bound on the Löwenheim–Skolem number there is a uniform threshold such that categoricity in any one cardinal at or above that threshold implies categoricity in every cardinal at or above the same threshold.

# Shelah's eventual categoricity and the prescribed-threshold obstruction

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [A CH obstruction to a prescribed categoricity threshold](../../preprints/A-CH-Obstruction-to-a-Prescribed-Categoricity-Threshold-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

Under the continuum hypothesis, the formalized result refutes the proposed transfer of categoricity down to the Hanf threshold. It gives an abstract elementary class in a countable relational language, with Löwenheim–Skolem number $\aleph_0$, that has two nonisomorphic models at $\beth_{\omega_2}=h(\aleph_0)$ but is categorical in every cardinal at least $\beth_{(2^{\aleph_1})^+}$.

No amalgamation, joint embedding, tameness, or absence-of-maximal-models assumption is imposed. The later canonical-point obstruction and the separate eventual-categoricity theorem are not included.
