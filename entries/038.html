Fujita 自由性猜想

Fujita's freeness conjecture

首发 · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

在任意维数下，以最优的界证明了 Fujita 自由性猜想：对于维数为 nnn 的光滑复射影簇 XXX 及丰沛线丛 LLL，每个满足 m≥n+1m\ge n+1m≥n+1 的整数都使伴随线丛 KX+mLK_X+mLKX​+mL 整体生成。

英文原文

Proves Fujita's freeness conjecture at its sharp bound in every dimension: for a smooth projective complex variety XXX of dimension nnn and an ample line bundle LLL, the adjoint KX+mLK_X+mLKX​+mL is globally generated for every integer m≥n+1m\ge n+1m≥n+1.

论文

  1. Fujita 自由性猜想

    Fujita's freeness conjecture

    摘要

    我们证明了 Fujita 的自由性猜想。

    若 X 是维数为 n 的光滑复射影簇，L 是丰沛线丛，则对每个整数 m≥n+1m\geq n+1m≥n+1，KX+mLK_X+mLKX​+mL 都是整体生成的。

    英文原文

    We prove Fujita's freeness conjecture.

    If X is a smooth complex projective variety of dimension n and L is an ample line bundle, then KX+mLK_X+mLKX​+mL is globally generated for every integer m≥n+1m\geq n+1m≥n+1.
