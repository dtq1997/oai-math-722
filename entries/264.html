双端 Kerr 数据附近的强宇宙审查

Strong cosmic censorship near two-ended Kerr data

OpenAI · 2026-10-063 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

证明了每个固定的旋转次极端 Kerr 桥附近的局部强宇宙监督猜想。

光滑完备的双端渐近平坦真空初始数据的某个加权光滑邻域内，有一个稠密的GδG_\deltaGδ​子集，其中每组数据的完整最大整体双曲发展都不存在弱联络局部平方可积的未来连续非退化延拓。

不施加任何对称性；延拓不必满足真空方程。

英文原文

Proves local strong cosmic censorship near each fixed rotating subextremal Kerr bridge.

A dense GδG_\deltaGδ​ subset of a weighted smooth neighborhood of smooth complete two-ended asymptotically flat vacuum data has full maximal globally hyperbolic developments with no future continuous nondegenerate extension whose weak connection is locally square-integrable.

No symmetry is imposed; extensions need not satisfy the vacuum equations.

论文

  1. 固定 Kerr 时空附近一般情形下在平方可积联络正则性下的未来不可延拓性

    Generic Future Inextendibility with Square-Integrable Connection Near a Fixed Kerr Spacetime

    摘要

    对于每个质量为 M > 0、旋转参数为 0<∣a∣<M0\lt |\mathfrak a|\lt M0<∣a∣<M 的固定旋转次极端 Kerr 背景，我们证明，在其完整双端桥附近，使整个极大全局双曲发展具有未来 C0∩Wloc1,2C^0\cap W^{1,2}_{\mathrm{loc}}C0∩Wloc1,2​ 延拓的光滑真空数据，在加权光滑拓扑中构成第一纲集。

    延拓度量是连续且非退化的，其弱联络局部平方可积。

    该邻域仅要求第十个加权半范数足够小，而拓扑则检验每个有限阶。

    英文原文

    For each fixed rotating subextremal Kerr background with mass M > 0 and rotation 0<∣a∣<M0\lt |\mathfrak a|\lt M0<∣a∣<M, we prove that the smooth vacuum data near its complete two-ended bridge whose full maximal globally hyperbolic development admits a future C0∩Wloc1,2C^0\cap W^{1,2}_{\mathrm{loc}}C0∩Wloc1,2​ extension form a meagre set in the weighted smooth topology.

    The extension metric is continuous and nondegenerate, and its weak connection is locally square-integrable.

    The neighborhood requires smallness of only the tenth weighted seminorm, while the topology tests every finite order.

  2. 旋转次极端 Kerr 时空附近一般情形下的未来 C1 不可延拓性

    Generic C1 Future Inextendibility Near Rotating Subextremal Kerr Spacetimes

    摘要

    对于每个质量为 M > 0、旋转参数为 0<a<M0\lt \mathfrak a\lt M0<a<M 的固定 Kerr 时空，我们证明，附近的光滑完备双端真空数据的一个稠密 Gδ 集，其极大全局双曲发展没有未来 C1 延拓。

    邻域和一般性是在加权光滑拓扑中定义的，而环境延拓可以是真空以外的。

    英文原文

    For each fixed Kerr spacetime with mass M > 0 and rotation 0<a<M0\lt \mathfrak a\lt M0<a<M, we prove that a dense Gδ set of nearby smooth, complete two-ended vacuum data has a maximal globally hyperbolic development with no future C1 extension.

    The neighborhood and genericity are defined in a weighted smooth topology, and ambient extensions may be nonvacuum.

  3. Kerr 附近演化的定量分析与一般情形下的未来 C2 不可延拓性

    Quantitative Near-Kerr Evolution and Generic C2 Future Inextendibility

    摘要

    对于每个固定的旋转次极端 Kerr 桥，我们证明，在由一个有限阶半范数定义的邻域中，使整个极大全局双曲发展具有 C2 未来延拓的光滑真空数据构成第一纲集。

    该拓扑允许任意满足符号界的渐近平坦尾部，且不施加任何对称性条件。

    英文原文

    For every fixed rotating subextremal Kerr bridge, we prove that smooth vacuum data admitting a C2 future extension of their full maximal globally hyperbolic development form a meagre set in a neighborhood defined by one finite-order seminorm.

    The topology allows arbitrary symbol-bounded asymptotically flat tails and imposes no symmetry.
