三角格的普适最优性

Universal optimality of the triangular lattice

首发 · 2026-10-064 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

证明了在中心圆盘密度为一的局部有限平面配置中，对每个以距离平方为自变量的非负完全单调势，三角格都使每粒子能量的下极限达到最小。

它还使 0<s<20<s<20<s<2 时单位背景下的重整化 Riesz 能量以及 Coulomb 能量达到最小，从而解决了 Sandier–Serfaty 猜想，以及二维 Brauchart–Hardin–Saff 关于最优球面对数能量线性项的猜想。

英文原文

Proves that the triangular lattice minimizes the lower limit of energy per particle for every nonnegative completely monotone potential of squared distance among locally finite planar configurations of centered-disk density one.

It also minimizes unit-background renormalized Riesz energies for 0<s<20<s<20<s<2 and Coulomb energy, resolving Sandier–Serfaty and the two-dimensional Brauchart–Hardin–Saff conjecture on the linear term of optimal spherical logarithmic energy.

  1. 三角格普适最优性的原子证书

    An atomic certificate for triangular-lattice universal optimality

    摘要

    我们证明，对于距离平方的每个非负完全单调函数，在所有按中心圆盘计算的密度为一的局部有限平面构型中，密度为一的三角格使每粒子的下能量最小。

    这一比较也包括无穷能量。

    证明利用一个原子插值证书构造最优的高斯 Fourier 下界函数。

    英文原文

    We prove that the density-one triangular lattice minimizes the lower energy per particle for every nonnegative completely monotone function of squared distance, among all locally finite planar configurations of centered disk density one.

    The comparison includes infinite energies.

    The proof constructs sharp Gaussian Fourier minorants using an atomic interpolation certificate.

  2. 三角格的普适最优性

    Universal optimality of the triangular lattice

    摘要

    我们证明平面上三角格的普适能量极小性。

    在中心密度为一的局部有限构型中，对于距离平方的每个非负完全单调函数，三角格使中心球能量平均值的下极限最小，包括能量为无穷的情形。

    我们还证明了三角格对于平面对数重整化能量和 Riesz 重整化能量的极小性，其中 Riesz 情形满足 0<s<20\lt s\lt 20<s<2，并证明了相应的 jellium 最小值。

    证明使用最优的高斯 Fourier 界、正混合以及热核比较。

    英文原文

    We prove universal energy minimality for the triangular lattice in the plane.

    Among locally finite configurations of centered density one, it minimizes the lower limit of centered-ball energy averages for every nonnegative completely monotone function of squared distance, including when the energy is infinite.

    We also prove triangular minimality for planar logarithmic and Riesz renormalized energies, with 0<s<20\lt s\lt 20<s<2 in the Riesz case, and the corresponding jellium minima.

    The proof uses sharp Gaussian Fourier bounds, positive mixtures, and heat-kernel comparison.

  3. 平面圆堆积中达到最优界的 Fourier 证书

    A sharp Fourier certificate for planar circle packing

    摘要

    我们解决平面的 Cohn–Elkies 最优性猜想：两点 Fourier 界达到最优圆堆积密度 π/(23)\pi/(2\sqrt3)π/(23​)。

    我们构造一个径向 Schwartz 证书，并利用严格区间算术和解析估计证明其全局符号条件。

    同一证书还重新得到三角堆积在周期性等号情形中的经典唯一性结论。

    英文原文

    We resolve the planar Cohn–Elkies sharpness conjecture: the two-point Fourier bound attains the optimal circle-packing density π/(23)\pi/(2\sqrt3)π/(23​).

    We construct a radial Schwartz certificate and prove its global sign conditions using rigorous interval arithmetic and analytic estimates.

    The same certificate recovers the classical uniqueness of the triangular packing among periodic equality cases.

  4. 三角格对平面 Coulomb 重整化能量的极小性

    Triangular minimality for planar Coulomb renormalized energy

    摘要

    我们证明 Sandier–Serfaty 猜想：在具有单位均匀背景的所有容许无旋场中，余体积为一的三角格使平面 Coulomb 重整化能量最小。

    结合 Bétermin 和 Sandier 的渐近公式，这还证明了关于单位二维球面上最优有序对对数能量之线性项的 Brauchart–Hardin–Saff 猜想。

    证明使用直接的 Voronoi 胞腔比较和严格区间算术。

    英文原文

    We prove the Sandier–Serfaty conjecture: the triangular lattice of covolume one minimizes planar Coulomb renormalized energy over all admissible curl-free fields with a unit uniform background.

    Combined with Bétermin and Sandier's asymptotic formula, this also proves the Brauchart–Hardin–Saff conjecture for the linear term of optimal ordered-pair logarithmic energy on the unit two-sphere.

    The proof uses a direct Voronoi-cell comparison with rigorous interval arithmetic.

# Triangular-lattice optimality, long-range Riesz and Coulomb energies, and spherical logarithmic energy

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [An atomic certificate for triangular-lattice universal optimality](../../preprints/An-atomic-certificate-for-triangular-lattice-universal-optimality-September-26-2026/paper.pdf)
- [A sharp Fourier certificate for planar circle packing](../../preprints/A-sharp-Fourier-certificate-for-planar-circle-packing-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The formalization proves that the density-one triangular lattice minimizes lower energy per particle among all locally finite planar configurations of centered-disk density one, for every nonnegative completely monotone function of squared distance. Infinite energies are allowed in the comparison.

It also constructs sharp radial Schwartz minorants for every Gaussian potential: each minorant lies below the Gaussian, has nonnegative real Fourier transform, agrees with the Gaussian at nonzero triangular-lattice points, and vanishes on nonzero dual-lattice points after Fourier transformation. For the stated parameter range, the formalization includes the explicit atomic interpolation construction.

The planar Cohn–Elkies sharpness conjecture asks whether the two-point Fourier method attains the optimal circle-packing density. The formalization constructs a radial Schwartz function $f$ on $\mathbb R^2$ with $\widehat f(0)=1$, $f(0)=2/\sqrt3$, $\widehat f$ real and nonnegative everywhere, and $f(x)\le0$ whenever $\|x\|\ge1$. These are the sharp Fourier certificate conditions yielding density $\pi/(2\sqrt3)$. The separate uniqueness statement for periodic equality cases is outside this selected theorem.
