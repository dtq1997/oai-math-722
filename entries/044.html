箭图的等变上同调 Hikita 猜想

The equivariant cohomological Hikita conjecture for quivers

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

对每个有限箭图，包括含环及重箭的情形，在维数向量、框架向量及其作用与规范群作用可交换的 flavor 环面任意给定时，证明等变上同调 Hikita 对应成立。

当每个半稳定点都稳定、且规范群的作用自由时，Nakajima 簇的等变上同调典范同构于其 flavor 形变 Coulomb 分支的余特征不动点子概形的坐标环。

英文原文

Proves the equivariant cohomological Hikita correspondence for every finite quiver, including loops and multiple arrows, with arbitrary dimension and framing vectors and commuting flavor torus.

When every semistable point is stable and the gauge action is free, the equivariant cohomology of the Nakajima variety is canonically the coordinate ring of the scheme-theoretic cocharacter-fixed locus of its flavor-deformed Coulomb branch.

论文

  1. 任意箭图的等变上同调 Hikita 猜想

    The equivariant cohomological Hikita conjecture for arbitrary quivers

    摘要

    我们证明了任意有限箭图的等变上同调 Hikita 猜想，包括带环和重箭头的箭图。

    对于任意维数向量和标架向量、任意其作用与规范作用可交换的 flavor 环面，以及半稳定点集均为稳定点且其上规范作用自由的稳定性特征，Nakajima 簇的等变上同调典范同构于 flavor 形变 Coulomb 分支上稳定性余特征的不动点子概形的坐标环。

    这是公共系数环上的分次代数同构，保留了幂零元，也包括空的情形。

    英文原文

    We prove the equivariant cohomological Hikita conjecture for arbitrary finite quivers, including loops and multiple arrows.

    For any dimension and framing vectors, any commuting flavor torus, and a stability character whose semistable locus is stable and has free gauge action, the equivariant cohomology of the Nakajima variety is canonically isomorphic to the coordinate ring of the scheme-theoretic fixed locus of the stability cocharacter on the flavor-deformed Coulomb branch.

    This is an isomorphism of graded algebras over the common coefficient ring, retaining nilpotents and including the empty case.
