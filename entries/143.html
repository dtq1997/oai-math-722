Hilbert 第十六问题的一致极限环界

Uniform limit-cycle bounds in Hilbert's sixteenth problem

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

解决了 Hilbert 第十六问题中的一致有界性断言：实平面多项式向量场的孤立周期轨道数有一个仅依赖于其次数的有限常数上界。

对于经典五次 Liénard 系统，极限环数的精确最大值为二。

英文原文

Resolves the uniform boundedness assertion in Hilbert's sixteenth problem: the number of isolated periodic orbits of a real planar polynomial vector field is bounded by a finite constant depending only on its degree.

For classical quintic Liénard systems, the exact maximum is two limit cycles.

  1. 平面多项式极限环的一致界

    Uniform bounds for planar polynomial limit cycles

    摘要

    对于每个次数，我们证明，实平面多项式向量场的孤立周期轨道数都有一个仅依赖于该次数的有限常数上界。

    这确立了 Hilbert 第十六问题第二部分中的一致有界性断言。

    证明使用嵌套复区域上渐近展开的分离，以及一个有限维计数论证。

    英文原文

    For every degree, we prove that the number of isolated periodic orbits of a real planar polynomial vector field is bounded by a finite constant depending only on that degree.

    This establishes the uniform boundedness assertion in the second part of Hilbert's sixteenth problem.

    The proof uses separation of asymptotic expansions on nested complex domains and a finite-dimensional counting argument.

    Two limit cycles for quintic Liénard systems

# Hilbert's sixteenth problem: uniform bounds for limit cycles

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Two limit cycles for quintic Liénard systems](../../preprints/two-limit-cycles-for-quintic-lienard-systems-September-24-2026/two-limit-cycles-for-quintic-lienard-systems-September-24-2026.pdf)

## Scope

For the quintic Liénard system $x'=y-F(x)$, $y'=-x$, the formalized result proves that every real polynomial $F$ of degree at most five yields at most two limit cycles, and that some such $F$ yields exactly two. Limit cycles are isolated images of nonconstant periodic solutions. No sign, parity, hyperbolicity, or amplitude restriction is imposed.
