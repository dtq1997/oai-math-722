Ryser 覆盖与 Gyárfás 树覆盖反例

Counterexamples to Ryser's covering and Gyárfás's tree-cover conjectures

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

对于每个充分大的素数 qqq，构造了覆盖数为 q+1q+1q+1 而非所预测上界 qqq 的相交 (q+1)(q+1)(q+1) 部、(q+1)(q+1)(q+1) 一致超图，从而否定了 Ryser 覆盖猜想。

在扩域上的另一个构造也否定了 Gyárfás 的单色树覆盖猜想。

英文原文

Disproves Ryser's covering conjecture by constructing intersecting (q+1)(q+1)(q+1)-partite, (q+1)(q+1)(q+1)-uniform hypergraphs with covering number q+1q+1q+1, rather than the predicted bound qqq, for every sufficiently large prime qqq.

A separate construction over extension fields also disproves Gyárfás's monochromatic tree-cover conjecture.

  1. 素数阶下 Ryser 猜想的平衡反例

    Balanced counterexamples to Ryser's conjecture at prime orders

    摘要

    对于每个充分大的素数 q，我们构造一个有限的相交 (q+1)(q+1)(q+1) 部 (q+1)(q+1)(q+1) 一致超图，其覆盖数为 q+1q+1q+1，且每一部恰有 q+1q+1q+1 个非孤立顶点。

    这否定了 Ryser 覆盖猜想，即使限定为各部大小相同的相交超图，该猜想也不成立。

    英文原文

    For every sufficiently large prime q, we construct a finite intersecting (q+1)(q+1)(q+1)-partite (q+1)(q+1)(q+1)-uniform hypergraph with covering number q+1q+1q+1 and exactly q+1q+1q+1 nonisolated vertices in each part.

    This disproves Ryser's covering conjecture, even for intersecting hypergraphs with equally sized parts.

  2. Ryser 覆盖猜想的一个反例

    A counterexample to Ryser's covering conjecture

    对于每个充分大的素数 s≡2(mod3)s\equiv2\pmod3s≡2(mod3) 和每个充分大的奇整数 n，其中后者的阈值依赖于 s，我们构造一个覆盖数为 sn+1s^n+1sn+1 的相交 (sn+1)(s^n+1)(sn+1) 部 (sn+1)(s^n+1)(sn+1) 一致超图。

    This disproves Ryser's covering conjecture in its intersecting case.

# Counterexamples to Ryser’s covering conjecture

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Balanced counterexamples to Ryser's conjecture at prime orders](../../preprints/Balanced-Counterexamples-to-Rysers-Conjecture-at-Prime-Orders-September-27-2026/paper.pdf)
- [A counterexample to Ryser's covering conjecture](../../preprints/A-Counterexample-to-Rysers-Covering-Conjecture-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

Ryser's covering conjecture predicts that an intersecting $r$-partite hypergraph has a vertex cover of size at most $r-1$. The formalization proves that every sufficiently large prime $q$ has a finite intersecting $(q+1)$-partite, $(q+1)$-uniform hypergraph with covering number $q+1$ and exactly $q+1$ nonisolated vertices in each part. Thus the conjecture fails even with equal part sizes. The prime threshold is existential.

Ryser's covering conjecture predicts $\tau\le(r-1)\nu$ for an $r$-partite hypergraph, where $\tau$ and $\nu$ are its covering and matching numbers. The formalized constructions give finite intersecting $r$-partite $r$-uniform hypergraphs with $\nu=1$ and $\tau=r$, contradicting the bound. The ranks have the form $r=p^n+1$: one fixed prime $p\equiv2\pmod3$ works for all sufficiently large prime degrees $n$, and a second result covers every sufficiently large such prime $p$ and every sufficiently large odd $n$, with the degree threshold allowed to depend on $p$. Infinitely many ranks occur. Equal part sizes and numerical thresholds are not asserted.
