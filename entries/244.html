划分原理不蕴含选择公理

The partition principle does not imply choice

首发 · 2026-10-061 篇论文部分形式化

假设 ZF 相容，构造一个模型，其中每个集合的任意满射像都可单射到该集合中，但选择公理不成立。

以序数为指标的族的选择公理仍然成立。

从 ZFC 的任意可数传递模型出发，另一个独立构造给出一个具有这些性质的传递对称扩张，而且不增加由基模型元素组成的可数序列。

英文原文

Assuming ZF is consistent, constructs a model in which every surjective image of a set injects into that set, yet the axiom of choice fails.

Choice for ordinal-indexed families still holds.

From any countable transitive model of ZFC, a separate construction gives a transitive symmetric extension with these properties and no new countable sequences of ground-model elements.

    The Partition Principle does not imply Choice

    已列入形式化清单
    摘要

    我们证明，划分原理不蕴含选择公理：如果 ZF 是相容的，那么 ZF 加上划分原理、序数索引族的选择公理以及选择公理的否定，也仍然相容。

    另外，在每个 ZFC 的可数传递模型之上，我们构造了该理论的一个传递对称模型，它具有相同的序数，且不添加由基模型元素构成的新的可数序列。

    英文原文

    We prove that the Partition Principle does not imply the Axiom of Choice: if ZF is consistent, then so is ZF with the Partition Principle, Choice for ordinal-indexed families, and the negation of the Axiom of Choice.

    Separately, over every countable transitive model of ZFC, we construct a transitive symmetric model of this theory with the same ordinals and no new countable sequences of ground elements.

# The Partition Principle does not imply Choice

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [The Partition Principle does not imply Choice](../../preprints/The-Partition-Principle-does-not-imply-Choice-September-24-2026/partition-principle-without-choice.pdf)

## Scope

The Partition Principle says that every surjection admits an injection in the reverse direction. The formalization proves the relative-consistency implication: if ZF is consistent, then so is ZF with the Partition Principle, Choice for ordinal-indexed families, and failure of the Axiom of Choice.

The linked model-construction result also constructs a transitive model of the Partition Principle without Choice from an externally countable transitive ground model satisfying the stated axioms, including Choice, and containing an internal strongly inaccessible cardinal. The paper's stronger transitive-model preservation assertions are outside that selected construction.
