PPAD 的准线性 PCP 猜想

The quasilinear PCP-for-PPAD conjecture

首发 · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

解决了准线性 PCP-for-PPAD 猜想。

一个长度为 NNN 的 End-of-Line 实例可归约为总长度为 N(log⁡N)O(1)N(\log N)^{O(1)}N(logN)O(1) 的数值电路约束，使得任何具有多项式长度编码的有理赋值，只要以固定精度满足除固定比例之外的全部约束，就能给出一个端点解。

这样的赋值总是存在，因此只需准线性的规模开销，就能实现稳健的局部验证。

英文原文

Resolves the quasilinear PCP-for-PPAD conjecture.

An End-of-Line instance of length NNN reduces to numerical circuit constraints of total length N(log⁡N)O(1)N(\log N)^{O(1)}N(logN)O(1) such that any polynomially encoded rational assignment satisfying all but a fixed fraction to fixed accuracy yields an endpoint solution.

Such assignments always exist, giving robust local verification with only quasilinear size overhead.

  1. PCP-for-PPAD 猜想：一个准线性归约

    The PCP-for-PPAD conjecture: a quasilinear reduction

    摘要

    我们证明 Babichenko、Papadimitriou 和 Rubinstein 的准线性大小 PCP-for-PPAD 猜想。

    存在固定的正有理常数 ε 和 δ，以及一个确定性多项式时间归约，将二进制长度为 N 的 End-of-Line 实例转换为总二进制长度为 N(log⁡N)O(1)N(\log N)^{O(1)}N(logN)O(1) 的广义电路。

    对于编码长度为多项式量级、且除占比不超过 δ 的门之外 ε-满足其余所有门的任意有理赋值，无论哪些门失效，都可以在多项式时间内从中恢复原 End-of-Line 实例的一个解。

    这样的赋值总是存在，且其编码长度由同一个固定多项式所界定。

    英文原文

    We prove the quasilinear-size PCP-for-PPAD conjecture of Babichenko, Papadimitriou, and Rubinstein.

    There are fixed positive rational constants ε and δ and a deterministic polynomial-time reduction that transforms an End-of-Line instance of binary length N into a generalized circuit of total binary length N(log⁡N)O(1)N(\log N)^{O(1)}N(logN)O(1).

    From any rational assignment of polynomial encoding length that ε-satisfies all but a δ fraction of the gates, a solution to the original End-of-Line instance can be recovered in polynomial time, regardless of which gates fail.

    Such assignments always exist, with one fixed polynomial bound on their encoding length.
