任意维数的 Hilbert–Smith 猜想

The Hilbert–Smith conjecture in every dimension

首发 · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

每个在连通有限维拓扑流形上忠实且联合连续作用的局部紧、第二可数 Hausdorff 群都是 Lie 群。

这对无边界的 Hausdorff 第二可数流形证明了所有有限维数下的 Hilbert–Smith 猜想。

英文原文

Every locally compact second-countable Hausdorff group acting faithfully and jointly continuously on a connected finite-dimensional topological manifold is a Lie group.

This proves the Hilbert–Smith conjecture in all finite dimensions, for Hausdorff second-countable manifolds without boundary.

OpenAI · overview.tex

论文

  1. 所有有限维数中的 Hilbert–Smith 猜想

    The Hilbert–Smith conjecture in every finite dimension

    摘要

    我们证明每个有限维数中的 Hilbert–Smith 猜想：每个忠实且联合连续地作用于连通、Hausdorff、第二可数、有限维、无边界拓扑流形的局部紧、第二可数 Hausdorff 群都是 Lie 群。

    英文原文

    We prove the Hilbert–Smith conjecture in every finite dimension: every locally compact second-countable Hausdorff group acting faithfully and jointly continuously on a connected Hausdorff second-countable finite-dimensional topological manifold without boundary is a Lie group.
