素数前驱的 Ford–Konyagin–Luca 猜想

The Ford–Konyagin–Luca conjecture on prime predecessors

OpenAI · 2026-10-063 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

Proves that the normalized ordered logarithms of the prime factors of p−1p-1p−1, counted with multiplicity, converge jointly to the Poisson–Dirichlet law PD(1)\mathrm{PD}(1)PD(1) as ppp ranges uniformly over primes up to xxx and x→∞x\to\inftyx→∞.

This resolves the Ford–Konyagin–Luca conjecture.

  1. 加权伸缩图、光滑的素数平移值与 Euler 函数的纤维

    Weighted dilation graphs, smooth shifted primes and totient fibers

    摘要

    我们证明了 Erdős 关于 Euler 函数最大纤维的猜想：对于每个 ε > 0，都有无穷多个正整数 n 具有多于 n1−εn^{1-\varepsilon }n1−ε 个原像。

    我们还证明，对于每个固定的 δ > 0，2x<p≤5x2x\lt p\le5x2x<p≤5x 中至少有 x1−o(1)x^{1-o(1)}x1−o(1) 个素数 p，其前驱数没有超过 xδ 的素因子。

    英文原文

    We prove Erdős's conjecture on the largest fibers of Euler's totient function: for every ε > 0, infinitely many positive integers n have more than n1−εn^{1-\varepsilon }n1−ε preimages.

    We also show that, for every fixed δ > 0, there are at least x1−o(1)x^{1-o(1)}x1−o(1) primes p in 2x<p≤5x2x\lt p\le5x2x<p≤5x whose predecessors have no prime factor exceeding xδ.

  2. 素数减一所得整数的 Poisson–Dirichlet 分布律

    The Poisson-Dirichlet law for prime predecessors

    摘要

    对于从 3≤p≤x3\le p\le x3≤p≤x 中均匀选取的素数 p，将 p−1p-1p−1 的素因子计重数按降序排列。

    当 x→∞x\to\inftyx→∞ 时，它们的对数除以 log⁡(p−1)\log(p-1)log(p−1) 后，在每个有限维联合分布意义下都收敛于参数为一的 Poisson–Dirichlet 分布。

    这证明了 Ford、Konyagin 和 Luca 的猜想。

    英文原文

    For a prime p chosen uniformly from 3≤p≤x3\le p\le x3≤p≤x, list the prime factors of p−1p-1p−1 in decreasing order, with multiplicity.

    As x→∞x\to\inftyx→∞, their logarithms, divided by log⁡(p−1)\log(p-1)log(p−1), converge in every finite joint distribution to the Poisson–Dirichlet distribution with parameter one.

    This proves the conjecture of Ford, Konyagin and Luca.

  3. 素数减一所得整数的素因子个数为偶数

    Prime Predecessors with an Even Number of Prime Factors

    摘要

    我们证明，存在无穷多个素数 p，使得 p−1p-1p−1 无平方因子且具有偶数个素因子。

    等价地，存在无穷多个满足 μ(p−1)=1\mu(p-1)=1μ(p−1)=1 的素数 p。

    英文原文

    We prove that there are infinitely many primes p for which p−1p-1p−1 is squarefree and has an even number of prime factors.

    Equivalently, there are infinitely many primes p with μ(p−1)=1\mu(p-1)=1μ(p−1)=1.
