正温 Bose–Einstein 凝聚与量子耗尽

Positive-temperature Bose–Einstein condensation and quantum depletion

OpenAI · 2026-10-065 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

证明了三维硬球气体的精确正则 Gibbs 态存在 Bose–Einstein 凝聚：对每个固定的排斥距离和充分小的固定密度，都存在一个与体积无关的严格正温度，使热力学极限下的凝聚分数为正。

在零温下，对于硬球以及具有正散射长度的固定、有界、非负、径向、有限程势，证明 Bogoliubov 量子耗尽的首项定律，其中先取热力学极限，再取稀薄极限。

英文原文

Proves Bose–Einstein condensation for the exact canonical Gibbs state of the three-dimensional hard-sphere gas: each fixed exclusion distance and sufficiently small fixed density admit a strictly positive temperature, independent of volume, with positive condensate fraction in the thermodynamic limit.

At zero temperature, proves the Bogoliubov leading quantum-depletion law for hard spheres and fixed bounded nonnegative radial finite-range potentials of positive scattering length, taking the thermodynamic limit before the dilute limit.

  1. 稀薄硬球气体在正温度下的 Bose–Einstein 凝聚

    Bose–Einstein condensation at positive temperature in the dilute hard-sphere gas

    摘要

    我们证明了三维稀薄硬球气体在正温度下的 Bose–Einstein 凝聚。

    对于每个固定的排斥距离和每个充分小的固定密度，都存在一个与体积无关的严格正温度，使该温度下精确的正则 Gibbs 态在热力学极限中具有正的凝聚比例。

    凝聚体占据常数轨道。

    英文原文

    We prove Bose–Einstein condensation at positive temperature in the three-dimensional dilute hard-sphere gas.

    For each fixed exclusion distance and every sufficiently small fixed density, there is a strictly positive temperature, independent of the volume, at which the exact canonical Gibbs state has a positive condensate fraction in the thermodynamic limit.

    The condensate occupies the constant orbital.

    Quantum Depletion and Momentum Distribution in the Dilute Hard-Sphere Bose Gas

    摘要

    我们证明了零温下三维硬球 Bose 气体中耗尽粒子的完整标度化 Bogoliubov 动量分布。

    在取稀薄极限之前，先在每个固定密度下取热力学极限，且关于所有纯基态和混合基态一致成立。

    英文原文

    We prove the full scaled Bogoliubov momentum distribution for the depleted particles in the three-dimensional hard-sphere Bose gas at zero temperature.

    The thermodynamic limit is taken at each fixed density before the dilute limit, uniformly over all pure and mixed ground states.

    All thermodynamic accumulation values have the same dilute asymptotic: the limiting nonzero-momentum occupation measure has total mass 8/(3π)8/(3\sqrt\pi)8/(3π​), giving the depletion fraction 83πρa3+o(ρa3)\frac{8}{3\sqrt\pi}\sqrt{\rho a^3}+o(\sqrt{\rho a^3})3π​8​ρa3​+o(ρa3​) for density ρ and hard-sphere exclusion distance a.

  3. 固定有界排斥势下的量子耗尽

    Quantum Depletion for Fixed Bounded Repulsive Potentials

    摘要

    对于具有固定的有界、非负、径向、有限程相互作用且散射长度 a 为正的三维 Bose 气体，我们证明了其基态的 Bogoliubov 量子耗尽渐近式。

    在取稀薄极限之前，先在每个固定密度 ρ 下取热力学极限。

    当 ρ↓0\rho\downarrow0ρ↓0 时，常数模之外的粒子比例的每个热力学聚值均为 83πρa3+o(ρa3)\frac{8}{3\sqrt\pi}\sqrt{\rho a^3}+o(\sqrt{\rho a^3})3π​8​ρa3​+o(ρa3​)。

    该断言关于基态密度矩阵一致成立，且不要求占据数具有唯一的热力学极限。

    英文原文

    We prove the Bogoliubov quantum-depletion asymptotic for the ground state of a three-dimensional Bose gas with a fixed bounded, nonnegative, radial interaction of finite range and positive scattering length a.

    The thermodynamic limit is taken at each fixed density ρ before the dilute limit.

    Every thermodynamic accumulation value of the fraction outside the constant mode is 83πρa3+o(ρa3)\frac{8}{3\sqrt\pi}\sqrt{\rho a^3}+o(\sqrt{\rho a^3})3π​8​ρa3​+o(ρa3​) as ρ↓0\rho\downarrow0ρ↓0.

    The assertion is uniform over ground-state density matrices and does not require the occupation to have a unique thermodynamic limit.

  4. 稀薄 Bose 气体凝聚比例关于密度的一致界

    A density-uniform condensate bound for dilute Bose gases

    摘要

    对三维中每个固定的、有界可测、非负、径向、有限作用程且并非几乎处处为零的相互作用，我们证明常数轨道凝聚比例有一个正下界，该下界对所有充分小的密度以及零与密度平方之间的所有温度一致成立。

    取热力学极限时，相互作用和密度保持固定。

    英文原文

    For each fixed bounded measurable, nonnegative, radial interaction of finite range in three dimensions that is not zero almost everywhere, we prove a positive lower bound on the constant-orbital condensate fraction that is uniform over all sufficiently small densities and all temperatures between zero and the square of the density.

    The interaction and density remain fixed in the thermodynamic limit.

# Positive-temperature Bose–Einstein condensation and exact quantum depletion

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Ground-state condensation in the dilute hard-sphere gas](../../preprints/Ground-state-condensation-in-the-dilute-hard-sphere-gas-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The formalization proves ground-state Bose–Einstein condensation in the dilute hard-sphere gas. There are absolute constants $\varepsilon_0,c_0>0$ such that, whenever the density $\rho$ and hard-sphere radius $a$ satisfy $\rho a^3<\varepsilon_0$, the condensate occupation fraction has limit inferior at least $c_0$ along every thermodynamic sequence with $N/L^3\to\rho$.

The bound covers every pure ground state, every mixed state supported on the ground space, and the normalized ground-space projection. It is uniform in the gas parameter within this range and concerns ground states, without a positive-temperature assertion.
