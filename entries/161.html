Sidorenko 与 forcing 猜想反例

Counterexamples to Sidorenko's conjecture and the forcing conjecture

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

利用一个具有 353535 个顶点和 666666 条边的连通二分图模式否定了 Sidorenko 猜想，该模式出现的频率低于相同边密度的随机图中的频率。

同一模式也否定了 Skokan 和 Thoma 的强迫猜想：使该模式的密度和边密度都与某个常值 graphon 相匹配，未必能强迫拟随机性。

英文原文

Disproves Sidorenko's conjecture with a connected bipartite pattern on 353535 vertices and 666666 edges that occurs less frequently than in a random graph of the same edge density.

The same pattern disproves the forcing conjecture of Skokan and Thoma: matching its density and the edge density of a constant graphon need not force quasirandomness.

  1. Sidorenko 猜想的一个反例

    A counterexample to Sidorenko's conjecture

    摘要

    我们用一个具有 35 个顶点和 66 条边的二分图否定了 Sidorenko 猜想：它在某个有限简单图中的同态密度小于猜想所提出的下界。

    同一个连通图也否定了强迫猜想：在某个固定密度下，边密度与模式密度渐近匹配并不蕴含拟随机性。

    英文原文

    We disprove Sidorenko's conjecture with a bipartite graph on 35 vertices and 66 edges: its homomorphism density in some finite simple graph is smaller than the conjectured lower bound.

    The same connected graph also disproves the forcing conjecture: at one fixed density, asymptotically matching the edge and pattern densities does not imply quasirandomness.

# Counterexamples to Sidorenko’s conjecture and the forcing conjecture

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [A counterexample to Sidorenko's conjecture](../../preprints/A-counterexample-to-Sidorenkos-conjecture-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

Sidorenko's conjecture predicts that every bipartite graph $H$ has homomorphism density at least the host graph's edge density raised to $|E(H)|$. The formalization disproves this for the paper's fixed bipartite graph with $35$ vertices and $66$ edges: it constructs a nonempty finite simple host graph with $t(H,G)<t(K_2,G)^{66}$. The separate forcing-conjecture consequence in the paper is outside this statement.
