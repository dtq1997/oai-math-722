Yau 单值化猜想

Yau's uniformization conjecture

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

证明了 Yau 的单值化猜想：每个具有严格正的全纯双截曲率的完备连通非紧 Kähler 流形都与 Cn\mathbb C^nCn 双全纯等价。

英文原文

Proves Yau's uniformization conjecture: every complete connected noncompact Kähler manifold with strictly positive holomorphic bisectional curvature is biholomorphic to Cn\mathbb C^nCn.

OpenAI · overview.tex

论文

  1. 具有正双截面曲率的完备 Kähler 流形的单值化

    Uniformization of complete Kähler manifolds with positive bisectional curvature

    摘要

    我们证明，每个具有严格正全纯双截面曲率的完备连通非紧 Kähler 流形都双全纯于复欧氏空间。

    这在每个复维数中肯定地解决了 Yau 的单值化猜想。

    英文原文

    We prove that every complete connected noncompact Kähler manifold with strictly positive holomorphic bisectional curvature is biholomorphic to complex Euclidean space.

    This resolves Yau's uniformization conjecture positively in every complex dimension.
