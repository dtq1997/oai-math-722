纠缠博弈的阈值重复

Threshold repetition for entangled games

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

对每个有限的双人单轮博弈，证明了指数阈值重复定理：若其纠缠值为 v<1v<1v<1，则对于 0<δ<1−v0<\delta<1-v0<δ<1−v，在 kkk 次独立重复中获胜比例至少为 v+δv+\deltav+δ 的概率随 kkk 指数衰减。

允许任意联合的有限维纠缠策略以及相关的提问分布。

英文原文

Proves exponential threshold repetition for every finite two-player one-round game: if its entangled value is v<1v<1v<1, the probability of winning at least a fraction v+δv+\deltav+δ of kkk independent repetitions decays exponentially in kkk, for 0<δ<1−v0<\delta<1-v0<δ<1−v.

Arbitrary joint finite-dimensional entangled strategies and correlated question distributions are allowed.

    摘要

    对每个纠缠值为 v < 1 的有限双人博弈，我们证明，在 k 次独立重复中赢得至少 v+δv+\deltav+δ 比例的概率呈指数衰减，且这一结论对所有有限维联合策略一致成立。

    该结果允许任意相关的问题分布，并对每个 0<δ<1−v0\lt \delta\lt 1-v0<δ<1−v 以及 k ≥ 1 成立。

    其普适速率正比于 δ5/(1+log⁡(∣A∣∣B∣))\delta^5/(1+\log(|\mathcal A||\mathcal B|))δ5/(1+log(∣A∣∣B∣))，其中 A,B\mathcal A,\mathcal BA,B 为回答字母表。

    对每个固定的问题分布，我们还得到关于 δ 的一个显式三次速率。

    英文原文

    For every finite two-player game with entangled value v < 1, we prove exponential decay for the probability of winning at least a v+δv+\deltav+δ fraction of k independent repetitions, uniformly over all finite-dimensional joint strategies.

    The result allows arbitrary correlated question distributions and holds for every 0<δ<1−v0\lt \delta\lt 1-v0<δ<1−v and k ≥ 1.

    Its universal rate is proportional to δ5/(1+log⁡(∣A∣∣B∣))\delta^5/(1+\log(|\mathcal A||\mathcal B|))δ5/(1+log(∣A∣∣B∣)), where A,B\mathcal A,\mathcal BA,B are the answer alphabets.

    For each fixed question distribution, we also obtain an explicit cubic rate in δ.

# Threshold repetition for entangled games

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Threshold parallel repetition for finite-dimensional entangled games](../../preprints/Threshold-parallel-repetition-for-finite-dimensional-entangled-games-September-25-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The formalization gives exponential threshold parallel repetition for every finite two-player game with arbitrary correlated questions. If its finite-dimensional entangled value is $v<1$, its answer sets are $A,B$, and $0<\delta<1-v$, then the probability of winning at least the fraction $v+\delta$ of $k$ repetitions is at most $\exp(-\kappa\delta^{13}k/(1+\log(|A||B|)))$ for one universal $\kappa>0$. The bound applies to every joint finite-dimensional strategy and to their supremum, without assuming attainment. The separate distribution-dependent cubic bound is outside this scope.
