BFSS 模型的阈值束缚态与正能束缚态

Threshold and positive-energy bound states of the BFSS model

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

证明了对于每个有限的 N≥2N\ge2N≥2，未形变的相对 SU(N)\mathrm{SU}(N)SU(N) BFSS 模型都恰有一个可归一化的零能态，从而解决了阈值束缚态猜想。

对于 SU(2)\mathrm{SU}(2)SU(2)，配套论文证明存在无穷多个可归一化的正能本征态，且其能量无界，这与 BFSS 原始论文在 N=2N=2N=2 时排除其他束缚态的说法相矛盾。

英文原文

Proves that the undeformed relative SU(N)\mathrm{SU}(N)SU(N) BFSS model has exactly one normalizable zero-energy state for every finite N≥2N\ge2N≥2, resolving the threshold-bound-state conjecture.

For SU(2)\mathrm{SU}(2)SU(2), a companion proves infinitely many normalizable positive-energy eigenstates with unbounded energies, contradicting the original BFSS paper’s exclusion of additional bound states at N=2N=2N=2.

  1. SU(N) BFSS 模型的唯一阈值束缚态

    The unique threshold bound state of the SU(N) BFSS model

    摘要

    对每个有限的 N ≥ 2，我们证明，未形变的 SU(N)\mathop{\mathrm{SU}}\nolimits (N)SU(N) BFSS 矩阵量子力学在去除质心后恰有一个可归一化的零能态。

    这确立了阈值束缚态猜想。

    英文原文

    For every finite N ≥ 2, we prove that undeformed SU(N)\mathop{\mathrm{SU}}\nolimits (N)SU(N) BFSS matrix quantum mechanics has exactly one normalizable zero-energy state after removing the center of mass.

    This establishes the threshold-bound-state conjecture.

  2. SU(2) BFSS 相对运动哈密顿量的正特征值

    Positive eigenvalues of the relative SU(2) BFSS Hamiltonian

    摘要

    我们证明，相对 SU(2)\mathop{\mathrm{SU}}\nolimits (2)SU(2) BFSS Hamiltonian 具有无穷多个趋于无穷大的正特征值，并具有平方可积的特征向量。

    该算子通过对规范不变的超荷型取闭包来定义。

    这在 N = 2 时反驳了 BFSS 原始论文中关于不存在可归一化正能态的论断。

    英文原文

    We prove that the relative SU(2)\mathop{\mathrm{SU}}\nolimits (2)SU(2) BFSS Hamiltonian has infinitely many positive eigenvalues tending to infinity, with square-integrable eigenvectors.

    The operator is defined by closing the gauge-invariant supercharge form.

    This refutes, at N = 2, the exclusion of normalizable positive-energy states stated in the original BFSS paper.
