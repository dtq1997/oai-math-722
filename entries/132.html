二次敏感度猜想反例

A counterexample to the quadratic sensitivity conjecture

首发 · 2026-10-061 篇论文全部形式化

对于某个固定的 α>2\alpha>2α>2，构造了块敏感度满足 bs⁡(f)≥s(f)α\operatorname{bs}(f)\ge s(f)^\alphabs(f)≥s(f)α 的全定义布尔函数，从而否定了敏感度猜想的二次加强版本。

这里，s(f)s(f)s(f) 计数的是会影响输出的单个比特翻转，而块敏感度允许按互不相交的比特组作整体翻转。

英文原文

Constructs total Boolean functions with block sensitivity bs⁡(f)≥s(f)α\operatorname{bs}(f)\ge s(f)^\alphabs(f)≥s(f)α for a fixed α>2\alpha>2α>2, disproving the quadratic strengthening of the Sensitivity Conjecture.

Here s(f)s(f)s(f) counts influential individual-bit flips, while block sensitivity allows disjoint groups of bits to change together.

    A superquadratic separation between sensitivity and block sensitivity

    已列入形式化清单
    摘要

    我们构造非常数的全定义布尔函数，使其块敏感度增长得比敏感度平方的任意常数倍都快，从而否定敏感度猜想的二次加强形式。

    事实上，对于某个固定的 α > 2，我们的例子的块敏感度无界，并满足 bs(f)≥s(f)α\mathop{\mathrm{bs}}\nolimits (f)\ge s(f)^\alphabs(f)≥s(f)α。

    英文原文

    We disprove the quadratic strengthening of the Sensitivity Conjecture by constructing nonconstant total Boolean functions whose block sensitivity grows faster than any constant multiple of sensitivity squared.

    In fact, for some fixed α > 2, our examples have unbounded block sensitivity and satisfy bs(f)≥s(f)α\mathop{\mathrm{bs}}\nolimits (f)\ge s(f)^\alphabs(f)≥s(f)α.

# A superquadratic separation of sensitivity and block sensitivity

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [A superquadratic separation between sensitivity and block sensitivity](../../preprints/A-superquadratic-separation-between-sensitivity-and-block-sensitivity-September-25-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The formalized result disproves a universal quadratic bound of block sensitivity by sensitivity for total Boolean functions. For every integer $d\ge1$, it constructs a nonconstant function with $\mathrm{bs}(f)/s(f)^2\ge2^d/(4(d+2)^2)$, making the ratio unbounded. The formalization also gives a fixed exponent $\alpha>2$ and a sequence with $s(f)^\alpha\le\mathrm{bs}(f,0)$ while the latter tends to infinity; here $\mathrm{bs}(f,0)$ is block sensitivity at the all-zero input.
