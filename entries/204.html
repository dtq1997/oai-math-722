偶维自旋群的张量饱和性

Tensor saturation for even spin groups

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

证明了 Spin⁡(2n)\operatorname{Spin}(2n)Spin(2n) 在 n≥2n\ge2n≥2 时的饱和因子为一：对三个和属于根格的支配整权，在任意共同的正整数倍伸缩后存在不变量，就能推出原权处已有不变量。

这解决了 simply-laced 饱和猜想的 DDD 型部分。

英文原文

Proves saturation factor one for Spin⁡(2n)\operatorname{Spin}(2n)Spin(2n), n≥2n\ge2n≥2: for three dominant integral weights whose sum lies in the root lattice, an invariant at any common positive integral dilation already gives an invariant at the original weights.

This resolves the type-DDD part of the simply-laced saturation conjecture.

    我们证明了 Spin(2n)\mathop{\mathrm{Spin}}\nolimits (2n)Spin(2n) 在 n ≥ 2 时的饱和猜想。

    如果三个支配整权的和属于根格，那么将它们放大一个正整数倍后存在非零张量不变量，就蕴含在原权处也存在非零张量不变量。

    英文原文

    We prove the saturation conjecture for Spin(2n)\mathop{\mathrm{Spin}}\nolimits (2n)Spin(2n), n ≥ 2.

    If three dominant integral weights sum to an element of the root lattice, then the existence of a nonzero tensor invariant after a positive integral dilation implies the existence of one at the original weights.
