仿射 Bernstein 刚性的最优维数阈值

The sharp dimension threshold for affine Bernstein rigidity

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

证明了每个维数为三至九、对诱导的欧氏度量完备的光滑局部一致凸仿射极大图像，都是椭圆抛物面。

十维中的一个在全空间上定义的光滑非二次例子表明这一维数范围是最优的。

在三至九维中，抛物面分类还适用于对仿射 Berwald–Blaschke 度量完备的连通开局部一致凸仿射极大超曲面。

英文原文

Proves that every smooth locally uniformly convex affine-maximal graph of dimension three through nine, complete for its induced Euclidean metric, is an elliptic paraboloid.

A smooth entire nonquadratic example in dimension ten makes this range sharp.

In dimensions three through nine, the paraboloid classification also holds for connected open locally uniformly convex affine-maximal hypersurfaces complete for the affine Berwald–Blaschke metric.

  1. 十维中的一个定义于全空间的光滑非二次仿射极大图像

    A Smooth Nonquadratic Entire Affine Maximal Graph in Dimension Ten

    摘要

    我们在十维中构造了一个定义于整个空间的光滑非二次函数的图像，该函数满足经典仿射极大方程，且 Hessian 处处正定。

    这给出了十维中针对全空间函数图像的仿射 Bernstein 断言的一个光滑反例。

    Hessian 的正定性是逐点的；我们并未断言存在全局一致下界，也未断言 Berwald–Blaschke 度量完备。

    英文原文

    We construct a smooth nonquadratic entire graph in dimension ten that solves the classical affine maximal equation and has positive-definite Hessian everywhere.

    This gives a smooth counterexample to the entire-graph affine Bernstein assertion in dimension ten.

    Hessian positivity is pointwise; no global uniform lower bound or completeness of the Berwald–Blaschke metric is asserted.

  2. 三至九维中的仿射 Bernstein 定理

    The affine Bernstein theorem in dimensions three through nine

    摘要

    我们在三至九维中证明了欧氏完备的仿射 Bernstein 猜想：一个光滑、局部一致凸的仿射极大图像，只要其诱导欧氏度量完备，就必为椭圆抛物面。

    在这些维数中，对于连通光滑开（非紧且无边界）、仿射完备、局部一致凸且在经典意义下仿射极大的浸入超曲面，即使不预先假定其为函数的图像，也有相同结论。

    英文原文

    We prove the Euclidean-complete affine Bernstein conjecture in dimensions three through nine: a smooth locally uniformly convex affine maximal graph is an elliptic paraboloid whenever its induced Euclidean metric is complete.

    The same conclusion holds, without an initial graph assumption, for connected smooth open (noncompact and without boundary) affine-complete locally uniformly convex immersed hypersurfaces that are classically affine maximal in these dimensions.

# Affine Bernstein rigidity through dimension nine and a smooth dimension-ten counterexample

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [The affine Bernstein theorem in dimensions three through nine](../../preprints/The-affine-Bernstein-theorem-in-dimensions-three-through-nine-September-24-2026/main.pdf)

## Scope

The formalization proves the Euclidean-complete affine Bernstein theorem for graph dimensions $3\le n\le9$. If a smooth function on a nonempty open convex domain has positive-definite Hessian, satisfies the affine maximal equation, and its graph is complete in the induced Euclidean metric, then the domain is all of $\mathbb R^n$ and the function is a positive-definite quadratic polynomial plus an affine term. Its graph is therefore an elliptic paraboloid.

The paper's extension to immersed hypersurfaces without an initial graph assumption is outside this selected statement.
