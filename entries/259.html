不具有固定代价性质的群

A group without fixed price

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

构造了一个有限生成群，它有两个代价不同的本质自由的保概率测度作用，从而对一般的固定代价问题给出否定回答。

其 Bernoulli 作用的代价与一保持正距离，而一列有限高度扩张的代价趋于一。

英文原文

Constructs a finitely generated group with two essentially free probability-measure-preserving actions of different costs, answering the general fixed-price problem negatively.

Its Bernoulli action has cost bounded away from one, while a sequence of finite height extensions has costs tending to one.

论文

  1. 一个不具有固定代价的群

    A group without fixed price

    摘要

    我们构造了一个有限生成群，它具有两个代价不同的本质自由的保概率测度作用。

    这否定地回答了 Gaboriau 的一般固定代价问题。

    该群是一个秩为 100 的自由群与一个直积的融合积。

    它的 Bernoulli 作用的代价与一保持正距离，而有限高度扩张的代价趋于一。

    英文原文

    We construct a finitely generated group with two essentially free probability-measure-preserving actions of different costs.

    This gives a negative answer to Gaboriau's general fixed-price problem.

    The group is an amalgam of a free group of rank 100 with a direct product.

    Its Bernoulli action has cost bounded away from one, whereas finite height extensions have costs tending to one.
