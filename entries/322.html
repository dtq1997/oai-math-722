Tingley 球面等距问题

Tingley's sphere-isometry problem

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

解决了 Tingley 问题：非零实 Banach 空间的单位球面之间的每个满射等距映射，都唯一地延拓为整个空间之间的满射实线性等距映射。

这里没有维数限制，因此单位球面的度量几何在线性等距意义下确定了该 Banach 空间。

英文原文

Resolves Tingley's problem: every surjective isometry between the unit spheres of nonzero real Banach spaces extends uniquely to a surjective real-linear isometry of the whole spaces.

No dimension restriction is imposed, so the metric geometry of the unit sphere determines the Banach space up to linear isometry.

论文

  1. Tingley 问题的肯定解答

    A positive solution to Tingley’s problem

    主结果已列入形式化清单
    摘要

    实 Banach 空间单位球面之间的每个满射等距映射，都唯一地延拓为一个满射实线性等距映射，从而肯定地解决 Tingley 问题。

    若不同半径之间的距离不被保持，我们就在一对可能经过扩大的 Banach 空间中实现正的最大缺陷。

    然后，我们利用共同支撑和 Darbo 不动点定理对齐极值弦，并由支撑估计与凸性估计导出矛盾。

    英文原文

    Every surjective isometry between the unit spheres of real Banach spaces extends uniquely to a surjective real-linear isometry, giving an affirmative solution to Tingley's problem.

    If distances between different radii are not preserved, we realize the positive maximal defect in a possibly enlarged pair of Banach spaces.

    We then align extremal chords using common supports and Darbo's fixed-point theorem and obtain a contradiction from support and convexity estimates.

# Tingley’s sphere-isometry problem

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [A positive solution to Tingley's problem](../../preprints/A-positive-solution-to-Tingleys-problem-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

Tingley's problem asks whether a surjective isometry between the unit spheres of Banach spaces extends to a linear isometry of the spaces. The formalized result gives a unique surjective real-linear isometric extension for arbitrary nonzero real Banach spaces, with no finite-dimensionality, separability, reflexivity, or convexity assumptions. For complex spaces viewed as real spaces, the conclusion is real linearity rather than complex linearity.
