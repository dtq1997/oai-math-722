整数多拟阵共同基的近似计数

Approximate counting of common integer polymatroid bases

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

给出了一个完全多项式随机近似方案，用于对两个总秩相同、由精确秩值预言机给定的整多拟阵的公共整数基进行计数。

容量以二进制编码，每个整数向量只计数一次，而且每次执行中的预言机调用次数以及预言机之外的位运算次数均为多项式。

对于由独立性预言机给出的拟阵，这些结果还涵盖基数指定、基数不受限制或基数最大的公共独立集，即使两个拟阵的秩不同也成立。

英文原文

Gives a fully polynomial randomized approximation scheme for counting common integer bases of two integral polymatroids of equal total rank, supplied by exact rank-value oracles.

Capacities are binary-encoded, each integer vector counts once, and oracle calls and bit operations outside the oracles are polynomial on every execution.

For matroids presented by independence oracles, the results also cover common independent sets of prescribed, unrestricted, or maximum cardinality, even when the ranks differ.

论文

  1. 二进制容量下多拟阵公共整数基计数的完全多项式随机近似方案

    An FPRAS for Common Integer Polymatroid Bases with Binary Capacities

    摘要

    对于由精确秩值预言机给出的两个总秩相同的多拟阵，我们给出一个计算其公共整数基数目的完全多项式随机近似方案。

    总秩和容量以二进制编码，且每个整数向量只计数一次。

    在每次执行中，预言机调用次数和预言机之外的位运算量，都由基集大小、二进制输入长度、相对误差容限的倒数以及失败概率倒数的对数的某个固定多项式所界定。

    该算法直接处理二进制容量，而不将其展开为带标号的副本。

    英文原文

    We give a fully polynomial randomized approximation scheme for counting common integer bases of two polymatroids with the same total rank, supplied by exact rank-value oracles.

    The total rank and capacities are encoded in binary, and each integer vector is counted once.

    On every execution, the number of oracle calls and the bit work outside the oracles are bounded by a fixed polynomial in the ground-set size, the binary input length, the inverse relative-error tolerance, and the logarithm of the inverse failure probability.

    The algorithm handles binary capacities directly, without expanding them into labelled copies.

  2. 两个拟阵的公共基的近似计数

    Approximate counting of common bases of two matroids

    主结果已列入形式化清单
    摘要

    对于由独立性预言机给出的两个任意同秩拟阵，我们给出一个计算其公共基数目的完全多项式随机近似方案。

    该算法不需要任一拟阵的显式表示，而且每次执行中的预言机调用次数和位运算次数均具有多项式界。

    英文原文

    We give a fully polynomial randomized approximation scheme for counting the common bases of two arbitrary matroids of the same rank, supplied by independence oracles.

    The algorithm requires no explicit representation of either matroid and has polynomial bounds on both oracle calls and bit operations on every execution.

# Approximate counting of common integer polymatroid bases

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Approximate counting of common bases of two matroids](../../preprints/Approximate-counting-of-common-bases-of-two-matroids-September-23-2026/main.pdf)

## Scope

The formalized result gives a fully polynomial randomized approximation scheme for the number of common bases of two equal-rank matroids on an enumerated finite ground set, using independence oracles. For rational $0<\varepsilon,\delta<1$, the nonnegative rational output has relative error at most $\varepsilon$ with probability at least $1-\delta$. A zero count produces zero on every execution. Oracle calls and bit operations have polynomial bounds on every random tape in the input size, $\varepsilon^{-1}$, and $\log(\delta^{-1})$.
