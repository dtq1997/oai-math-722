三维 Sogge 局部光滑化猜想

Sogge's local smoothing conjecture in dimension three

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

解决了三个空间维数下欧氏波动方程的 Sogge 局部光滑化猜想。

该估计在整个严格范围 2<p<∞2<p<\infty2<p<∞ 内成立，所需 Sobolev 正则性高于 max⁡{0,1−3/p}\max\{0,1-3/p\}max{0,1−3/p}。

特别地，允许任意正的 Sobolev 损失时，临界 L3L^3L3 估计成立。

英文原文

Resolves Sogge's local smoothing conjecture for the Euclidean wave equation in three spatial dimensions.

The estimate holds throughout the full strict range 2<p<∞2<p<\infty2<p<∞, with Sobolev regularity above max⁡{0,1−3/p}\max\{0,1-3/p\}max{0,1−3/p}.

In particular, the critical L3L^3L3 estimate holds with every positive Sobolev loss.

论文

  1. 三维波动方程的临界局部光滑化

    Critical local smoothing for the three-dimensional wave equation

    摘要

    我们证明了三维空间中波动方程的临界 L3 局部光滑化估计，允许任意正的 Sobolev 损失。

    这解决了三维情形的 Sogge 欧氏局部光滑化猜想。

    英文原文

    We prove the critical L3 local smoothing estimate for the wave equation in three spatial dimensions, with every positive Sobolev loss.

    This resolves Sogge's Euclidean local smoothing conjecture in dimension three.
