Wall 有限 D(2) 猜想反例

A counterexample to Wall's finite D(2) conjecture

首发 · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

构造了一个有限连通三维 CW 复形，其万有覆盖在次数高于二时的整系数同调均为零，且对每个局部系数模，其三次上同调均为零，但它没有有限二维同伦模型。

这否定了 Wall 的有限 D(2) 猜想；该例子的基本群是无限群。

英文原文

Constructs a finite connected three-dimensional CW complex whose universal cover has no integral homology above degree two and whose third cohomology vanishes for every local coefficient module, but which has no finite two-dimensional homotopy model.

This disproves Wall's finite D(2) conjecture; the example has infinite fundamental group.

OpenAI · overview.tex

论文

  1. Wall 的 D(2) 问题的一个反例

    A Counterexample to Wall's D(2) Problem

    摘要

    我们对 Wall 的有限 D(2)D(2)D(2) 问题给出否定回答。

    我们构造一个满足 D(2)D(2)D(2) 有限性条件的有限连通三维 CW 复形，它却不与任何维数至多为二的有限 CW 复形同伦等价。

    该例子的基本群是无限群。

    英文原文

    We give a negative answer to Wall's finite D(2)D(2)D(2) problem.

    We construct a finite connected three-dimensional CW complex satisfying the D(2)D(2)D(2) finiteness condition but not homotopy equivalent to any finite CW complex of dimension at most two.

    The example has infinite fundamental group.
