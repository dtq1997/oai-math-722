Toms–Winter 与等变 Jiang–Su 稳定性

Toms–Winter and equivariant Jiang–Su stability

OpenAI · 2026-10-064 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

对每个可数离散 可均群在单、可分、含幺、无限维、核、稳定有限且 Jiang–Su 稳定的 C∗C^*C∗ 代数上的作用，证明了等变 Jiang–Su 稳定性，从而在不限制迹上的动力学的情况下解决了 Szabó 猜想的这一情形。

这一系列论文还证明了含幺 Toms–Winter 猜想：在单、可分、含幺、无限维核代数的情形下，严格比较性、有限核维数与 Jiang–Su 稳定性等价。

英文原文

Proves equivariant Jiang–Su stability for every countable discrete amenable group action on a simple separable unital infinite-dimensional nuclear stably finite Jiang–Su-stable C∗C^*C∗-algebra, resolving this case of Szabó's conjecture without restrictions on trace dynamics.

The family also proves the unital Toms–Winter conjecture, equating strict comparison, finite nuclear dimension and Jiang–Su stability in the simple separable unital infinite-dimensional nuclear setting.

论文

  1. 有单位元稳定有限情形下可均作用的等变 Jiang–Su 稳定性

    Equivariant Jiang–Su Stability for Amenable Actions in the Unital Stably Finite Case

    摘要

    可数离散可均群在一个已经 Jiang–Su 稳定的单、可分、有单位元、无限维、核、稳定有限的复 C∗ 代数上的每个作用，都在余循环共轭意义下吸收 Jiang–Su 代数上的平凡作用。

    对迹态单纯形上的作用不作任何限制。

    这证明了 Szabó 关于自动等变 Jiang–Su 稳定性的猜想 A 的有单位元稳定有限情形。

    英文原文

    Every action of a countable discrete amenable group on a simple, separable, unital, infinite-dimensional, nuclear, stably finite complex C∗-algebra that is already Jiang–Su stable absorbs the trivial action on the Jiang–Su algebra up to cocycle conjugacy.

    No restriction is imposed on the action on the tracial-state simplex.

    This proves the unital, stably finite case of Szabó's Conjecture A on automatic equivariant Jiang–Su stability.

  2. Cuntz 比较与 Jiang–Su 吸收性

    Cuntz comparison and Jiang–Su absorption

    摘要

    我们证明，对于可分、单、核、非初等的 C∗ 代数，扩展泛函意义下的严格比较蕴含 Jiang–Su 吸收性。

    这解决了 Toms–Winter 正则性问题中的相应蕴含关系，涵盖无单位元代数并允许无界迹。

    更一般地，每个 Cuntz 半群几乎无孔且完全几乎可除的可分核 C∗ 代数都吸收 Jiang–Su 代数。

    英文原文

    We prove that strict comparison in the extended-functional sense implies Jiang–Su absorption for separable simple nuclear non-elementary C∗-algebras.

    This resolves the corresponding implication of the Toms–Winter regularity problem, including nonunital algebras and allowing unbounded traces.

    More generally, every separable nuclear C∗-algebra whose Cuntz semigroup is almost unperforated and fully almost divisible absorbs the Jiang–Su algebra.

  3. 无初等子商情形下的核维数与 Jiang–Su 稳定性

    Nuclear dimension and Jiang–Su stability without elementary subquotients

    摘要

    对于没有非零初等理想子商的可分核 C∗ 代数，有限核维数与 Jiang–Su 稳定性等价。

    更一般地，对每个可分核代数 A0，都有 dim⁡nuc(A0⊗Z)≤1\dim_{\mathrm{nuc}}(A_0\otimes\mathcal Z)\le1dimnuc​(A0​⊗Z)≤1。

    这证明了 Robert 和 Tikuisis 的猜想 (C1)，以及非单 Toms–Winter 正则性问题中的核维数等价关系。

    英文原文

    For separable nuclear C∗-algebras with no nonzero elementary ideal subquotients, finite nuclear dimension is equivalent to Jiang–Su stability.

    More generally, dim⁡nuc(A0⊗Z)≤1\dim_{\mathrm{nuc}}(A_0\otimes\mathcal Z)\le1dimnuc​(A0​⊗Z)≤1 for every separable nuclear A0.

    This proves Robert and Tikuisis's Conjecture (C1) and the nuclear-dimension equivalence in the nonsimple Toms–Winter regularity question.

  4. 迹投影方法与一致性质 Gamma

    Tracial projection methods and uniform property Gamma

    主结果已列入形式化清单
    摘要

    对于一个具有迹的单、可分、有单位元、无限维、核、稳定有限的 C∗ 代数，其一致迹完备化的一致迹超幂的实秩为零，蕴含一致性质 Γ。

    这肯定地回答了 Schafhauser、Tikuisis 和 White 的问题 XXI。

    独立地，对于单、可分、有单位元、无限维核代数，严格比较蕴含 Jiang–Su 吸收性，从而解决有单位元情形的 Toms–Winter 猜想。

    在通过有限目标秩的迹检验的比较条件下，对于稠密有限迹均有界且具有非空紧归一化基的单、可分、核、稳定无投影代数，我们还得到一致性质 Γ 和吸收性。

    英文原文

    For a simple, separable, unital, infinite-dimensional, nuclear, stably finite C∗-algebra with traces, real rank zero of the uniform tracial ultrapower of its uniform tracial completion implies uniform property Γ.

    This answers Problem XXI of Schafhauser, Tikuisis and White affirmatively.

    Independently, strict comparison implies Jiang–Su absorption for simple, separable, unital, infinite-dimensional nuclear algebras, resolving the unital Toms–Winter conjecture.

    Under comparison tested on traces of finite target rank, we also obtain uniform property Γ and absorption for simple, separable, nuclear, stably projectionless algebras whose densely finite traces are all bounded and have a nonempty compact normalized base.

# Cuntz comparison, nuclear dimension, and equivariant Jiang–Su stability

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Tracial projection methods and uniform property $\Gamma$](../../preprints/Tracial-projection-methods-and-uniform-property-Gamma-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The formalization proves that real rank zero of the uniform tracial ultrapower of the uniform tracial completion implies uniform property $\Gamma$ for a simple separable unital infinite-dimensional nuclear stably finite $C^*$-algebra with traces. The conclusion holds at every specified free ultrafilter under the corresponding real-rank-zero hypothesis.

The linked comparison results also prove Jiang–Su absorption and uniform property $\Gamma$ for simple separable unital infinite-dimensional nuclear algebras with strict comparison, and for the stated stably projectionless nuclear algebras with bounded densely finite traces, a nonempty compact normalized trace base, and the prescribed finite-target-rank comparison condition.
