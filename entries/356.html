Gigli 的 Alexandrov 曲率刻画

Gigli's characterization of Alexandrov curvature

首发 · 2026-10-062 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

证明了 Gigli 猜想：在每个整数维数 n≥2n\ge2n≥2 中，Alexandrov 曲率至少为 κ\kappaκ，当且仅当以 Hn\mathcal H^nHn 为参照测度的满支撑 RCD⁡((n−1)κ,n)\operatorname{RCD}((n-1)\kappa,n)RCD((n−1)κ,n) 条件成立，并且在原有的全局测试类中，分布意义下的截面曲率至少为 κ\kappaκ。

这里的 RCD 条件是未约化的。

英文原文

Proves Gigli's conjecture: in every integer dimension n≥2n\ge2n≥2, Alexandrov curvature at least κ\kappaκ is characterized by the full-support RCD⁡((n−1)κ,n)\operatorname{RCD}((n-1)\kappa,n)RCD((n−1)κ,n) condition with reference measure Hn\mathcal H^nHn and distributional sectional curvature at least κ\kappaκ in the original global test classes.

The RCD condition is unreduced.

  1. Gigli 对 Alexandrov 空间的分布曲率刻画

    Gigli’s distributional curvature characterization of Alexandrov spaces

    摘要

    对于每个整数 n ≥ 2 和 κ ∈ ℝ，我们证明，一个完备可分度量空间是曲率至少为 κ 的 n 维 Alexandrov 空间，当且仅当以 Hn\mathcal H^nHn 为参考测度时，它是一个具有全支撑的 RCD((n−1)κ,n)\mathrm{RCD}((n-1)\kappa,n)RCD((n−1)κ,n) 空间，且其分布意义下的截面曲率在 Gigli 原始的全局测试类中至少为 κ。

    这在至少二维时解决了 Gigli 的刻画猜想。

    英文原文

    For every integer n ≥ 2 and κ ∈ ℝ, we prove that a complete separable metric space is an n-dimensional Alexandrov space of curvature at least κ if and only if, with reference measure Hn\mathcal H^nHn, it is a full-support RCD((n−1)κ,n)\mathrm{RCD}((n-1)\kappa,n)RCD((n−1)κ,n) space whose distributional sectional curvature is at least κ in Gigli's original global test classes.

    This resolves Gigli's characterization conjecture in dimensions at least two.

# Gigli’s characterization of Alexandrov curvature

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Weak Hessian bounds along every geodesic in RCD spaces](../../preprints/Weak-Hessian-bounds-along-every-geodesic-in-RCD-spaces-September-24-2026/weak-hessian-geodesics.pdf)

## Scope

The formalization transfers a weak Hessian upper bound to every prescribed minimizing geodesic in an $\mathrm{RCD}(K,N)$ space with finite $N>1$. If a bounded globally Lipschitz function $F$ has weak Hessian bounded above by $G$ times the metric, where $G$ is bounded and continuous, then every constant-speed geodesic $\gamma:[0,1]\to X$ satisfies $(F\circ\gamma)''\le G(\gamma)\,d(\gamma(0),\gamma(1))^2$ in the distributional sense. Constant geodesics are included. The space is complete and separable with full support and measure finite on bounded sets; compactness and metric nonbranching are not assumed.
