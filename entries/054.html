Kuznetsov 有理性猜想反例

Counterexamples to Kuznetsov's rationality conjecture

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

对每个充分大的允许 Hassett 判别式，非常一般的光滑复三次四维簇，即使同时具有无扭曲的几何 K3 范畴及整 Hodge 理论意义下的关联 K3 曲面，也不是有理的。

这否定了 Kuznetsov 有理性猜想，以及关联 K3 判据作为有理性充分条件的断言；判别式阈值是非有效的。

英文原文

For every sufficiently large admissible Hassett discriminant, a very general smooth complex cubic fourfold is irrational despite having both an untwisted geometric K3 category and an integral Hodge-theoretic K3 association.

This disproves Kuznetsov's rationality conjecture and the sufficiency of the associated-K3 criterion for rationality; the discriminant threshold is ineffective.

论文

  1. 具有几何 K3 范畴的非有理三次四维簇

    Irrational cubic fourfolds with geometric K3 categories

    摘要

    对于每个充分大的可容许 Hassett 判别式，我们证明，具有该判别式的很一般的三次四维簇是非有理的，尽管其 Kuznetsov 分量等价于一个射影 K3 曲面的通常导出范畴。

    这一判别式阈值是非有效的。

    这否定了 Kuznetsov 的有理性猜想。

    这些三次四维簇在 Hodge 理论意义下也有关联的非扭曲极化 K3 曲面，因此它们还否定了关联 K3 有理性预测的充分性方向。

    英文原文

    For every sufficiently large admissible Hassett discriminant, we prove that a very general cubic fourfold of that discriminant is irrational, although its Kuznetsov component is equivalent to the ordinary derived category of a projective K3 surface.

    The discriminant threshold is ineffective.

    This disproves Kuznetsov's rationality conjecture.

    The same cubics have associated untwisted polarized K3 surfaces in the Hodge-theoretic sense, so they also disprove the sufficiency direction of the associated-K3 rationality prediction.
