单个边界片段恢复度量与联络

Joint metric and connection recovery from one boundary patch

OpenAI · 2026-10-063 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

在维数至少为三的紧连通流形上，任意非空开边界片上的零频测量都能确定一个光滑度量以及平凡 Hermitian 秩二丛上的一个光滑酉联络，精确到在该边界片上固定的微分同胚与规范变换。

输入与观测使用同一个边界片。

相反，在三维球上，不同的一致正、有界可测的标量电导率可以具有完全相同的全边界数据。

英文原文

Zero-frequency measurements on any nonempty open boundary patch determine a smooth metric and smooth unitary connection on a trivial Hermitian rank-two bundle over a compact connected manifold of dimension at least three, up to diffeomorphism and gauge fixed on that patch.

Inputs and observations use the same patch.

In contrast, distinct uniformly positive bounded measurable scalar conductivities on a three-dimensional ball can have identical full-boundary data.

论文

  1. 由一个边界片确定度量与酉联络

    Determination of a metric and a unitary connection from one boundary patch

    摘要

    我们证明，在一个具有光滑边界、维数至少为三的紧连通光滑流形上，任意非空开边界片上的零频边界测量都可以同时确定一个光滑 Riemann 度量，以及平凡 Hermite 秩二丛上的一个光滑酉联络。

    输入和观测都限制在同一个边界片上。

    度量和联络的确定只差一个微分同胚和一个酉规范变换，且它们在被测边界片上的限制均为恒等。

    英文原文

    We prove that zero-frequency boundary measurements on any nonempty open boundary patch determine both a smooth Riemannian metric and a smooth unitary connection on the trivial Hermitian rank-two bundle over a compact connected smooth manifold of dimension at least three with smooth boundary.

    Both inputs and observations are restricted to the same patch.

    The metric and connection are determined up to a diffeomorphism and a unitary gauge that restrict to the identity on the measured patch.

  2. 单个边界片测量下光滑各向异性 Calderón 问题的唯一性

    Smooth anisotropic uniqueness in the Calderón problem from one boundary patch

    摘要

    我们解决了在任意同一边界片上进行测量的光滑各向异性 Calderón 唯一性问题。

    维数至少为三的紧连通流形上的一个光滑 Riemann 度量，可以由其零频 Dirichlet-to-Neumann 能量形式确定，唯一的自由度是固定被测边界片的微分同胚，其中输入和观测都位于任意一个非空开边界片上。

    英文原文

    We resolve the smooth anisotropic Calderón uniqueness problem with arbitrary same-patch measurements.

    A smooth Riemannian metric on a compact connected manifold of dimension at least three is determined, up to a diffeomorphism fixing the measured patch, by its zero-frequency Dirichlet-to-Neumann energy form with both input and observation on any nonempty open boundary patch.

  3. 三维有界可测标量电导率的非唯一性

    Nonuniqueness for bounded measurable scalar conductivities in three dimensions

    主结果已列入形式化清单
    摘要

    我们在 ℝ3 的一个球上构造了两个不同的一致正、有界可测的标量电导率，它们具有相同的完整 Dirichlet-to-Neumann 算子。

    两个电导率在边界附近都等于一。

    这给出了标量 Calderón 问题在有界可测正则性下的非唯一性。

    英文原文

    We construct two distinct uniformly positive bounded measurable scalar conductivities on a ball in ℝ3 with the same full Dirichlet-to-Neumann operator.

    Both conductivities equal one near the boundary.

    This gives nonuniqueness in the scalar Calderón problem at bounded measurable regularity.

# Joint metric and connection recovery from one boundary patch

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Nonuniqueness for Bounded Measurable Scalar Conductivities in Three Dimensions](../../preprints/Nonuniqueness-for-Bounded-Measurable-Scalar-Conductivities-in-Three-Dimensions-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The Calderón inverse problem asks whether boundary measurements determine an interior conductivity. The formalized result gives nonuniqueness for bounded measurable scalar conductivities on the ball $B(0,3)\subset\mathbb R^3$: two uniformly positive conductivities differ on a set of positive volume, equal $1$ near the boundary, and have the same full weak Dirichlet-to-Neumann operator. Weak solutions exist uniquely for every boundary trace.
