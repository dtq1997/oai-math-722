准传递图上的临界无限簇不存在

No infinite critical clusters on quasi-transitive graphs

首发 · 2026-10-062 篇论文全部形式化

对每个满足 pc<1p_c<1pc​<1 的无限、连通、局部有限准传递图上的键渗流，解决了 Benjamini–Schramm 临界性猜想：在临界概率处，几乎必然不存在无限簇。

这组结果还对 Z3\mathbb Z^3Z3 上的最近邻键渗流和点渗流都确立了这一结论。

英文原文

Resolves the Benjamini–Schramm criticality conjecture for bond percolation on every infinite connected locally finite quasi-transitive graph with pc<1p_c<1pc​<1: at the critical probability, there is almost surely no infinite cluster.

The family also establishes this conclusion for both nearest-neighbor bond and site percolation on Z3\mathbb Z^3Z3.

  1. 立方晶格上的临界键渗流与点渗流

    Critical bond and site percolation on the cubic lattice

    已列入形式化清单
    摘要

    我们证明了 ℤ3 上的最近邻 Bernoulli 键渗流和点渗流在各自的临界参数处都不存在无穷簇。

    证明将独立超边的有限连接不等式、有限尺度的延拓估计与自适应探索相结合。

    英文原文

    We prove that nearest-neighbor Bernoulli bond and site percolation on ℤ3 have no infinite cluster at their respective critical parameters.

    The proof combines a finite connection inequality for independent hyperedges with a finite-scale extension estimate and an adaptive exploration.

  2. 准传递图在临界点不发生渗流

    No percolation at criticality on quasi-transitive graphs

    摘要

    我们证明了，在任何临界概率小于一的无穷、连通、局部有限的准传递图上，临界 Bernoulli 键渗流都不存在无穷簇。

    这解决了 Benjamini 和 Schramm 的临界性猜想的键渗流形式。

    英文原文

    We prove that critical Bernoulli bond percolation has no infinite cluster on any infinite connected locally finite quasi-transitive graph with critical probability less than one.

    This resolves the bond form of the criticality conjecture of Benjamini and Schramm.

# Critical percolation on every quasi-transitive graph

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Critical bond and site percolation on the cubic lattice](../../preprints/Critical-bond-and-site-percolation-on-the-cubic-lattice-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)
- [No percolation at criticality on quasi-transitive graphs](../../preprints/No-percolation-at-criticality-on-quasi-transitive-graphs-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The critical-percolation question asks whether an infinite cluster can remain at the threshold. The formalized result proves that, at their respective critical probabilities, nearest-neighbor bond and site percolation on $\mathbb Z^3$ almost surely have no infinite cluster. Equivalently, almost surely every vertex belongs to a finite cluster in each model.

The formalization proves absence of an infinite cluster at criticality for Bernoulli bond percolation on every infinite connected locally finite quasi-transitive graph with critical probability $p_c<1$. At $p=p_c$, the probability that any infinite cluster exists is zero. The graph model permits bond multiplicities.
