Bloch 定律与自发铁磁序

Bloch's law and spontaneous ferromagnetic order

OpenAI · 2026-10-064 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

对每个正的量子自旋，证明了三维量子 Heisenberg 铁磁体的 Bloch T3/2T^{3/2}T3/2 定律及其精确系数，允许非负、对称、有限程且支集生成 Z3\mathbb Z^3Z3 的耦合。

在零场处求导和取低温极限都在热力学极限之后进行。

这一系列论文还证明了每个维数 d≥3d\ge3d≥3 下最近邻模型的自发磁化，并确定了三维最近邻耦合的首个晶格修正。

英文原文

Proves Bloch's T3/2T^{3/2}T3/2 law with its exact coefficient for three-dimensional quantum Heisenberg ferromagnets at every positive quantum spin, allowing nonnegative symmetric finite-range couplings whose support generates Z3\mathbb Z^3Z3.

The thermodynamic limit precedes the zero-field derivative and low-temperature limit.

The family also proves spontaneous magnetization for nearest-neighbor models in every dimension d≥3d\ge3d≥3 and determines the first lattice correction for three-dimensional nearest-neighbor couplings.

论文

  1. 三维有限作用程 Heisenberg 铁磁体的 Bloch 定律

    Bloch's Law for Finite-Range Heisenberg Ferromagnets in Three Dimensions

    摘要

    我们证明，对每个固定的正量子自旋，三维量子 Heisenberg 铁磁体的自发磁化强度满足 Bloch 的 T3/2 定律。

    该结果对支集生成 ℤ3 的每个非负对称有限作用程相互作用成立，包括空间各向异性的耦合。

    磁化强度亏损的主项具有由二次单磁振子色散的行列式所确定的精确系数。

    先取热力学极限，再取零场处关于磁场的右导数，最后取低温极限。

    英文原文

    We prove Bloch's T3/2 law for the spontaneous magnetization of three-dimensional quantum Heisenberg ferromagnets at every fixed positive quantum spin.

    The result holds for every nonnegative symmetric finite-range interaction whose support generates ℤ3, including spatially anisotropic couplings.

    The leading magnetization deficit has the exact coefficient determined by the determinant of the quadratic one-magnon dispersion.

    The thermodynamic limit is taken before the right field derivative at zero, and the low-temperature limit is taken last.

  2. Bloch 定律的首个晶格修正

    The first lattice correction to Bloch's law

    摘要

    我们证明，对每个固定自旋 S=12,1,32,…S=\tfrac12,1,\tfrac32,\ldotsS=21​,1,23​,…，三维最近邻量子 Heisenberg 铁磁体的 Bloch 定律具有首个晶格修正项。

    自发磁化强度亏损与完整理想磁振子密度之差为 o(β−5/2)o(\beta^{-5/2})o(β−5/2)。

    除 Bloch 主项之外，这给出了修正项 3ζ(5/2)(βS)−5/2/(128π3/2)3\zeta(5/2)(\beta S)^{-5/2}/(128\pi^{3/2})3ζ(5/2)(βS)−5/2/(128π3/2)。

    磁化强度是热力学压强在零场处关于磁场的右导数；先取体积极限，再取磁场导数，最后取低温极限。

    英文原文

    We prove the first lattice correction to Bloch's law for the three-dimensional nearest-neighbor quantum Heisenberg ferromagnet at every fixed spin S=12,1,32,…S=\tfrac12,1,\tfrac32,\ldotsS=21​,1,23​,….

    The spontaneous-magnetization deficit agrees with the full ideal-magnon density up to o(β−5/2)o(\beta^{-5/2})o(β−5/2).

    In addition to the leading Bloch term, this gives the correction 3ζ(5/2)(βS)−5/2/(128π3/2)3\zeta(5/2)(\beta S)^{-5/2}/(128\pi^{3/2})3ζ(5/2)(βS)−5/2/(128π3/2).

    The magnetization is the right derivative at zero field of the thermodynamic pressure; the volume limit precedes the field derivative, and the low-temperature limit is taken last.

  3. 三维量子 Heisenberg 铁磁体的球面磁化分布律

    The spherical magnetization law for the three-dimensional quantum Heisenberg ferromagnet

    摘要

    我们证明，对每个固定的正量子自旋以及每个充分低的固定正温度，三维最近邻各向同性量子 Heisenberg 铁磁体满足球面磁化分布律。

    随着偶数边长周期立方体增大，对称的零场磁化强度依矩收敛到方向均匀分布、大小为确定正常数的极限。

    这一大小等于无限体积压强在零场处关于磁场的右导数。

    该分布律通过自旋分量的自伴线性组合表述，无须对不对易的可观测量进行联合测量。

    英文原文

    We prove the spherical magnetization law for the three-dimensional nearest-neighbor isotropic quantum Heisenberg ferromagnet at every fixed positive quantum spin and every sufficiently low fixed positive temperature.

    As the even periodic cubes grow, the symmetric zero-field magnetization converges in moments to a uniform direction with a deterministic positive magnitude.

    This magnitude equals the right derivative at zero field of the infinite-volume pressure.

    The law is expressed through self-adjoint linear combinations of the spin components and requires no joint measurement of noncommuting observables.

  4. 量子 Heisenberg 铁磁体中的自发磁化

    Spontaneous magnetization in the quantum Heisenberg ferromagnet

    摘要

    对每个维数 d ≥ 3 和每个自旋 S∈{12,1,32,…}S\in\{\frac12,1,\frac32,\ldots\}S∈{21​,1,23​,…}，我们证明，最近邻各向同性量子 Heisenberg 铁磁体在每个充分低的正温度下都具有平移不变且自发磁化的平衡态。

    同一状态满足零场动力学的 KMS 条件，且磁化强度至少为 S/4S/4S/4。

    这解决了以自发磁化表述的低温有序问题。

    英文原文

    For every dimension d ≥ 3 and every spin S∈{12,1,32,…}S\in\{\frac12,1,\frac32,\ldots\}S∈{21​,1,23​,…}, we prove that the nearest-neighbor isotropic quantum Heisenberg ferromagnet has a translation-invariant, spontaneously magnetized equilibrium state at every sufficiently low positive temperature.

    The same state satisfies the KMS condition for the zero-field dynamics and has magnetization at least S/4S/4S/4.

    This resolves the low-temperature ordering problem in the spontaneous-magnetization formulation.

# Bloch's law, its lattice correction, and the spherical magnetization law

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Spontaneous magnetization in the quantum Heisenberg ferromagnet](../../preprints/Spontaneous-magnetization-in-the-quantum-Heisenberg-ferromagnet-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The formalization proves spontaneous magnetization for the nearest-neighbor isotropic quantum Heisenberg ferromagnet on $\mathbb Z^d$ for every $d\ge3$ and every spin $S\in\{\tfrac12,1,\tfrac32,\ldots\}$. At every sufficiently low positive temperature, it constructs a translation-invariant equilibrium state satisfying the KMS condition for the zero-field dynamics and having magnetization at least $S/4$.

It also proves convergence of the finite-volume dynamics to the infinite-volume dynamics used in the KMS statement.
