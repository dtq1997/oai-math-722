Popa–Vaes 二次强算子铺砌猜想

The Popa–Vaes quadratic strong-operator paving conjecture

首发 · 2026-10-062 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

证明了复 von Neumann 代数中的每个自伴元，相对于任意极大交换子代数都容许具有 O(ε−2)O(\varepsilon^{-2})O(ε−2) 个块的强算子铺砌。

用一个在强拓扑中任意接近恒等元的投影进行压缩后，该范数界成立，从而解决了 Popa–Vaes 二次铺砌猜想。

英文原文

Proves that every self-adjoint element of a complex von Neumann algebra admits strong-operator paving relative to any maximal abelian subalgebra with O(ε−2)O(\varepsilon^{-2})O(ε−2) blocks.

The norm bound holds after compression by a projection arbitrarily close to the identity in the strong topology, resolving the Popa–Vaes quadratic paving conjecture.

  1. 相对于任意极大交换子代数的逼近铺砌

    Approximation Paving over Arbitrary Maximal Abelian Subalgebras

    摘要

    我们对复 von Neumann 代数的每个极大交换子代数证明了 Popa 和 Vaes 的逼近铺砌猜想。

    对每个 0<ε<10\lt \varepsilon\lt 10<ε<1，每个自伴算子都是一族范数至多为其范数三倍的自伴算子的强极限，且每个逼近算子都容许误差至多为其自身范数的 ε 倍的范数铺砌。

    投影数量至多为 Cε−6C\varepsilon^{-6}Cε−6，其中 C 为一个普适常数。

    无须可分性或条件期望假设。

    英文原文

    We prove the approximation-paving conjecture of Popa and Vaes for every maximal abelian subalgebra of a complex von Neumann algebra.

    For each 0<ε<10\lt \varepsilon\lt 10<ε<1, every self-adjoint operator is a strong limit of self-adjoint operators of norm at most three times its norm, each admitting a norm paving with error at most ε times the approximant's own norm.

    The number of projections is at most Cε−6C\varepsilon^{-6}Cε−6 for a universal constant C.

    No separability or conditional-expectation hypothesis is required.

  2. 相对于任意极大交换子代数的具有二次界的强算子铺砌

    Quadratic Strong-Operator Paving over Arbitrary Maximal Abelian Subalgebras

    摘要

    我们证明了 Popa 和 Vaes 的二次强算子铺砌猜想。

    对每个 0<ε<10\lt \varepsilon \lt 10<ε<1，von Neumann 代数的每个自伴元素，都容许相对于每个极大交换子代数的、使用至多 5×108ε−25\times10^8\varepsilon ^{-2}5×108ε−2 个投影的强算子铺砌。

    该上界对所有表示一致成立，无须可分性或条件期望假设。

    英文原文

    We prove the quadratic strong-operator paving conjecture of Popa and Vaes.

    For every 0<ε<10\lt \varepsilon \lt 10<ε<1, every self-adjoint element of a von Neumann algebra admits strong-operator paving over each maximal abelian subalgebra with at most 5×108ε−25\times10^8\varepsilon ^{-2}5×108ε−2 projections.

    The bound is uniform over representations and requires no separability or conditional-expectation assumption.
