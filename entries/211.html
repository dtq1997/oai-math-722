随机平面地图的几何、扩散与谱

Geometry, diffusion, and spectra of random planar maps

首发 · 2026-10-067 篇论文部分形式化

临界 Fortuin–Kasteleyn 平面地图在 0<q≤40<q\le40<q≤4 时收敛于 Liouville 量子引力球面，在 q>4q>4q>4 时收敛于布朗连续随机树，从而确立了从曲面到树的几何转变。

对于 FK–Ising 地图和按生成树加权的地图，平稳随机游走收敛于极限球面上的 Liouville 布朗运动。

Critical Fortuin–Kasteleyn planar maps converge to Liouville quantum gravity spheres for 0<q≤40<q\le40<q≤4 and to the Brownian continuum random tree for q>4q>4q>4, establishing the surface-to-tree geometric transition.

For FK–Ising and spanning-tree-weighted maps, stationary random walks converge to Liouville Brownian motion on the limiting sphere.

The FK–Ising spectral result also gives convergence of eigenvalues and heat traces, using the stated Brownian/LQG inputs.

    Random Walks on Critical FK–Ising Maps and Liouville Brownian Motion

    摘要

    利用谱问题姊妹论文中的几何和电学结果，我们证明了临界球面 FK–Ising 平面地图上的平稳随机游走收敛于通常的单位面积 3\sqrt33​-量子球面上的 Liouville 布朗运动。

    该游走从所有关联的半边中均匀选取，保留自环和重边。

    以角测度作为平稳分布时，确定性的时间加速因子恰好等于地图的边数。

    对于任何产生度量测度极限的确定性度量尺度，该收敛都保留同一个曲面，以及任意固定有限个条件独立的游走及其时间参数。

    英文原文

    We prove that stationary random walks on critical spherical FK–Ising planar maps converge to Liouville Brownian motion on the ordinary unit-area 3\sqrt33​-quantum sphere, using the geometric and electrical results of the spectral companion.

    The walk chooses uniformly among all incident half-edges, retaining loops and multiple edges.

    With the corner measure as the stationary law, the deterministic time acceleration is exactly the number of map edges.

    For any deterministic metric scale giving the metric-measure limit, convergence retains that same surface and any fixed finite number of conditionally independent walks with their time parameters.

  2. 临界 FK–Ising 平面地图的谱收敛

    Spectral convergence for critical FK–Ising planar maps

    摘要

    利用关于共形和度量测度的姊妹论文结果，以及文中陈述的布朗运动/Liouville 量子引力输入结果，我们证明了临界球面 FK–Ising 地图到通常的单位面积 3\sqrt33​-量子球面上的 Liouville 布朗运动的谱收敛。

    离散游走的总尝试速率为一，使用地图的每条边，包括自环并计入重数，且以角测度作为平稳分布。

    将时间加速地图边数倍，可得到度量测度空间、计入重数并作补齐的全部有序特征值，以及热迹的联合收敛，其中热迹在严格正的时间上局部一致收敛。

    在这些约定下，电导率和时钟常数均为一。

    英文原文

    Using the conformal and metric-measure companion results and the stated Brownian/Liouville quantum gravity inputs, we prove spectral convergence for critical spherical FK–Ising maps to Liouville Brownian motion on the ordinary unit-area 3\sqrt33​-quantum sphere.

    The discrete walk has total attempt rate one, uses every map edge including loops and multiplicities, and has the corner measure as its stationary law.

    Accelerating time by the number of map edges gives joint convergence of the metric-measure space, all ordered eigenvalues with multiplicities and padding, and the heat trace locally uniformly at strictly positive times.

    The conductivity and clock constant are one in these conventions.

  3. 生成树加权平面地图上随机游走的线性时钟

    A Linear Clock for Random Walk on Tree-Weighted Planar Maps

    摘要

    我们证明了以生成树数目为权重抽取的平面地图上的平稳随机游走，收敛于单位面积 2\sqrt22​-Liouville 量子球面上的 Liouville 布朗运动。

    该收敛联合保留条件路径分布与带测度的度量空间。

    该游走从所有关联的半边中均匀选取，并从平稳的度数测度出发。

    当总尝试速率为一，且连续 Dirichlet 型带有因子 1/2 时，时间加速因子恰好等于地图的边数。

    该结果使用了同一系综在姊妹论文中的轮廓极限和度量极限。

    英文原文

    We prove that stationary random walk on a planar map sampled with weight equal to its number of spanning trees converges to Liouville Brownian motion on the unit-area 2\sqrt22​-Liouville quantum sphere.

    The convergence retains the conditional path law jointly with the measured metric space.

    The walk chooses uniformly among all incident half-edges and starts from the stationary degree measure.

    For total attempt rate one and the continuum Dirichlet form with factor 1/2, the time acceleration is exactly the number of map edges.

    The result uses the companion contour and metric limits for this same ensemble.

  5. 临界 Liouville 量子球面与 q=4 时 FK 地图的几何极限

    The critical Liouville quantum sphere and geometric limits of FK maps at q=4

    摘要

    我们将单位面积临界 Liouville 量子球面的场与面积分布构造为通常的次临界量子球面的极限，并为其赋予临界内蕴度量。

    随后我们证明，通过等边 flag 单值化嵌入的 q = 4 时的球面 Fortuin–Kasteleyn 平面地图，联合收敛于由独立嵌套共形环系综 CLE4 装饰的这个球面。

    在确定性距离归一化下，该收敛包括面积测度、完整的嵌入距离函数以及每个宏观界面，并沿所有正整数边数成立。

    英文原文

    We construct the field and area law of the unit-area critical Liouville quantum sphere as a limit of ordinary subcritical quantum spheres, and equip it with its critical intrinsic metric.

    We then prove that spherical Fortuin–Kasteleyn planar maps at q = 4, embedded by their equilateral flag uniformizations, converge jointly to this sphere decorated by an independent nested conformal loop ensemble CLE4.

    With deterministic distance normalization, the convergence includes the area measure, the full embedded distance function, and every macroscopic interface through all positive integer edge counts.
