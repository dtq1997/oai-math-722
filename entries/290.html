Connes 双中心化子猜想与相对双中心化子

Connes' bicentralizer conjecture and relative bicentralizers

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

对每个具有可分预对偶的 III1\mathrm{III}_1III1​ 型因子及每个忠实正规态，证明了 Connes 双中心化子猜想。

更一般地，对于每个具有可分预对偶且容许忠实正规条件期望的 von Neumann 代数包含 N⊂MN\subset MN⊂M，构造了一个容许条件期望的 可均子代数 P⊂NP\subset NP⊂N，满足 P′∩c(M)=N′∩c(M)P'\cap c(M)=N'\cap c(M)P′∩c(M)=N′∩c(M)，从而解决了相对双中心化子猜想。

英文原文

Proves Connes' bicentralizer conjecture for every type III1\mathrm{III}_1III1​ factor with separable predual and every faithful normal state.

More generally, for every inclusion N⊂MN\subset MN⊂M of von Neumann algebras with separable preduals admitting a faithful normal conditional expectation, constructs an amenable expected subalgebra P⊂NP\subset NP⊂N with P′∩c(M)=N′∩c(M)P'\cap c(M)=N'\cap c(M)P′∩c(M)=N′∩c(M), resolving the relative bicentralizer conjecture.

论文

  1. 保持连续核内交换子代数的带条件期望可均子代数

    Expected amenable subalgebras preserving core commutants

    摘要

    我们证明，每个具有可分预对偶及忠实正规条件期望的 von Neumann 代数包含关系 N⊂MN\subset MN⊂M，都包含一个带条件期望的可均子代数 P⊂NP\subset NP⊂N，使得 P′∩c(M)=N′∩c(M)P'\cap c(M)=N'\cap c(M)P′∩c(M)=N′∩c(M)，其中 c(M)c(M)c(M) 表示 M 的连续核。

    这解决了此情形下的相对双中心化子猜想。

    英文原文

    We prove that every inclusion N⊂MN\subset MN⊂M of von Neumann algebras with separable preduals and a faithful normal conditional expectation contains an expected amenable subalgebra P⊂NP\subset NP⊂N such that P′∩c(M)=N′∩c(M)P'\cap c(M)=N'\cap c(M)P′∩c(M)=N′∩c(M), where c(M)c(M)c(M) denotes the continuous core of M.

    This resolves the relative bicentralizer conjecture in this setting.

  2. 模谱平均的有界恢复

    Bounded recovery for modular spectral averages

    主结果已列入形式化清单
    摘要

    对具有一个忠实正规态且该态的中心化子仅由标量组成的 von Neumann 代数，我们证明一个有界谱恢复定理。

    任意有界 Hilbert 空间算子在收缩的模谱带上所具有的范数平方的正平均值，可以在谱支集收缩且一致有界的代数元素上恢复。

    我们将该定理应用于谱交织刚性，并得到可分预对偶的 III1 型因子之双中心化子平凡性的另一证明。

    英文原文

    We prove a bounded spectral recovery theorem for a von Neumann algebra with a faithful normal state whose centralizer consists only of scalars.

    A positive averaged squared norm for an arbitrary bounded Hilbert-space operator on shrinking modular spectral bands can be recovered on uniformly bounded algebra elements with shrinking spectral support.

    We apply this theorem to spectral intertwining rigidity and obtain an alternative proof of bicentralizer triviality for type III1 factors with separable predual.

# Relative bicentralizers and modular spectral recovery

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Bounded recovery for modular spectral averages](../../preprints/Bounded-recovery-for-modular-spectral-averages-September-23-2026/Bounded-recovery-for-modular-spectral-averages-September-23-2026.pdf)

## Scope

For a faithful normal state with scalar centralizer in the stated standard-space representation, the formalized result converts positive modular spectral averages into uniformly bounded algebra elements. Given unit vectors in shrinking spectral bands around a real number $s$ and a positive limiting symmetric average for a bounded operator $T$, it finds a subsequence of bounded algebra elements whose images under $T$ stay uniformly nonzero and whose spectral bands have four times the original widths. The absolute bicentralizer conjecture is not included.
