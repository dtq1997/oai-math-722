完美匹配的指数半正定复杂度

Exponential semidefinite complexity of perfect matching

OpenAI · 2026-10-061 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

证明了完美匹配多胞体的每个精确半正定提升都具有指数规模，从而对 Rothvoss 关于多项式规模提升的问题给出了否定回答。

即使对其奇割松弛矩阵作任意固定的平移 0<ρ<10<\rho<10<ρ<1，这一界仍对所得矩阵的半正定秩成立，且允许任意实半正定因子。

英文原文

Proves that every exact semidefinite lift of the perfect matching polytope has exponential size, answering Rothvoss's polynomial-size lift question negatively.

The bound holds even for the positive semidefinite rank of its odd-cut slack matrix after any fixed shift 0<ρ<10<\rho<10<ρ<1, allowing arbitrary real positive semidefinite factors.

# Exponential semidefinite complexity of perfect matching

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Exponential PSD rank of positively shifted matching matrices](../../preprints/Exponential-PSD-rank-of-positively-shifted-matching-matrices-October-5-2026/shifted-matching-psd.pdf)

## Scope

The paper proves exponential PSD-rank lower bounds for positively shifted matching matrices. The linked formalization records the related superpolynomial lower bounds: for every fixed $C>0$, the PSD rank of the selected perfect-matching slack matrix exceeds $n^C$ for all sufficiently large even $n$. Every exact affine semidefinite lift of the perfect-matching polytope likewise requires matrix size greater than $n^C$.

These selected statements give superpolynomial growth. They do not state the paper's exponential bound for every fixed positive shift.
