弱相互作用及无序 Ising 模型的共形普适性

Conformal universality for weakly interacting and disordered Ising models

OpenAI · 2026-10-066 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

方格 Ising 模型的弱、有限程、具有方格对称性的涉及偶数个自旋的多自旋扰动保持其临界体相自旋和能量极限；具有方格对称性的弱轮廓相互作用也产生弦状SLE3\mathrm{SLE}_3SLE3​界面极限。

对于服从任意固定的有界、非退化、均值为零分布的充分弱的独立同分布键无序，临界自旋界面关于环境依概率收敛到同一分布。

英文原文

Weak finite-range square-symmetric even multispin perturbations of the square-lattice Ising model preserve critical bulk spin and energy limits; weak square-symmetric contour interactions also yield chordal SLE3\mathrm{SLE}_3SLE3​ interface limits.

With sufficiently weak iid bond disorder of any fixed bounded nondegenerate mean-zero law, critical spin interfaces converge to the same law in probability over environments.

  1. 一般弱随机键 Ising 模型的淬火 SLE₃ 极限

    Quenched SLE₃ limits for general weak random-bond Ising models

    摘要

    对于键为 Je=1+εξeJ_e=1+\varepsilon\xi_eJe​=1+εξe​ 的平面 Ising 模型，其中 ξe 独立同分布，且共同分布为任意固定的有界、非退化、均值为零的分布，我们证明了在 ε > 0 充分小时，淬火弦 SLE3 收敛成立。

    温度取为自发磁化阈值。

    在确定性的 Jordan 区域逼近中，完整有向曲线分布的收敛对环境依概率成立。

    英文原文

    For the planar Ising model with bonds Je=1+εξeJ_e=1+\varepsilon\xi_eJe​=1+εξe​, where the ξe have any fixed bounded, nondegenerate, mean-zero iid law, we prove quenched chordal SLE3 convergence for sufficiently small ε > 0.

    The temperature is the spontaneous-magnetization threshold.

    Convergence holds in probability over environments for the full oriented curve law in deterministic Jordan-domain approximations.

  2. 弱随机键 Ising 模型的淬火 SLE₃ 普适性

    Quenched SLE₃ Universality for the Weak Random-Bond Ising Model

    摘要

    对于具有充分弱的独立、对称、二值铁磁键的平面 Ising 模型，我们证明了淬火弦 SLE3 收敛。

    温度取为由自发磁化定义的临界点。

    在确定性的 Jordan 区域逼近中，完整有向曲线分布的收敛对环境依概率成立。

    英文原文

    We prove quenched chordal SLE3 convergence for the planar Ising model with sufficiently weak independent symmetric two-valued ferromagnetic bonds.

    The temperature is the critical point defined by spontaneous magnetization.

    Convergence holds in probability over environments for the full oriented curve law in deterministic Jordan-domain approximations.

  3. 弱无序平面 Ising 模型中的对数级相对涨落

    Logarithmic Relative Fluctuations in the Weakly Disordered Planar Ising Model

    摘要

    假设所述的确定性临界参照估计成立，我们证明了，对于每个充分小的固定 ε > 0，具有独立等概率键 1±ε1\pm\varepsilon1±ε 的方格 Ising 模型中，淬火临界自旋关联的相对二阶矩按 (log⁡r)1/4+o(1)(\log r)^{1/4+o(1)}(logr)1/4+o(1) 增长。

    该关联在物理临界温度处计算，并先取热力学极限，再取无序矩和长距离极限。

    英文原文

    Assuming the stated deterministic critical-reference estimates, we prove that the relative second moment of the quenched critical spin correlation in the square-lattice Ising model with independent fair bonds 1±ε1\pm\varepsilon1±ε grows as (log⁡r)1/4+o(1)(\log r)^{1/4+o(1)}(logr)1/4+o(1) for each sufficiently small fixed ε > 0.

    The correlation is evaluated at the physical critical temperature, with the thermodynamic limit taken before the disorder moments and the large-distance limit.

  4. 弱相互作用下 Ising 体内关联的共形普适性

    Conformal universality of bulk Ising correlations under weak interactions

    摘要

    对于受到充分小、具有正方形对称性、有限程且可取任一符号的偶数自旋多体扰动的方格 Ising 模型，我们证明了体内自旋与能量混合关联的共形普适性。

    同一个临界温度分支和两个场归一化适用于每个带自由、全正或全负边界条件的有界单连通 C2 Jordan 区域，也适用于周期热力学平面态。

    能量在其实际状态下作中心化。

    英文原文

    We prove conformal universality of mixed bulk spin and energy correlations for the square-lattice Ising model with sufficiently small, square-symmetric, finite-range even multispin perturbations of either sign.

    One critical-temperature branch and two field normalizations apply in every bounded simply connected C2 Jordan domain with free, plus, or minus boundary conditions, and in the periodic thermodynamic plane state.

    Energies are centered in their actual states.

  5. 弱有限程 Ising 相互作用的 SLE3 普适性

    SLE3 universality for weak finite-range Ising interactions

    摘要

    我们证明了，平面 Ising 轮廓能量的每个充分小、具有正方形对称性的有限程扰动，都存在一个与区域无关的逆温度，使其自旋界面收敛于弦 SLE3。

    英文原文

    We prove that every sufficiently small, square-symmetric finite-range perturbation of the planar Ising contour energy has a domain-independent inverse temperature at which its spin interface converges to chordal SLE3.

    The result allows interactions of either sign, arbitrary admissible exterior contours, and uniformly approximated Jordan domains.

    Convergence holds for the full oriented curve law in uniform distance modulo increasing reparametrization.

  6. 临界 Ising 模型中的缓冲比较与停止带逼近

    Buffered comparison and stopping-band resolution in critical Ising

    摘要

    我们证明了方格上临界最近邻 Ising 模型的逐点比较定理和有限停止带逼近定理。

    比较允许任意共同钉扎的自旋：似然比由删去这些顶点后的图上的零场 FK 连接概率控制。

    对于穿过固定正宽度带状区域的界面探索，有限多个带符号的正则屏障可在全变差意义下逼近每个良好的条件目标分布，且这种逼近对有界可观测量和充分细的网格是一致的。

    英文原文

    We prove pointwise comparison and finite stopping-band approximation theorems for the critical nearest-neighbor Ising model on the square lattice.

    Arbitrary common pinned spins are allowed in the comparison: the likelihood ratio is controlled by a zero-field FK connection probability on the graph with those vertices deleted.

    For an interface exploration across a band of fixed positive width, finitely many regular signed barriers approximate every good conditional target law in total variation, uniformly over bounded observables and sufficiently fine meshes.

# Conformal universality for weakly interacting and random-bond Ising models

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Buffered comparison and stopping-band resolution in critical Ising](../../preprints/Buffered-comparison-and-stopping-band-resolution-in-critical-Ising-September-23-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The formalized result compares conditional Ising probabilities on finite graphs when two boundary mixtures differ but a common set of spins is pinned. If $q_0$ is the associated zero-field Fortuin–Kasteleyn connection probability after deleting the pinned vertices, the likelihood ratio lies between $\exp(-4\mathrm{artanh}\,q_0)$ and $\exp(4\mathrm{artanh}\,q_0)$. The statement allows arbitrary external fields and arbitrary probability mixtures satisfying the stated disjointness conditions. The paper's finite stopping-band approximation theorem is outside this selected statement.
