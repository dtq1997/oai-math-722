三维及四维 Kakeya

Kakeya in three and four dimensions

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

解决了三维 Kakeya 极大猜想和四维 Kakeya Hausdorff 维数猜想。

在三维情形中，半径为 δ\deltaδ 的管极大算子将 L3(R3)L^3(\mathbb R^3)L3(R3) 映到 L3(S2)L^3(S^2)L3(S2)，且对每个 ε>0\varepsilon>0ε>0，其范数为 Oε(δ−ε)O_\varepsilon(\delta^{-\varepsilon})Oε​(δ−ε)。

在四维情形中，每个在所有方向上都包含单位线段的集合，其 Hausdorff 维数均为四。

英文原文

Resolves the Kakeya maximal conjecture in three dimensions and the Hausdorff-dimension conjecture in four.

In three dimensions, the radius-δ\deltaδ tube maximal operator maps L3(R3)L^3(\mathbb R^3)L3(R3) to L3(S2)L^3(S^2)L3(S2) with norm Oε(δ−ε)O_\varepsilon(\delta^{-\varepsilon})Oε​(δ−ε) for every ε>0\varepsilon>0ε>0.

In four dimensions, every set containing a unit segment in every direction has Hausdorff dimension four.

  1. 三维 Kakeya 极大函数猜想

    The Kakeya maximal conjecture in three dimensions

    摘要

    我们证明了三维 Kakeya 极大猜想。

    对于每个 ε > 0，半径为 δ 的单位长度管上的极大平均将 L3(R3)L^3(\mathbb R^3)L3(R3) 映到 L3(S2)L^3(S^2)L3(S2)，范数至多为 Cεδ−εC_\varepsilon\delta^{-\varepsilon}Cε​δ−ε。

    英文原文

    We prove the Kakeya maximal conjecture in three dimensions.

    For every ε > 0, the maximal average over unit tubes of radius δ maps L3(R3)L^3(\mathbb R^3)L3(R3) to L3(S2)L^3(S^2)L3(S2) with norm at most Cεδ−εC_\varepsilon\delta^{-\varepsilon}Cε​δ−ε.

  2. 每个四维 Kakeya 集都具有满 Hausdorff 维数

    Every four-dimensional Kakeya set has full Hausdorff dimension

    摘要

    我们证明了四维 Hausdorff 维数 Kakeya 猜想：ℝ4 中每个在所有方向上都包含单位线段的子集，其 Hausdorff 维数均为四。

    对该集合以及见证这一性质的线段族，均不施加紧性或正则性假设。

    英文原文

    We prove the four-dimensional Hausdorff-dimension Kakeya conjecture: every subset of ℝ4 containing a unit line segment in every direction has Hausdorff dimension four.

    No compactness or regularity assumption is imposed on the set or its witnessing line family.
