素数次、四次及六次的环面包等分布

Torus-packet equidistribution in prime, quartic, and sextic degrees

OpenAI · 2026-10-063 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

证明了来自全实数域、按体积加权的完整环面包按 Haar 测度等分布，且不发生质量逃逸：范围包括固定为至少五的素数次数时的任意格及指定局部类型，本原四次数域中任意整序的 Picard 包，以及本原六次数域中极大整序的理想类包。

这里“本原”指不存在真中间域；相关整序或数域的判别式趋于无穷。

英文原文

Proves Haar equidistribution without escape of mass for complete volume-weighted torus packets from totally real fields: arbitrary lattices and prescribed local types in fixed prime degree at least five, arbitrary-order Picard packets in primitive quartic fields, and maximal-order ideal-class packets in primitive sextic fields.

Here primitive means having no proper intermediate field; the relevant order or field discriminant tends to infinity.

  1. 任意局部类型的素数次环面包的等分布

    Equidistribution of Prime-Degree Torus Packets with Arbitrary Local Type

    摘要

    对于任意固定的至少为五的素数次数的全实域，我们证明了高维 Duke 等分布问题的包形式，允许满格具有任意局部位似类型。

    当乘子整序的判别式趋于无穷时，以体积加权的包测度收敛到 Haar 概率测度，且没有质量逃逸。

    英文原文

    We prove the packet form of the higher-dimensional Duke equidistribution problem for totally real fields of any fixed prime degree at least five, allowing arbitrary local homothety types of full lattices.

    As the multiplier-order discriminant tends to infinity, the volume-weighted packet measures converge to Haar probability measure, with no escape of mass.

  2. 任意整序的本原四次环面包的等分布

    Equidistribution of primitive quartic torus packets for arbitrary orders

    摘要

    设 K 遍历没有真中间域的全实四次域，并设 O\mathcal OO 是 K 中的任意整序。

    我们证明，当 ∣Disc(O)∣|\mathop{\mathrm{Disc}}\nolimits (\mathcal O)|∣Disc(O)∣ 趋于无穷时，与可逆 O\mathcal OO 理想类对应的周期对角轨道包在按轨道体积加权后趋于等分布，且没有质量逃逸。

    该证明结合了测度刚性与一个三次预解式估计，后者在整除整序指数的素数处仍保持一致性。

    英文原文

    Let K range over totally real quartic fields with no proper intermediate field, and let O\mathcal OO be any order in K.

    We prove that the packets of periodic diagonal orbits attached to invertible O\mathcal OO-ideal classes, weighted by orbit volume, equidistribute with no escape of mass as ∣Disc(O)∣|\mathop{\mathrm{Disc}}\nolimits (\mathcal O)|∣Disc(O)∣ tends to infinity.

    The proof combines measure rigidity with a cubic-resolvent estimate that remains uniform at primes dividing the order index.

  3. 本原六次环面包的等分布

    Equidistribution of Primitive Sextic Torus Packets

    摘要

    我们证明，当域判别式趋于无穷时，没有真中间域的全实六次域的极大整序所对应的完整理想类包，在幺模格空间中趋于等分布，且没有质量逃逸。

    其极限是 Haar 概率测度。

    每个包都包含在所有坐标符号变换作用下的像，并以对角轨道的体积加权。

    英文原文

    We prove that the complete ideal-class packets of maximal orders in totally real sextic fields with no proper intermediate fields become equidistributed, with no escape of mass, in the space of unimodular lattices as their field discriminants tend to infinity.

    The limit is Haar probability measure.

    Each packet includes all coordinate-sign translates and is weighted by diagonal-orbit volume.

# Torus-packet equidistribution in prime, quartic, and sextic degrees

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Equidistribution of Prime-Degree Torus Packets with Arbitrary Local Type](../../preprints/Equidistribution-of-Prime-Degree-Torus-Packets-with-Arbitrary-Local-Type-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The formalization proves equidistribution of volume-weighted torus packets for totally real number fields of every fixed prime degree at least five. For any sequence of full lattices whose multiplier-order discriminants tend to infinity, the packet measures converge weakly to Haar probability measure and form a tight family, so no mass escapes. Arbitrary local homothety types are allowed, and the fields may vary along the sequence.
