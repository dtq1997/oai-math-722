时空 Penrose 不等式与刚性

Spacetime Penrose inequalities and rigidity

OpenAI · 2026-10-0613 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

对每个空间维数n≥3n\ge3n≥3，在主能量条件、弱未来俘获条件、正包围面积及所述衰减假设下，证明光滑单端渐近平坦初始数据的最优包围面积时空 Penrose 不等式。

它利用最小包围面积给出不变 ADM 质量的下界，并在附加视界假设下给出等号刚性。

带电情形的面积上界处理含电荷和磁荷的三维数据；高维纯电推广使用与之匹配的中性定理。

英文原文

Proves the sharp enclosing-area spacetime Penrose inequality for smooth one-ended asymptotically flat initial data in every spatial dimension n≥3n\ge3n≥3, under dominant energy, weak future trapping, positive enclosing area, and the stated decay assumptions.

It bounds invariant ADM mass below using minimum enclosing area, with equality rigidity under additional horizon hypotheses.

Charged upper-area bounds treat dyonic three-dimensional data; the higher-dimensional purely electric extension uses the matched neutral theorem.

    该定理允许任意第二基本形式、非零 ADM 动量及不连通边界。

    以 m 表示不变 ADM 质量、Q 表示总荷量的大小、rA 表示最小包围面积半径，则相应的界为 m ≥ Q 及 rA≤m+m2−Q2r_A\le m+\sqrt{m^2-Q^2}rA​≤m+m2−Q2​。

    Using the companion neutral spacetime Penrose theorem exactly at its stated strong-decay, future-trapped, positive-area, and future-timelike scope, we prove the sharp purely electric upper-area inequality in every spatial dimension n ≥ 4 for one-ended charged data satisfying the charged dominant energy condition and divgE=0\mathop{\mathrm{div}}\nolimits _g E=0divg​E=0, with the specified decay, integrability, and finite-flux assumptions.

  3. 时空 Penrose 不等式：包围面积、电荷与刚性

    Spacetime Penrose inequalities: enclosing area, charge, and rigidity

    摘要

    我们证明了三维和四维空间中关于不变 ADM 质量和最小包围面积的最优时空 Penrose 不等式。

    在各自指定端的约定下，中性情形的数值结论允许任意第二基本形式、非零动量、不连通的弱未来捕获边界以及有限多个端。

    在所述视界假设下，中性不等式中的等号可将原始外部数据重构为三维情形中 Schwarzschild 时空、四维情形中 Schwarzschild–Tangherlini 时空的类空切片；四维等号定理涉及连通的单端外部区域。

    我们还对具有非零动量的单端数据证明了一个三维电磁带电面积上界不等式，并分别给出了允许有限多个端的纯电静止参考系推论。

    最后，对于每个固定的横向无迹种子和指定的衰减解分支，我们证明了一个局部 Schwarzschild–anti-de Sitter 不等式。

    英文原文

    We prove sharp spacetime Penrose inequalities for invariant ADM mass and minimum enclosing area in three and four spatial dimensions.

    Under their respective designated-end conventions, the neutral numerical results allow arbitrary second fundamental form, nonzero momentum, disconnected weakly future trapped boundary, and finitely many ends.

    Under the stated horizon hypotheses, equality for the neutral inequalities reconstructs the original exterior data as spacelike slices of Schwarzschild spacetime in three dimensions and Schwarzschild–Tangherlini spacetime in four; the four-dimensional equality theorem concerns a connected, one-ended exterior.

    We also prove a three-dimensional electric–magnetic charged upper-area inequality for one-ended data with nonzero momentum, with separate purely electric rest-frame corollaries allowing finitely many ends.

    Finally, for each fixed transverse-traceless seed and prescribed decaying solution branch, we prove a local Schwarzschild–anti-de Sitter inequality.

  4. 轴对称电磁真空外部区域的 Kerr–Newman Penrose 不等式

    The Kerr–Newman Penrose Inequality for Axisymmetric Electrovacuum Exteriors

    摘要

    我们证明了最优的 Kerr–Newman Penrose 不等式 m2≥A16π+Q22+π(Q4+4J2)A.\displaystyle m^2\ge \frac{A}{16\pi}+\frac{Q^2}{2} +\frac{\pi(Q^4+4J^2)}{A}.m2≥16πA​+2Q2​+Aπ(Q4+4J2)​.

    这里 Q2=Qe2+Qb2Q^2=Q_e^2+Q_b^2Q2=Qe2​+Qb2​，且 J 是守恒的总角动量，包括其电磁贡献。

    该结果适用于所述单端拓扑及衰减类别中的光滑轴对称电磁真空外部区域，其边界连通、处于最外层、外侧面积极小且为未来边缘外捕获边界，具有 Coulomb 电磁渐近行为、零 ADM 动量，并满足显式假设的物理面积条件 A≥4πQ4+4J2A\ge4\pi\sqrt{Q^4+4J^2}A≥4πQ4+4J2​。

    不作极大性假设。

    在严格面积分支上，该类别内的等号刻画了原始数据为一个次极端的带电磁双荷 Kerr–Newman 时空的容许类空外部切片，且边界光滑地映射到一个未来视界截面或分岔球面。

    英文原文

    We prove the sharp Kerr–Newman Penrose inequality m2≥A16π+Q22+π(Q4+4J2)A.\displaystyle m^2\ge \frac{A}{16\pi}+\frac{Q^2}{2} +\frac{\pi(Q^4+4J^2)}{A}.m2≥16πA​+2Q2​+Aπ(Q4+4J2)​.

    Here Q2=Qe2+Qb2Q^2=Q_e^2+Q_b^2Q2=Qe2​+Qb2​, and J is the conserved total angular momentum, including its electromagnetic contribution.

    The result applies to smooth axisymmetric electrovacuum exteriors in the stated one-ended topological and decay class, with a connected outermost, outer-area-minimizing future marginally outer trapped boundary, Coulomb electromagnetic asymptotics, zero ADM momentum, and the explicitly assumed physical-area condition A≥4πQ4+4J2A\ge4\pi\sqrt{Q^4+4J^2}A≥4πQ4+4J2​.

    No maximality assumption is made.

    On the strict area branch, equality within this class characterizes the original data as an admissible spacelike exterior slice of a subextremal dyonic Kerr–Newman spacetime, with the boundary mapped smoothly to a future-horizon cross-section or the bifurcation sphere.

  5. 电磁尾部与 Kerr–Newman Penrose 不等式

    Electromagnetic tails and the Kerr–Newman Penrose inequality

    摘要

    我们构造了光滑轴对称电磁真空外部区域，当 J 是裸引力 ADM 角动量、而电磁场仅具有 O(r−2)O(r^{-2})O(r−2) 衰减且允许首项尾部随角度变化时，这些外部区域违反 Kerr–Newman Penrose 不等式。

    这些例子的总电荷和总磁荷均为零，尽管电场和磁场都非零。

    它们具有连通、最外层、外侧面积极小的未来边缘外捕获边界，并且严格位于物理面积分支上。

    我们还得到一些等号成立的例子，其原始数据不能在具有相应质量、角动量及电磁荷的 Kerr–Newman 时空中实现为类空切片。

    这些反例并未否证采用包含电磁修正的守恒总角动量或更强 Coulomb 渐近行为的表述。

    英文原文

    We construct smooth axisymmetric electrovacuum exteriors that violate the Kerr–Newman Penrose inequality when J is the bare gravitational ADM angular momentum and the electromagnetic fields have only O(r−2)O(r^{-2})O(r−2) decay, allowing angularly varying leading tails.

    The examples have zero total electric and magnetic charge even though both electromagnetic fields are nonzero.

    They have a connected outermost, outer-area-minimizing future marginally outer trapped boundary and lie strictly on the physical area branch.

    We also obtain equality examples whose original data admit no Kerr–Newman spacelike realization with the corresponding mass, angular momentum, and charges.

    These counterexamples do not refute formulations using conserved total angular momentum with its electromagnetic correction or stronger Coulomb asymptotics.

  6. 不要求极大性的反 de Sitter Penrose 不等式与原始数据的刚性

    The nonmaximal anti-de Sitter Penrose Inequality and original-data rigidity

    摘要

    我们对具有一个球面渐近 anti-de Sitter 端的光滑三维初始数据外部区域证明了时空 Penrose 不等式，这些区域满足所述衰减及可积性假设、主导能量条件，并具有紧致的弱未来外捕获边界。

    我们假设双曲度量四通量为未来类时；其 Lorentz 范数就是质量，而面积是对完整包围截面所取的下确界。

    在连通、最外层、外侧面积极小的视界子类中，等号刻画了原始数据为具有相匹配度量质量的 Schwarzschild–anti-de Sitter 时空中的一个类空超曲面。

    不使用极大性、演化或辅助可解性假设。

    英文原文

    We prove the spacetime Penrose Inequality for smooth three-dimensional initial-data exteriors with one spherical asymptotically anti-de Sitter end, satisfying the stated decay and integrability assumptions, the dominant energy condition, and a compact weakly future outer trapped boundary.

    We assume that the hyperbolic metric four-flux is future timelike; its Lorentz norm is the mass, and the area is the infimum over full enclosing cuts.

    On the connected outermost, outer area-minimizing horizon subclass, equality characterizes the original data as a spacelike hypersurface in Schwarzschild–anti-de Sitter spacetime with matching metric mass.

    No maximality, evolution, or auxiliary solvability assumption is used.

  7. 极大渐近双曲初始数据的 Penrose 不等式

    The Penrose inequality for maximal asymptotically hyperbolic initial data

    摘要

    我们对具有球面共形无穷远的三维极大渐近双曲初始数据，证明了最优的 Penrose 不等式。

    在主导能量条件、所述衰减及可积性假设，以及质量余向量为未来类时的条件下，不变质量以下述 Schwarzschild–anti-de Sitter 质量为下界：该质量与弱未来外捕获边界的最小包围面积相对应。

    边界可以不连通，且不对紧致部分的拓扑施加任何限制。

    英文原文

    We prove the sharp Penrose inequality for three-dimensional maximal asymptotically hyperbolic initial data with spherical conformal infinity.

    Under the dominant energy condition, the stated decay and integrability assumptions, and a future-timelike mass covector, the invariant mass is bounded below by the Schwarzschild–anti-de Sitter mass associated with the minimum enclosing area of a weakly future outer-trapped boundary.

    The boundary may be disconnected, and no restriction is imposed on the compact topology.

  8. Schwarzschild–反 de Sitter 数据共形扰动的局部 Penrose 不等式

    A local Penrose inequality for conformal perturbations of Schwarzschild–anti-de Sitter data

    摘要

    我们对正质量 Schwarzschild–anti-de Sitter 外部区域的充分小的极大真空共形扰动证明了渐近双曲 Penrose 不等式，这对每个固定的衰减横向无迹种子及每个满足所述衰减和质量假设的解分支都成立。

    该界使用边缘外捕获边界本身的面积；既不要求其为最外层，也不要求其外侧面积极小，更不要求体积项与边界项之间的支配条件。

    对于充分小的正参数，径向种子使等号成立，否则不等式严格成立。

    英文原文

    We prove the asymptotically hyperbolic Penrose inequality for sufficiently small maximal vacuum conformal perturbations of a positive-mass Schwarzschild–anti-de Sitter exterior, for every fixed decaying transverse-traceless seed and every solution branch satisfying the stated decay and mass assumptions.

    The bound uses the area of the marginally outer trapped boundary itself; it requires neither outermostness nor outer area-minimization, and no bulk-versus-boundary domination condition.

    For sufficiently small positive parameters, equality holds for radial seeds and the inequality is strict otherwise.

  9. 时空 Penrose 不等式与包围面积

    The spacetime Penrose inequality and enclosing area

    摘要

    在主导能量条件、弱未来捕获条件、所述带导数的衰减条件及正包围面积条件下，我们对每个空间维数 n ≥ 3 中的光滑单端初始数据外部区域，证明了最优的时空 Penrose 不等式。

    这些假设迫使 ADM 能量动量为未来类时。

    面积是在原始度量中对完整包围截面所取的下确界。

    在三维和四维中，度量的二阶导数和张量的一阶导数就已足够，且包围面积为正和未来类时性均由假设推出。

    英文原文

    We prove the sharp spacetime Penrose inequality for smooth one-ended initial-data exteriors in every spatial dimension n ≥ 3, under the dominant energy condition, weak future trapping, the stated differentiated decay and positive enclosing area.

    These hypotheses force the ADM energy-momentum to be future timelike.

    Area is the infimum over full enclosing cuts in the original metric.

    In dimensions three and four, two metric derivatives and one tensor derivative suffice, and both positive enclosing area and future timelikeness follow from the hypotheses.

  10. 共形流与具有面积最小化边界的 Riemann Penrose 不等式

    Conformal flow and the Riemannian Penrose inequality with minimizing frontiers

    摘要

    我们给出了每个维数 n ≥ 3 中数值形式的 Riemann Penrose 不等式的详细共形流证明，适用于标量曲率非负、完整紧致边界既在外侧极小又局部周长极小的完备渐近平坦外部区域。

    度量光滑地延伸穿过可能奇异的边界；既不假设自旋性，也不假设边界连通。

    该证明沿用了 Bray、Bray–Lee 和 Bi–Zhu 的共形流方法。

    英文原文

    We give a detailed conformal-flow proof of the numerical Riemannian Penrose inequality in every dimension n ≥ 3 for complete asymptotically flat exteriors with nonnegative scalar curvature and full compact frontiers that are outer minimizing and locally perimeter minimizing.

    The metric extends smoothly through the possibly singular frontier; neither spin nor frontier connectedness is assumed.

    The proof follows the conformal-flow approach of Bray, Bray–Lee, and Bi–Zhu.

  11. 时空 Penrose 不等式中的等号与刚性

    Equality and rigidity in the spacetime Penrose inequality

    摘要

    在所述最外层及衰减假设下，时空 Penrose 不等式中的等号将原始初始数据识别为 Schwarzschild–Tangherlini 外部区域的一个类空切片，且该切片在其视界处光滑拼接。

    我们在每个空间维数 n ≥ 3 中对一个强衰减外部区域类别证明了这一结论，并在三维和四维中对较弱衰减也证明了它。

    在三维中，有限分支定理迫使视界为单个球面，并对不连通边界给出定性的严格不等式。

    英文原文

    Equality in the spacetime Penrose inequality identifies the original initial data as a spacelike slice of a Schwarzschild–Tangherlini exterior, smoothly attached to its horizon, under the stated outermostness and decay hypotheses.

    We prove this in every spatial dimension n ≥ 3 for a strong-decay exterior class and for weaker decay in dimensions three and four.

    In dimension three the finite-component theorem forces a single spherical horizon and gives qualitative strictness for disconnected boundaries.

  12. 用于时空 Penrose 不等式的边界图像变形

  13. 面积受控的端替换与 CKS 类中的 Bondi Penrose 不等式

    Area-controlled end replacement and the Bondi Penrose inequality in the CKS class

    摘要

    我们将三维 Cha–Khuri–Sakovich 双曲面端替换为渐近平坦端，同时保留主导能量条件和每个固定的紧致内部区域。

    当 CKS 初始数据荷严格为未来类时时，这些替换渐近地不损失包围面积，且其 ADM 质量趋于不变 Bondi 质量。

    将这一构造与配套的数值形式的时空 Penrose 定理相结合，可得到这一类别中可能不连通的弱未来捕获边界的最优 Bondi 界。

    在视界处正则的 Schwarzschild 外部区域对每个正质量都达到等号。

    英文原文

    We replace a three-dimensional Cha–Khuri–Sakovich hyperboloidal end by an asymptotically flat end while preserving the dominant energy condition and each fixed compact interior.

    For strictly future-timelike CKS initial-data charge, the replacements lose asymptotically no enclosing area and their ADM masses tend to the invariant Bondi mass.

    Combining this construction with the companion numerical spacetime Penrose theorem yields the sharp Bondi bound for possibly disconnected weakly future trapped boundaries in this class.

    Horizon-regular Schwarzschild exteriors attain equality at every positive mass.

# Spacetime Penrose inequalities: enclosing area, charge, rotation, and anti-de Sitter extensions

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Area-controlled end replacement and the Bondi–Penrose inequality in the CKS class](../../preprints/Area-controlled-end-replacement-and-the-Bondi-Penrose-inequality-in-the-CKS-class-September-27-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The formalization replaces a three-dimensional Cha–Khuri–Sakovich hyperboloidal end by asymptotically flat ends while preserving each fixed compact interior, completeness, and the dominant energy condition. For strictly future-timelike initial-data charge, the ADM masses approach the invariant Bondi mass and the loss of enclosing area tends to zero.

The Comparator also includes canonical Schwarzschild equality examples at every positive mass, with $m_{\mathrm{Bondi}}=m$ and enclosing area $16\pi m^2$. The paper's general Bondi–Penrose inequality for possibly disconnected weakly trapped boundaries remains unformalized.

The implementation retains a conditional inequality for the connected marginal-boundary case, assuming the asymptotically flat exterior Penrose inequality. This supporting result is not selected as a substitute for the paper's main inequality.
