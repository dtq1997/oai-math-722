删除迹重构的超多项式下界与准多项式算法

Superpolynomial lower bounds and quasipolynomial reconstruction from deletion traces

OpenAI · 2026-10-063 篇论文Lean 代码已公开

对于 (0,1)(0,1)(0,1) 中每个固定的删除概率，要能重建任意长度为 nnn 的二进制串，需要 nΩ(log⁡log⁡n)n^{\Omega(\log\log n)}nΩ(loglogn) 条独立删除轨迹，从而排除了多项式样本量的重建。

对于已知且固定的有理数保留概率，一个一致解码器实现了准多项式的样本量界和运行时间界。

当删除概率至多为 n−εn^{-\varepsilon}n−ε，且 ε>0\varepsilon>0ε>0 固定时，这两个界都成为关于输入与参数编码长度的多项式界。

英文原文

At every fixed deletion probability in (0,1)(0,1)(0,1), reconstructing an arbitrary length-nnn binary string requires nΩ(log⁡log⁡n)n^{\Omega(\log\log n)}nΩ(loglogn) independent traces, ruling out polynomial-sample reconstruction.

A uniform decoder achieves quasipolynomial sample and running-time bounds for known fixed rational retention probabilities.

When the deletion probability is at most n−εn^{-\varepsilon}n−ε for fixed ε>0\varepsilon>0ε>0, both bounds become polynomial in the input and parameter encoding.

  1. 一致的准多项式时间迹重构

    Uniform quasipolynomial-time trace reconstruction

    摘要

    当字符串长度和有理保留概率已知时，我们给出一个从独立删除迹重构每个二元字符串的一致算法。

    对于每个固定保留概率，所需迹的数目和位复杂度关于字符串长度均为准多项式。

    更一般地，我们给出一个对所有有理保留概率一致成立的显式样本界，运行时间关于样本预算和二进制输入长度为多项式。

    如果对于固定的 ε > 0，删除概率至多为 n−εn^{-\varepsilon}n−ε，那么样本数和运行时间的界都是多项式。

    对于每个输入字符串，重建成功的概率至少为 2/3。

    英文原文

    We give a uniform algorithm that reconstructs every binary string from independent deletion traces when its length and rational retention probability are known.

    For each fixed retention probability, both the number of traces and the bit complexity are quasipolynomial in the string length.

    More generally, we give an explicit sample bound uniform over all rational retention probabilities, with running time polynomial in the sample budget and the binary input length.

    If the deletion probability is at most n−εn^{-\varepsilon}n−ε for fixed ε > 0, the sample and running-time bounds are polynomial.

    Reconstruction succeeds with probability at least 2/3 for each input string.

  2. 最坏情形迹重构：带频谱紧致掩码的最新锚点归纳法

    A latest-anchor induction with spectrally compact masks for worst-case trace reconstruction

    摘要

    在字符串长度和保留概率已知时，我们给出一个从独立删除迹重构字符串的改进最坏情形样本界。

    对于每个固定保留概率，迹的数目为准多项式量级：样本预算的对数为 O((log⁡n)3(1+log⁡log⁡(2n))6)O((\log n)^3(1+\log\log(2n))^6)O((logn)3(1+loglog(2n))6)。

    如果对于固定的 ε > 0，删除概率至多为 n−εn^{-\varepsilon}n−ε，那么多项式多个迹就足够。

    这些界适用于二元字符串，也适用于符号能够被精确观测的一般符号字符串。

    这些结论涉及样本复杂度，并不声称存在高效重建算法，也不声称具有相匹配的最优性。

    英文原文

    We give an improved worst-case sample bound for reconstructing a string from independent deletion traces, with its length and retention probability known.

    For each fixed retention probability, the number of traces is quasipolynomial: the logarithm of the sample budget is O((log⁡n)3(1+log⁡log⁡(2n))6)O((\log n)^3(1+\log\log(2n))^6)O((logn)3(1+loglog(2n))6).

    If the deletion probability is at most n−εn^{-\varepsilon}n−ε for fixed ε > 0, polynomially many traces suffice.

    These bounds apply to binary strings and to strings of general symbols observed exactly.

    They concern sample complexity and do not assert an efficient reconstruction algorithm or matching optimality.

  3. 迹重构的定量下界

    Quantitative lower bounds for trace reconstruction

    摘要

    对于每个固定删除概率 q∈(0,1)q\in(0,1)q∈(0,1)，从独立删除迹精确重建二元词在最坏情形下需要 nΩ(log⁡log⁡n)n^{\Omega(\log\log n)}nΩ(loglogn) 个样本，即使计算不受限制且只要求任意固定的正成功概率也是如此。

    这对二元迹重构的多项式样本问题给出否定回答。

    更一般地，当 q3log⁡n→∞q^3\log n\to\inftyq3logn→∞ 时，我们对每个固定的 0<c<1/(4log⁡2)0\lt c\lt 1/(4\log2)0<c<1/(4log2) 证明了 nclog⁡(q3log⁡n)n^{c\log(q^3\log n)}nclog(q3logn) 个样本的下界。

    这里 q 是已知的删除概率，且所有对数均为自然对数。

    英文原文

    Exact worst-case reconstruction of a binary word from independent deletion traces requires nΩ(log⁡log⁡n)n^{\Omega(\log\log n)}nΩ(loglogn) samples for every fixed deletion probability q∈(0,1)q\in(0,1)q∈(0,1), even with unrestricted computation and any fixed positive success probability.

    This gives a negative answer to the polynomial-sample question for binary trace reconstruction.

    More generally, when q3log⁡n→∞q^3\log n\to\inftyq3logn→∞, we prove a lower bound of nclog⁡(q3log⁡n)n^{c\log(q^3\log n)}nclog(q3logn) samples for every fixed 0<c<1/(4log⁡2)0\lt c\lt 1/(4\log2)0<c<1/(4log2).

    Here q is the known deletion probability, and all logarithms are natural.

# Quantitative trace-reconstruction bounds with a uniform decoder

The following describes the scope of the Lean formalization related to the following accompanying paper(s):

- [Quantitative lower bounds for trace reconstruction](../../preprints/quantitative-lower-bounds-for-trace-reconstruction-September-24-2026/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The formalization proves superpolynomial sample lower bounds for exact worst-case reconstruction of binary words from independent deletion traces, with unrestricted computation and any fixed positive success probability. For every fixed deletion probability $q\in(0,1)$, sample complexity grows faster than every fixed power of the word length; the minimum total-variation distance between two one-trace laws decays faster than every inverse power.

More quantitatively, along sequences with $q^3\log n\to\infty$, the required number of samples is at least $n^{c\log(q^3\log n)}$ eventually for every fixed $0<c<1/(4\log2)$. All logarithms are natural.
