  1. 随素数变化的有限 Smith–Toda 复形

    Finite Smith–Toda Complexes at Varying Primes

    摘要

    对每个非负整数 n，我们在某个依赖于 n 的素数 p 处构造一个 Smith–Toda 复形 V(n)V(n)V(n)。

    它是一个有限 p 局部谱，其 Brown–Peterson 同调为 BP∗/(p,v1,…,vn)\mathrm{BP}_*/(p,v_1,\ldots,v_n)BP∗​/(p,v1​,…,vn​)，配备典范余模结构，生成元位于零次。

    每个列出的生成元均在一次幂处就被消去。

    英文原文

    For every nonnegative integer n, we construct a Smith–Toda complex V(n)V(n)V(n) at some prime p depending on n.

    It is a finite p-local spectrum whose Brown–Peterson homology is BP∗/(p,v1,…,vn)\mathrm{BP}_*/(p,v_1,\ldots,v_n)BP∗​/(p,v1​,…,vn​) with its canonical comodule structure and generator in degree zero.

    Each listed generator is killed to its first power.
