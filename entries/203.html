域及离散赋值环上的 Donovan 猜想

Donovan's conjecture over fields and discrete valuation rings

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

证明了 Donovan 猜想：对每个素数 ppp，在每个固定的特征 ppp 的代数闭域上，有限群中缺陷群阶数有界的那些块只有有限多个 Morita 等价类。

还对每个固定的、剩余域为代数闭域的完备混合特征离散赋值环，证明了该猜想的整系数形式。

英文原文

Proves Donovan's conjecture: over each fixed algebraically closed field of characteristic ppp, blocks of finite groups with bounded defect-group order have only finitely many Morita-equivalence classes, for every prime ppp.

Also proves the integral form over each fixed complete mixed-characteristic discrete valuation ring with algebraically closed residue field.

  1. 代数闭域上的 Donovan 猜想

    Donovan's Conjecture over Algebraically Closed Fields

    摘要

    我们证明了每个特征为 p > 0 的代数闭域 K 上的 Donovan 猜想。

    对于每个固定的 K 和亏群阶数的界，有限群的块仅代表有限多个 K-线性 Morita 等价类。

    亏群不必是阿贝尔群，且该结果涵盖 p = 2 的情形。

    英文原文

    We prove Donovan's conjecture over every algebraically closed field K of characteristic p > 0.

    For each fixed K and bound on defect-group order, blocks of finite groups represent only finitely many K-linear Morita equivalence classes.

    The defect groups need not be abelian, and the result includes p = 2.

  2. Witt 向量上的整系数 Donovan 有限性

    Integral Donovan Finiteness over Witt Vectors

    摘要

    我们证明了整系数 Donovan 有限性：对于每个素数 p 和正整数 M，所有有限群中亏群阶数至多为 M 的块，在 W(F‾p)W(\overline{\mathbb F}_p)W(Fp​) 上仅有有限多个 Morita 等价类。

    亏群不必是阿贝尔群，且该结果涵盖 p = 2 的情形。

    对于每个固定的特征零完备离散赋值环，只要其剩余域是特征为 p 的代数闭域，同样的亏群阶数有界时的有限性就成立，其中包括分歧环。

    英文原文

    We prove integral Donovan finiteness: for every prime p and positive integer M, the blocks of all finite groups with defect groups of order at most M have only finitely many Morita equivalence classes over W(F‾p)W(\overline{\mathbb F}_p)W(Fp​).

    The defect groups need not be abelian, and the result includes p = 2.

    The same bounded-defect finiteness holds over each fixed complete discrete valuation ring of characteristic zero with algebraically closed residue field of characteristic p, including ramified rings.
