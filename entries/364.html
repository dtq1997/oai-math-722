Boltzmann 解存在时间内的动理学极限与涨落

Kinetic limits and fluctuations over the Boltzmann lifespan

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

在具有一致高斯衰减的每个正则动理学时间区间内，从三维巨正则 Newton 气体推导出非线性 Boltzmann 方程。

稳定的有限程径向势可以具有吸引势阱和奇异排斥核心；假设满足初始粒子对排除条件，并具有空间上可求和的高斯密度界与梯度界。

一篇配套工作给出了有限维的硬球高斯涨落，其中心为精确的微观期望，并由线性涨落 Boltzmann 方程支配。

英文原文

Derives the nonlinear Boltzmann equation from three-dimensional grand-canonical Newtonian gases throughout every regular kinetic interval with uniform Gaussian decay.

Stable finite-range radial potentials may have attractive wells and a singular repulsive core; initial pair exclusion and spatially summable Gaussian density and gradient bounds are assumed.

A companion gives finite-dimensional hard-sphere Gaussian fluctuations, centered at the exact microscopic expectation and governed by the linear fluctuating Boltzmann equation.

论文

  1. 动理学解正则区间上稳定径向势的 Boltzmann–Grad 极限

    The Boltzmann–Grad limit for stable radial potentials on regular kinetic intervals

    摘要

    我们从一个具有初始粒子对排除条件的巨正则 Newton 气体推导非线性 Boltzmann 方程。

    我们处理稳定的有限作用范围径向势，它们除了在原点处允许有一个排斥奇点以外均为 C2，并处理 C1 初始概率密度，该密度及其空间梯度满足在空间上可求和的高斯界。

    所有固定阶的重标度阶乘边缘分布都在 L1 中收敛，且该收敛在经典动理学解具有一致高斯界的每个有限区间上是一致的。

    该结果允许吸引势阱和动力学过程中形成的团簇，且对微分散射截面不作限制。

    英文原文

    We derive the nonlinear Boltzmann equation from a grand-canonical Newtonian gas with initial pair exclusion.

    We treat stable, finite-range radial potentials that are C2 except for an allowed repulsive singularity at the origin, and C1 initial probability densities with spatially summable Gaussian bounds on the density and its spatial gradient.

    All fixed-order rescaled factorial marginals converge in L1, uniformly throughout every finite interval on which the classical kinetic solution has a uniform Gaussian bound.

    The result allows attractive wells and dynamically formed clusters, without restrictions on the differential scattering cross-section.

  2. Boltzmann 解正则生存期内的硬球涨落

    Hard-sphere fluctuations on the regular Boltzmann lifespan

    摘要

    我们对三维确定性巨正则硬球气体证明了远离平衡态的有限维中心极限定理。

    初始概率密度是光滑的，且该密度及其空间梯度满足在空间上可求和的速度高斯界。

    该极限在经典 Boltzmann 解具有一致的速度高斯衰减的每个有限区间上成立。

    经验测度以其精确的微观期望为中心化项。

    其涨落收敛到线性涨落 Boltzmann 方程的高斯解。

    英文原文

    We prove a finite-dimensional central limit theorem away from equilibrium for a deterministic grand-canonical hard-sphere gas in three dimensions.

    The initial probability density is smooth, with spatially summable Gaussian velocity bounds on the density and its spatial gradient.

    The limit holds on every finite interval on which the classical Boltzmann solution has uniform Gaussian velocity decay.

    The empirical measure is centered by its exact microscopic expectation.

    Its fluctuations converge to the Gaussian solution of the linear fluctuating Boltzmann equation.
