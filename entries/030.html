虚二次域上的模性

Modularity over imaginary quadratic fields

首发 · 2026-10-061 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

证明了虚二次数域上椭圆曲线的模性猜想：每个虚二次数域上的每条椭圆曲线都具有模性，且在每个地方的局部参数都相匹配。

英文原文

Proves the modularity conjecture for elliptic curves over imaginary quadratic fields: every elliptic curve over every imaginary quadratic field is modular, with matching local parameters at every place.

OpenAI · overview.tex

论文

  1. 虚二次域上椭圆曲线的模性

    Modularity of elliptic curves over imaginary quadratic fields

    摘要

    我们证明了虚二次域上椭圆曲线的模性猜想：每条这样的曲线都具有模性，且在每个位点的局部参数均匹配。

    英文原文

    We prove the modularity conjecture for elliptic curves over imaginary quadratic fields: every such curve is modular, with matching local parameters at every place.
