特征零 Eisenbud–Green–Harris 与字典序加幂

Eisenbud–Green–Harris and lex-plus-powers in characteristic zero

OpenAI · 2026-10-062 篇论文未见公开 Lean 代码

在每个特征零域上证明了 Eisenbud–Green–Harris 猜想和字典序加幂猜想。

任意齐次理想，只要包含一个长度任意且各项次数均至少为二的正则序列，就存在一个具有相同 Hilbert 函数、各分次 Betti 数均不小于原理想对应数值的字典序加幂理想。

英文原文

Proves the Eisenbud–Green–Harris and lex-plus-powers conjectures over every characteristic-zero field.

Any homogeneous ideal containing a regular sequence, of arbitrary length and degrees at least two, admits a lex-plus-powers ideal with the same Hilbert function and no smaller graded Betti numbers.

论文

  1. Artin 情形下的字典序加幂 Betti 定理

    The Artinian Lex-Plus-Powers Betti Theorem

    摘要

    对于每个特征为零的域上任意正长度、各项次数至少为二的齐次正则序列，我们证明了 Eisenbud–Green–Harris 猜想和字典序加幂猜想。

    对于每个包含这种序列的齐次理想，相应的字典序加幂理想具有相同的 Hilbert 函数，并且在每个同调次数和内部次数上的分次 Betti 数都至少与之一样大。

    英文原文

    We prove the Eisenbud–Green–Harris and lex-plus-powers conjectures over every characteristic-zero field for homogeneous regular sequences of any positive length, with degrees at least two.

    For every homogeneous ideal containing such a sequence, the corresponding lex-plus-powers ideal has the same Hilbert function and at least as large a graded Betti number in every homological and internal degree.

  2. 相互交换的除法系数形式与 Artin 情形下的 Eisenbud–Green–Harris 猜想

    Commuting Division-Coefficient Forms and the Artinian Eisenbud–Green–Harris Conjecture

    摘要

    对于每个特征为零的域上任意正长度、各项次数至少为二的齐次正则序列，我们证明了 Eisenbud–Green–Harris 猜想。

    因此，每个包含这种序列的齐次理想，其 Hilbert 函数都等于一个包含相应纯幂的单项式理想的 Hilbert 函数。

    英文原文

    We prove the Eisenbud–Green–Harris conjecture over every characteristic-zero field for homogeneous regular sequences of any positive length, with degrees at least two.

    Thus every homogeneous ideal containing such a sequence has the Hilbert function of a monomial ideal containing the corresponding pure powers.
